Sol Fortuna
Astral Elf Paladin of Beshaba; resilient and resourceful

Sol Fortuna is a tall, pale, dark-haired astral elf paladin of Beshaba with white eyes, steady even after simulated death. He anticipates danger before it arrives and puts himself between it and his crew as their gunner and front line. Beshaba has laughed at his shoulder, and now a curious voice speaks to him of fate and whether he has truly escaped his origin.

PursuitA paladin of Beshaba whose luck turns unlucky, now having heard the goddess herself laugh at his shoulder.
