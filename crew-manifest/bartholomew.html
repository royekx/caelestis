Bartholomew
Human · Wilder · Spelljammer Academy

Bartholomew Grayson arrived at Caelestis a step behind the others, his orientation already finished elsewhere, and slid into a crew that had spent a day becoming one without him. He is a wilder — trained in the study of fauna, flora, and the living systems that tie a world together — and a druid of the Circle of Stars, reading the sky the way other people read a room. His own shape is not the only one available to him. He carries a staff that belonged to his family before him and a quiet Rashemi steadiness that the noise of the academy has not yet worn down.

Where the others chase the mission, Bartholomew tends to chase the living thing at the edge of it — the animal, the growth, the pattern in the stars nobody else stopped to notice. He took to the helm the first time he touched it, as if the ship had been waiting for him to ask. Boatswain Tarto told him plainly that what he was changed nothing once he was aboard a crew. He seems to have taken her at her word.
