Tumak Swan
Rogue (Soulknife); searching for somewhere they cannot yet name

Tumak Swan is a slight rogue swallowed by an oversized coat, muttering reassurances to himself, whose mind often seems divided. He holds the captain's chair for the crew and keeps a curtained corner of his bunk for things he would rather not explain. He is searching for a family he cannot yet name — and has begun to manifest powers and glowing half-runes he does not understand, with a tether now pulling him toward home.

PursuitSearching for his own family, now trusting the crew to help him find them.
