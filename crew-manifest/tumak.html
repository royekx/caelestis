Tumak
Human · Rogue · Captain

Slight of build, swallowed by an oversized coat that seems to belong to someone larger. Tumak mutters reassurances to himself in the spaces between actions, as though keeping steady requires constant negotiation. He is tougher than he looks — takes hits that should drop him and stays standing.

He is searching for family. He voiced it plainly, unprompted, during the walk to Mirt's quarters. Boogie told him simply: now they have a crew, and the crew will help him find them.

He carries two marks, each of them half of something: a curved, flowerlike half-rune on his foot with half a star above it, and a different half-mark on his shoulder. Two halves of two separate wholes. When he threw a spectral blade for the first time and it went wide, a voice — the Rune Keeper — said quietly that all things find their way home, and the blade curved and struck true. In that moment he felt a tether to home for the first time. Not its location. A connection to it.
