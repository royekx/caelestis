Boogie
Plasmoid Sorcerer; excels at spelljamming; carries part of a ship within himself

Boogie is a plasmoid cadet at Caelestis in a semi-transparent blue form with a glittering stone inside him and an inner rhythm that pulls him into dance. He instinctively helps and repairs rather than commands, and has taken the crew's medical role rather than the helm. The incomplete model ship that formed inside him matches one stolen from an instructor, and his sleep has begun to fill with visions of other versions of himself and a presence converging on him.

PursuitA drifting plasmoid with no family or lasting home, now carrying an incomplete model spelljamming ship that formed inside him for reasons he cannot explain.
