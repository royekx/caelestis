Aspects

Bartholomew Grayson · Circle of the Stars

In Vaelorix's garden, among the cultivated walls, you find a flower that has only just bloomed. You breathe it in. Its scent is familiar, but you can't place it.

You feel your body wanting to change. You know the feeling from every shape you've taken, but this pulls toward something else, something you've never been. You try to let it happen, but the pull fades. The flower has already given everything it had.

You take one of the garden's glass vials to keep it in. The stopper is stuck. You twist harder, and without meaning to, you feel the pull of the ape. You brace for the change, but only its strength comes. Your shoulders thicken and the glass creaks in your hand, and you wait for the rest of the ape to follow. It stops at your shoulders, half made.

You ease off, and the strength drains away. The vial slips from your fingers. You grab for it, and this time the spider answers. The glass sticks to your palm and stays there. Again, only that much comes: a spider's grip on a human hand, and nothing behind it.

Each piece settles into you and leaves the same hollow behind, the space the flower opened. Something is missing, a shape you've never taken, and none of your beasts fill it. Each of them gives you a piece toward making yourself whole.

You feel the two stars in your chest burn brighter, answering your call. Faintly, beside them, other stars begin to form, waiting to complete their shape.

You seal the flower in the vial, wondering what else it might reveal.

Feature

You can channel a single trait of a creature you know without taking its form.

Manifest Aspect. As a Bonus Action, you manifest one of your prepared aspects. It lasts for 1 minute, until you manifest another aspect, or until you have the Incapacitated condition. You can't manifest an aspect while in a Wild Shape form. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

Prepared Aspects. When you finish a Long Rest, choose a number of aspects from your catalog equal to your Proficiency Bonus. Each aspect is tied to the ability score it's listed under, and you can't prepare two aspects tied to the same score.

Using an Aspect. If an aspect lets you take an action or a Bonus Action, you can do so once per manifestation. Speeds, senses, and other passive benefits last for the full duration.

Learning Aspects. You can learn an aspect from a living creature that isn't humanoid in form. Constructs, Elementals, Oozes, Plants, and Undead have no aspects to learn. There are two ways to learn one, and the DM sets the DC:

  • Track. Spend 1 hour following and watching the creature, and make three checks: Wisdom (Survival) to track it, Wisdom (Perception) to observe it, and Intelligence (Nature) or Wisdom (Animal Handling) to understand it. If at least two succeed, you learn the aspect.
  • Glimpse. Take the Study action while you can see the creature within 30 feet of you. Make one of the checks above with Disadvantage. If it succeeds, you learn the aspect.

When you succeed, add one of that creature's aspects to your catalog. When you fail, you can't try that creature again for 24 hours. You can learn only one aspect per day.

Starting Catalog. Your catalog starts with 10 aspects: the four marked Known, plus six more of your choice from the catalog below. Together they must cover at least four ability scores.
