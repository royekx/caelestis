Bridge Crew Roles
Your role adds to what you can do aboard a spelljamming vessel.
Your role adds to your turn. You still take your action, bonus action, movement and reaction as normal, and every role gives you three things on top: a passive that always works, a menu of bonus actions, and at most one reaction.
  • Optional. You can spend your bonus action on your own features instead of your role's.
  • Fixed for the fight. If someone goes down, anyone can take their seat as an action.
  • Growing with you. Role numbers use your proficiency bonus (PB), and each role steps up at levels 5, 10 and 15.
  • Ship rolls. A ship weapon attack, a role check, a Grapple, an escape check, or a save against a ship effect.
  • AC cap. Role bonuses to the ship's AC add up to +5 at most.
  • The ship rules are on Ship Mechanics, and the eye stations on the tyrant ship's ship sheet.
Captain Command · Cha or Wis · Tumak
Controls the order and lifts the crew.
Passive — Command You roll your side's initiative. Allies who can hear you have advantage on saves against being frightened.
Rally. Bonus action. One ally gets a Command die to add to one ship roll before your next turn.
Order. Bonus action. One ally uses their reaction to take one of their role's bonus actions.
Grapple. Action, Close range only. Make a Cha (Intimidation or Persuasion) check contested by the enemy captain, or against DC 12 if there is none. On a success the ships lock together and move at half speed.
Fear eye. On the tyrant ship, your station holds the Fear eye.
Level 4Command die d6. Fear eye DC 14.
Level 10Command die d10. Fear eye DC 17.
Stepsd8 at level 5, d10 at level 10, d12 at level 15, when Rally also reaches two allies.

Spelljammer Pilot · spellcasting ability · Bartholomew
Chooses the range, and with it what the fight looks like.
Passive — Helm You move the ship and shift the range band for free, and you sense everything on and around the ship. You can cast your own spells at anything you can see on or around the ship. Takeoff is a DC 13 spellcasting check.
Shields Up. Bonus action, spend a spell slot. The ship gains +3 AC, plus 1 per slot level above 1st, until your next turn.
Jink. Bonus action. The first ship weapon attack against the ship before your next turn has disadvantage.
Burn. Bonus action. Shift the range band one more step.
Onboard Defense. Bonus action. A creature aboard makes a Wis save. On a failure it takes psychic damage and has disadvantage on its next roll.
Escape. Action, Long range only. Make the escape check (Ship Mechanics, Escape).
Level 4DC 14. Onboard Defense 1d6. Jink covers 1 attack.
Level 10DC 17. Onboard Defense 3d6. Jink covers 2 attacks.
StepsOnboard Defense gains 1d6 at levels 5, 10 and 15. Jink covers 2 attacks from level 10. From level 15, one Shields Up a fight costs no slot.

Navigator Coordination & hazards · Int or Wis · Gregory
Reads the fight so everyone else's rolls land.
Passive — Plot Once per hazard, reroll one failed check.
Read the Line. Bonus action. Your side's ship weapon attacks against one target have advantage until your next turn.
Find Cover. Bonus action. Roll d20 + PB + Int or Wis against DC 13. On a success the ship has +2 AC against one ship until the end of your next turn.
Find the Gap. Bonus action. Your side's next escape check has advantage.
Slowing eye. On the tyrant ship, your station holds the Slowing eye.
Level 4Find Cover +6. Slowing eye DC 14.
Level 10Find Cover +9. Slowing eye DC 17. Read the Line also crits on 19–20.
StepsAt level 5, Find Cover needs no check. At level 10, Read the Line crits on 19–20. At level 15, it covers two targets.

Gunner Artillery · Str or Dex, Cha at an eye · Sol
Takes the enemy ship apart.
Passive — Gun Captain You add your modifier to ship weapon damage. Class features that apply to weapon attacks apply to ship weapons, and Extra Attack lets you fire a second weapon.
Aim. Bonus action. Advantage on your next ship weapon attack this turn.
Called Shot. Bonus action, added to a shot. A hit deals half damage and adds one effect: a weapon is disabled; or the rigging is hit, so the target cannot Jink or Burn; or, on an open deck, 1d4 + PB crew drop. Through the Disintegration eye, the part is destroyed.
Quick Load. Bonus action, PB uses per long rest. Fire the same weapon once more. It does not work on an eye.
Disintegration eye. On the tyrant ship, your station holds the Disintegration eye.
Level 4A ballista at +5 for 3d10 + 3. Quick Load 2 per long rest.
Level 10+8 with two shots. Quick Load 4 per long rest. Called Shot deals full damage.
StepsLevel 5 brings Extra Attack instead. At level 10, Called Shot deals full damage. At level 15, ship weapons crit on 19–20.

Boatswain Ship operations · also called Bosun · Str, Dex, or Int with tools · Casey
Keeps the ship in the fight.
Passive — Upkeep You run the repair days, and only you repair Hull. You carry PB + 2 Patch dice (d10s), refreshed on a long rest.
Patch. Bonus action, from any zone. Restore 1d10 + PB Bulwark, adding any Patch dice you spend. Patch never repairs Hull.
Quick Fix. Bonus action, in the zone. DC 13 to end a mishap or fix a disabled part. Anyone else can try it as an action.
Brace. Reaction. Spend a Patch die to reduce the damage of one hit by the roll + PB.
Shore Up. Action, Below, once per fight. Restore 2d10 + PB Hull.
Expel. Action, tyrant ship, once per fight. Creatures in the Cargo Deck make a DC 13 Str save or are blown out through the intake.
Telekinesis eye. On the tyrant ship, your station holds the Telekinesis eye.
Level 4Patch about 7.5. 4 Patch dice. Brace about 7.5. Shore Up about 13.
Level 10Patch 3d10 + 4, about 20.5. 6 Patch dice. Brace about 9.5. Shore Up about 15.
StepsPatch rolls one more d10 at levels 5, 10 and 15.

Medic Triage & recovery · Wis or Cha · Boogie
Keeps officers and crew working.
Passive — Vigil Creatures within 30 ft of you add 1d4 to death saves. You carry PB + 2 medical dice (d8s), refreshed on a long rest.
Triage. Bonus action. Spend a medical die on a creature within 5 ft. It regains the roll + PB + your modifier, and one condition ends: poisoned, frightened, or a latched wisp.
Muster. Bonus action, in the zone. 1d4 + PB downed crew stand up.
Level 4Triage about 9.5. 4 medical dice.
Level 10Triage 2d8 + 8, about 17. 6 medical dice.
StepsOne more die at level 5. Triage heals 2d8 at level 10. At level 15, Triage heals two creatures.
