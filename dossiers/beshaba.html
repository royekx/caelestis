Beshaba
Goddess of misfortune; Sol’s patron

The goddess Sol serves. She answered him directly for the first time when he took a stroke of misfortune onto himself to save Boogie’s jump — a woman laughed three times, and by the third he knew whose laughter it was. She has since spoken to him once in the night, unhurried and curious, about fate and whether he has truly escaped his origin.
