Petty Officer Winston Ryeback
Giff · Refectory · Galley Command

Ryeback holds the Weeping Goddess kitchen at a bellow that carries over a room full of autognomes, thri-kreen, and faces drawn from every corner of wildspace. He names his own dishes and does not apologise for the names. The soup is knock-your-mama's-socks-off soup. He served it to Sol without a trace of irony.

He came to the table himself rather than sending anyone, and what he put in front of each cadet was chosen rather than allotted — a silvery starfruit the colour of moonlight on metal for Boogie, spare utensils offered to Gregory without comment, a second meatloaf for Tumak brought without question. Brusqueness on the surface. Attention underneath it.

Casey asked him what he did after work — what he kept for himself. Hot baths. Knitting. Ryeback paused before answering, and afterward gave Casey an extra portion. He came aboard the tyrant ship to cook and to command the deckhands.
