Winston Ryeback
Petty officer and galley cook at the Weeping Goddess

Petty Officer Winston Ryeback runs the kitchen at the Weeping Goddess refectory, commanding the din with a bellow and feeding the cadets with brusque affection. Beneath the gruffness he keeps hot baths and knitting for himself, and warms to anyone who asks kindly.
