Ostekk-6
Autognome · Clerical Unit

Found in the cargo hold with its lower half gone and its voice box destroyed, eyes and mouth still moving, trying to speak. It had been waiting a long time for anyone to come.

Gregory looked at its face and saw his own. He cast before he had fully decided to. The crew tried to heal it and learned the damage was mechanical rather than arcane — the core sound, the body past anything they could restore aboard.

Its designation came off the collection manifest it had been keeping: Ostekk-6. When Tumak reached for a way to speak with it and found a telepathic link he did not understand, Ostekk-6 finally answered. It warned of a specimen that takes the shape of other objects, and of a bloom that has spread through much of the ship.
