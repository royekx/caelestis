Saerthe Abizjn
Helm instructor at the Spelljammer Nexus

Saerthe Abizjn is a helm instructor at Caelestis's Spelljammer Nexus who runs the cadets' first true helm exercise over the Astral Sea. He reads each cadet's approach sharply, naming their difficulties as they arise.
