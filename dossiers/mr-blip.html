Mr. Blip
Autognome at the quartermaster's stores

Mr. Blip is an autognome running Caelestis's quartermaster's stores, unnervingly similar in appearance to Gregory. He supplies the cadets and keeps the lore of the academy's great spelljammers.

  • Charmed by Boogie into giving up a light hammer
  • Named Boatswain and Joffrey as great spelljammers of Caelestis, with older absences hanging over the stories
  • Arrived with officers in Mirt's chambers and confirmed the attack was likely a diversion amid a wave of burglaries
