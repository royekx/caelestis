Ostekk-10
Mechanical behir built by Ostekk

Ostekk-10 is a mechanical behir, serpentine and bluish-purple metal, with horns, four stout legs and dull blue eyes. It boarded the tyrant ship and destroyed most of its automaton units, sent by the original Ostekk to recover a device: the containment engine, fourth series. It speaks in clicks a thri-kreen can almost follow. It asked the crew to bring the device out of the Command Deck and promised to leave if they did. They kept it, told it they had found nothing, and put it out through the cargo hold while it searched the ship.
