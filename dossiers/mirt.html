Mirt the Merciless
Head of Caelestis

Mirt the Merciless is the head of Caelestis, occupying cluttered chambers full of status and excess, including an impossible wardrobe that opens onto astral night. He rewarded the cadets and let slip the wardrobe's origin in the Viren system.
