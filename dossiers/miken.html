Miken
Cadet · Rival Crew

Nervous, easy company, and quick to talk about the simulation as though he and Bartholomew had both come through it. He took the seat at a table with an empty chair neither of them named, though his eye returned to it more than once.

Boogie said quietly to Sol that Miken might be a better fit for their crew than the one they'd been given. When Boogie then let him know the crew had things to discuss, Miken picked up his bowl and went without taking offence.

He casts a bubble of silence over his bunk at night — a spell above a cadet's year. In the infirmary after the blast, unhurt but shadowed by a wince he could not quite hide, he asked to stay beside his crewmates. This is my crew, he said, and the door closed between them.
