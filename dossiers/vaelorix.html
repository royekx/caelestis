Vaelorix
Beholder master of the tyrant ship

Vaelorix was the beholder who owned the tyrant ship and flew it from its Command Deck, needing no helm and floating between its decks. Ostekk-6 served him and has not seen him since the creatures boarded. The crew found a beholder's body on the Command Deck platform, overgrown by a great flower, its central eye still cancelling magic in the room.
