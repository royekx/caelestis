Ostekk-2
Automaton unit of the tyrant ship

Ostekk-2 is one of the tyrant ship's automaton units, found on the Hollow Deck whole but for a missing arm, a dim light still in its eyes. Gregory woke it with a jolt of lightning, and it named the mechanical behir as the thing that destroyed its comrades, sent by the original Ostekk. The jolt holds it for only a few moments.
