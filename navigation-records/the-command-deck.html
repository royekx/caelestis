The Command Deck
Room

The Command Deck lies at the bottom of the tyrant ship’s shaft, past a plane where gravity turns over. It is a large room with side passages, its air drifting with colored light and its walls overgrown with flowers that loose wisps. At its center the beholder Vaelorix lies dead on the helm platform, a great dark flower grown out of his body, and his central eye still cancels magic in the room. Clockwork horrors stand frozen mid-stride among the flowers.
