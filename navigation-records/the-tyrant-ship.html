The Tyrant Ship
Vessel

The tyrant ship is a stone-carved beholder vessel drifting above Toril, believed abandoned when the crew boarded it. They mean to install a helm on its command deck and fly it through H’cathan space unnoticed, but its interior is choked with a lantern-wisp bloom, prowled by clockwork horrors, and said to hide a shape-shifting mimic.
