Gregory
Autognome; navigation-minded; senses direction and anomalies

Gregory is a bronze autognome cadet at Caelestis with glasses and a mustache, hesitant and observant, quick to notice what others miss. He has settled as the crew's navigator, his course-plotting proven under pressure. Far from his dead master, he feels a pull in his core drawing him toward somewhere he cannot yet name, and has begun to reach for others before the damage does.

Before the Academy
Came aboard Caelestis through the cargo docks, carrying his sealed letter. He caught a crate when its latch snapped and mended the latch by touch, and the whole cargo line steadied with it. Rindle Gearloft, a rock gnome working the docks, welcomed him; for a moment Gregory took Rindle for someone else.

Arrival orders

Course Correction

You step down from the gangway into the motion of Caelestis, a folded letter held carefully in your grip as cargo glides along guided tracks and voices rise and fall over the steady hum of engines. A crate jolts loose as a latch snaps with a sharp crack, and you catch it mid-fall, metal biting into your other hand as you steady the weight. The line shifts around you, workers adjusting, calls echoing across the platform as ships drift past in slow, deliberate paths.

"Hey—set that down to the side!" a voice calls. You look up. A rock gnome moves toward you through the crowd—dark hair tied back, goggles pushed up and smudged from use, a vest crowded with tools that clink softly as he walks. Oil marks his hands where he's wiped them without care. The sight of him sparks a brief, sharp lift in your chest. The details settle into place. Not him. The moment fades, quieter now. He gestures past you without slowing. "We'll cycle it later—don't hold the line." He's already moving on before the sentence finishes.

You hold the crate a moment longer. The broken latch hangs loose, edges split where the metal gave way. You shift the letter into your arm, freeing your hand, and rest your fingers against the break. The seam pulls together under your touch, the fracture smoothing, metal reforming as if the damage had never taken hold. Behind you, a crewman swears as the line jerks—then stops. The chain of crates settles all at once with a dull, unified thud, every inch of motion dropping out of it before easing back into its slow crawl. You turn and set the crate back into place, pressing it into line and securing it with a clean, final click.

"—I said set it aside, not—" The gnome returns, slowing as the words fall away. His eyes move first to the crate, then to the latch, then past you—back along the line. He watches it for a second longer than he should, like he's waiting for it to misbehave again. He steps in close and crouches, thumb tracing the edge where the break should still be, pressing, testing, working the hinge once, twice. It holds. He glances over his shoulder at the track, then back to you, the question already forming. "...You a cadet?" His gaze flicks to the letter tucked against your arm, the seal catching the light. "Why'd they send you through the docks…" he mutters, letting it hang before dropping it. He straightens, brushing his hands together, a small, easy smile returning. "Sorry—I thought you were on the cargo line. Speak up next time, yeah? Makes it easier on everyone." He taps his chest lightly. "Rindle Gearloft." A short nod follows. "Welcome to Caelestis."
