Bartholomew Grayson
Human; Druid; Wilder; newly arrived cadet with a natural helm affinity

Bartholomew is a human druid and wilder, a cadet at Caelestis with an uncanny natural affinity for the helm. Quiet and untroubled, he has slipped into the crew and settled as its spelljammer. When he shifts into a starry form, a two-starred constellation surfaces — a shape he carries from a fall he took as a child.

PursuitA newly arrived wilder cadet finding his place aboard a crew, with an uncanny natural gift for the helm.

Garden Bloom
Aspects

In Vaelorix's garden Bartholomew breathed a flower that had only just bloomed, and his body wanted to become something he had never been. Since then he can call a single trait out of a creature he knows, the ape's strength or the spider's grip, without taking its form.

Two stars burn within him. Others are faint.

Each star is a place for an aspect. A creature he studies can give him a trait, and the trait finds its star. What shape the whole constellation makes, he does not yet know.
