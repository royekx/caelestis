Boogie
Plasmoid Sorcerer; excels at spelljamming; carries part of a ship within himself

Boogie is a plasmoid cadet at Caelestis in a semi-transparent blue form with a glittering stone inside him and an inner rhythm that pulls him into dance. He instinctively helps and repairs rather than commands, and has taken the crew's medical role rather than the helm. The incomplete model ship that formed inside him matches one stolen from an instructor, and his sleep has begun to fill with visions of other versions of himself and a presence converging on him.

PursuitA drifting plasmoid with no family or lasting home, now carrying an incomplete model spelljamming ship that formed inside him for reasons he cannot explain.

Arrival orders

Born to Boogie

The crate sits crooked among the others, its lid bowed and stamped in worn ink: DO NOT TOUCH. The wood cracks under your hand as you pry it open. Inside, a single crystal rests against the bare interior—pitch black until it shifts and something beneath its surface stirs. Iridescent color moves through it in slow, liquid folds, sliding over itself, never settling into the same pattern twice. Your gaze drifts, then returns, pulled back each time it slips.

Your fingers brush it, and a violent current tears through your core. It seizes you in place, stretching a single instant far beyond its shape. The thought hits with it—I shouldn't have touched that. The room pulls inward. Sound thins. Space tightens. The crystal deepens in your grasp, its shifting colors widening, unfolding toward you. Your form begins to give. Edges loosen. Sections of you thin and draw out, others compress, your shape slipping as the crystal draws you inward.

Your sense of self fractures under the pull. Voices you've worn, movements you've borrowed, shapes you've tried on rise and dissolve, one after another, leaving space where they once sat. The pull continues, steady and certain, drawing you deeper into that shifting depth. Then a different feeling rises through it—sharp, insistent, undeniable. You want. The word carries weight as it forms. You want to be seen. You want to be someone. The thought holds.

Your form answers. The motion changes—gathering, pulling inward around that single point of want. The rhythm tightens, centered, yours. The crystal surges in response, its colors collapsing inward as the space between you disappears. Pressure builds, vast and immediate, then folds. The pull turns. The crystal compresses, shrinking into itself until it vanishes into you in a single, seamless instant.

The room settles around you. Sound returns. Your shape holds, steady and defined. Something rests at your center, dense and certain, a presence that remains as you move. Every motion follows it, clean and deliberate. For the first time, the movement carries intention. It carries weight. It carries you.
