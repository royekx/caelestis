Order of the Averters
The tip of the Order of All's spear: once the Discerners judge and the Vanguard sanctions, the Averters go in.

The Order of the Averters is one of the three orders of the Order of All, and the one that acts. In the Order's chain the Watchers gather intel, the Discerners interpret and judge it, the Averters act on it, and a sector's Vanguard sanctions. Once an intervention is sanctioned, the Averters form the team and go in to stop the ritual, the person or the plan driving a world toward catastrophe.

Beliefs

The Averters revere the All-Father, the Order's one god, and understand his charge to them as a calling to avert: to turn a catastrophe aside before it lands. With the rest of the Order they accept a Zenith as part of the order of things. What they turn aside is what would carry a Zenith's outcome past its own world.

Operations

  • Interventions. Stopping a ritual, a person or a plan that drives a world toward catastrophe, once the Vanguard has sanctioned it on the Discerners' counsel. A Monolith's arrival in the run-up is the clock they work against.
  • The three. For a Zenith the Order judges truly terrible, an Averter goes in as one of the three, with a Watcher and a Discerner.
  • Corrupted Monoliths. A corrupted Monolith holds what it takes and sends it to Tharizdun's prison. The Averters are the order that goes to one.
  • Broken worlds. Going where a Zenith or a disaster has broken a world and people can still be saved, and bringing them out, in Fleet ships.

History

The Averters are an old order. They have been one of the three since the Order of All, founded as one body, split as its following grew and like minds drew together.

Seats and branches

The Averters have a wing in each of the Order's houses. In the Caelestis Sector house they answer to the Watcher Vanguard, Captain-Commander Dejexic. An Averter Vanguard commands the Order's house in another sector.

Relationships

The Order of the Discerners

The Discerners judge whether an intervention is worth its cost, and the Averters carry it out. An Averter waiting on a Discerner's counsel while a Monolith rises is the Order's oldest argument in one room.

The Order of the Watchers

The Watchers' reports are what the Averters act on. Watchers mostly do not act; Averters exist to.

The Restorers

A movement inside the Order with members in every order, Averters among them. Restorers want the Order to go back for what it failed to save, not only to turn aside what is coming.

Caelestis and the Fleet

The Fleet carries Averters to where they are sent.

How to run them

The Averters are the tip of the spear: the Order of All at its most direct, and the order the crew is likeliest to meet in the middle of a crisis. They arrive with a sanction, a clock and a target.
