Vocath’s crew
A trading house with a Fleet contract, a grievance and a captive line, run by a merchant who believes his quarrel is the whole of it.

Vocath's crew is the family trading house of Vocath, a mercane merchant of long standing in the trade circles around Realmspace. It holds the Fleet contract for stores on Cairn Station's lane, and since the Fonains Monolith broke it has traded on Fonains. Vocath is in active commercial conflict with Mirt, headmaster of the Academy at Caelestis, and the feud is well known in the trade and older than any recent trouble at Caelestis. His aim is to disgrace Mirt enough to pry him loose from the school's protection, so that someone else can finish him. Vocath is not a fighter.

Beliefs

A trade is a trade. Vocath holds that what he buys is what he gets and that an asset who takes his money answers to him. He owns his grudge openly, and a merchant loudly feuding with a headmaster is doing something everyone can see.

Structure and ranks

Vocath is the top of his house. Qitru, his intermediary, recruits, pays and collects, and she is the one an asset actually meets. She serves Vocath and does not care who stands above him; for her, the top is Vocath. Below her are the crews of the house's ships, field agents, hired hands, and people paid to look away.

History

The house has traded along the lanes around Realmspace for a long time, and it has supplied the stations on Cairn's lane under the Fleet's stores contract for years. After the Fonains Monolith broke, Vocath moved fast: his people started their trade on Fonains and carry its gems and shards home. Before Boogie came to Caelestis, he pried open one of Vocath's crates of these shards, sealed with Vocath's personal seal, and a shard of the broken Monolith collapsed into him.

Operations

The house moves goods along Vocath's line under its house mark. Its ships supply the lane's stations and call at Cairn as a matter of course, and the Fonains trade runs gems and Monolith shards back into Realmspace. Its quarrel with Mirt is commercial, legal and social, and Qitru handles the assets the house buys.

How they look

The house carries two marks. Its house mark is the public trade mark of the family business, on its ordinary cargo and on the stores contract; the Fleet knows it from that contract and has never seen it on anything wrong. Vocath's personal seal is a balance scale with coins on one pan and a crate on the other. It is registered nowhere, so the Fleet registry returns no match for it, and Mirt's trusted circle knows it on sight. Vocath signs grudges, never crimes against the Fleet. Against the Fleet means Fleet ships, cargo and contracts; he counts the Academy as Mirt's, which is why the rod carries his seal. It is distinct from the Watchers' white spire crowned with blue-violet light.

What's hidden

  • The illithid arrangement. Vocath deals with an illithid, Zhauveth. His line supplies captives, and she supplies what N'ghathrod's taken know: which cargo moves when, which inspection is coming, where the patrols and escorts run. That knowledge is why the house prospers. Vocath deals with her, never with N'ghathrod, and has never heard of the colony she serves. He is not taken; a willing partner costs the colony nothing, and Zhauveth keeps him content.
  • The captive line. Pirates in Vocath's hire run the line, with one of his own crew riding with them as overseer, the one who pays them. They carry captives up Cairn's lane, and every few weeks, out on the lane near Cairn, the captives change hands from the pirates' ships to a colony courier. Vocath's personal seal is on the crates aboard their ships, which outsiders never see, and the overseer puts it on at a handover to show the pirates whom he speaks for. Zhauveth touches down at Cairn now and then to make the colony's deals with his overseers. After the raid the handovers stopped for a while. The Fleet put escorts on the lane against the pirate ship Crow's survivors named (SA-005), with orders to watch for transfers between vessels. When the handovers resumed they ran around the escort routes, which N'ghathrod's taken pass to Vocath through Zhauveth, and Cairn's taken station master has reported a clean lane every week since. The Fleet looked, found nothing on Cairn's word, and let it lapse. The stores contract and the captive line share the lane; the clean house mark covers the one, and hired pirates run the other.
  • The raid on Bellwether Crow. About a year ago Bellwether Crow, sent to look in on Cairn, came on his pirates mid-handover, the captives half moved from their two ships to a colony courier. With the captives strung between the hulls they could not scatter, so they ran up pirate colors, a crossbones, and struck to drive it off, and bit off more than they could chew. To draw the fight off his crew, Clint Dashforest went aboard the smaller ship, the one carrying Vocath's overseer, and took its helm, and a color pool opened by chance and took that ship, with Clint, the overseer and its pirates aboard. With the overseer who paid them and the sealed cargo gone through the pool, the fight was not worth it to the main ship, and Crow's crew drove her off. She stayed free. Crow's survivors reported the main ship's name and hull mark, recorded in WM-002, and a figure on the smaller ship wearing a seal, drawn from their accounts in FR-016; the Fleet hunts the ship (SA-005), and the seal matches nothing in its registry. The colony courier at the handover pulled away from the fight and carried word to Zhauveth that Crow was coming to Cairn. The house knows nothing of any device Clint may carry.
  • Fonains. The pool set the taken ship down on Fonains about a year ago, with the overseer and the crates under Vocath's seal aboard. Because the sealed cargo had gone through the pool with the ship, the overseer called Vocath by sending stone as soon as they were down. Clint got away, and the overseer and the pirates with him hunted him for a while, so that he could not report what he had seen on the lane. The overseer told Vocath that Clint died in the hunt, and Vocath believes his witness is dead. The house's trade on Fonains began after the Monolith broke. The shards travel in small crates, one to a crate, each under Vocath's personal seal, and Boogie pried one open before he came to Caelestis.
  • Joffrey, a decorated officer of the Caelestis Corps, is the informant Vocath believes is his. Zhauveth told Vocath he has an informant inside Caelestis, and Vocath uses it for his grudge against Mirt. In name Joffrey answers to Qitru; in fact he is the colony's, taken at Cairn during Crow's days of repair, and N'ghathrod runs him through Zhauveth. His reports to Qitru have never contained a lie. He tracks the shipments that carry Vocath's seal and handles what comes in. Fleet row · Dossier →
  • Three insiders. Joffrey is the hub of three insiders at Caelestis. He paid Jeffrey to pass the sealed crate the cadets later found, and the rod and the beacon came in the same way, under Mr. Blip's oversight without his knowing. Miken sabotages: Joffrey left the rod where he would pick it up. A third insider, the thief, steals, and Joffrey uses the thief to learn where things are kept and the Brig's protocols. Qitru told Joffrey about Miken; Miken does not know of Joffrey. Neither Miken nor the thief knows the other exists, and nothing either of them knows points at Joffrey.
  • The Academy thefts. The thefts at Caelestis took small things: station property, personal effects and Saerthe's miniature spelljamming ship. They never reached a working vessel. They ran small before Joffrey was placed and have grown since.
  • The rod. Vocath signed it with his personal seal on purpose, a message to Mirt that he can reach him, and he meant it to hurt. Qitru recruited Miken with money enough to set up him and his fiancé and told him it would only spoil the drill. He placed it, his own crewmates took the worst of it, and he regrets it. In the interlude after Voyage 006 he planted the beacon Qitru follows, and he reports to her by sending stone.
  • The Brig. Its location and access protocols are classified well enough that Vocath could never have found that vault. Joffrey found it through the thief and passed it to Qitru, so the strike happens because the informant named a target Vocath already wanted, and Vocath experiences the plan as his own. The grudge campaign against Mirt is cover that Vocath pays for himself.

How to run them

The crew already has Vocath's name; the real mystery is the second principal underneath his operation. He never appears in a fight. Pressure on him is commercial, legal and social, and the most dangerous thing the crew can do to him is prove he was used, which costs him the standing the feud was meant to win. Jeffrey is the softest thread in the operation and the easiest for the crew to pull. Qitru is a competitor for knowledge before she is a threat: anyone asking the same questions in the same port will cross her. Describe the personal seal plainly whenever the house's darker side surfaces; the house mark never leads anywhere.

Their arc

Qitru follows the crew to H'Catha on Miken's beacon. The house's Act 1 work at Caelestis ends in the graduation attack and its raid on the Brig; the sequence is on Opposition: the Vth'orama. The Fonains trade gives the house a reach beyond Realmspace.

  • The stores contract and the handovers both run on Cairn's lane; the station is on its entry.
