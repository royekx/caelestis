The eye-in-a-wheel

Vocath's own merchant mark. It appears on his goods wherever they move, which is why it turns up on the recovered rod, on the Sky Dock crate, and in the Bellwether Crow's last transmission from the Cairn approach. It marks a merchant's cargo.

It is a private house mark, so the Fleet registry has no entry for it. FR-016's cross-reference returned nothing, and WM-002 records it as matching nothing in registry. Anyone who trades in the right circles reads it on sight. Mirt is one of them, and the gap between what is obvious to a rival and what is provable in a filing is the whole of his position on the Tetherbreak.

Joffrey
Fleet officer · decorated
Survivor of the Bellwether Crow. Decorated, capable, and above suspicion.

A Fleet sailor who went out on the Bellwether Crow to find out why Cairn Station had stopped answering. With the senior deck officers dead and Clint Dashforest gone over the side with the boarders' craft, he held the surviving complement together, organised the withdrawal, and brought them off alive. He was decorated on their unanimous account, posted to Caelestis, and has risen since on genuine merit.

Competent, well-liked, and entirely convincing. Cadets know him. Instructors trust him. His record is clean and earned.

He is the colony's only operative on the station, and his record is clean because it is true. Full account — the taking, the two employers, and how to run the investigation: DM dossier →

Jeffrey
Dock hand · bribed
Sky Dock hand. Taking money to look away, and in well over his head.

A Sky Dock hand and a friend of Joffrey's. He is the seam in the station's inventory handling — paid to let certain manifests go unchecked and certain crates move at odd hours.

Bribed rather than taken. He has no idea what his friend is, assumes he is helping with smuggling at worst, and has begun to suspect the sums are too large for what he is being asked to do. He is the softest thread in the whole operation and the easiest one for the crew to pull.

The similarity of the names is a nuisance for everyone on the station and has already caused Mirt to point cadets at the wrong man.

Clint Dashforest
Caelestis crewmate · unrecovered
Took the boarders' craft and broke away. Carried as unrecovered.

A Caelestis crewmate aboard the Bellwether Crow. When the first boarding came, he seized the boarders' own craft and broke away with it, pulling the pursuit off the Crow and buying the vessel its only clear minutes of the engagement. SA-005 carries him as unrecovered.

A psychic wind carried him out of the system entirely. He reached W-21: Fonains, where he brought laser pistols into a world that had none. How he crossed is undecided.

He is alive and unaccounted for, and the salvage that came back from Fonains — through a crew sent looking for him, sold on through Vocath's line as ordinary cargo — is how a fragment of the Fonains Monolith ended up inside Boogie.

M. Tarroway
Cartographer · Strake Station
Keeps the Cairn approach packet. The most useful person in the sector.

Station cartographer at Strake, which is open, crewed, and the authorised limit of the Cairn bearing. He maintains the only staged copies of the Cairn approach packet — charts, survey material and timing tables — and they are duplicated nowhere else in the sector.

He has revised those tables three times in a single cycle and filed a note saying so: whatever is out there moves, and the timing tables do not hold. It reads as a cartographer's complaint and is the most accurate observation anyone has made about the Tetherbreak.

What he has and what he lacks He has the approach, the filings, the history of the bearing and a professional grievance he will happily explain to anyone who asks. He has no idea the patrol his tables track was a ship under command rather than an animal keeping a rhythm, or that it stopped keeping the patrol because it left. He is a live, willing, talkative source sitting exactly where the crew will need one.

Vocath
Mercane merchant
A mercane merchant. In active commercial and personal conflict with Mirt.

A mercane merchant of long-standing reputation in the trade circles around Realmspace. In active commercial conflict with Mirt of Caelestis Academy. The conflict is well-known in the relevant trade community and predates any of the recent incidents at Caelestis.

His aim is to disgrace Mirt enough to prise him loose from the school's protection so that someone else can finish him. He is not a fighter and takes no part in the assault on Caelestis.

What he believes: that the agent inside Caelestis answers to him, that the illithid who dealt with him supplied a thrall, and that his line carries what his manifests say it carries. Each of those is wrong. DM dossier →
