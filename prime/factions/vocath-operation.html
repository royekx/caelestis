Vocath’s crew
A trading house with a grievance, run by a merchant who believes his quarrel is the whole of it.

Vocath's crew is the trading house of Vocath, a mercane merchant of long standing in the trade circles around Realmspace. Vocath is in active commercial conflict with Mirt, headmaster of the Academy at Caelestis, and the feud is well known in the trade and older than any recent trouble at Caelestis. His aim is to disgrace Mirt enough to pry him loose from the school's protection, so that someone else can finish him. Vocath is not a fighter.

Beliefs

A trade is a trade. Vocath holds that what he buys is what he gets, that an asset who takes his money answers to him, and that his manifests describe his cargo. He owns his grudge openly, and a merchant loudly feuding with a headmaster is doing something everyone can see.

Structure and ranks

Vocath is the top of his house. Qitru, his intermediary, recruits, pays and collects, and she is the one an asset actually meets. She serves Vocath and does not care who stands above him; for her, the top is Vocath. Below her are field agents, hired hands, and people paid to look away.

History

When Cairn Station went dark, Vocath's people took it and waited, because a silent Fleet post brings a relief run and a relief run has one bearing to take. The Bellwether Crow came, and Vocath's crew ambushed it for a sister artifact of A.T.L.A.S. that it carried. Clint Dashforest took the device and left the ship to draw the boarders off, and went through a color pool with them behind him. Someone from Vocath's crew went looking for the device, followed Clint to Fonains, and learned about the gems there; Vocath now trades in them. Salvage from Fonains came back through Vocath's line as ordinary cargo, and that is how a shard of the broken Monolith reached Caelestis.

Operations

The house moves goods along Vocath's line, each piece marked with his house mark. The crates at the Sky Dock carry it. The thefts at Caelestis, the sabotage plates, the leverage on Miken and the marked crates are Vocath's work.

How they look

Vocath's mark is the eye in a wheel, his private house mark, on his goods wherever they move. It turned up on the recovered rod, on the Sky Dock crate, and in the Bellwether Crow's last transmission from the Cairn approach. Because it is a private mark, the Fleet registry has no entry for it; FR-016's cross-reference and WM-002 both returned nothing. Anyone who trades in the right circles reads it on sight. It is distinct from the Order's white tower with its blue-violet eye.

Relationships

Mirt and Caelestis

The feud is the house's public face. Vocath wants Mirt disgraced and pried loose from the Academy's protection.

What's hidden

  • The illithid arrangement. Vocath dealt with an illithid, Zhauveth: his line supplies captives, and in exchange he has an asset inside Caelestis. He takes it for ordinary thrall work and has never heard of the colony she serves. She supplied a colony operative, not a thrall, and she arranged the deal; she did not accept it.
  • Joffrey, a decorated officer of the Caelestis Corps, reports to Vocath, who believes he has bought an informant inside the Academy. Joffrey is the colony's. Zhauveth arranged him as a conduit feeding N'ghathrod; his reports to Vocath have never contained a lie, and every one passes through Ith'varn too. Fleet row · Dossier →
  • The line has been moving colony material for years.
  • The Brig. Its location and access protocols are classified well enough that Vocath could never have found that vault. Joffrey found it and told him it was there, so the strike happens because an asset named a target Vocath already wanted, and Vocath experiences the plan as his own. The grudge campaign against Mirt is cover that Vocath pays for himself.

How to run them

Name Vocath to the players; the name means nothing to them yet, and the real mystery is the second principal underneath his operation. He never appears in a fight. Pressure on him is commercial, legal and social, and the most dangerous thing the crew can do to him is prove he was used, which costs him the standing the feud was meant to win. Jeffrey is the softest thread in the operation and the easiest for the crew to pull. Qitru is a competitor for knowledge before she is a threat: anyone asking the same questions in the same port will cross her.

Their arc

The house's Arc 1 work at Caelestis ends in the graduation attack and the run on the Brig; the sequence is on Colony Operations. After graduation Qitru shadows the crew, and the Fonains gem trade gives the house a reach beyond Realmspace.
