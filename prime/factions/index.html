Factions

Every organized interest with a stake in the campaign. Each has an entry carrying its beliefs, its structure and the people who belong to it; a named person there expands in place for a quick read and links on to their dossier. The gods are on Deities; what the gods are, and the pressure the Order and the Restorers read, are on Cosmology. Merchants, ports and governments are under Other powers, below the list.

UF United Intergalactic FleetFour or five sector branches; Caelestis is one Home

The sector branches that carry, guard and contract across the Erevos Galaxy, under a council of admirals. The Caelestis Sector is the crew's own: the Caelestis Corps, the Caelestis Spelljammer Academy, and the Bridge.

The faction
OA Order of the All-FatherThe Watchers, sworn to keep things as they are Allied

Serves the All-Father, a god it knows as a pressure on the cosmos, and posts Watchers across the Erevos Galaxy to watch worlds nearing a Zenith. Ezra is a Glimpse finishing his training with the crew.

The faction
RK The RunekeepersWayfinders of a world that keeps itself unfindable Allied

A small, hidden order whose Runekeeper carries the ritual that hides their homeworld. Wan and Tu, two of its Wayfinders, are cadets at Caelestis as one person.

The faction
SK The SeekersVecna's faith, in chapters named for what they seek Chapter by chapter

Chapters on many worlds, each serving Vecna under another name and each unaware of the rest. The Fracture Cult and the Archivists are chapters.

The faction
VO The Vth’oramaN'ghathrod's illithid colony aboard a living dreadnought Opposed

All who go toward home: the Seekers of a New Home. They open and link hearts in search of a home, and plant the taken in key positions on the worlds they reach.

The faction
RS The RestorersScholars reaching for the source of the Monoliths to bring back what was lost Not hostile

Cells of scholars who keep the Ledger of losses, survey dead worlds, and walk into Zeniths on purpose. Their doctrine posits a mother of Monoliths, the All-Mother, that holds everything taken.

The faction
VC Vocath’s crewA mercane trading house with a grudge against Mirt Opposed

Vocath's house runs his feud with Mirt and his trade along the lanes around Realmspace, and now as far as Fonains. His eye-in-a-wheel mark turned up on the recovered rod, the Sky Dock crate and the Bellwether Crow's last transmission.

The faction
FO The FortunedTymora's clerics on Aethris, mid-ritual Opposed

A sect grown from a temple order of Tymora, whose ritual brought Aethris to its Zenith and stands incomplete. They want Sol back to finish it, and sent the Crystalline Retrieval Construct after him.

The faction
CT The Cre'TezKeepers of Tinderstrike on Fonains, at war over the inheritance Divided

A fire genasi line turned tiefling, bound to the fire key by a pact with Asmodeus. The Alpha has been invoked; Lusion and Xero ride with Lola's Boys, and Vanifer waits.

Other powers

The Rock of Bral, merchant powers, mercane traders, private captains, local governments and world authorities. Few of them know much of how the cosmos works. They control lanes, supplies, politics, cover stories, contracts, rumour and access: practical powers that often decide whether an operation can be run at all. A power among them earns an entry here when the crew has to deal with it by name.
