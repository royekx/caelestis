Factions

Every organized interest with a stake in the campaign. Each one has a page carrying its doctrine, its structure, and the people who belong to it; a named person there expands in place for a quick read, and links on to their dossier.

AF Caelestis Academy & FleetHome · one station in Realmspace Home

The Academy trains, the Fleet takes the contracts, and graduation is a walk from one wing to the other. Authority is self-bestowed by reputation rather than granted by any council of worlds. It holds A.T.L.A.S. and does not know what that makes it.

The faction
OA Order of the All-FatherInterprets the All-Father, preserves Continuity Allied

Recruits across many worlds and runs two field arms: the sanctioned Watchers, and the ones who left. Ezra is a Watcher cadet finishing his field training with the crew.

The faction
RK RunekeepersRunic transit, on an unfindable world Allied

A small hidden order whose existence is rumour at best. Their homeworld defends itself by being unfindable, and the ritual that does it is carried in one living practitioner’s body.

The faction
SK The SeekersChapters after a secret each believes was kept from it Chapter by chapter

No chapter calls itself this and no two share a name, a symbol, a practice or a god. What they share is an origin shape, which is why they can be dropped on any world the campaign needs one.

The faction
SP SpectrumMid-tier ideological and operational forces Varies

Independent powers with their own histories — Concordance, Fracture Cult, Archivists, Syndicate. Some of their cells began as Seeker chapters that grew in that direction.

The faction
RS RestorersRecovering what the cosmos has lost Not hostile

They keep archives of vanished civilizations, survey dead worlds, and trace a pathway toward the Core — which their doctrine names the Mother of the Universe. They are not hostile and they are aimed at the single most dangerous place in the cosmology.

The faction
VO The Vth’oramaAn illithid colony aboard a taken dreadnought Opposed

Named for the ship, because the ship is what they are. N’ghathrod is fused into it, the bearers and the unthinned live aboard, and the whole of it is a society going about an ordinary day.

The faction
VC Vocath’s OperationA trading house running a private grudge Opposed

A merchant who believes his quarrel with Mirt is the whole of it, and is wrong about that. His eye-in-a-wheel mark turns up on the recovered rod, the Sky Dock crate, and the Crow’s last transmission.

The faction
AE The Aethris powersA world mid-Zenith that wants its ritual finished Opposed

The Fortuned need Sol back so the ritual of perfection can close. They do not know a third party is pushing the same event from outside.

The faction
DP Deities & Cosmic PowersGods, and the powers that function like them Varies

The divine and the cosmic-but-not-divine. What a god is in this cosmology, and the bounds of what one can know, are set out in Cosmology.

The faction
EX External PowersMerchants, ports, captains and local authorities Indifferent

The Rock of Bral, mercane traders, private captains and world governments. They control lanes, supplies, politics and access — the practical things that decide whether an operation happens — and understand none of the structural ones.
