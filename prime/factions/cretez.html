The Cre'Tez
The family that keeps the fire key of the Princes, bound to it by a pact with Asmodeus, and tearing itself apart over who inherits.

The Cre'Tez are a family of Fonains, a fire genasi line that has turned tiefling over the generations, seated at the Cre'Tez estate in Orerius. They keep Tinderstrike, the dagger that is the fire key to Imix's prison, and the key is bound to their blood by a pact Asmodeus made with their ancestors. Two years ago the head of the family invoked the Alpha Inheritance, and the heirs have been fighting over the birthright since. Two of the brothers, Lusion and Xero, ride with Lola's Boys.

Beliefs

The family's charge is to keep Tinderstrike, which in the wrong hands could release Imix. The price of Asmodeus's pact was that Imix be forgotten, and with each generation more of the family's truth burned away. The Cre'Tez keep the key, the Bloodstones and the inheritance rituals; what Imix is, and what the Alpha first meant, the family has lost.

Structure and ranks

Bloodstones

Each heir receives a Bloodstone at birth. It is bound to the heir's soul, imprinted with the souls of mother and father, and proves the birthright. Power in the family has passed through rituals worked with the Bloodstones, and two of those rituals remain: the Alpha and the Beta Inheritances.

The Alpha Inheritance

The Alpha is a battle royale between the heirs. The winner becomes head of the family and inherits the birthright of each heir defeated. The Bloodstones judge when a soul truly concedes, which usually happens only at death.

The Beta Inheritance

For five generations the family has used the Beta instead: heirs concede their birthrights willingly to one designee. The Beta can be contested in two ways. If the heirs are not unanimous, or if the head objects to the designee, any heir may invoke the Alpha, and it runs until every birthright has conceded. A head who objects concedes his own birthright to his appointee by doing so.

Who invokes

Only the head of the family can invoke an inheritance, except through the head's own objection to a Beta. If the head dies before invoking one, both rituals invoke at once, and the heirs must settle the leadership among themselves, by a new ritual if need be.

History

The fire genasi who became the Cre'Tez have guarded the fire key since the Accordance sealed the Princes of Elemental Evil, about twelve thousand years ago. About three and a half centuries ago a sect of the fire genasi tried to summon Imix. The few who opposed it were badly outnumbered and fought anyway, and in desperation they called out for power. Asmodeus answered. He gave them Tinderstrike, which amplified their elemental gifts, and with it they sealed Imix again. He bound the key to their blood, and the price was that Imix be forgotten. The family devoted itself to keeping the key, and over the generations since, its bloodline has turned tiefling. In those years the Cre'Tez and Chloe Chespetes's clan fought a sect of her clan that tried to use the Pedra Ardente to open a portal and release Imix.

For five generations the heads of the family have passed on by the Beta. Cenvinzo is head now. His wife Benedetta, mother of the younger siblings, fell ill at Rihiri's birth, about twelve years ago, and died two years later. Vanifer, Cenvinzo's daughter by another mother and the eldest of the siblings, raised most of the younger ones after Benedetta died, filling in for a father who showed them little affection.

About two years ago Cenvinzo invoked the Alpha. Asmodus, the eldest brother, began hunting his siblings' birthrights. Asmodeus spared Lusion from the Alpha and sent him to the Temple of Moloch, where he joined Lola's Boys. Kevulan willed his birthright to Asmodus, then rescinded it and willed it to Xero, and fled. Asmodus stabbed Rihiri; she survived, and Dharra got her out. Fire cultists later took both sisters, and Lola's Boys freed them from Elizar Dryflagon's tower in Baratol, where they found a letter from Asmodus and Elizar wearing a brand that matches the Cre'Tez emblem. Kevulan heard of the heroes of the Monolith, tracked Lola's Boys, and is now bound to Xero.

Operations

The family's one charge is Tinderstrike, kept in the Eternal Flame temple. An heir needs every birthright to claim it. The Blooming Glass bounty was posted out of Orerius for the Cre'Tez, “for the eldest brother”, by a woman calling herself Finerva; the Pedra Ardente that Clint Dashforest's crew recovered went to her.

Relationships

Asmodeus

The pact's maker. He gave the family Tinderstrike and bound it to their blood, and he spared Lusion from the Alpha.

The fire cult

Imix's cult, grown from Tharizdun's influence. Its cultists took Dharra and Rihiri, Elizar Dryflagon's brand matches the Cre'Tez emblem, and the cult treats Asmodus as its prophet.

Lola's Boys

Lusion and Xero ride with them, and Kevulan is bound to Xero. Lola's Boys freed Dharra and Rihiri.

The Seekers

A Seeker chapter reached Vanifer and traded her the lost meaning of the Alpha and the secret in the Cre'Tez blood.

What's hidden

  • Vanifer has no birthright. Her mother was not Benedetta, so no Bloodstone of hers carries both parents' souls. She made an illegitimate Bloodstone by slowly siphoning Benedetta's soul, and the siphoning was Benedetta's illness and her death. Dossier.
  • Asmodus's memory. Asmodus learned that Vanifer killed their mother, and they fought; Kevulan witnessed part of it. Vanifer modified his memory so he believes the younger siblings' births wore Benedetta out, which turned him against them.
  • Why the Alpha was invoked. Vanifer tried to make her father concede his birthright to her. He invoked the Alpha instead, locking them both out of the inheritance.
  • Cenvinzo. He is barely alive, a shadow of himself, held captive somewhere and bound to Vanifer.
  • The phoenix dreams. Vanifer sends Asmodus dreams in Benedetta's likeness, promising that the inheritance will release a phoenix and raise his mother. She leads the fire cult, and on her orders it plays along and treats him as its prophet. Kevulan received the dream too.
  • Imix. The voice told Vanifer that Imix, a phoenix, would cleanse the world of transgressors like her father. Imix was sealed to stop his true goal: to burn everything.

How to run them

Play the family as a house with one secret it has forgotten and one it is keeping. The siblings each believe a different story about Benedetta's death and the Alpha, and every clue the party finds sets two of them against each other: Kevulan knows of a fight between Asmodus and Vanifer after Benedetta died, the illegitimate Bloodstone exists, and the crest is Asmodeus's. Vanifer's plan, and how she moves, are on her dossier.

Threads

  • Three of the four keys are out of their keeping: Windvane with Aerisi, Ironfang with Marlos, Drown with Gar Shatterkeel. Tinderstrike is still in the temple, and Vanifer is waiting to take it from whichever heir claims it.
  • The Pedra Ardente is the gem the sect used three and a half centuries ago to try to open Imix's portal, and it is in Vanifer's hands now.
  • Asmodeus is the Accordance's face; the family's pact ties it to the gods who chose Lola's Boys (the Accordance).
