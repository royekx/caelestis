Gods, and the paracausal powers that function like them.

The divine, and the cosmic-but-not-divine — gods, and the paracausal powers that function like them. For what gods are in this cosmology and the bounds of what they know, see Cosmology → The Gods.

Vecna
God of secrets · later god
A later god, ascended through Transformation. Known to no one as the hand behind anything.

The god of secrets, and a later god — he ascended after the Reach rather than being born from it, which is why the approach to the centre harms him less than it harms the Reach-born and why he can still cross nothing at all.

In the wider cosmos he is a god like other gods: worshipped in the usual places, catalogued in the usual registries, and understood to be about hidden knowledge. Nothing he is actually doing is attributed to him anywhere. His chapters have never heard the name, the colony has never heard the name, and the institutions tracking the symptoms lack any instrument that reaches a cause.

What he is doing Taking the Convergence seat and becoming an Axiom. Full account in the Ascension. He works entirely through people who can go where he cannot. The result is an organisation that eludes every search and an instruction that was never issued.
The half of him in the Citadel His mortality was archived at the Zenith of his own ascension and has been inside the Core ever since. That half wanted godhood and had begun thinking past it; the Axiom plan was conceived by what continued without him. He holds the mind and not the method, he has had an age to reconsider, and he has.
Beshaba
Maid of Misfortune
Goddess of misfortune and bad luck. Tymora's twin.

Maid of Misfortune. Lady Doom. Goddess of bad luck, misfortune, and random mischance. Tymora's twin sister. Speaks to her chosen in brief, direct utterances rather than extended communion.

Tymora
Lady Luck
Goddess of good fortune. Beshaba's twin.

Lady Luck. Goddess of good fortune, skill, and victory. Beshaba's twin and opposite. The two are bound: where one is worshipped, the other's shadow falls.

Astra Virex
Wounded celestial
A conscious body that fell. Wounded, calling people to it, and expanding through those who answer.

A sentient paracausal celestial body, wounded by a Seam — Transformation and Boundary overlapping across something that was already conscious, opening its distinction and altering its nature at once. Astra is what the Order calls it from the outside; Virex is nearer to what it is. Not a god in the doctrinal sense; functions like one in the experience of those tethered to it.

What it wants: to be whole, to be strong, and to never let this happen to it again. It is neither good nor evil. It is hurt, and it is acting on that.

Its method is calling. It reaches across distance, finds someone who can hear it, and draws them toward it. Those who answer and are changed by it are Starchildren — practitioners whose existence extends its presence into the regions they inhabit, altering the cosmological character of those regions over time.

What it does not know Astra Virex is oriented toward constellations. It has no knowledge of world-hearts. It may only learn of them through a Starchild who touches one — which is a turn, not a given.

Open: how much of what it was survived the Seam, and whether anything of the original is recoverable.

Polly Geim
Casey's brother · missing
A person, not a power. Casey Geim's brother, changed at the fallen star, gone looking for what came down.

Polly is a person. Casey Geim's brother, and nothing more exotic than that to begin with.

A star fell on their world. He went to it, and he was changed by what he found. He left home to learn where it had come from, and went looking for it — it being Astra Virex. He has been missing since.

DM only Astra Virex uses Polly's voice to reach Casey — the directions, the hints, the presence at the rail, "Go there." At the table Polly is simply Casey's patron and his brother, and that is where it stays until the campaign moves it.

Open: what actually became of Polly. Alive, consumed, or something in between — undecided, and best left so until the table needs it.

Zerathis
Prior Starchild
A spellcaster. A prior Starchild, now consumed into Virenspace's paracausal layer.

A spellcaster of historical note. Gifted Mirt the extradimensional wardrobe. A prior Starchild whose tether ultimately drew him into Virenspace's paracausal layer, where she now exists as a consumed presence rather than an embodied being.

Her allegiance was to Astra Virex. A name the crew will hear long before they meet anything of her, spoken differently by everyone who says it.
