The Aethris powers
A world mid-Zenith that wants Sol back so its ritual can finish.

A Tymora-devoted world in the middle of an incomplete Zenith, pursuing a ritual of perfection it believes was answered from above. It wants Sol Fortuna back so the ritual can finish. See Realms → Aethris.

Crystalline Retrieval Construct (CRC)
Retrieval construct
A construct commissioned by the Fortuned of Aethris to retrieve Sol Fortuna.

A crystalline construct commissioned by the Fortuned of Aethris after Sol Fortuna's escape. Mission: capture, correction, and return of the named target. Signature address to the target: "Sol Fortuna. You are required."

Operates with sterile certainty, beautiful-yet-wrong aesthetic, and no taunts. Aethris's own machine, answering to Aethris, pursuing the assignment it was built for.

There is one of it. It stands seven to eight feet tall and occupies a single square. Its face progressively resolves into a perfect mirror of Sol's — visual only, with no mechanic attached, so the horror costs the player nothing.
