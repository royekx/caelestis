The Fortuned
Tymora's clerics on Aethris, who perfected fortune by cursing others to carry their misfortune, and want Sol back to finish their ritual.

The Fortuned are a sect that grew from a temple order of Tymora on Aethris. They are clerics for the most part, led by a priest or priestess. They are not the rulers of the whole world, and not all of Aethris follows Tymora. Their ritual of perfection brought Aethris to its Zenith, and the ritual is incomplete. They want Sol Fortuna back to complete it.

Beliefs

Tymora's tenet is “fortune favours the bold”. The Fortuned misread it. They pushed luck until it stopped being luck, and in perfecting fortune they removed the risk that luck needs. The Prism Ascendant, the Monolith that answered their ritual, they hold to be Tymora's answer.

History

Long ago the Fortuned cast a generational curse to increase their own fortune: a group of people were made to gather their misfortune. Those people are the Misfortuned, and they have been born on Aethris for generations since. Sol was born one of them.

The Fortuned's long-prepared ritual of perfection at the temple site brought Aethris to its Zenith, and the Prism Ascendant manifested above the temple in response. Sol escaped before the ritual closed. The Zenith has passed; the ritual stands incomplete because the Fortuned have not completed it.

Operations

They want Sol back alive because he is a component of their ritual; they do not care about him as a person. After his escape they commissioned the Crystalline Retrieval Construct to capture, correct and return him. Their wanted notice reaches lane-marker boards far from Aethris: it seeks an astral elf marked by misfortune, “RETURN REQUESTED”, with a reward paid on his return to Aethris and none for remains.

How they look

They dress in pristine robes of white and gold, the colours of purity and good fortune.

She finds them heretical, a spit in her face, and calls what they do unnatural. She thinks Tymora is fuelling them and blames her sister for it.

Sol Fortuna

A Misfortuned who walked out of their ritual, and the component they need to finish it.

What's hidden

  • Vecna is pushing the Fortuned's ritual toward completion from outside; he wants it completed for his own ends. Nobody on Aethris knows he is involved. Tymora likely would not want the ritual finished.
  • The Prism Ascendant is not Tymora's answer. It is reality's response to the Zenith. It has archived what it can and stays where it is; it does nothing.
  • Sol's escape. A Watcher extracted him, against the Order's remit.
  • The sisters. Neither Tymora nor Beshaba knows what both of them need: each other.

How to run them

The Fortuned are serene, certain and polite, and Sol is a part to them. They never need to appear: their construct, their notice and their reward do the work, and the CRC runs as an escape, not a fight (its dossier has the block).

Their arc

The CRC hunts Sol, and every meeting with it is harder than the last. Aethris is a world the crew can visit to see a world after its Zenith, with its Monolith overhead and its ritual still open.
