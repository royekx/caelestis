Oerth
Vecna's homeworld in Greyspace, where he became a god, and the darkest timeline a world can have.

Oerth is a world in Greyspace, in the Erevos Galaxy. It is Vecna's homeworld and the world where he became a god, and it is called the Ascension World for it. Oerth is a darkest-timeline world: heroes rose against him there and failed, and his hold on Oerth is the strongest he has anywhere. His worship is large and public there, and his faithful keep Oerth's Monolith as their holiest site.

Description

A medium, earthlike world that people can live on, home to a civilization.

History

Vecna was born on Oerth. He became an archmage, then a lich, and spent his long unlife gathering magical secrets and knowledge. At least a few centuries ago he worked a ritual to become a god. It pushed Oerth to its Zenith, and the Zenith made him one: the Godhood Ascension.

The Monolith that answered Oerth's Zenith archived his mortal form, because his mortality was the part about to cease, and like every Monolith it relayed what it took to the Core Monolith. That form, Vecna (Lich), sits now in the Radiant Citadel. His ascension charged him to Oerth's heart and pushed him off the world, and he has not stood on Oerth since; he works through vessels.

Peoples and culture

Vecna is an open god on Oerth, worshipped largely and in public, where elsewhere his faith lives in small hidden pockets. His divine realm, Cavitius, is anchored to the Erevos Galaxy, where his worship is densest, and Oerth is the centre of that worship. His faith across the galaxy is the Seekers.

Points of interest

Oerth's Monolith

The Monolith that answered Vecna's Zenith still stands on Oerth. It archived his mortal form, and his faithful treat it as the holiest site in the cosmos.

Greyspace

Oerth's system lies far across the Erevos Galaxy and high above its plane, over the opposite spiral arm from Realmspace, and no lane on Caelestis's charts reaches it. Caelestis has surveyed Greyspace.
