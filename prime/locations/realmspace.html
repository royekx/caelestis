Realmspace
Caelestis's home system, named for Toril: nine charted bodies around a yellow sun, and a Fleet watch on its edge.

Realmspace is the star system Caelestis calls home, named for its principal world, Toril. It lies in the Erevos Galaxy, within the Caelestis Sector of the United Intergalactic Fleet. The system is populous and well charted on standard Fleet maps, and its bodies are known by their names rather than by letters. The Caelestis station orbits Toril, which is the nearest destination on most Academy shipping routes. At the system's edge the Caelestis Sector keeps Strake Station and its sister satellites.

Description

A yellow sun holds five inner worlds and three outer ones. The inner worlds lie within days of one another: Anadia, Coliar, Toril, Karpri and Chandos. Karpri rides well above the common lanes and Chandos below the plane of the other inner worlds. Measured as sailing time out from the sun, Anadia lies at twelve hours, Coliar at one day, Toril at two, Karpri at three and Chandos at four. The outer worlds lie much farther out: ringed Glyth at ten days, Garden at twelve and H'Catha at sixteen, which puts H'Catha fourteen days' sail from Toril.

History

About a year ago Cairn Station, a satellite on the system's edge, reported something on its long scope that matched no vessel profile, then went silent. The relief vessel sent to find out why, the Bellwether Crow, was ambushed on the run past Strake, and standing order SA-005 has closed that run since.

The perimeter watch

The Caelestis Sector watches the edge of Realmspace from three posts: Strake Station and its two sister satellites, Hollis Post and Cairn Station. Strake is a waystation with a small Fleet presence, and the satellites are smaller posts around it. Each files instrument readings, transit sightings and anomaly reports on a shared rotation, and those readings pass on to the other sectors.

Strake stands directly out from Toril's part of the system, on the bearing called the Tetherbreak, and a charted lane runs to it. Hollis Post holds the western approach. Cairn lies at the far end, between the Realmspace and Krynnspace bearings, reached from Strake along the edge run. Strake and Hollis both appear in the FR-005 perimeter survey. The lanes to Strake and Hollis are open; the edge run past Strake is closed without Bridge authorization.

Hollis Post

The western satellite of the watch, on a charted lane from Toril's part of the system. It is crewed and files instrument and transit readings on the perimeter rotation, as recorded in FR-005. It does the same job as Strake and Cairn with the same equipment, and nothing has happened to it.

What's hidden

  • The mass on the Cairn bearing. The unidentified mass the watch reported a year ago was Vth'oramu, which sat on the Cairn bearing for days. It has moved since and keeps to the dark between systems.

How to run it

The inner worlds are days apart, so the crew can reach most of them between voyages; the outer worlds are a commitment. Everything beyond Strake is closed without Bridge authorization, which makes the Cairn run a destination the crew has to be sent to or has to steal. Either way it raises the question of who sent them and what they were told.

Hollis Post is where a crew learns what a normal filing looks like. Its quiet is what makes Cairn's silence read as an event rather than the ordinary attrition of a remote post.
