Realmspace
Caelestis's home system, named for Toril: nine charted bodies around a yellow sun, midway along the Caelestis Sector's arm.

Realmspace is the star system Caelestis calls home, named for its principal world, Toril. It lies in the Erevos Galaxy, within the Caelestis Sector of the United Intergalactic Fleet. The system is populous and well charted on standard Fleet maps, and its bodies are known by their names rather than by letters. The Caelestis station orbits Toril, which is the nearest destination on most Academy shipping routes. Realmspace lies midway along the Caelestis Sector's arm.

Description

A yellow sun holds five inner worlds and three outer ones. The inner worlds lie within days of one another: Anadia, Coliar, Toril, Karpri and Chandos. Karpri rides well above the common lanes and Chandos below the plane of the other inner worlds. Measured as sailing time out from the sun, Anadia lies at twelve hours, Coliar at one day, Toril at two, Karpri at three and Chandos at four. The outer worlds lie much farther out: ringed Glyth at ten days, Garden at twelve and H'Catha at sixteen, which puts H'Catha fourteen days' sail from Toril.

Lanes out of the system

Fleet lanes run out of Realmspace to the sector's posts. Cairn Station's lane runs outward along the arm into the sector's outer zone, some weeks out by ordinary helm, and the Fleet runs escorts against raiders on it under standing order SA-005. Strake Station sits on its own lane nearer home, off to the side of Cairn's. Hollis Post keeps a lane of its own. Krynnspace and Fonains's system, CSC 50743, lie inward along the arm.

Points of interest

Anadia
Inner world
Closest of the inner worlds, twelve hours from the sun.

A small, earthlike world with a civilization of its own, the closest of the inner worlds. Among its settlements, Dunmarrow and Kesh Hollow share a water compact four generations old.

Coliar
Inner world, a giant of air
Open air and floating islands, a day from the sun.

A huge world of air with no ground to stand on. Its settlements sit on floating islands, and living there takes protection from the open sky.

Toril

The principal world of Realmspace, two days from the sun, with its moon Selûne.

Garden
Outer bodies, a chain
An asteroid chain bound together by one vast plant, twelve days from the sun.

A chain of asteroids bound together by one vast plant, turning as a set. Vine-bridges run between the rocks. Claim-families hold them and trade by barter, and Garden has no government. The Fleet keeps nine beacons there, and Lane Marker 14 stands on the lane between Toril and Garden.

H'Catha
Outer world
The beholders' toroidal water world, fourteen days' sail from Toril.

A disk of sea around the Spindle, split among ten beholder rulers under the Sovereign Eye they dreamt together. Entry →

Hollis Post

An ordinary Fleet post on a charted lane of its own out of Realmspace. It is crewed and files instrument and transit readings up the chain to Caelestis.

What's hidden

  • Cairn's lane. The Vth'orama hold Cairn Station, and Vocath's captive line runs along its lane. Vth'oramu crosses the lane now and then, and Strake's long scope caught it there at the limit of its range. The full account is on Cairn's page.

How to run it

The inner worlds are days apart, so the crew can reach most of them between voyages; the outer worlds are a commitment. Cairn's lane, some weeks out, is a voyage of its own.

Threads

  • Ilsevaine Corr rules a hidden polity outside Realmspace, at the edge of a system or between systems. She has noticed more traffic than usual near her borders; the thread is on the campaign page.
  • Two graduation contracts are set here: the dark beacon at Lane Marker 14 on the Toril–Garden lane, and the dry well shared by Dunmarrow and Kesh Hollow on Anadia.
