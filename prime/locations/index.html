World awareness

Every world is Aware, Read-in or Unaware of space travel. Low-grade contracts sit on Aware worlds. Work on Unaware worlds is covert: ship-shaped hulls land at sea and come in through ports, and a fugitive is retrieved without the world noticing. Watchers issue contracts and tips to head off trouble before it reaches a Zenith.

Hostile worlds can be navigated when they are divided. H'Catha can be entered because its beholders are divided; Glyth is closed because it is one civilization.

A world's record

In DM terms, each world's base record holds its name; a one-line physical description; its peoples; how it works; its awareness (Aware, Read-in or Unaware); who speaks for it; a fact the crew can learn; a rumour; a rabbit hole; and hooks. A fugitive retrieval on an Unaware world runs in the Men in Black style.
