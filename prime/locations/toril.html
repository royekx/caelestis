Toril
The principal world of Realmspace and the system's namesake, two days from the sun, with Caelestis in its orbit.

Toril is the principal inhabited world of Realmspace and the world the system is named for. By Fleet cataloguing it is a standard world: atmosphere, oceans, continents and varied civilizations. Its moon is Selûne. The Caelestis station orbits it, and it is the nearest destination on most Academy shipping routes.

Description

A medium, earthlike world among the inner worlds, two days from the sun. Selûne, its small moon, can be lived on only with protection and holds no life. A market works in Toril orbit, where brokers deal for buyers across the system.

Peoples and culture

Diplomatic and trade contact with Caelestis is established and limited. The Fleet's standing practice on Toril is to work discreetly rather than show the wider cosmos to the people on the surface.

Points of interest

The Cogfast Enclave, Lantan

An enclave on Lantan. In Cycle 1489 a brief aurora crossed the enclave's airspace, and mechanical installations across it worked in step while it lasted. Two enclave constructs were recorded as surviving the event, and one of them later entered Academy intake. The archive's entry is SA-002.

Selûne

Toril's moon, small and lifeless, livable only with protection.
