Strake Station
The Caelestis Sector's station at the edge of Realmspace, and the last open deck before the closed run to Cairn.

Strake Station is the Caelestis Sector's station at the edge of Realmspace, directly out from Toril's part of the system on the bearing called the Tetherbreak. It has a commander, and two sister satellites watch the edge with it: Hollis Post and Cairn Station. The lane to Strake is open. Everything past it is closed without Bridge authorization, which makes Strake the last open deck on the run to Cairn.

Description

Strake keeps the staged Cairn approach packet of charts and survey material. It holds an item in secure storage under seal. Its watch files instrument readings, transit sightings and anomaly reports on the perimeter rotation, and Cairn's filings came through Strake on the resupply rotation. Readings from Strake pass on to the other sectors.

History

A year ago Strake's readings began to show something disturbing the satellites at the edge of the system. Its cartographer's readings were the first hint, and Strake reported an unidentified mass. Cairn's final report, LG-003, came in through Strake and then Cairn went silent. On its way to find out why, the Bellwether Crow called at Strake, drew the approach packet and went out on the edge run; it came back to Strake under damage, with most of its complement lost.

Points of interest

The approach packet

The staged material for a run to Cairn: charts and survey material for the edge run, kept on station.

Secure storage

An item held indefinitely under Quartermaster Verrow's seal, its contents classified above the clearance of the station's own filings.

What's hidden

  • Strake's readings. The unidentified mass Strake reported was Vth'oramu, sitting on the Cairn bearing. It has moved since, and keeps to the dark between systems.

How to run it

Run Strake as the staging point. It is the last friendly deck before anything happens: everyone who went out went through here, and everyone who came back came back through here, so every account of the engagement and every signature on the manifest is on this one station. Tarroway is a thread the players piece together, mostly through the archive, not a figure the crew meets.

Its arc

Any run to Cairn launches from Strake, whether the crew is sent or goes without leave.
