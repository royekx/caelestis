Strake Station
A Fleet waystation on its own lane nearer home, whose long scope reaches Cairn's stretch of lane at its very limit.

Strake Station is a Caelestis Sector waystation with a small Fleet presence, on its own lane out of Realmspace. Its lane lies nearer home than Cairn Station's and off to the side of it, and Cairn's stretch of lane lies at the very limit of Strake's long scope.

Description

Strake's watch files instrument readings, transit sightings and anomaly reports, and its quartermaster sends them up the chain. Its long scope picks up faint, odd returns at the limit of its range, out where Cairn's lane runs.

History

Strake's cartographer, M. Tarroway, pulled a pattern out of the noise at the edge of the scope's range: Strake saw things on Cairn's lane that Cairn did not report. On his filing the Fleet sent Bellwether Crow to look in on Cairn. Crow found Cairn in order, and its report answered Tarroway's filing. He kept filing, and his later filings were set aside.

Points of interest

The long scope

Strake's long scope reaches Cairn's stretch of lane at the very limit of its range. Its returns there are faint and odd.

In the Fleet's records

LG-003 holds Strake's filings on Cairn's lane: the first, which sent Crow; the later ones, set aside; Strake's contacts against Cairn's silence on that bearing; the plate of sweeps; and Tarroway's notes. FR-005 places Strake on its lane.

What's hidden

  • What Strake sees. Every return Strake logs on Cairn's stretch is Vth'oramu, crossing Cairn's lane now and then on its way somewhere. Cairn's reports leave it out. Strake never saw the handovers or the raid on Crow.
  • The sweeps. The return on Tarroway's plate, a different shape on every pass, is Vth'oramu seen from different angles.
  • Nobody in the colony knows Strake can see that far. The colony never learned that Tarroway raised the question; as far as it knows, the Fleet looked at Cairn and got its answer.

How to run it

Strake is where the discrepancy lives. Tarroway is a witness who noticed, and the players piece him together mostly through the archive. Without context the sweeps read as a sensor fault or debris; once the crew has seen Vth'oramu, the four shapes are a body seen from different angles.
