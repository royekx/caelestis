Cairn Station
The far satellite of the watch at Realmspace's edge, silent for a year, beyond a closed run.

Cairn Station is the far satellite of the watch at the edge of Realmspace, between the Realmspace and Krynnspace bearings. It is reached from Strake Station along the edge run. A year ago it reported something on its long scope whose readings made no sense, holding on the station's bearing and closing slow. Then it went silent, and it has stayed silent. The Fleet's records name an astral dreadnought's territory as the hazard there.

Description

A small post with a small standing complement. Cairn filed through Strake on the resupply rotation rather than directly, so its filings were always irregular. The edge run that reaches it has been closed by standing order since the Bellwether Crow came back.

History

When Cairn went dark, Vocath's crew took it and waited, because a silent Fleet post brings a relief run and a relief run has one bearing to take. The Bellwether Crow was sent to find out why Cairn had stopped answering, with Clint Dashforest and Joffrey aboard. It called at Strake, drew the approach packet and went out on the edge run.

Vocath's crew ambushed it short of Cairn for a sister artifact of A.T.L.A.S. that it carried. Clint took the device and left the ship to draw the boarders off. He steered for a color pool he could see opening and went through it, and the boarders who followed went in after him. A second boarding killed the senior deck officers and most of the complement. The few survivors were let go, and Joffrey held them together and brought them off alive; he was decorated on their unanimous account. The Crow made Strake under damage. Nobody has reached Cairn since.

The Fleet Intelligence Archive holds the chain: LG-003, Cairn's final logistics report, filed through Strake with the last transmission; WM-002, the relief vessel engaged by an eye-in-a-wheel craft past Strake; SA-005, the standing order closing everything beyond Strake; and FR-005, which puts Cairn beyond the sector limit, with gravitational variance strongest along its bearing.

What's hidden

  • The mass on the scope. Vth'oramu sat on the Cairn bearing for days. That is the signature in Cairn's final transmission: closing slow, matching no vessel profile, reading differently on every sweep, because it was not a vessel. N'ghathrod's taking and converting of the dreadnought took decades, perhaps a century, and generations of the colony were born aboard. It has moved since, and keeps to the dark between systems.
  • The deal. Vocath's ambush ran on an arrangement with the illithid Zhauveth: captives for an asset inside Caelestis. Joffrey was taken in the second boarding by a tadpole of N'ghathrod's. The survivors were let live so that there would be a story with him as its hero, and they believe every word of it.
  • Clint went through the pool on purpose and came out on Fonains; the device is somewhere there. Someone from Vocath's crew followed him to Fonains.
  • What was left. Vocath's people withdrew after the ambush and left something behind. It has been alone for a year, reports to nobody who answers, and will register the first Fleet vessel to come up the bearing since the standing order.

How to run it

The archive records the whole event accurately and understands none of it: Cairn lost to something it could not identify, and a relief vessel mauled by a craft bearing a merchant's mark. Let the crew assemble the two halves. Whatever waits at Cairn should open onto something larger rather than resolve in one room; if it is a person or a taken thing, it is a witness as well as a tripwire.

Still to decide

  • What Vocath's people left behind is not set: a taken creature, a construct, a brine cache with something growing in it, or a person.
  • What remains of the station, what became of its complement, who built onto it, and whether any of Cairn's own instrument record survives are not set.
