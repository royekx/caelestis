Cairn Station
A small Fleet watch post on a lane some weeks out from Realmspace, whose reports read all clear week after week.

Cairn Station is a small Fleet watch post with a handful of crew, and the Fleet's authority on its lane. The lane runs outward from Realmspace along the Caelestis Sector's arm, into the sector's outer zone, some weeks out by ordinary helm. Cairn's long scope watches the lane, and its reports go to Caelestis and feed the Fleet's charts. They have read all clear for years.

Description

The post is small and quiet. Its station master writes the reports and keeps the log, and the signals officer sends what he writes. Supply ships call on a regular round. Visitors find the place a little off in a way they cannot name, and the feeling fades once they have gone.

Strake Station sits on its own lane nearer home, off to the side of Cairn's, and Cairn's stretch of lane lies at the very limit of Strake's long scope.

History

The station master has deferred the crew's rotation for years. Each of them signs a waiver and stays, and the same few people serve at Cairn year on year. Nobody in the Fleet finds that odd: many who serve have few ties to any world below, and a quiet post is easy to stay at.

About a year ago the Fleet sent Bellwether Crow to look in on Cairn, with Clint Dashforest leading its crew and Joffrey as its junior spelljammer. On the lane short of Cairn it came on raiders flying a band's own colors and was mauled. Clint was lost to a color pool, many of the crew died, and Joffrey took the helm and brought the survivors in. Cairn took the crippled ship in, and its repairs took days. The station master had Caelestis told that Crow was safe and under repair, and relayed the raid as its crew told it. Crow went home, its report found Cairn in order, and the Fleet thanked the station, recorded the raiders' colors and ordered escorts on the lane against that band. Escort crews call at Cairn as they pass.

Peoples and culture

A handful of Fleet crew keep the watch, the signals officer among them, under the station master. They work their watches as normal.

Notable people

  • The station master. Commands the post, writes every report that leaves it and keeps the log.
  • The signals officer. Sends the station master's reports to Caelestis.

Points of interest

The long scope

Cairn's long scope watches the lane in both directions. Its log is written up as routine, watch after watch, and shows the lane clear.

The supply contract

Vocath's house holds the Fleet contract for stores on Cairn's lane, under its clean house mark. The family business supplies the lane's stations, and its ships call at Cairn as a matter of course.

In the Fleet's records

The Fleet Intelligence Archive holds what the Fleet saw of Cairn: LG-003, Strake's filings on returns along Cairn's lane that Cairn never reported; WM-002, Crow's report of the raid and its days at Cairn; SA-005, the escort order on the lane; and FR-005, which places Cairn on its lane reporting to Caelestis.

What's hidden

  • The colony holds Cairn with a pair. The Vth'orama hold the station with one of N'ghathrod's taken and one of Zhauveth's illithids.
  • The station master is taken. One routine supply call carried one of N'ghathrod's tadpoles to Cairn. The station master writes the reports and keeps the log, so every word that leaves Cairn is his before the signals officer sends it, and nobody can read his mind.
  • The rest of the crew are held. One of Zhauveth's spawn, a malformed illithid whose one strength is holding minds, lives at Cairn out of sight and holds everyone else, the signals officer among them. The hold works only by nearness and grows with every day spent near it. A crew that has lived beside it for years accepts it as part of the station and stops seeing it. They work their watches as normal and set aside what they see on the lane as nothing worth remarking. Out of range the hold fades, so the illithid makes no attempt on anyone who will leave: a visitor would carry nothing away but a feeling. Visitors feel the place is a little off, and anyone psychically aware feels it more. The illithid feeds from the captives that pass near Cairn. Zhauveth has few such offspring, and this one is spent where a hold matters most.
  • The rotation. The deferred rotation keeps the same held crew at Cairn year on year.
  • The handovers. Vocath's captive line runs along the lane to the colony's couriers. Mercenaries in his hire run the handovers, flying their own colors, with a few of Vocath's own crew aboard as overseers. Every few weeks captives change hands out on the lane near Cairn, from a mercenary ship to a colony courier, and Cairn's scope log shows nothing. Vth'oramu crosses the lane now and then on its way somewhere.
  • Zhauveth's visits. Zhauveth touches down at Cairn now and then, not often, and makes the colony's deals with Vocath's overseers. Vocath's ships call at Cairn as a matter of course, under the supply contract and its clean house mark.
  • Crow's days at Cairn. Vocath's overseers, pulled back down the lane with the mercenaries after the raid, warned Zhauveth that Crow was coming. Crow's crew would leave, so the illithid kept out of sight and made no attempt on them, and Cairn's crew worked as normal around them. The survivors felt the place was a little off, put it down to shock and grief, and lost the feeling on the way home. Cairn's own scope log for the watch of the raid, written up as routine, shows no contact, though Strake logged one inside Cairn's range. Sending the ship home was less trouble than the questions a missing ship would raise.
  • Joffrey was taken at Cairn. The station master reported up to N'ghathrod, and Zhauveth saw what they had: a ship going home to Caelestis, and a spelljammer its survivors follow. A colony courier brought one of N'ghathrod's tadpoles up the lane within days, and the station master took Joffrey during the stay. The colony wanted a man inside Caelestis and knew nothing of A.T.L.A.S.; a hold cannot go home with a man, and a taking can. Joffrey is also insurance, a man inside Caelestis who can warn the colony if anyone looks at Cairn again. He knows Crow was sent to look in on Cairn, and nothing of who asked.
  • The report that closed the question. Crow went to look at Cairn and found it in order, so its report answered Tarroway's filing, and his later filings were set aside. The colony never learned that Tarroway raised the question. Nobody in the colony knows Strake can see Cairn's stretch of lane.
  • The escorts. Escort crews that call at Cairn are visitors like Crow's: they leave, and the hold leaves them alone. N'ghathrod's taken tell Vocath, through Zhauveth, where the escorts run, and the line runs around them.

How to run it

The archive records Cairn accurately and understands none of it: a post that reports all clear, a raid on its lane, and a cartographer at Strake whose returns disagree with Cairn's. Let the crew find the discrepancy before they find the station. Cairn is held, not empty, and it should feel wrong in small ways that do not add up, never in one reveal.

If the crew goes

The illithid is always there; Zhauveth only sometimes. If she is there, she could end a crew of the cadets' level with a word. She cannot do violence, and domination is not violence: she tells a crew to lay down their arms and hold still, and they do.

The crew feel the place is off from the moment they arrive. If they stay, the hold reaches for them a little more each day, and they can resist it. A fight at Cairn is a choice between the crew around them, who are not acting of their own will, and the thing holding them: knock the crew down or go for the source.

Proposed touches
  • The crew all pause at once, mid-sentence, and pick up again.
  • A sighting in the watch log, struck through in the station master's steady hand.
  • The duty roster names the same people as the roster a year ago.
  • Nobody asks the visitors for news from home.
  • A crewman answers a question a beat before it is asked.
  • A cold room nobody goes into, and nobody mentions.
  • Something moves at the edge of a corridor, and the crewman beside them does not look up.

Threads

  • Strake and Tarroway. The filings that disagree with Cairn are the way in. Tarroway is a witness who noticed.
  • Joffrey. He was taken here. Anyone who looks at Cairn again is what he is placed to warn the colony about.
  • Vocath. The supply contract puts his house mark on Cairn in plain sight. The captive line flies the mercenaries' colors, and his seal travels only on the cargo. The Fleet is on alert for the colors, which lead to a hired band, and has nothing that matches the seal.
  • Vth'oramu. Strake's sweeps at the limit of its range caught it crossing the lane.

Still to decide

  • Whether Cairn itself serves as the waystation for the handovers, or only watches over them, is not fixed, and need not be.
  • The station master's name, the names of Cairn's crew and their number are not set.
  • The hold has no rules yet: an ability that works only by nearness, deepens with each day spent near it and fades out of range. It is built in a mechanic chat.
  • The illithid that holds Cairn has no stat block yet.
