Ondrel Anchorage
A neutral port on the moon Ostrel, where the Caelestis Sector's Fleet operations run.

Ondrel Anchorage is a neutral port on the moon Ostrel, in a system neighbouring Realmspace within the Caelestis Sector, away from Toril. Fleet operations for the Caelestis Sector run out of it. Captains of the Caelestis Corps work from Ondrel, overseeing Driftwarden crews and handing them their contracts.

Its arc

The crew graduates as Driftwardens of the Caelestis Sector and, in Arc 2, takes contracts out of Ondrel, handled by Captain Haymond Rolt. By then N'ghathrod's landed operations ripple outward at contract scale; the shape is on Colony Operations.
