Krynn
The world of the Dragon Queen, on the eve of the War of the Lance, where Takhisis holds an aspect of Bahamut.

Krynn is a world of Krynnspace, CSC 16423 b. Its gods are gods known elsewhere under other names: Takhisis is Tiamat, and Paladine is Bahamut (see One god, many names). Krynn's present is level with Caelestis's. Takhisis is moving but not free, and the Dragon Armies are marching on Solamnia: the prelude to the War of the Lance.

Description

A large, earthlike world, home to many civilizations.

History

An aspect of Bahamut is trapped by Takhisis in his own ruined temple on Krynn, the temple of Paladine. It speaks through Eldree's egg on Fonains. The dragons that left Fonains and most other worlds set out to face Takhisis and have not reached Krynn. Their own politics hold them.

Points of interest

The temple of Paladine

Paladine's temple lies in ruins, and the aspect of Bahamut that Takhisis has trapped is held there.

How to run it

Krynn is a complete setting in its own right and needs nothing from this campaign. It plays as the prelude to the War of the Lance, as Shadow of the Dragon Queen runs it. Here it is where a question about dragons leads.

Threads

  • Eldree's egg on Fonains links the two worlds: the trapped aspect of Bahamut speaks through it.

Still to decide

  • What the dragons' politics are, and what holds them short of Krynn, are not set.
