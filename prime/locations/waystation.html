The Astral waystation
The last reliable resupply before the long haul to Virenspace.

The Astral waystation is the well-known stop on the charted lane between Realmspace and Virenspace, where the lane splits, and the last reliable resupply before the long haul onward. Any crew heading out that way stops here.

How to run it

Every crew going out toward Virenspace stops here, which makes it the natural place to put a rumour, a meeting or a tail.
