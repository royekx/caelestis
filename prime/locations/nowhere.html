Nowhere
The wreck left where a lesser god forced its way onto a world and both were destroyed. The charts mark a world there, and there is none.

Nowhere is what remains where a lesser god forced its way into a system and down onto a world. The push between god and world, polarity, destroyed them both. It is called Nowhere because the charts mark a world there and there is none. A Monolith answered, and every god since has taken Nowhere as a warning.

History

The god forced its way past the system's edge, which pushes lesser gods back, and down onto the world. The world and the god were destroyed together, and Continuity answered the destruction with a Monolith, as it answers a Zenith. The account is in Cosmology.

Points of interest

The exposed heart

The wreck's heart lies exposed, the easiest in the Erevos Galaxy to find and open.

The Monolith

The Monolith that answered the death of the god and the world stands over the wreck.

What's hidden

  • Nowhere's heart is found, and not yet opened. Vecna chose it for his working because it lies exposed; how it fits is on Opposition: Vecna.
