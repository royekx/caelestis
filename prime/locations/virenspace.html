Virenspace
An overlap site that speaks in a dead Starchild's voice, and the place Mirt's wardrobe opens onto.

Virenspace, CSC 69387, is a star system beyond H'Catha, sparsely documented in standard Fleet archives. It is reached by the charted lane from Realmspace to the Astral waystation and a long haul onward. It holds the stellar feature that matches the place inside Mirt's wardrobe; Mirt has placed the match in the Viren system without narrowing it further.

Description

Virenspace is an overlap site. Zerathis, a Starchild, tethered here again and again, took every gift offered, and dissolved into the system's paracausal layer. Paracausal change here runs faster than a single tether would make it, and it is loud enough to feel on arrival. What is at Viren now is part Zerathis, part Astra Virex's reach and part the site itself. It speaks, sometimes in his voice and with his memories, and it is malevolent in no direction at all.

Points of interest

The feature Mirt's wardrobe replicates

A stellar feature somewhere in the system. The interior of Mirt's wardrobe at Caelestis corresponds to it, a bounded stretch of astral night under unfamiliar constellations.

In the campaign

  • Voyage 1. Mirt named the Viren system, beyond H'Catha, as the place his wardrobe replicates. It was where Casey's vision and guiding voice pointed.

What's hidden

  • The voice. The voice Casey hears as Polly is Astra Virex wearing Polly's shape, and it grows clearer with each tether. Astra Virex is Casey's true patron.
  • The wardrobe. Zerathis gave Mirt the wardrobe. It is one of Zerathis's tethers, a paracausal replica of his anchor site here.
  • The feature is Casey's first tether point.

How to run it

What is at Viren may call Casey brother or Starchild without preamble, which is how he first hears the word applied to himself.

Still to decide

  • What Casey's first tether point is, and how many tethers Viren already holds, are not set; together they decide how deep the overlap runs.
