Aethris
Sol's homeworld, a Tymora-devoted world whose Zenith stands unfinished under the Prism Ascendant.

Aethris, CSC 56973 b, is Sol's homeworld. Much of it is devoted to Tymora, and its culture frames fortune as destiny and compresses fated outcomes into ritual. Its Zenith is unfinished, because the Fortuned have not completed their ritual. The Prism Ascendant, the Monolith that answered it, hangs above the temple site.

Description

A large, earthlike world with a civilization across it. Above the temple site hangs the Prism Ascendant.

History

Long ago the Fortuned cast a generational curse to increase their own fortune, and the people made to gather their misfortune have been born Misfortuned on Aethris for generations since. The Fortuned's long-prepared ritual of perfection at the temple site began Aethris's Zenith, and the Prism Ascendant manifested above the temple in answer. Sol escaped before the ritual closed. The full account is on the Fortuned.

Peoples and culture

Two named kinds of person exist in relation to Tymora's faith: the Fortuned, born marked by her favour and treated as the natural elite, and the Misfortuned, born marked against it and treated as aberrations. The Fortuned misread Tymora's tenet, "fortune favours the bold": they pushed luck until it stopped being luck.

Aethris is a whole world, and not all of it follows Tymora. Beshaba keeps a smaller following there; she is the lesser of the two sisters on Aethris, because people favour luck over misfortune. Other gods of fortune, major and minor, are worshipped too. Tymora and Beshaba are both strongest on Aethris: worship concentrated on one world buys them force there that they have nowhere else.

Points of interest

The Zenith and the Prism Ascendant

The Prism Ascendant is a Monolith, Continuity's answer to Aethris's Zenith. The temple holds that it is Tymora's answer to the ritual of perfection; it is the Monolith that answered. Neither Tymora nor Beshaba owns or controls it, and their rivalry plays out beneath it. It is the first Monolith Aethris has had, though the Misfortuned have been born there for generations. It has archived what it can and stays where it is. It does nothing.

CSC 56973

Aethris's system, with one known world. It has no name on record. It lies a long haul from Realmspace, past the carved-meteor hub, and a long haul from Virenspace.

What's hidden

  • Vecna is pushing Aethris's Zenith toward completion from outside; he wants a completed Zenith he can use, and nobody on Aethris knows he is involved. The rest is on the Fortuned.

Its arc

The CRC hunts Sol, and every meeting with it is harder than the last. Aethris is a world the crew can visit to see what a Zenith in progress looks like.
