Caelestis
The Fleet station in orbit around Toril that houses the Caelestis Corps and the Caelestis Spelljammer Academy.

Caelestis is a Fleet station in orbit around Toril, in Realmspace. It houses the two parts of the Caelestis Sector of the United Intergalactic Fleet: the Caelestis Corps, the sector's branch of the Fleet, which oversees the sector, and the Caelestis Spelljammer Academy, which trains cadets to become Driftwardens. The sector's command, the Bridge, sits here, and so does its archive, the Fleet Intelligence Archive. Mirt is headmaster of the Academy and runs the Academy only; the Corps and the Bridge operate outside him.

Caelestis is stretched thin. Zeniths come more often, the Watchers ask the Fleet for help more often (see the Fleet), rumour of an illithid sect has reached the station, and something is moving at Realmspace's edge. Vocath sees the thinning and uses it to put pressure on the Academy.

Description

The station is a tower overlooking the Astral Sea, organised as a layered order of halls, ranks and thresholds. It holds the Simulation Deck, administration, cadet quarters, the Sky Dock and the Sea Dock, the quartermaster's stores, the Spelljammer Nexus, the gymnasium, an infirmary, and the Bridge with the headmaster's quarters on its level.

Security

Caelestis is under permanent Forbiddance, kept by Saerthe Abizjn as senior caster. Teleportation into, out of or within the warded area fails. The ward is renewed nightly across a thirty-day cycle and tested monthly at the dock perimeters. The mechanics are in Rules.

Authorized transit inside the station uses bound Teleportation Circle sigils linking a small number of fixed points: the Bridge, the Brig, the Sky Dock control house and a handful of operational nodes. Sigil sequences are issued by role, on a need-to-know basis, and rotated from time to time. Saerthe keeps the registry. Cadets and most personnel never see a sigil. The wards are standard Fleet security for an installation of consequence.

Caelestis is also planar-dead: it has no echo, and a thin place aboard leads nowhere.

Peoples and culture

Driftwardens of the Corps, cadets of the Academy, instructors, dock workers and visiting Fleet staff share the station. Rank shows in gems or pins; the Corps' seated officers wear something close to a uniform, and everyone else wears what they like. Cadets live communally, and the Weeping Goddess is the one room where every faction's people are in each other's hearing.

Points of interest

The Brig

The Bay of Restricted Inert Goods is Caelestis's high-security holding: a vault, in plain Fleet language, for objects whose cosmological character is unknown, unstable or inconvenient to discuss. Access is heavily compartmentalised. The full inventory is known to a small handful at Bridge level, and individual handlers know what passes through their own post and nothing about what is held elsewhere. Routes to and from the Brig are themselves classified, and couriers know only the next handoff. Teleportation fails into and out of it. Its holdings include A.T.L.A.S., catalogued as a navigational instrument of antique origin.

Simulation Deck

Where the Trial by Fire simulations run: pods, scenario controls and instructor stations, under Boatswain Tarto. A sabotage incident here injured cadets and left metallic plates and a jagged sigil in the chamber walls, the same hand as the brand on the astrally collared neogi.

Weeping Goddess

The station's main refectory, under blue-glowing orbs. Cadets, instructors, visiting personnel, dock workers and Fleet staff all eat here, and Petty Officer Winston Ryeback runs its kitchen. A board of handwritten notices runs along the back wall: well-wishes, odd jobs, dock requests and missing-item notices.

Sky Dock

The dock facing Toril, where most cadet ship-handling exercises take place and spelljamming ships of every shape rest. Kip and Pik, two rock gnomes, run ship-anatomy drills and dock operations. Jeffrey, a dock hand, is paid to let certain manifests go unchecked.

Sea Dock

The lower cargo dock, a working dock for bulk supply, maintenance and salvage handling. Rindle Gearloft works here, and he hides the Latchling, a paracausal entity, at the aligner berths along the mooring struts.

Spelljammer Nexus

The chart room and navigational training hall, overlooking the Astral Sea. Cadets guide miniature spelljamming ships over a floating field, and it holds galaxy charts, registered route logs, ledger archives and library terminals between the mock helms and Saerthe's desk.

Stores

The station's supply office, Mr. Blip's domain. Issued kit, ration rotation and miscellaneous logistics pass through here.

Fleet Intelligence Archive

The Caelestis Sector's archive, locked behind clearance beyond a cadet's star. It holds what Caelestis knows of its own sector and only some of what the other sectors share.

Cadet quarters

Bunk space for the Academy. Communal, with a few windows onto the astral dark in the wings that face outward.

Gymnasium and assault course

Physical training: fitness, combat drill and obstacle assessment.

Mirt's quarters

The headmaster's private quarters, on the Bridge level, cluttered with clothes and curiosities, with a fishbowl holding two octopi, Flotsam and Jetsam. They hold an extradimensional wardrobe whose interior is a bounded stretch of astral night under unfamiliar constellations: a magical replica of a place beyond H'Catha in Virenspace, gifted to Mirt by the spellcaster Zerathis.

In the campaign

  • Voyage 2. The crew first gathered over a meal at the Weeping Goddess and sorted out their shipboard roles. Casey worked cargo for Rindle at the Sea Dock.

What's hidden

  • The target. Caelestis holds A.T.L.A.S. in the Brig, and Joffrey, the colony's operative, has been mapping routes to it from inside the Corps since his conversion finished, only recently. The thefts, the sabotage and Vocath's grudge against Mirt are noise over that one objective. Fleet Intelligence sees incidents and has not assembled them. How Vocath came to know of the Brig is on Vocath's crew.
  • The secret part. A part of the station is kept secret from cadets and most of its staff.
  • The Sky Dock seam. The manifests Jeffrey lets through are how the colony's material moves in and out of Caelestis.
  • The wardrobe. It is one of Zerathis's tethers, a paracausal replica of his anchor site in Virenspace carrying a thread of the Viren shift across space, and Zerathis followed Astra Virex, not Vecna. It is why Casey was drawn to it, and why Astra Virex, in Polly's voice, could reach him there. Mirt has kept a piece of Astra Virex's reach in his quarters for years.

How to run it

Nobody reads the wards as evidence that Caelestis holds anything unusual, because they are what any important Fleet installation has. Anyone working out where a lane goes without Bridge authorization comes to the Spelljammer Nexus first. The Weeping Goddess is the place for overheard talk.

Its arc

The crew's Arc 1 runs at Caelestis and ends in the graduation attack and the run on the Brig; the sequence is on Colony Operations. After graduation the crew takes its contracts out of Ondrel Anchorage.

For H'Catha the crew chooses one instructor to go with them, Tarto or Saerthe (see Stat Blocks). If they choose Saerthe, someone has to keep the Forbiddance while he is away.
