Rykoe
The remains of the Reach world at the centre of the cosmos, beneath the Core Monolith and the Radiant Citadel.

Rykoe is at the centre of the cosmos: the remains of the Reach world, a black hole beneath the Core Monolith and the Radiant Citadel. The five galaxies lie around it, and every galaxy's core is to its galaxy what Rykoe is to the cosmos. Rykoe opens only when the cores of all five galaxies are awake and joined; through it lies the way into Continuity itself (see Waking a core).

Description

The approach

Rykoe is not a plane, so reaching it is travel. The approach is dangerous to mortals, and they can still cross it. It harms gods without destroying them: the First Heart beneath Rykoe is the deepest heart there is, so the push of polarity is strongest there. Because a taking is underway somewhere at almost every moment, the Core Monolith's side of the intake never shuts, and matter, light, structures and people arrive continuously from everywhere. That makes the approach the most violent region in the cosmos.

Scholars of Caelestis know their galaxy is one part of something larger, with something at its centre. No one they know has reached it.

History

The people of the first world, the only world in an earlier version of reality, reached into Continuity and changed it: the Reach. Their world was destroyed by it. Continuity answered for the first time, and the Core Monolith manifested over the world and remained after it was gone. The Reach world collapsed into Rykoe, and its fragments spread across the cosmos as residue. Its people's own name for their world went into the Core Monolith with them, and their descendants in the Citadel carry it for as long as the forgetting leaves it with them.

Peoples and culture

Everyone a Monolith has ever archived arrives here, relayed through the intake, and lives in the Radiant Citadel: the Reach world's descendants first, and every taken people since.

Points of interest

The Core Monolith

The first Monolith, far larger than any raised since, and the terminus of every relay. Those who dwell in it call it Aurath. It has a degree of sentience and presses an idea outward through the cosmos. Few in the cosmos know it exists; how its pressure is read is in Cosmology.

The Radiant Citadel

The Radiant Citadel is the civilization around the Core Monolith, built by the taken. It began with the people of the Reach world and has grown with every archiving since: cities, languages, species no one outside has seen in ages, and the ships people arrived in, sitting where they landed.

Zeniths are often caused, and the people who cause them stand in the middle when the Monolith manifests. The Citadel holds an age of archmages, ritualists, druids, hierophants and architects of transcendence, taken by the response their own workings summoned. It is the densest concentration of capability in existence.

On arrival it is beautiful: preserved people, an inheritance of craft and knowledge from every world that ever crossed a threshold, and abundance that has never had to be fought over. The Order's doctrine looks borne out. The Restorers' objection stands beside it undamaged, because nobody asked the taken. Underneath, the Citadel is broken the way every world is broken, and faster. Each archiving delivers a population that chose nothing, a district of strangers with their own gods, laws, languages, debts and grudges, set down inside a city that already had an order. Every grievance is permanent and every settlement provisional.

Precedence by arrival

The Reach world's descendants are the oldest lineage and sit accordingly. The long-settled hold the good ground, the deep archives and the offices that decide where a new arrival is housed, and abundance thins the further a district lies from the centre. The oldest hold that they were chosen: reached first, at the first Zenith, by the first Monolith. Every later arrival is a guest admitted to something already underway.

The forgetting

Time in the Citadel takes the outside away. A street name goes first, then a face, then the shape of a sky, until people can no longer say what their world was called. The long-settled regard it as healing; the newly taken fear it most, and they are the ones who most want out.

The way out is known in theory: find an intake that is open, because a taking is underway somewhere, and go up it against the flow. In practice a traveller must predict where a Zenith will peak, find that intake among a permanent inbound flood, and move in the brief window it runs, to arrive on an unknown world at the hinge of its history with no ship and no charts. Those who went have sent no word back.

Vecna (Lich)

Vecna (Lich) is Vecna's mortal form. Oerth's Monolith archived it when Vecna ascended and, like every Monolith, relayed it to the Core Monolith. He sits in the Citadel. He has had centuries to conclude he was wrong, and he is calm, almost redeemed.

The First Heart

The Reach world's heart, the deepest heart in the cosmos, lies beneath Rykoe. It has been closed since the Reach. Every other heart pulls faintly toward it, a pull called the Yearning.

How to run it

The Citadel's layout runs concentric: precedence, abundance and certainty all run from the centre outward, with the oldest lineages innermost and the recently taken at the rim. Build its districts when the crew heads there. Vecna (Lich) is calm and almost redeemed, and meeting him should unsettle everything the crew thinks they know.

Its arc

The Radiant Citadel and the Core Monolith are late Arc 3 or Arc 4 destinations. The crew reaches them by a long voyage or through a linked heart. A part of Vecna can manifest in the First Heart, so a confrontation with him could come there. What happens if Rykoe opens is on Opposition: Vecna.
