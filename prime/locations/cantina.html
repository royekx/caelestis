The travelling cantina
A cantina that works a circuit of the charted lanes instead of holding a position.

The travelling cantina works a circuit of the charted lanes instead of holding a position. Because it moves, a crew can meet it in one stretch of the drift and again in another.

How to run it

Drop it wherever the crew is travelling. It answers a long, quiet leg that needs a scene, and because it moves it can turn up twice without explanation. It is in the current prep ring, with H'Catha.

Still to decide

  • Who tends bar is not set.
