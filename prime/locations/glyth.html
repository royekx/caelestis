Glyth
The ringed illithid world that cast out the unthinned, hard to reach and closed to the Fleet.

Glyth is the illithid world of Realmspace, ringed and remote, ten days from the sun and eight days' sail from Toril. It is a settled civilization with elder brains, thrall populations and the full apparatus of illithid society. Glyth cast out N'ghathrod's people, the unthinned, and so set the Vth'orama on its long search for a home. It is hard to reach, and it is closed.

Description

Glyth is an outer world on a long orbit, with rings and three moons. Its approach window comes rarely. Its own defences guard it, and the Fleet bans approach. Glyth is one civilization under its elder brains, which is why it stays closed; a hostile world that is divided against itself, like H'Catha, can be entered.

History

About five hundred years ago N'ghathrod and the five who became his lieutenants escaped Thessalar's planar craft as it fell. The journey back to Glyth, a system away, took decades. Glyth rejected them and exiled the unthinned, and N'ghathrod chose to go with them, because they are his blood. That was roughly four to four and a half centuries ago. The colony's history from there is on the Vth'orama.

The Order of the Watchers posted Watchers to Glyth, one to a region. One of them lived within an elder brain's range for years and came out alone, and she still hears it. The others posted there did not come back. The Fleet lost a force at Glyth during the world's last approach window, and Glyth has been closed to Fleet traffic since (see the Fleet).

Peoples and culture

Glyth's illithids are raised under its elder brains, and thralls serve them. To Glyth, an illithid raised without an elder brain is unfit; that judgement is what the unthinned were exiled for.

How to run it

Glyth is kept unreachable. The crew learns about it from someone who has come out of it, the Watcher who still hears the elder brain, not from a visit. If a crew ever reaches Glyth, it is talking to illithids who threw the colony out.
