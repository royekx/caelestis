Krynnspace
Krynn's star system, reached from Realmspace on an uncharted route. Fonains lies in the neighbouring system.

Krynnspace, CSC 16423, is a star system of the Erevos Galaxy with one known world, Krynn, CSC 16423 b. It is reached from Realmspace on an uncharted route, far beyond the watch at Realmspace's edge. Fonains lies in the neighbouring system, Fonains's system, CSC 50743.

Description

Krynn is the system's only charted world, lettered b. Fonains, CSC 50743 c, circles its own star in the neighbouring system, and the two worlds stay tied across the gap: the aspect of Bahamut trapped on Krynn speaks through Eldree's egg on Fonains.

History

The dragons that left Fonains, in the neighbouring system, and most other worlds set out to face Takhisis and have not reached Krynn.
