Glossary

The settled vocabulary of the cosmology, in one place — definitions rather than explanations. Every term has an anchor; pages across the wiki link their first mention here rather than re-defining, and where two terms are easy to confuse the distinction is stated under the pair. The reasoning behind each term lives in Cosmology, the people who use them in Factions, and what the antagonist does with them in the Ascension.

Origin

TermMeaning
The First World The world that attempted contact with the Axiom layer. Destroyed by its own Zenith. Every world in the cosmos formed from its debris.
The Reach The attempt itself, and the event that followed. The founding catastrophe of the cosmos.
Residue Matter of the First World, scattered by the Reach. Paracausal by origin, which is the only reason it can act on a paracausal lock. Four fragments, seventeen pieces, split 2 / 3 / 5 / 7 by Axiom bias. Pieces of one fragment draw toward each other.
The Core The first Monolith. Manifested over the First World during its Zenith and remained after the world was destroyed. It stands at the center of the cosmos, it is the terminus of every relay in existence, and it is permanently receiving. Its intake is what every chart marks as the black hole at the center.
The Citadel The inhabited interior of the Core. Everything ever archived is here, including an age of the powerful, the people of the First World, and the ships they arrived in. Presents as a golden age and is stratified underneath by order of arrival. Nothing has ever come out.
The All-Father The Order's reading of the presence that accumulated in the Core. Preserve. Hold the line. Accept what is lost.
The All-Mother The Restorers' reading of the same presence. Come back. Recover what was taken.
All-Father / All-Mother One entity, one silence, two readings. The Core has never issued a directive to anyone. Both orders draw real power from it and neither can produce a sentence. That is the whole of the schism between them.

Structure

TermMeaning
The Prime Continuum The cosmos as a whole: every sphere, the Wildspace between, the Astral Sea, the Flow, and the center.
The Axioms Boundary, Dissolution, Transformation, Convergence. The four structural principles the Continuum maintains equilibrium through. They are sentient and constrained entirely by their function: no Axiom can act contrary to its own nature. Together they are the universe.
Continuity The persistence the Axioms hold between them. What Monoliths exist to preserve.
Paracausality A thing whose place in the structure of reality has been altered. Operating outside normal cause and effect. An Axiom-level property, orthogonal to the Weave. It has two sources: residue, and Seams.
Seam A place where two Axioms overlap. Rare, local and brief. The observed pairing is Transformation and Boundary: a thing changes category and the line that should have held it in the old one gives way. See Cosmology → Seams.
Seam-product Something a Seam left behind. Gregory, the Latchling and the wound in Astra Virex. Unlike residue, Seams are ongoing, so these keep appearing.
Starchild Someone who answered Astra Virex's call and was changed by it. Their presence extends Astra into the regions they inhabit, which alters the cosmological character of those regions over time. Casey is one; Zerathis was.
Zenith The moment a world, culture, species or system reaches an irreversible change to what it fundamentally is. Not inherently a disaster — a coronation, an ascension, a transcendence and a collapse are all Zeniths. The outcome is unknown while it happens, which is why the archive comes first. Produced by Convergence, with or without anyone's involvement.
Monolith An emergent manifestation of Continuity, raised when a Zenith's strain exceeds tolerance. It archives the world before the change and then stands permanently. It belongs to no one and serves nothing beyond preservation.
Ortholith The Order of the All-Father's technical term for a Monolith. Same object, institutional register. The Watchers use it in registry entries, survey logs and anything written for the record, where Monolith is what everyone else says. A fragment of one is a fragment of a Monolith.
Archiving What a Monolith does. It takes people, creatures, structures and knowledge bodily, as a sample of the world, and relays them to the Core. A Monolith holds nothing locally.
Intake The channel a Monolith opens while it is archiving, and closes when the taking ends. The only passage between a Monolith and the Core. It runs both ways and almost nobody has ever used it outward.
The relay The network of every Monolith reporting to the Core. The one structure in the cosmos that is everywhere at once.
Zenith / Monolith A Zenith is the event. A Monolith is the response, and only when the strain is great enough. Zeniths are constant across the Continuum; Monoliths are rare.
What a Monolith will not take A Monolith archives what the Zenith threatens with loss. A god standing in a dying world is unbound from it and continues past it, so the mechanism finds nothing at risk and takes nothing. The exception proves the rule: Vecna's mortality was archived at the Zenith of his own ascension, because his mortality was precisely the part about to cease.

Hearts

TermMeaning
Heart A world's metaphysical interior — its accumulated self, everything it has been and settled into being. Paracausal, and the closest thing in existence to the Axiom layer. Every world has one, and a heart persists after its world is destroyed.
Atrium The one entrance to a heart. Folded away rather than buried — a door in a temple that appears only under certain light, the still point at the center of a maelstrom, a courtyard that becomes the way in for a few minutes each year. Paracausal, which is why residue is the key and ordinary searching is not.
Child heart The heart of any world other than the First. All of them are residue of the First Heart.
The First Heart The First World's heart. The deepest, and the one point from which the Axiom layer can be reached. Closed. Known to almost no one.
The Yearning The pull every child heart holds toward the First Heart. Before there were worlds there was one thing, and the separation left this in each of them. Latent and inert while the First Heart stays closed.
Opening Reaching a heart's center with residue in hand and unfolding it. An opened heart can hold a link. Opening grants no control over the world.
Heart-link A live passage between two opened hearts — moments instead of a crossing, permanent, bidirectional, usable by anyone who reaches either end. A Monolith over a world is what licenses its heart to hold a link. The Monolith is a permission, never a route.
Bleed What linked worlds do to each other over months and years. A tide arrives on a world with no moon; a crop fails on one and appears on the other; people dream in a language that is not theirs. It does not reverse.
Found / opened / linked Three separate steps. Finding the Atrium is the hard one and is what A.T.L.A.S. solves. Opening takes residue and a descent, and can be done on any world. Linking requires a Monolith standing over that world.
Hearts of dead worlds A heart is paracausal, so it persists where its world does not. An Atrium can hang in empty space with a Monolith above it and nothing else for a million miles. Inside is the concentrated essence of what that world was — a place to enter and take something out of, rather than a place to live.

The Ascension

TermMeaning
The Ascension Vecna's whole project: to reach the First Heart, cross into the Axiom layer, and take the Convergence seat. His third — he ascended to lich, then to god, each time by changing category rather than gaining degree. Full account in the Ascension.
The Figure The pattern of opened, linked child hearts the Ascension is built out of. Fifteen hearts, three per sphere, across five spheres, so the pull is drawn from the whole cosmos rather than one corner of it.
The Cast The single execution. The massed Yearning of the Figure forces the First Heart; the same structure carries him through. Arrival and trigger are one act, and it gets one attempt — a failed cast lets Convergence re-stabilize around the existing configuration, permanently.
Vessel A taken body carrying a portion of him. He cannot pass through one heart whole, so he distributes across every mouth at once and reassembles at the far end. Fifteen at minimum, one per opened heart.
The downward tithe Worship that pools rather than rises. N'ghathrod venerates him without knowing the name, and what returns fills the colony's taken. The oldest hold the most; a fresh one holds a trace. Any taken is a vessel — they differ only in capacity.
The Remaking What the cosmos becomes once a mind that can choose is sitting in the Convergence seat. The consequence, not the goal. He may never have thought about it.
Lich Vecna The mortal half, taken by the Monolith raised at his own ascension and held in the Citadel since. He wanted godhood and had already begun thinking past it — what a god would still lack, and what would be worth wanting next. He holds the shape of the ambition and none of the method, because the method required knowing things only a god could learn. He has had an age to reconsider, and has.
The Ascension / the Remaking The Ascension is what he is doing. The Remaking is what happens to everything else if he finishes. Treating them as the same thing is the mistake the Order makes.

The hands

Three bodies move the pieces. Two of them are doing the Ascension's labour and would stop if they understood it. The third has nothing to do with him at all.

TermMeaning
The Seekers Chapters on dozens of worlds, each taught once by a voice that gave a method rather than an answer. Their searching drives worlds to Zeniths, which raises Monoliths. They have never heard his name. Full treatment.
The Vth'orama The illithid colony under N'ghathrod. Finds Atria, opens child hearts with residue, links them under Monoliths, and manufactures the vessels. Believes it is building a home that needs no crossing. Full treatment.
The Restorers An order after the archive. Everything lost went somewhere; find it and dead worlds can be restored. That points them at the Core and nothing else. No part of the Ascension. Full treatment.
N'ghathu · Enna The taken. N'ghathu is any of them; the Enna are the five oldest, who offered themselves and carry the most.
Why the party matters Lich Vecna holds the villain's mind. A Restorer holds the archive. A Watcher holds the relay and the institutional record. The colony holds the hearts. No one of them holds two. The crew is the only thing in the cosmos that can carry all four, which is the whole reason this campaign can be won by understanding rather than force.
