Glossary

One or two lines for each term, every one anchored so other pages can link their first mention here. How the terms fit together is in Cosmology; the people who use them are in Factions; places are under Locations and on the Chart.

Energy and the Axioms

TermMeaning
Energy The only thing there is. It has always existed, is never made or destroyed, only arranged; everything that exists is energy held in a pattern for a while.
The Axioms The five laws of how energy behaves: Consolidation, Expansion, Dissolution, Distinction and Determination. Balanced but not equal; none is a substance. Cosmology.
Consolidation The Axiom that draws things together.
Expansion The Axiom that spreads things apart.
Dissolution The Axiom that pulls everything toward sameness, the way ink spreads through water. Time runs one way because of it.
Distinction The Axiom that makes and keeps things different from each other.
Determination The Axiom by which every outcome must be decided, while nothing sets which way it goes. Once decided, an outcome stays decided.
Collapse Consolidation meeting Dissolution: one place, one state. It closes possibilities.
Proliferation Expansion meeting Distinction: more room, more distinct things. It opens possibilities.
Formation Consolidation meeting Distinction: stars, worlds, life. It holds a few possibilities and makes them last.
Dispersal Expansion meeting Dissolution: cooling, thinning, the Astral Sea between systems. It scatters possibilities too thin to meet.
5-cell The figure of five points all equally far apart, which exists one dimension up. It pictures the five Axioms around Continuity; drawn flat, it matches the five galaxies around Rykoe.
Continuity The interplay of the five Axioms: reality itself. One thing with the Prime Continuum. Whoever reaches into it can remake reality.
The Prime Continuum All of existence; another name for Continuity.
Paracausality The state of a thing whose place in the structure of reality has been altered, so that it operates outside ordinary cause and effect. Hearts, Atria and residue are paracausal.
The Weave The fabric mortals reach into to cast spells. Anti-magic severs local access to it. A separate axis from paracausality.

Origin

TermMeaning
The Reach The act of the people of the first world, the only world in an earlier version of reality, who reached into Continuity and changed it. Their world was destroyed, and reality rebalanced into its present form. Cosmology.
Reach world The first world, where the Reach happened. It collapsed into Rykoe, and its fragments became residue.
Rykoe The center of the cosmos: the remains of the Reach world, a black hole beneath the Core Monolith and the Radiant Citadel. Entry.
Core Monolith The first Monolith, standing where Continuity first answered, over Rykoe. Every Monolith relays to it, except a corrupted one. It has a degree of sentience and presses an idea outward through the cosmos.
Aurath The name those who dwell in the Core Monolith give it.
Radiant Citadel The civilization around the Core Monolith, built by everyone ever archived. Vecna (Lich) sits there. Entry.
The All-Father The god the Order serves. The Order knows it as a pressure and reads it as a charge to maintain things as they are. The pressure comes from the Core Monolith, which the Order has never heard of.
The All-Mother The source the Restorers posit for every Monolith, where everything taken goes; they read its pressure as a call to bring back what was lost. It is the Core Monolith, the same presence as the All-Father.
The First Heart The Reach world's heart, the deepest heart in the cosmos, beneath Rykoe. Closed since the Reach.
The Yearning The faint, latent pull every heart holds toward the First Heart.

Zeniths and Monoliths

TermMeaning
Zenith The moment a world reaches its tipping point: its peak of Distinction, the most fully itself it will ever be. It happens once, and a Monolith answers it. Cosmology.
Monolith Continuity's response when a world is pushed to an extreme, most often a Zenith. It records the world at that peak, archives what is about to be lost, and makes links between hearts possible. Cosmology.
Ortholith The Order's word for a Monolith, used in registries and survey logs.
Archiving What a Monolith does: it takes people, creatures, structures and knowledge bodily, as a sample of the world, and relays them to the Core Monolith.
Intake The channel a Monolith opens while it archives and closes when the taking ends; the only passage between a Monolith and the Core Monolith. It runs both ways.
The relay Every Monolith reporting to the Core Monolith, taken together.
Corrupted Monolith A Monolith that is a tunnel to Tharizdun's prison: it holds what it takes and sends it to her instead of to the Core Monolith. The only one known formed on Fonains.
Axiom strain The strain on the Axioms' balance from the century's rise in Zeniths and Monoliths, a byproduct of Vecna's work. It ripples through the planes and is starting to reach mortals. Cosmology.
Weather of reality Reality behaving strangely where the Axiom strain reaches: a pressure that builds and belongs to no one. Few have seen it, and fewer have a name for it. Cosmology.
The Day of Chaos The day the Princes of Elemental Evil break the barriers between the planes so Tharizdun can leave her prison.

Galaxies and space

TermMeaning
Galaxy One of the five great bodies of star systems around Rykoe, each circling a black hole core.
Core (of a galaxy) The black hole at a galaxy's center, where Consolidation and Dissolution win outright. It is to its galaxy what Rykoe is to the cosmos, and it can be woken.
Erevos Galaxy GAL-01, Caelestis's galaxy, the only one Caelestis charts.
Erevos The black hole at the core of the Erevos Galaxy. It sleeps.
Eldritch galaxy GAL-02, whose gods are eldritch and none of them good. Its core is awake, and it has no Weave.
Astral Sea The material space that fills the dark between systems and between galaxies. Entry.
The drift The Fleet's name for the Astral Sea.
System A star and the bodies that circle it, named for its primary.
Greyspace The system of Oerth, in the Erevos Galaxy. Entry.
Oerth Vecna's homeworld, in Greyspace, where he became a god; the Ascension World. Every link in his eye runs back to it. Entry.
Nowhere The wreck left where a lesser god forced its way onto a world and both were destroyed. The charts mark a world there, and there is none. Entry.

Designations

TermMeaning
Designation What Caelestis's charts call a body. Names are what people say; a body nobody has charted has no designation. Cosmology.
Epithet A byname for a body, local ("the falling star of Gethros") or wider (the Blood Moon), carried alongside its name.
CSC The Caelestis Survey Catalogue. Every star Caelestis has charted has a CSC number, old and scattered: CSC 48213.
Constellation A region of Caelestis's sky, numbered by its bearing in degrees: CON-312. Its unnamed stars go by brightness, from Alpha down.
Planet and moon designations A planet takes its star's designation and a lowercase letter from b, in charting order (CSC 48213 c); a moon adds a Roman numeral (CSC 48213 c II).
Provisional designation The label a small body seen once gets from the Cycle it was sighted. P/ marks a returning comet, C/ one that passes once: P/1490 K2.
Cycle One Galactic time cycle: one full turn of Erevos, the heart at the galaxy's core, with all four spiral arms lit in order. The current cycle is 1491.
Arm lighting A quarter of a cycle. Erevos throws an unseen beam as it turns; when the beam reaches an arm's root, a wave of light ripples out along that arm to its tip, lighting marker clusters as it passes. Entry.
Galactic time cycles (GTC) The galaxy's standard count of time, made by the Order of the All-Father, tied to no planet: a cycle is one full turn of Erevos, read in the four arm lightings, and the spread of each lighting gives the finer step. A date reads as a cycle and a step, from the Order's founding, Cycle 0. The Fleet adopted it about 250 cycles ago and spread it; most people call it cycles or Fleet time. The current cycle is 1491. Entry.
GAL A galaxy's designation, GAL-01 to GAL-05.

Gods

TermMeaning
Worship The source of a god's power, influence and reach. It reaches a god from any galaxy, weaker the farther it comes. Cosmology.
Concentration and reach The two ways a god spends worship: force on one world, or a thin foothold almost everywhere.
Greater god A god of great standing, dwelling in the Astral Sea between galaxies and kept out of them by the push of their cores.
Lesser god A god that can pass into a galaxy and travel between its systems, pushed back at a system's edge.
Polarity The push between a god and a world's heart, which carry the same charge. It grows as a god comes closer until going on is unbearable, and keeps gods off worlds and away from Rykoe. Avatars carry too little of a god to be pushed.
Godhood Ascension Vecna's first ascension, on Oerth: his ritual pushed Oerth to its Zenith, and the Zenith made him a god.
Axiomic Ascension The climb Vecna is making now, into Continuity itself, through opened Rykoe.
Vecna (Lich) Vecna's mortal form, archived by Oerth's Monolith when he ascended, and held in the Radiant Citadel since. He has concluded he was wrong, and he is calm, almost redeemed. Dossier.
The First Seeker One of the names Vecna's faithful use for him away from Oerth, where he is worshipped as Vecna.
The Accordance The six gods who sealed the Princes of Elemental Evil on Fonains. Asmodeus and Lord Primus are its eldest, and Asmodeus is its face. Deities.

Hearts and residue

TermMeaning
Heart A world's paracausal interior, its accumulated self. Every world has one, and it persists after its world is destroyed. Cosmology.
Atrium A heart's own hidden entrance, manifesting differently on each world according to what that world is.
Opening Carrying residue into an Atrium, which opens the heart so it can hold a link. It needs no Monolith.
Link A passage between two opened hearts, Atrium to Atrium, possible only where both worlds have a Monolith. It runs within one galaxy and cannot be undone.
Bleeding What linked worlds do to each other over time, one gaining what the other loses.
Wayfinding A Wayfinder's travel to anywhere they have personally been and marked. It needs a heart to attune to.
Residue The pieces of the Reach fragments. Paracausal by origin; carried into an Atrium, a piece opens the heart. Cosmology.
Fragment One of the five bodies of residue the Reach left, one per Axiom, each broken into a prime number of pieces: Dissolution 2, Distinction 3, Expansion 5, Consolidation 7, Determination 11. Pieces of one fragment draw toward each other.

People and powers

TermMeaning
The Fleet The United Intergalactic Fleet: four sector branches, one on each of the Erevos Galaxy's four spiral arms, under a council of admirals. Caelestis founded it. One of the largest organized bodies of spelljammers, though not every spelljammer belongs to it. Entry.
Sector One of the Fleet's four branches, one along each arm of the galaxy, overseeing a cluster of several systems in the Erevos Galaxy under its own command. Entry.
The Caelestis Sector The Fleet's sector that includes Toril's system, in two parts: the Caelestis Corps and the Caelestis Spelljammer Academy. Entry.
The Caelestis Corps The Caelestis Sector's branch of the Fleet, which oversees the sector; its operations run out of Ondrel Anchorage. Entry.
The Caelestis Spelljammer Academy The school at Caelestis that trains cadets to become Driftwardens, one of a handful of spelljammer academies in the galaxy. Entry.
The Bridge The Caelestis Sector's name for its command. Other sectors call their commands something else. Entry.
Fleet Intelligence Archive (FIA) The Caelestis Sector's archive: what Caelestis knows of its own sector, and some of what the other sectors share. Entry.
Driftwarden Anyone in the Fleet's service, from the newest graduate to a commander. Caelestis trains cadets to become Driftwardens.
The Order and its Watchers The Order of the All-Father, whose Watchers watch over Monoliths and fight oddities across the Erevos Galaxy. Entry.
The Restorers An order trying to reach a heart, and through it the All-Mother, the source they posit for every Monolith, where they believe everything lost is stored. Entry.
The Seekers Vecna's faith. Each chapter is named for what it seeks, worships Vecna, often under another name, and knows nothing of the other chapters. Entry.
The Vth'orama N'ghathrod's illithid colony, a Seekers chapter: the Seekers of a New Home, "all who go toward home". They believe they answer to an elder brain. Entry.
n'ghathu · Enna Vth'ghathu An n'ghathu is one of the taken, grown from N'ghathrod's tadpoles. The Enna Vth'ghathu, the near-held, are his five lieutenants.
The tithe The portion of every taken person that passes up through the rungs of the taken to N'ghathrod and extends his life. The flow is paracausal.
The eye in a wheel The Vth'orama's mark: an eye inside a wheel whose top half has ordinary spokes and whose bottom half turns into illithid tentacles. The wheel is navigation, finding a way home. Entry.
Vessel A body Vecna works through, since his ascension pushed him off worlds. Once hearts are opened and linked, his vessels pass through Atria.
Circle of Fifths Vecna's own name for his working: five galaxies, each a fifth of what he needs, around Rykoe. The name is his alone. Vecna.
Starchild Someone who answered Astra Virex's call and was changed by it; their presence extends Astra into the regions they inhabit. Casey is one; Zerathis was.
