Gregory
An autognome who casts, which no other autognome in this world can. Calibration is downstream of that.

Autognome abjurer. His paracausality is that he casts at all — autognomes are constructs and no other one in this world can. Calibration is downstream of that change rather than the definition of it, and the distinction matters when the arc reaches his origin.

Mr. Blip is the control case: an autognome with personhood inside the normal range who cannot cast. OSTEKK-6 is the contrast from the other side — an automaton, built rather than ensouled, who knows the difference and says so.

The tug

Near anything the ripple touched — a place, a person, an object, an event — you describe a sensation in his core. A directional pull, like a compass needle trying to settle, and no malfunction or pain. Then you offer it: do you want to calibrate?

You initiate, always The offer is yours to make, which keeps the pacing yours, and the answer is his. He can decline, ask what it costs, or hesitate — and the offer itself is a roleplay beat rather than a gate.

Good moments: he is physically near the anomaly or something connected to it; a quiet beat where he is alone or reflective; he is doing navigation, construct work or spatial reasoning; something nearby resonates briefly.

The roll

Intelligence (Arcana), no declared DC. He never learns the threshold. His modifier is +2, and Built for Success gives him a d4 after seeing the roll twice per long rest, which means he can push a static result into a signal and will feel the decision.

A failed roll yields interference and costs him nothing, which keeps the instrument reading like a sensor rather than a skill check: the anomaly is real, he is working, the signal is still unresolved.

Anomaly 01 — the Latchling

At the Sea Dock, hidden by Rindle Gearloft, who is protecting it from the academy. Its change is sentience where its kind has none, and Gregory's prologue repair of a latch may have been the local condition that let the ripple resolve into it. He has a causal and ambiguous connection to its waking, mirroring his own uncertain origin.

Rindle sits outside the mechanic. Calibration delivers location and nature; Rindle appears when somebody physically looks, asks questions or pokes around the berths, and the discovery there is that someone already knew. Calibration gets him to the dock, and Rindle is what is waiting. The arc is the journey, since he knows the destination.

Open: the anomaly queue past the Latchling. Each one should reframe his origin again, and the question his existence poses is cosmological rather than personal.
