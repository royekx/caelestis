A Starchild who sees constellations nobody else can, and binds himself to the points he reads.

Hadozee warlock of The Many. A Starchild, and his faculty is visual: he sees constellations nobody else can. Stellar Reading is that faculty at the table, and it is also the mechanism of everything below.

The loop

Every constellation point Casey attunes to becomes a tether. He believes he is building a chart. He is anchoring Astra Virex into another thread of the cosmos, and the chart he keeps is that network drawn from the inside.

StepWhat happensWho sees it
1. He attunesHe is present, he witnesses, he binds. Astra cannot extend itself and needs a hearer to do the stitching.Casey. He experiences it as progress.
2. Astra reaches throughThe anchored region drifts. Slowly, ambiently, uncannily. Mortal politics warp around it; people who go there return altered.Nobody, for a long time.
3. Polly clarifiesHis brother's voice comes through cleaner than before.Casey. This is the reward signal.
Casey never pays. The cosmos does. The reward arrives at the same moment as the cost and masks it, which is how the affection works as a trap. He notices the voice getting clearer and never the place going wrong, and he is the one writing it in.

What withdrawal costs

Held in reserve for the moment he learns enough to choose. If he resists or stops:

CostAt the table
Polly's voice thinsThe immediate one, and the one that lands. He feels his brother slipping.
The chart goes quietStars he could read turn inert. Visible, meaningless. Stellar Reading degrades or stops.
The cloak stops matteringThe stitched constellations read as a costume rather than a record.

Walking away costs him the only family he has left, the practice that ever made him feel chosen, and the most concrete piece of his own identity. That is what makes it a real warlock choice rather than a reveal.

Overlap

Where more than one Starchild has tethered, paracausal change accelerates, and the drift becomes loud enough for Casey to register. A single tether sits below his threshold; an overlap site does not.

Virenspace is point one and it is already occupied Zerathis was a prior Starchild who went deep, took every gift offered, and dissolved into the paracausal layer of the system. What is at Viren now is part Zerathis, part Astra's reach, part the site. It can speak, sometimes in his voice and with his memories, and it is malevolent in no direction at all.

It may call Casey brother or Starchild without preamble, which is how he first hears the word applied to himself. The cautionary tale does not have to be explained to him. He gets to meet it.

Mirt's wardrobe is a paracausal replica of Zerathis's anchor, carrying a thread of the Viren shift across space. That is why Casey was drawn to it, and why Polly could reach him there: Astra's presence was already established at that location. He read it as luck, and he was being magnetized. Deep overlap sites elsewhere in wildspace are available as bartender stories and a that planet — strange properties, no stated cause.

Open: whether he has met the word Starchild before, and whether Zerathis recruited others, which decides how deep Viren's overlap runs.
