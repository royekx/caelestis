Bartholomew Grayson
A druid of the stars whose body answers before his reasoning does, carrying a constellation he has not finished.

Bartholomew Grayson is a human druid of the Circle of the Stars and a wilder, a cadet at Caelestis with an uncanny natural gift for the helm. He reads the night sky as the working tool of his practice. A two-starred constellation, CON-312, surfaces when he takes his starry form, the shape he saw during a fall as a child, and since Voyage 6 he can take single traits of the creatures he knows without taking their forms. He is the crew's spelljammer.

Appearance

He carries a family staff worn smooth and held together with tape, and a star map that is heirloom, instrument and spellcasting focus at once. In his starry form he becomes a figure of light bearing two stars. When an aspect takes him, only that part of the creature comes: an ape's shoulders thickening, a spider's grip on a human hand.

Personality

Quiet and untroubled, he slipped into a crew that had already formed and settled there. He reads a situation by feel before his reasoning catches up, and his body answers first. He is watchful in a practical way: he strings noisemakers around a shaft and drops a lit match down a dark one to see what rises. He came to Caelestis because his practice was already about the sky.

Biography

Background

His tradition pointed outward before he ever reached the Academy: his circle draws power from stellar patterns, and his staff and star map came to him through his family. As a child he fell, and during the fall he saw a constellation, the two-starred shape that now shows in his starry form. He arrived at Caelestis after the other cadets of his cohort had formed a crew, and missed their first voyage for a wilder's training.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 2. Boatswain Tarto oriented him and told him being a wilder changed nothing once he was aboard a crew. He found a seat at the Weeping Goddess beside Miken, ordered the dragon scale pizza Tarto had recommended, and joined the gathering crew. In a Nexus practice he took instantly to the helm and settled the open spelljammer's seat. He flew the hammerhead through an electrical storm and led the salvage race, called a fog cloud over the hull, and slowed the ship during the githyanki attack to give Boogie a way back aboard.
  • Voyage 3. He helped the rival crew up in the wrecked simulation chamber.
  • Voyage 4. He shifted into a starry form of light bearing two stars and recognised it as the shape he saw during his childhood fall. He dropped a lit match down the bottomless quarters shaft and watched wisps rise toward the light, and took a full shock in the wisp fight in the choked shafts.
  • Voyage 5. He lit his staff for the descent, loosed frost at the behir and soaked it with a jet of water that scattered the lightning in its mouth. He went down in its jaws and came back on Tumak's potion, then threw back a clockwork horror with a wave of thunder during the retreat. On the Command Deck his magic would not answer and he drank a potion instead, touched a wisp and was drained, and learned to slap them off.
  • Voyage 6. On the first watch he strung noisemakers around the garden shaft and caught Miken speaking into a sending stone. In Vaelorix's garden he breathed a newly bloomed flower that made his body want to become something it had never been, and sealed it in a vial. As an ape he tore the great flower from the beholder's body, and he struck the expulsion that put Ostekk-10 out of the ship.

Abilities

A druid of the stars: frost, water, thunder and fog, a light on his staff, and a starry form of constellation-light. He takes the shapes of an ape, a boar, a giant wolf spider and a wolf. He can also take one trait of a creature he has studied without taking its form, an ape's strength or a spider's climb, and each trait settles into a star of his constellation. At the helm he has a natural affinity, and Saerthe keeps a note of it on his desk.

Where it heads

Recognition, not search. The Almost is already present in him and he has been circling it, so there is no creature to hunt down: the climax is whether he understands the shape well enough to hold it. The Almost is the creature Bartholomew builds. His constellation is its blueprint, six stars for six ability scores, and each aspect he catalogues seats into the matching star. When the constellation is complete, its shape is his full Almost form. The bloom from Vaelorix's garden is the catalyst, and finding where its parent plant grows is his adventure.

Gates and escalation

  • The bloom. He will think the bloom causes his change. Searching for where it grows gives him reason to travel and to identify creatures, and Vaelorix's notes teach more about it.
  • The alien ladder. Every form and aspect he learns should teach his body something the Almost already knows, so the recognition reads as inevitable rather than granted (the ladder).
  • The name. An old druid or a text can one day tell him, “They used to call people like you Almosts.”
  • Vecna's working. His arc crosses the strain Vecna's working puts on living systems, indirectly until that plot arrives.

Build

Druid, Circle of the Stars, with the Wilder specialisation. Wisdom 18 against an unremarkable Intelligence. His spellcasting focus is the star map. Wild Shape forms: ape, boar, giant wolf spider, wolf. Aspects woke in Voyage 6.

Aspects

The player text, catalogue and handout are on Aspects. In summary:

  • Manifest Aspect. A bonus action; the aspect lasts 1 minute. Uses equal his proficiency bonus, regained on a long rest. He cannot manifest an aspect in a Wild Shape form.
  • Prepared aspects. Equal to his proficiency bonus, seated once per long rest, at most one per ability score.
  • What can be learned. Only creatures with a heart, blood and a heartbeat that are not humanoid in shape. Humanoids, plants, constructs and elementals are excluded, and the handout adds oozes and undead. Rule gray areas as they arise.
  • Learning. One success per day, against a DC you set from the creature's CR. A failure locks that creature out for 24 hours. Non-beast aspects come only from study, which is where the feature's value lives.
  • Wild Shape stays separate. Wild Shape runs by the book on its own track. Swapping into a known form never feeds the catalogue.

Full Almost form

Unlocks when the constellation is complete, and costs a Wild Shape use. His physical scores become exactly the seated creatures' scores, with no cap; his mental scores stay his own. AC is 10 + Dexterity unless a seated aspect grants hide or armour. Dinosaurs are off the aspect menu and must be earned.

The bloom

Consumable: inhaling it restores a Wild Shape use. The Lantern of Flourishing can renew it, and renewing it with a Lantern charge is the crew's active choice. Its parent plant is a carrot, and no parent plant is aboard.

Slips

A slip is a partial, involuntary slippage toward a shape he has not taken. It is flavour by default: a hand that holds wrong for a moment, a gait that is briefly another animal's. One slip per scene carries mechanical weight, and the rest are texture.

His four shapes and his starry form exist so that a slip belonging to neither has somewhere to be noticed from. Third-register slips seed the Almost, and they arrive attached to something: a creature studied, a biome in collapse, a place where living systems are being rewritten. The strain of a slip is the breadcrumb, with no separate clue track.

What's hidden

  • CON-312. CON-312 is the blueprint of the creature Bartholomew is becoming: six stars, one per ability score. It is what lets him hold two natures at once. Every aspect he learns has a star to sit in, so he can take one living piece of a creature into his own pattern and stay himself; without it he would come apart. Two stars from his childhood fall burn, and the rest fill as he studies. When the constellation is complete, its shape is his full Almost form.
  • The Almosts. “The Almosts” is an old druid name. Druidic tradition hands down whole beast shapes, worked out long ago, and Wild Shape is a clean swap into one. Keeping yourself while taking part of something else is harder, because two natures in one body want to separate. The tradition never taught it, and druids caught partway were warned about as the Almosts.
  • The bloom. The ability is innate. The bloom feeds it and does not cause it.
