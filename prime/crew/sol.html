Sol Fortuna
A paladin of a homebrew oath, and the one crew member whose homeworld is mid-Zenith.

Astral elf paladin. The oath is homebrew and complete, written deity-agnostic so it stands on its own: paladins of Misfortune hold that adversity is the only honest teacher, that comfort breeds weakness, and that hardship endured is strength earned. They face misfortune in understanding rather than fear, and they wear it.

Tenets. Never Yield — failure is not the end, only surrender is. Trial by Fire — a life without adversity is a life unlived. Risk Is Not Ruin — calculated danger is wisdom, malicious harm is not.

Jinx

The core resource, at 3rd level. Charges equal his Charisma modifier, minimum one, all regained on a long rest. Two modes, both free actions in play:

ModeEffect
Jinx a creatureSpend 1 charge. One creature he can see within 30 feet gains the Jinxed condition — choose one ability score, and it has Disadvantage on d20 tests using that score until the start of his next turn. Jinxed is a curse and can be removed as one.
Jinx himselfSpend any number of charges before a d20 test. Each shifts his critical and failure thresholds by 1 in both directions. On a critical failure he takes 1d4 psychic per charge spent.
The self-Jinx is the character in one mechanic Buying a wider crit means buying a wider failure at the same price, and paying for the failure in psychic damage. He gets better odds of greatness by accepting worse odds of ruin, which is the oath stated as arithmetic and is the reason the subclass needs no flavor text at the table.

Regaining charges: on a critical hit, on a critical failure, or on being the target of a critical hit. The economy rewards the extremes in both directions and keeps him reaching for them.

Oath spells run Arms of Hadar and Hex at 3rd, Detect Thoughts and Warding Bond at 5th, Bestow Curse and Life Transference at 9th, Death Ward and Phantasmal Killer at 13th, Steel Wind Strike and Synaptic Static at 17th. Full D&D Beyond snippets exist outside the wiki.

Beshaba's voice

Minimal and capricious, and she talks at him rather than to him. "Stand." "Again." "Continue." The acknowledgment scene set the register: "Well, you had my curiosity but now, you have my attention… I must say, it was a bold move leaving your 'perfect' home, but what makes you so sure you escaped? Fate can be a cruel and twisted thing. Wield it wisely. Or not." Her lines leave deliberate air for him to answer into.

The Crystalline Retrieval Construct

Built by the Fortuned of Aethris after his escape, which makes it bespoke and personal: his leaving was the reason it exists. CR 8, on an advanced detention drone chassis. Traits: Bespoke Target Lock, Regeneration suppressed by thunder damage, Refracting Shielding, Adaptive Reconfiguration at HP thresholds, and Non-Lethal Retrieval.

Run the first encounter as an escape It is built to retrieve rather than to kill, so the first meeting is objective-based and survivable by moving. Its line is "Sol Fortuna. You are required." — and the thunder vulnerability is the kind of thing a crew can find out and use, which is the better version of beating it.

Open: whether the Fortuned want him back as a person or as a component of an unfinished ritual. The answer decides whether his thematic question gets answered by his enemies or by himself.
