The crew together

Each of them relates to the stars differently. Casey connects stars into his own constellation. Bartholomew fills in a constellation the Circle of the Stars already knows, CON-312. Tumak builds a planetary web of marks to find his way home, and Gregory senses paracausal signals. Sol has no star thread, while Boogie carries a shard of a corrupted Monolith in his nucleus.

Three homeworlds are placed:

  • Gregory. Toril, in Realmspace: the Cogfast Enclave on Lantan.
  • Sol. Aethris, CSC 56973 b, mid-Zenith.
  • Tumak. The Wayfinders' homeworld, in a hidden system of the Erevos Galaxy, concealed by the Dissolution fragment's cycle. Lunarfoot is his clan, not the world.

Casey's homeworld is the world the star fell on, and its system is uncharted. Bartholomew comes from a celestial druid tradition with no world named. Boogie drifted, and his origin is unplaced.

Pasts

The player characters remember where they came from. Nobody arrived with amnesia; their pasts get filled in retroactively, as play needs them, and each new detail is written onto the character's page when it lands.
