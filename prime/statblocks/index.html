What the choice actually decides

Saerthe changes the battlefield; Tarto changes what is happening on it. They barely overlap, which is what you want from two possible allies rather than two versions of the same help.

At H'Catha specifically: the terrain cancels magic in places and the domains impose ray effects, so Saerthe arrives as capability — the magic problems shrink and the crew gains a bridge-builder on a water world. Tarto arrives as authority — she is their own chain of command, so their decisions get supervised, and she improves the party's own turns rather than taking the spotlight.

What her block is

A light Battle Master and nothing else: five dice, seven maneuvers, and a decision every round about whether this is the moment. The choice is capability against force, which is the version worth putting in front of the players.

Ryeback asked his question in V006 and Tumak answered that the crew could finish; the swap is now planned rather than invited. The players choose the teacher at the V007 rendezvous.
