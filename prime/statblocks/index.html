Tarto or Saerthe — the players choose Canon

One instructor joins the crew for H'Catha, and the party picks which. The comparison lives here rather than on either dossier, because it belongs to neither of them.

What the choice actually decides Saerthe changes the battlefield; Tarto changes what is happening on it. They barely overlap, which is what you want from two possible allies rather than two versions of the same help.

At H'Catha specifically: the terrain cancels magic in places and the domains impose ray effects, so Saerthe arrives as capability — the magic problems shrink and the crew gains a bridge-builder on a water world. Tarto arrives as authority — she is their own chain of command, so their decisions get supervised, and she improves the party's own turns rather than taking the spotlight.

Tarto's revision widened the gap Restoring her superiority dice and freeing Clear the Deck from her Attack action made her decisively the muscle pick rather than a slightly worse Saerthe. The choice is now capability against force, which is the version worth putting in front of the players.

Both answer Ryeback's question at the Cargo debrief, which is how the reinforcement gets invited rather than imposed. The crew is several sessions from the choice.
