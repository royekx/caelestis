What the choice actually decides

Saerthe changes the battlefield; Tarto changes what is happening on it. They barely overlap, which is what you want from two possible allies rather than two versions of the same help.

At H'Catha specifically: the terrain cancels magic in places and the domains impose ray effects, so Saerthe arrives as capability — the magic problems shrink and the crew gains a bridge-builder on a water world. Tarto arrives as authority — she is their own chain of command, so their decisions get supervised, and she improves the party's own turns rather than taking the spotlight.

What her block is

A light Battle Master and nothing else: five dice, seven maneuvers, and a decision every round about whether this is the moment. The choice is capability against force, which is the version worth putting in front of the players.

Ryeback asked his question in V006 and Tumak answered that the crew could finish; the swap is now planned rather than invited. The crew picks the teacher by a Discord vote before V007, so the instructor scenes can be prepped. The vote has not been held yet.

Colony illithid — archetypes

Every illithid of the Vth'orama runs from the Monster Manual mind flayer plus one archetype and one form, the way summon fiend changes a few lines by type. The archetypes are lesser echoes of the five gifts the enna carry. Read the base block and one row; a new illithid is one choice and nothing new to track. A climactic one starts from its card and is tuned by hand: the illithid that holds Cairn Station, and each of the enna.

True-formedThe archetype's strength, light: one feature, or 1/Day. It keeps everything else: the colony's broad spread, with a leaning.
MalformedThe strength in full, with the skew and the losses. Disproportionate: on par with a mind flayer in total, and it plays very differently. It has Advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened; nothing cultivated it, so nothing reaches it easily. What is wrong with its body goes in the description, not the numbers.

The Cairn illithid is malformed, Voice. Its Voice at a distance is the hold; in a fight it has command at will on held crew, and the crew around it is the fight.
