Sor'Kur
Thri-kreen administrator. Telepathic, and the gate every piece of paperwork passes through.

A thri-kreen on Academy administration. Telepathic, so the voice arrives in minds rather than through air. Issues paperwork, vouchers, and temporary access stars.

The functional gatekeeper for access and routing. Anything a cadet needs permission for passes through this desk, which makes it the quietest point of leverage on the station.

A second thri-kreen speaker

Krik’Lit is the other, and the tyrant ship's clockwork horrors speak thri-kreen. Two people can translate a construct, and Krik’Lit is leaving.

The record is the institution. Access is granted by paper, paper is issued correctly, and a request that arrives in order is approved without commentary.

Telepathy makes the manner spare rather than warm. There is no small talk in a voice that arrives directly.

What's hidden

None established. What the desk knows is the useful part: who was issued access to what, and when.

Threads

The pattern is in this office

Joffrey has been building a route to a classified vault for a year. Every piece of that route was authorised by somebody. The access record that makes him legible runs through this desk, and nobody has thought to read it as a sequence.

The investigation use

Asking for the access history on the Brig routing is a legitimate request that an administrator can fulfil, and it is the cleanest path to the evidence against Joffrey that avoids leaning on Jeffrey.

How to run them

Run the office as friction rather than obstruction. Correct requests succeed; incorrect ones are returned. A crew that learns to use the paperwork gets more out of Caelestis than one that works around it.
