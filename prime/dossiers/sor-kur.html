Sor'Kur
The telepathic thri-kreen at the Academy's administration desk, the gate every piece of paperwork passes through.

Sor'Kur is a thri-kreen on Academy administration at Caelestis, and telepathic, so the voice arrives in minds and not through the air. The desk issues paperwork, vouchers and temporary access stars, and anything a cadet needs permission for passes through it.

Appearance

A tall thri-kreen behind an administration desk, four-armed and chitinous, working several stacks of paperwork at once. The mandibles do not move when Sor'Kur speaks.

Personality

The record is the institution. Access is granted by paper, paper is issued correctly, and a request that arrives in order is approved without commentary. Telepathy makes the manner spare, never warm; a voice that arrives directly has no room for small talk.

Biography

In the campaign
  • Voyage 1. Sor'Kur greeted the cadets in administration with a voice that entered their minds instead of their ears, and issued their packets, vouchers and temporary access stars.

Relationships

Krik'Lit

The other thri-kreen speaker the crew knows, a deckhand who leaves at the V006 swap.

Abilities

Telepathy, and the access record of the whole Academy: who was issued access to what, and when.

How to run Sor'Kur

Run the office as friction, never obstruction. Correct requests succeed and incorrect ones are returned. A crew that learns to use the paperwork gets more out of Caelestis than one that works around it.

Threads

  • Joffrey has been building a route to a classified vault for a year, and every piece of it was authorised by somebody. The access record that makes him legible runs through this desk, and nobody has read it as a sequence. Asking for the access history on the Brig routing is a legitimate request, and it is the cleanest path to the evidence that avoids leaning on Jeffrey.
  • The tyrant ship's clockwork horrors speak thri-kreen, and once Krik'Lit leaves at the V006 swap, Sor'Kur is the thri-kreen speaker left within reach.
