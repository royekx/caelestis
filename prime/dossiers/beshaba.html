Beshaba
Lady Doom, goddess of misfortune, who talks at her chosen and lets him answer into the silence.

Beshaba, the Maid of Misfortune and Lady Doom, is the goddess of bad luck, misfortune and random mischance, and Tymora's twin sister. Her chosen is Sol Fortuna, a Misfortuned who walked out of Aethris, and she speaks to him in brief, direct utterances. She is strongest on Aethris, where she is also the lesser of the two sisters.

Personality

She is capricious, curious and amused. She talks at Sol, not with him, and leaves air for him to answer into without waiting for the answer. Her normal volume is one to three words: “Stand.” “Again.” “Continue.” When she says more, it comes as an event.

Adversity is the only honest teacher, to her. The Oath of Misfortune Sol swore is written without a named god, and it states her position all the same: comfort breeds weakness, hardship endured is strength earned, and misfortune is worn, not worshipped. She is not cruel for the pleasure of it. Sol interests her because he walked out of a world built on her sister's favour, and that amuses her more than anything has in a long while.

She finds the Fortuned heretical, a spit in her face. She calls what they do unnatural, believes Tymora is fuelling them, and blames her sister for it.

Biography

Background

Beshaba and Tymora are twins, luck and misfortune, two sides of one coin. Fortune cannot exist without misfortune. On Aethris both are worshipped more densely than anywhere else, and worship concentrated on one world gives them a force there that they have nowhere else. Most of Aethris favours luck over misfortune, so Beshaba keeps the smaller following.

On Aethris the Fortuned, a sect grown from Tymora's temple order, cast a generational curse long ago and made a people carry their misfortune: the Misfortuned. Beshaba had no hand in it. The category was Aethris's own invention, and she is the goddess who answered someone it threw away.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 2. Sol took a stroke of misfortune onto himself to save Boogie's jump. As it left him, a woman laughed three times, and by the third he knew it was Beshaba. Gregory and Bartholomew saw a hair-thin arc of black lightning flicker out of him and back.
  • Voyage 3. She visited Sol in the night, unhurried and curious, and asked why he embraced chaos instead of fleeing it: “Well, you had my curiosity but now, you have my attention… I must say, it was a bold move leaving your ‘perfect’ home, but what makes you so sure you escaped? Fate can be a cruel and twisted thing. Wield it wisely. Or not.” Sol swore the Oath of Misfortune.

Relationships

Sol Fortuna

Her chosen. She rewards his oath with power, and small misfortunes around him now feel attended to, never random. He knows the voice.

Tymora

Her twin and her rival. Beshaba blames her for the Fortuned. Their rivalry plays out beneath the Prism Ascendant on Aethris, and it stays indirect: it reaches mortals through followers and chosen, never through open war.

The Fortuned

Heretics, in her eyes. What they did to luck on Aethris she calls unnatural.

Abilities

She is a god, and her power over Sol shows as mischance bent his way and as the black lightning of the Oath of Misfortune. She reaches him by voice and by laughter. Like every god she cannot set foot on a world, because of polarity, so her hand on a world comes through those who serve her.

What's hidden

  • The sisters. Neither Tymora nor Beshaba knows what both of them need: each other. Each sister's power leans on the other's.
  • Sol's escape. Beshaba did not arrange Sol's escape from Aethris. A Watcher of the Order extracted him.
  • Aethris. Vecna is pushing Aethris's Zenith toward completion from outside. Beshaba does not know it.

How to run her

Keep her short. One to three words is her normal volume, and the long speech from Voyage 3 should stay rare enough to land as an event. Her register is capricious: she can run past three words, but she never explains, reassures or answers questions. She responds to what Sol does, and the response is usually an instruction to do it again.

Beshaba's reward for the oath is power. To give that power shape she has to want something, and she lays that want on Sol as a quest. Until her want is set, play her as entertained and attentive, and let small misfortune around Sol read as her attention.

Her arc

Her quest for Sol is the next step: it gives his power its shape. Aethris is where her rivalry with Tymora can be seen, and the unfinished Zenith there is her sister's ritual being completed. The thread is on the plot hook.

Threads

  • The Crystalline Retrieval Construct hunts her chosen for the Fortuned, the sect she calls heretical.
  • Neither sister needs to appear. The crew meets their rivalry through Sol, the Fortuned and the CRC.

Still to decide

  • Beshaba's want, the quest she lays on Sol to give his power shape, is not set.
  • Whether Beshaba knows of Vecna's working, and whether she takes a side on Aethris's unfinished Zenith, are not set.
  • How Beshaba looks when she shows herself is not set.
  • Whether Beshaba speaks a warning before the CRC first arrives, and in what words, is not set.
