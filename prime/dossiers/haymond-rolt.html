Captain Haymond Rolt
A captain of the Caelestis Corps at Ondrel Anchorage who oversees several Driftwarden crews and hands them their contracts.

Captain Haymond Rolt is a captain of the Caelestis Corps and works out of Ondrel Anchorage on the moon Ostrel, a neutral port away from Toril, where Fleet operations for the Caelestis Sector run. He oversees several Driftwarden crews at once and hands them their contracts.

Biography

In the campaign

The crew has not met him.

Relationships

Driftwarden crews

He oversees several at once from Ondrel and hands them their contracts.

How to run him

He is an homage to Captain Raymond Holt of Brooklyn Nine-Nine. He is the voice that hands the crew its work in Arc 2, and the contracts that come through him are where N'ghathrod's landed operations first reach the crew at contract scale.

His arc

He is the crew's handler in Arc 2: after graduation the crew becomes one of the Driftwarden crews he oversees, and takes its contracts from him. The contract categories and the run into Arc 2 are on the spine.
