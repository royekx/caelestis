Polly Geim
Casey's older brother, changed at a fallen star, gone looking for where it came from, and missing since.

Polly Geim is Casey Geim's older brother: same surname, same world, same house. A star fell on their world. Polly went to it and was changed by what he found there, then left home to learn where it had come from. He has been missing since.

Biography

Background

Polly grew up with Casey on the Geims' homeworld. After the star fell he went to it, and it changed him. He set out to find its source and did not come back. What he was looking for was Astra Virex, though he did not know it by that name, and he may never have learned it.

In the campaign

Polly has not been seen. Casey hears a familiar voice he knows as his brother's: it gave him directions and hints, waited with him at the rail, and told him “Go there.”

Relationships

Casey Geim

His younger brother. Casey does not know his full identity and finds it in having an older brother. With Polly gone he clings to Tumak, and he answers the voice as his brother.

What's hidden

  • The voice. The voice Casey hears as Polly is Astra Virex wearing his shape, and it grows clearer with each tether. Casey never pays the cost himself; the anchored region does.
  • The real Polly. What became of him is one of two things: he was consumed, or he is caught between life and death. No one knows which.

How to run him

He is the weightiest NPC the players have never met. Every word in his voice is a word Casey trusts, and the cruellest beat available is the gap between the brother and the thing using his shape. In narration the voice is “a familiar presence” or “a familiar voice”, and it stays there. Use it sparingly: it nudges Casey toward things, and it does not direct him.

BeatWhat it does
The voice clarifies after a tetherThe reward. Casey reads it as progress toward finding him.
The voice thins if Casey resistsThe hold. He feels his brother slipping, and that cost lands.
Something speaks that Polly would never sayThe crack. One line, used once, well before any reveal.
Meeting what is left of himEndgame, once his fate is set.

Threads

  • Zerathis went deep, took every gift and dissolved into Virenspace, and what remains there still speaks in his voice. If Polly did the same, the voice reaching Casey is as real and as lost. Casey can meet that answer before he knows it is about his brother.

Still to decide

  • Which of the two became of the real Polly, consumed or caught between life and death, is not set; it waits until a scene needs it.
  • Polly's species and appearance are not set, and neither is the name of the Geims' homeworld.
  • Whether the star that fell on the Geims' homeworld is the part of Astra Virex that broke away is not set.
