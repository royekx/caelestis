Polly Geim
A person, not a power. Changed at the fallen star, and gone looking for what came down.

Polly is a person — Casey's brother, and nothing more exotic than that to begin with. Same surname, same world, same house.

A star fell on their world. He went to it, and he was changed by what he found there. Then he left home to learn where it had come from, and went looking for it. He has been missing since.

What he went looking for Astra Virex. He did not know that was the name of it, and he may never have found out.
DM only Astra Virex uses Polly's voice to reach Casey — the directions, the hints, the presence at the rail, “Go there.”

As far as anyone aboard knows, Polly is simply Casey's patron and his brother, and that is where it stays. In narration the voice is “a familiar presence” or “a familiar voice”; Casey knows it as his brother and answers it as his brother.

The reward loop runs on him. Every tether Casey makes clarifies Polly's voice, which is the whole trap: the affection is the hook, and Casey never pays the cost himself — the anchored region does.

Open: what actually became of him — alive, consumed, or something between. Worth leaving open until a scene needs the answer.

He is the most load-bearing NPC the players have never met. Every word in his voice is a word Casey trusts, and the campaign's cruellest available beat is the gap between the brother and the thing using him.

BeatWhat it does
The voice clarifies after a tetherThe reward. Casey reads it as progress toward finding him.
The voice thins if Casey resistsThe hold. He feels his brother slipping, which is the cost that actually lands.
Something speaks that Polly would not sayThe crack. One line, used once, well before any reveal.
Meeting what is left of himEndgame, and only if the answer has been decided.
The precedent is already in play Zerathis went deep, took every gift, and dissolved into Virenspace's paracausal layer — and what remains there still speaks in his voice and with his memories. If Polly did the same, then the voice reaching Casey is exactly as real and exactly as lost. Casey can meet that answer before he knows it is about his brother.
