Vecna (Lich)
The mortal half Vecna left behind when he became a god, who has had centuries in the Radiant Citadel to conclude he was wrong.

Vecna (Lich) is Vecna's mortal form. When Vecna ascended on Oerth, the Monolith that answered Oerth's Zenith archived that form, because his mortality was the part about to cease, and relayed it, like everything a Monolith takes, to the Core Monolith. He sits in the Radiant Citadel, a person apart from the god. He holds the shape of Vecna's ambition and none of its method, and he has had centuries to conclude he was wrong.

Appearance

He is the lich Vecna was on Oerth: gaunt and withered, grey skin drawn tight over bone, in the dark, layered robes of an archmage. His left eye socket is empty and his left arm ends at the wrist.

Personality

He is calm, and almost redeemed. Where the god plans in layers and keeps every secret close, the lich is patient and candid about what he was and what he wanted. He still has the ambition's shape, the wish to understand where everything came from, and he has set down the means.

Biography

Background

He was an archmage of Oerth who became a lich and spent his long unlife gathering magical secrets and knowledge. At least a few centuries ago his ritual pushed Oerth to its Zenith, and the Zenith made him a god. In that moment the Monolith took the mortal part of him. The god went on, charged to Oerth's heart and pushed off the world; the lich arrived in the Citadel.

The Citadel ranks its people by arrival, and he came late, among the archmages and ritualists taken by the responses their own workings summoned. Time there takes the outside away, a street name first, then a face, then the shape of a sky.

In the campaign

The crew has not met him or heard of him.

Relationships

Vecna

The god he became, and the part of himself that went on without him. Vecna (Lich) has concluded that the climb was wrong; Vecna is still climbing.

The Radiant Citadel

His home since his archiving, a city of everyone ever taken by a Monolith, ordered by precedence of arrival.

Abilities

He keeps an archmage's learning and a lich's long memory. He has no part in Vecna's godhood or its domains.

What's hidden

  • Who he is. The god of secrets keeps his name and origin close, and the lich in the Citadel is the most direct witness to both.

How to run him

Run him as the god's opposite in manner: patient, courteous and open about what he was. Meeting him should unsettle everything the crew thinks they know, about Vecna and about the Citadel. He is the last rung of what the crew can learn of Vecna, after the eye and the figure from lore.

His arc

He is a late destination. The Radiant Citadel is reached in late Arc 3 or Arc 4, by a long voyage or through a linked heart. The reveal ladder is on Opposition: Vecna.

Threads

  • A part of Vecna can manifest in the First Heart, beneath Rykoe, near where the lich sits. The two can be met close together.

Still to decide

  • What he knows of Vecna's working, and whether he would help the crew against it, are not set.
  • Where he sits in the Citadel, and how the forgetting has touched him, are not set.
