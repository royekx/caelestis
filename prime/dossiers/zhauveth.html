Zhauveth
The Voice · Vth'ulhan

Unthinned, barely four feet. Something in the rearing left her with no capacity for violence at all and an overdeveloped faculty for being agreed with. She carries domination and suggestion, and does the negotiating and the arrangements.

She made the arrangement with Vocath

Which makes her the reason Caelestis has a problem. Vocath believes his grudge campaign against Mirt is the whole of it; she is the other end of a deal he does not know he is in. Her instrument on the station is Joffrey.

Tell People who have met her remember the conversation fondly and cannot reconstruct what was said.

Why they offered

An illithid's proper end is absorption into an elder brain — the mind preserved inside a greater one, service repaid with union. It is the promise their whole culture rests on, and almost none of them live to collect it.

A Hold is that promise delivered early, and while still alive. Union with a greater mind without having to die for it. None of the five experiences it as subjugation, because their upbringing gives them no word for it that way. They were offered the thing illithids want most, a century ahead of schedule.

The three unthinned offered in gratitude, because he gave them what bad rearing denied them. The two bearers offered in loyalty, because they could have stayed on Glyth and chose him instead.

They defected from a world rather than from being illithids. The convictions they kept are in Factions → Beliefs, and none of them is an argument a crew can win.

In Qualith, an enna is n'ghath, one who holds — and also n'ghathu, one of the held. Being both at once is what Enna Vth'ghathu, the near-held, actually names.

Threads

Reaching one of the five means reaching N'ghathrod in the only way that costs him anything permanent.

She is the other end of Vocath's deal, and Joffrey is her instrument on the station.

How to run them

Each of the five carries a Hold — a portion of N'ghathrod himself. He speaks through them at will and does not drive them. They are still themselves, and they are also him, and the colony holds both ideas without difficulty.

Killing one ends that Hold permanently

He cannot re-form it without spending more of himself than he has left, and the colony loses the function entirely.

The party will not know they have done this. They will think they killed a mind flayer. The consequence surfaces later, as something the colony can no longer do.

Not yet built CR 12–16, deliberately lopsided. None of the five is a balanced encounter — each is overwhelming at the one thing their Hold carries and soft everywhere else.

The unthinned template applies to the three raised in untended pools: remove something visible from the body, add +2 AC, +30 HP, and one malformation feature. All five have advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened, because nothing cultivated them and so nothing reaches them easily.
