Zhauveth
Barely four feet and unable to strike a blow, with a Voice that nobody near her refuses.

Zhauveth is one of the enna, the five lieutenants of N'ghathrod. Her power is the Voice: domination and suggestion. She is malformed, barely four feet tall, and she makes the colony's arrangements with outsiders.

Appearance

Barely four feet tall. People who have met her remember the conversation fondly and cannot reconstruct what was said.

Personality

She is malformed, as most of the colony's illithids are: very good at one thing and poor at the rest. She cannot do violence and has an overdeveloped faculty for being agreed with. She is pleasant, patient and very hard to refuse.

Biography

Background

Zhauveth is one of the first three malformed illithids. She was made aboard Thessalar's planar craft about five hundred years ago from one of N'ghathrod's own tadpoles, brought aboard before the experiment and cut off from his elder brain. Thessalar experimented on her too and put her into a body he supplied. The experiment with the seventh piece of the Rod of Seven Parts gave her a power of her own, the Voice. She escaped with N'ghathrod in the craft's fall. When he brought the three to Glyth, Glyth saw what they were, refused them and exiled them, about four centuries ago, and N'ghathrod went with them.

She offered herself for a Hold in gratitude, because N'ghathrod chose exile with the three over his own world. A Hold is a portion of N'ghathrod, carried permanently, so that the one who carries it and he are one. To an illithid, absorption into an elder brain is the proper end of a life, the mind kept inside a greater one and service repaid with union, and almost none live to collect it. A Hold is that promise delivered early and while alive, and none of the five experiences it as subjugation. In Qualith an enna is n'ghath, one who holds, and also n'ghathu, one of the held; being both at once is what Enna Vth'ghathu, the near-held, names.

Her long life comes from the taking. A portion of every taken person passes up to N'ghathrod as the tithe, and he shares the life it gives with a chosen few, the enna among them. She has outlived every illithid of her age by centuries.

In the campaign

The crew has not met her.

Relationships

N'ghathrod

Her centre. She speaks for the colony to outsiders.

Vocath

The merchant she deals with for the colony. His line supplies captives, and she supplies what the taken know: which cargo moves when, which inspection is coming, where the patrols and escorts run. He deals with her and never with N'ghathrod, and her gift for being agreed with keeps him content.

Her spawn

Like every illithid aboard, she spawns into the pools, and her spawn inherit some of the Voice. She has few that are very good at holding minds, and she spends them where a hold matters most.

Abilities

The Voice is her own, from the experiment. She cannot do violence, and domination is not violence: she tells a crew to lay down their arms and hold still, and they do. She is overwhelming in a conversation and needs nothing else in a fight. N'ghathrod can speak through her at will.

What's hidden

  • Vocath's deal. Zhauveth made the arrangement with Vocath: captives from his line for what the taken know. Later she told him he had an informant inside Caelestis, and Vocath uses that informant for his grudge against Mirt. The informant is Joffrey, taken at Cairn Station with one of N'ghathrod's tadpoles. In name Joffrey answers to Qitru, Vocath's intermediary; in fact N'ghathrod runs him through Zhauveth.
  • Why Vocath is not taken. A willing partner costs no tadpole, his crews follow him, and a copy drifts over time. Zhauveth keeps him content instead.
  • Cairn. The colony holds Cairn Station with a pair: a taken station master and one of Zhauveth's spawn, a malformed illithid very good at holding minds, which lives there out of sight and holds the rest of the crew by nearness. Vocath's captives change hands on the lane near Cairn every few weeks, and Zhauveth touches down at Cairn now and then to make the colony's deals with his people.

How to run her

Play her as charming and harmless, because she cannot strike a blow. The danger is in agreeing with her. If she is at Cairn when the crew is, she can end a crew of the cadets' level with a word.

Killing one of the five ends that power for good: it came from the experiment, nobody can make it again, and her spawn carry only some of it. The crew will not know they have done it; they will think they killed a mind flayer, and the consequence surfaces later as something the colony can no longer do. Build her between CR 12 and 16 and lopsided: overwhelming at the Voice and soft everywhere else, with advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened. The malformed template applies (the Vth'orama, Stat block): remove something visible on the body, add +2 AC, +30 HP and one malformation feature.

Threads

  • Reaching one of the five means reaching N'ghathrod in the only way that costs him anything permanent.
  • She is the colony's one face toward Vocath's house, and Joffrey's direction runs through her: a crew that follows either thread far enough reaches her.
  • Away from Vth'oramu, she can be found at Cairn Station now and then.
