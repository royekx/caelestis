Zhauveth
Barely four feet and incapable of violence, with an overdeveloped faculty for being agreed with.

Zhauveth is one of the enna, the five lieutenants of N'ghathrod, and carries the Hold of the Voice: domination and suggestion. She is unthinned, barely four feet tall, and does the colony's negotiating and its arrangements.

Appearance

Barely four feet tall, small and slight for a mind flayer. People who have met her remember the conversation fondly and cannot reconstruct what was said.

Personality

Something in her rearing left her with no capacity for violence at all and an overdeveloped faculty for being agreed with. She is pleasant, patient and very hard to refuse.

Biography

Background

Zhauveth is unthinned: an illithid raised without an elder brain to cull and tend her. She was made aboard Thessalar's planar craft about five hundred years ago, from a body Thessalar supplied for his experiments and one of N'ghathrod's own tadpoles. The experiments with the seventh Rod piece gave her a greatly extended lifespan; she is not immortal. She escaped with N'ghathrod in the craft's fall and was exiled from Glyth with the rest of the unthinned.

She offered herself for a Hold in gratitude, because N'ghathrod gave her what bad rearing denied her. To an illithid, absorption into an elder brain is the proper end of a life, the mind kept inside a greater one and service repaid with union, and almost none live to collect it. A Hold is that promise delivered early and while alive, and none of the five experiences it as subjugation. In Qualith an enna is n'ghath, one who holds, and also n'ghathu, one of the held; being both at once is what Enna Vth'ghathu, the near-held, names.

In the campaign

The crew has not met her.

Relationships

N'ghathrod

Her centre. She speaks for the colony to outsiders.

Vocath

A merchant she made an arrangement with: his line supplies captives.

Abilities

She carries N'ghathrod's domination and suggestion, and she makes the colony's deals. She is overwhelming in a conversation and helpless in a fight. N'ghathrod can speak through her at will.

What's hidden

  • Vocath's deal. Zhauveth made the arrangement with Vocath: his line supplies captives, and the colony placed an asset inside Caelestis that Vocath believes is his own informant. Vocath thinks his grudge against Mirt is the whole of it; she is the other end of a deal he does not know he is in. Her instrument on the station is Joffrey.

How to run her

Play her as charming and harmless, because she is harmless in a fight. The danger is in agreeing with her.

Killing one of the five ends that Hold for good. N'ghathrod cannot re-form it without spending more of himself than he has left, and the colony loses the function. The crew will not know they have done it; they will think they killed a mind flayer, and the consequence surfaces later as something the colony can no longer do. Build her between CR 12 and 16 and lopsided: overwhelming at what her Hold carries and soft everywhere else, with advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened. The unthinned template applies (the Vth'orama, Stat block): remove something visible on the body, add +2 AC, +30 HP and one malformation feature.

Threads

  • She is the reason Caelestis has a problem: Vocath's grudge is her cover, and Joffrey is her instrument.
