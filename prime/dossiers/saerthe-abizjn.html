Saerthe Abizjn
The Academy's helm instructor and senior caster, who keeps the station's Forbiddance and walks on squares of air.

Saerthe Abizjn is the helm instructor at the Spelljammer Nexus and the senior caster at Caelestis. He maintains the station's permanent Forbiddance and the registry of bound Teleportation Circle sigils. He is a Materializer wizard, and the barriers he builds out of nothing are his signature.

Appearance

He dresses like a noble, in a fitted coat with sapphires set at the cuffs. His eyes are quicksilver, an origin trait of Nimbral and nothing his casting does. When he walks on open air, translucent squares form under his steps, visible to anyone watching. A leather band of the lost and found is on his wrist.

Personality

Stern by default and given to wide grins in unguarded moments. He lets fragments of larger knowledge slip in conversation, never full pictures. He is far more attached to his deck of illusions, his classroom in a coat pocket, than to his cape, and he never uses the cape quietly.

Biography

Background

Saerthe came to Caelestis from Nimbral and is its senior caster. He was not aboard the Bellwether Crow when Clint Dashforest went overboard, and he carries the guilt of it. Since then he and Boatswain Tarto have worn a matched pair of bands of the lost and found.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 1. He brought the cadets into the Nexus under the tension of an ectoplasmic breach elsewhere in the tower and ran their first helm exercise over a floating field of miniature ships. He named Gregory's habit of thinking too far ahead and noticed the divided quality of Tumak's mind.
  • Voyage 2. He ran a second helm exercise for Boogie, Gregory and Bartholomew. He briefly lost his composure over the theft of his own named miniature spelljamming ship, then caught himself and apologised. He ran the salvage simulation briefing with Tarto.
  • Voyage 3. He helped usher the injured from the wrecked simulation chamber, took the recovered rod and drew it back before Boogie could examine it, and said he did not yet know what it was. At the bridge he produced an illusion of the rod for Mirt and named its marking: Vocath.

Abilities

He forms five-foot cubes of force out of nothing, four at a time: walls, blocks, a sealed cell, or a line of squares corner to corner that works as stairs or a bridge. He crushes them inward, mends them, and throws one into the path of an attack on an ally. He can mentally call a fleet of tethered spelljamming ships after a minute of concentration, at the cost of prolonged helm-blindness afterward.

How to run him

He is a pool, not a per-rest resource: a cube returns when dismissed, collapsed or destroyed, so he is never out of barriers, only out of placed ones. On a water world the line of squares matters most. The fleet call sits outside his subclass and costs helm-blindness, which is what stops it solving every problem; it is why Caelestis can respond to anything, and why he is rarely sent out.

The simulation in the Nexus is pure illusion. Small mundane objects can bleed through at the exit threshold; magic items never cross. The deck is the drill he runs when the Nexus is unavailable, and a card thrown in a corridor is the same lesson at a tenth the scale.

His arc

He is one of the two instructors the crew can bring to H'Catha, and he arrives as capability: the magic problems shrink and the crew gains a bridge-builder on a water world. If he goes, someone has to keep the Forbiddance. At H'Catha one of the two instructors goes and the other stays, and the one who stays knows which way the other is; that is the bands' payoff. The comparison with Tarto is on Stat Blocks.

Threads

  • Dematerialize answers the CRC's orb, so he must stay out of that fight. The encounter is an escape, and a CR 11 ally in the way turns it into a fight the crew does not need (CRC).
  • The bands give heading and nothing else, so they will never solve a problem. They would not have found Clint either.

Traits

Barrier Footing.
He walks on open air, each step forming a translucent square underfoot, visible to others, that vanishes as he leaves it. Ending his turn in the air drops him. No bridge is left for anyone else.
Deck of Illusions (uncommon).
Thirty-four cards in a lacquered box. Drawn and thrown, a card becomes an illusory creature that moves and speaks as he directs it, until it takes damage or someone studies it and sees through it. It is his classroom in a coat pocket, and he is far more attached to it than to the cape.
Band of the Lost and Found (common).
One of a matched pair of leather bracelets. Each compass points at the other while both are worn, giving heading but no height or depth, and spins uselessly if the two are on different planes. Tarto wears the other. They have worn them since Clint Dashforest went overboard, and neither has said who proposed it.
Cape of the Mountebank (rare, attuned).
A fitted coat with sapphires set at the cuffs. Once a day it casts dimension door, and he leaves a cloud of smoke behind him and arrives in another — lightly obscuring both spaces for a round. He never uses it quietly.
Dematerialize.
Double damage to objects and structures, and an extra 4 (1d8) force damage to Constructs.

Actions

Multiattack.
Three Barrier Spikes. He can replace one with Barrier, Encase or a spell.
Barrier Spike.
Ranged Attack Roll: +9, range 120 ft. Hit: 16 (2d10 + 5) force damage.
Barrier.
He has four cubes and forms one within 60 ft. It holds a 5-ft. block, a 5-ft. wall one foot thick, or breaks into 1-ft. squares laid corner to corner as a line 25 by 2 ft. that runs straight, angles or arches into stairs or a bridge. Each has AC 15, 15 hit points, immunity to force, poison and psychic damage, and lasts 1 hour. Blocks and walls stop movement and line of effect; the line does not. A cube returns to him when it is dismissed, collapsed or destroyed.
Encase (Recharge 5–6).
The hard lockdown. A Large or smaller creature within 60 ft. makes a DC 17 Dexterity save or is sealed inside and Restrained — one cube for Medium or smaller, two for Large. It escapes by breaking the barrier or on a DC 17 Athletics check as an action. A willing creature can choose to fail, which is the lifeboat.
Spellcasting.
Intelligence, spell save DC 17, +9 to hit. Tenth-level slots: four 1st, three 2nd, three 3rd, three 4th, two 5th.
  • Cantrips. light, mage hand, mending, message, minor illusion, prestidigitation
  • 1st (4 slots). feather fall, magic missile, shield, silent image
  • 2nd (3 slots). invisibility, mirror image, misty step
  • 3rd (3 slots). counterspell, dispel magic, fly, major image
  • 4th (3 slots). fabricate, greater invisibility, hallucinatory terrain
  • 5th (2 slots). passwall, wall of force

Bonus Actions

Collapse.
Crushes any number of cubes inward. A sealed creature makes a DC 17 Constitution save for 27 (5d10) force damage, half on a success; anyone within 5 ft. makes a DC 17 Dexterity save for 16 (3d10), half on a success. Each creature is hit once however many cubes fall.
Mend.
Casts mending on one of his own cubes, restoring all its hit points. Anyone else can do it with a normal one-minute casting — so holding a patch under fire is a job to hand a cadet, and Gregory knows the spell.
Release.
Dismisses any number of cubes.

Reactions

Interpose.
When anyone within 60 ft. is hit by an attack, a cube forms in its path and the target gains +5 AC against it. If the attack still lands, the cube breaks.
