Saerthe Abizjn
Helm instructor and senior caster, Caelestis

Helm instructor at the Spelljammer Nexus. Stern in default register, given to wide grins in unguarded moments. Lets fragments of larger knowledge slip in conversation, never full pictures.

Maintains the station's permanent Forbiddance and the registry of bound Teleportation Circle sigils. Senior caster on station.

His miniature spelljamming ship is missing; half of it is inside Boogie. He treats the loss as part of the thefts on the station. The quicksilver eyes are an origin trait tied to Nimbral rather than anything his casting does.

One of the two instructors the crew can choose to bring to H'Catha. He arrives as capability. The comparison.

The core mechanic Four active 5-foot cubes. A pool rather than a per-rest resource: a cube returns when dismissed, collapsed or destroyed, so he is never out of barriers, only out of placed ones.

Cubes configure contiguously into walls and blocks — or, corner to corner, into a checkerboard line roughly 25 by 2 feet that works as stairs or a bridge by exploiting corner elevation. On a water world that matters.

Traits

Barrier Footing.
He walks on open air, each step forming a barrier underfoot that vanishes as he leaves it. Ending his turn in the air drops him. No bridge is left for anyone else.
Robe of Useful Items.
The fitted coat with silver at the cuffs. Its patches become real objects — a ladder, a door, a window, a rowboat — which is the Materializer's trick in a form anyone can carry. He will not say whether he stitched them himself.
Dematerialize.
Double damage to objects and structures, and an extra 4 (1d8) force damage to Constructs.

Actions

Multiattack.
Three Barrier Spikes. He can replace one with Barrier, Encase or a spell.
Barrier Spike.
Ranged Attack Roll: +9, range 120 ft. Hit: 16 (2d10 + 5) force damage.
Barrier.
He has four cubes and forms one within 60 ft. It holds a 5-ft. block, a 5-ft. wall one foot thick, or breaks into 1-ft. squares laid corner to corner as a line 25 by 2 ft. that runs straight, angles or arches into stairs or a bridge. Each has AC 15, 15 hit points, immunity to force, poison and psychic damage, and lasts 1 hour. Blocks and walls stop movement and line of effect; the line does not. A cube returns to him when it is dismissed, collapsed or destroyed.
Encase (Recharge 5–6).
The hard lockdown. A Large or smaller creature within 60 ft. makes a DC 17 Dexterity save or is sealed inside and Restrained — one cube for Medium or smaller, two for Large. It escapes by breaking the barrier or on a DC 17 Athletics check as an action. A willing creature can choose to fail, which is the lifeboat.
Spellcasting.
Intelligence, spell save DC 17, +9 to hit. Tenth-level slots: four 1st, three 2nd, three 3rd, three 4th, two 5th.
  • Cantrips. light, mage hand, mending, message, minor illusion, prestidigitation
  • 1st (4 slots). feather fall, magic missile, shield, silent image
  • 2nd (3 slots). invisibility, mirror image, misty step
  • 3rd (3 slots). counterspell, dispel magic, fly, major image
  • 4th (3 slots). dimension door, greater invisibility, hallucinatory terrain
  • 5th (2 slots). passwall, wall of force

Bonus Actions

Collapse.
Crushes any number of cubes inward. A sealed creature makes a DC 17 Constitution save for 27 (5d10) force damage, half on a success; anyone within 5 ft. makes a DC 17 Dexterity save for 16 (3d10), half on a success. Each creature is hit once however many cubes fall.
Mend.
Casts mending on one of his own cubes, restoring all its hit points. Anyone else can do it with a normal one-minute casting — so holding a patch under fire is a job to hand a cadet, and Gregory knows the spell.
Release.
Dismisses any number of cubes.

Reactions

Interpose.
When anyone within 60 ft. is hit by an attack, a cube forms in its path and the target gains +5 AC against it. If the attack still lands, the cube breaks.
The ceiling, and its price He can mentally call a fleet of tethered spelljamming ships after a minute of concentration. It sits outside the subclass entirely and costs prolonged helm-blindness afterward, which is what stops it solving every problem. It is why Caelestis can respond to anything, and why he is rarely sent out.

Against the CRC: Dematerialize answers the orb, so he should not engage the construct directly. That encounter is built as an escape, and a CR 11 ally in the way converts it into a fight the crew does not need.

One ruling worth keeping handy: the simulation in the Spelljammer Nexus is pure illusion. Small mundane objects can bleed through at the exit threshold; magic items never cross.
