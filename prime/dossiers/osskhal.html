Osskhal
An unremarkable mind flayer who is better at violence than anyone around him.

Osskhal is one of the enna, the five lieutenants of N'ghathrod. His power is the Flesh: reach, grip and the killing. He is whole, one of the colony's illithids who call themselves the true-formed, and an unremarkable mind flayer who is better at violence than anyone around him. He walks into a room and the fight is over.

Appearance

Ordinary for his kind, and unremarkable in a crowd of mind flayers. He has no size and no spectacle.

Personality

He is the one who argues with N'ghathrod in front of others, and he has done it for centuries. His loyalty is the kind that speaks its mind.

Biography

Background

Osskhal is a whole illithid, and he was captured with N'ghathrod when Thessalar's planar craft took their ship about five hundred years ago. The experiment with the seventh piece of the Rod of Seven Parts changed him with N'ghathrod and gave him a power of his own, the Flesh. He escaped with N'ghathrod in the craft's fall and went with him to Glyth. When Glyth refused the three malformed illithids made aboard the craft and exiled them, about four centuries ago, N'ghathrod went with them, and Osskhal went too.

He offered himself for a Hold in loyalty. A Hold is a portion of N'ghathrod, carried permanently, so that the one who carries it and he are one. To an illithid, absorption into an elder brain is the proper end of a life, the mind kept inside a greater one and service repaid with union, and almost none live to collect it. A Hold is that promise delivered early and while alive, and none of the five experiences it as subjugation. In Qualith an enna is n'ghath, one who holds, and also n'ghathu, one of the held; being both at once is what Enna Vth'ghathu, the near-held, names.

His long life comes from the taking. A portion of every taken person passes up to N'ghathrod as the tithe, and he shares the life it gives with a chosen few, the enna among them. Osskhal has outlived every illithid of his age by centuries.

In the campaign

The crew has not met him.

Relationships

N'ghathrod

His centre, and the one he argues with openly. He chose N'ghathrod over Glyth.

Khaleth

The other whole illithid among the enna, captured with him aboard the craft.

Abilities

The Flesh is his own, from the experiment: the reach, the grip and the killing. He is overwhelming at violence and nothing else. Like every illithid aboard he spawns into the pools, and illithids grown from his tadpoles lean toward his power. N'ghathrod can speak through him at will.

How to run him

Play him plain and efficient. He needs no size or spectacle to end a fight, and that is the surprise.

Killing one of the five ends that power for good: it came from the experiment, nobody can make it again, and his spawn carry only some of it. The crew will not know they have done it; they will think they killed a mind flayer, and the consequence surfaces later as something the colony can no longer do. Build him between CR 12 and 16 and lopsided: overwhelming at the Flesh and soft everywhere else, with advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened.

Threads

  • Reaching one of the five means reaching N'ghathrod in the only way that costs him anything permanent.
  • He is one of the true-formed. They keep Glyth's ideal in a colony where most illithids are malformed, three of the enna among them, and N'ghathrod does not accept the name. The colony has never resolved that, and the true-formed are where it is loudest.
