Joffrey
The Corps spelljammer who took Bellwether Crow's helm in the raid a year ago, commended for it and liked by everyone at Caelestis.

Joffrey is an officer of the Caelestis Corps, named the present-day great spelljammer of Caelestis. About a year ago he was the junior spelljammer aboard Bellwether Crow when pirates struck it near Cairn Station. With the senior helm and deck officers dead, he took the helm, brought the ship to Cairn and held the survivors together, and he was commended on their account. Cadets know him and instructors trust him. He is a different man from Jeffrey, a Sky Dock hand and his friend.

Personality

Competent, well liked and convincing. He frames the spelljamming life as duty, vigilance and survival, and he told Sol it offers no riches. Crow's survivors still look to him.

Biography

Background

On a filing from Strake Station, the Fleet sent Crow to look in on Cairn, with Clint Dashforest leading its crew and Joffrey aboard as its junior spelljammer. Short of Cairn, Crow came on two pirate ships in the middle of a handover, pirates in Vocath's hire with his overseer aboard the smaller ship, and they ran up a crossbones and struck to drive it off. To draw the fight off his crew, Clint went aboard the smaller ship and took its helm, and a color pool opened by chance and took that ship with Clint, the overseer and its pirates aboard. With their paymaster gone, the main ship had little left to fight for. Many of Crow's crew had died, the senior helm and deck officers among them. Joffrey took the helm, brought Crow to Cairn and held the survivors together, and from then on they looked to him. Cairn took the crippled ship in, and repairs took days before it went home. He was commended on the survivors' account, posted to Caelestis, and has made his name since on the strength of what he did aboard Crow.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 1. Mr. Blip named him the present-day great spelljammer of Caelestis. Sol sought him out to ask what the life offered; he answered duty, vigilance and survival, not riches. The cadets saw a Sky Dock hand take a bribe.
  • Voyage 2. Casey looked for him at the Sea Dock and the Sky Dock to ask about hadozee birth signs, and did not find him.
  • Voyage 3. Tumak told Mirt that Joffrey was the one who paid the bribe at the Sky Dock. Mirt told the cadets to look into him carefully, given his standing, and named a man called Jeffrey with him.

Abilities

A gifted spelljammer and a steady officer under fire. He knows Caelestis, its people and its routines from the inside.

What's hidden

  • Taken at Cairn. While Crow lay at Cairn Station for repairs, the station master reported up to N'ghathrod, and Zhauveth saw what they had: a ship going home to Caelestis, and a spelljammer its survivors follow. A colony courier brought one of N'ghathrod's copies up the lane within days, and the station master took Joffrey with it. The colony wanted a man inside Caelestis and knew nothing of A.T.L.A.S.; the copy carried no directive. He is also the colony's warning if anyone looks at Cairn again. He knows Crow was sent to look in on Cairn, and nothing of who asked.
  • The turning. He turned over some months, not a year, and made his name on what he did aboard Crow. He keeps part of himself. One survivor of Crow has seen that he changed.
  • The plan. In his first months A.T.L.A.S. came into the Brig, catalogued as an antique navigational instrument, or he learned it was there. N'ghathrod wants residue, and the plan for it started then.
  • The hub. Three insiders work against Caelestis, and Joffrey is the one between them. He watches for shipments carrying Vocath's personal seal and handles what comes in. He paid Jeffrey to pass the sealed crate the cadets later found, and the rod and the beacon came in the same way; he left the rod where Miken would pick it up. Miken sabotages. A third insider, the thief, steals, and Joffrey uses the thief to learn where things are kept and the Brig's protocols, because he has no access to them himself. Miken and the thief each know only that someone else is involved, and nothing either of them knows points at Joffrey.
  • Two masters. In name he answers to Qitru, Vocath's intermediary, who told him about Miken. Vocath believes Joffrey is his own informant. In fact N'ghathrod runs him, through Zhauveth. His reports to Qitru have never contained a lie; he keeps from her that a specific instrument is in the Brig, and that it is the whole point.

How to run him

He shows that a taken person is not a disguise. His file, his account of Crow and the survivors' account all hold up, because they are true. His mind cannot be read.

Catching him is a Fleet scandal before it is a victory. He is commended, liked and provably heroic, so the institution's first instinct will be to disbelieve the crew. How a taking reads is on Rules.

His arc

At graduation, under cover of Vocath's raid and the attack on Caelestis, he runs the Brig for A.T.L.A.S. The run and what follows are on the Fork.

Threads
