Joffrey
Decorated Fleet officer. The colony's only operative on the station.

A Fleet sailor who went out on the Bellwether Crow to find out why Cairn Station had stopped answering. With the senior deck officers dead and Clint Dashforest gone over the side with the boarders' craft, he held the surviving complement together, organised the withdrawal, and brought them off alive.

He was decorated on their unanimous account, posted to Caelestis, and has risen since on genuine merit. Competent, well-liked, entirely convincing. Cadets know him. Instructors trust him.

His record is clean and earned, and that is the problem Nothing about his file is falsified. He did hold them together, and they did come home because of him. There is no forged document to find and no inconsistency to catch, because the story is true in every particular — it is only incomplete.

He is already being looked for. The quest board reads look into Joffrey and the man named Jeffrey, and the name collision has already sent cadets at the wrong man once.

DM only He was taken in the second boarding action. A tadpole of N'ghathrod's, implanted with its directive already taught to it before it was carried.

The survivors who came home with him were allowed to, so that there would be a story and he would be its hero. They believe every word of their account, because every word of it happened.

The taking matured over the following year, which is why he read true for most of it. He thinks, speaks and works as he always did. He is not a puppet wearing a man; he is the man, pointed somewhere else.

He is the colony's only operative on the station. His allegiance runs to N'ghathrod, and his report to Vocath has never once contained a lie.

Two employers, one of whom is wrong about the arrangement Vocath believes he has bought an informant inside the academy. What he actually has is a conduit Zhauveth arranged, feeding a colony Vocath has never heard of. The grudge campaign against Mirt is cover that Vocath is paying for himself.

The clearest expression of it: the Brig's location and access protocols are classified well enough that Vocath could never have found them. Joffrey found the vault and told him it was there — so the strike happens because the asset proposed a target the merchant already wanted to want, and Vocath experiences the whole plan as his own initiative.

Everything he sends passes through Ith'varn before it reaches the core, which means the colony's picture of Caelestis is exactly as good as one man's view of it.

He is the campaign's demonstration that a taken person is not a disguise. Divination, interrogation and long acquaintance all return the same answer, because the answer is true.
