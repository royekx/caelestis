Qitru
Githyanki intermediary. Handles the assets, and is following the crew.

An intermediary in Vocath's commercial operation, handling direct contact with assets and field agents. Githyanki, working from a star lancer.

She is the operation's human-facing layer: the one who recruits, pays and collects, and the one an asset actually meets.

An asset is a liability the moment it stops being useful. She wants Miken because he is leverage someone else now has reach over, rather than out of any loyalty to him or malice toward him.

She works in information before force. The star lancer is for arriving, not for fighting.

How she finds them A beacon Miken planted aboard the ship, rather than any tracking of her own. That is the whole mechanism, and it means she is never where the crew expects a pursuer to be.
The dial she can turn at H'Catha Miken reports that the crew killed a beholder. She arrives ahead of them and tells the ports that beholder killers are coming in.

It need not be lethal, because on that world the reputation reads eight different ways. A claimant wants them hired. A rival wants them gone. The port wants them watched. The exile, hunted by every beholder there, wants them very much indeed. One rumour, and every faction's opening move changes.

Her intelligence is priced by how the crew treated Miken. A friend tells her less than a cornered asset does, so the galley aftermath sets this arc's difficulty without anyone connecting the two.

She shadows them outbound and chooses her moment on H'Catha rather than intercepting in transit. The module's version — a demand for Miken in a debris field, with a giff-captained damselfly tailing — is available as the harder opening if the crossing needs an event.

She docks at the southern port on Arcane access and buys information. She is a competitor for knowledge before she is a threat, which makes her findable: anyone asking the same questions in the same port will cross her.

Open: whether she can be bought, traded with, or turned once she learns Vocath is not the top of his own operation.
