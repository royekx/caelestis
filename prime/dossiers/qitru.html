Qitru
Vocath's githyanki intermediary, following the crew by Miken's beacon toward H'Catha.

Qitru is a githyanki, the intermediary of Vocath's crew, working from a star lancer. She recruits, pays and collects, and she is the one an asset actually meets. She serves Vocath and does not care who stands above him. She is following the crew.

Appearance

A githyanki: lean and yellow-skinned, with dark hair bound high, in travelling armour, a silver sword at her hip. She arrives in a star lancer.

Personality

An asset is a liability the moment it stops being useful. She works in information before force; the star lancer is for arriving, not fighting. She pursues Vocath's aim, to embarrass Mirt by making the cadet mission fail, and Miken is her channel.

Biography

In the campaign

The crew has not met her. She follows them by the beacon Miken planted aboard their ship.

Relationships

Vocath

Her employer. For her, the top is Vocath.

Miken Haverstance

Her channel: the cadet who planted the beacon and reports to her.

Abilities

A githyanki warrior and a practised broker of information, with a star lancer to carry her.

What's hidden

  • How she finds them. The beacon Miken planted, nothing of her own. It means she is never where the crew expects a pursuer to be.
  • The report. Miken's Voyage 6 report has reached her: the crew killed a beholder.

How to run her

She shadows the crew outbound and chooses her moment on H'Catha instead of intercepting in transit. The module's version, a demand for Miken in a debris field with a giff-captained damselfly tailing, is available as the harder opening if the crossing needs an event. She docks at the southern port on mercane access and buys information. She is a competitor for knowledge before she is a threat, which makes her findable: anyone asking the same questions in the same port will cross her.

At H'Catha she arrives ahead of the crew and tells the ports that beholder killers are coming. On a world of ten domains the reputation reads ten different ways. A claimant wants them hired, a rival wants them gone, a port wants them watched, and Ondessa, the beholder ruler the others want dead, wants them very much indeed. Her intelligence is priced by how the crew treated Miken: a friend tells her less than a cornered asset does.

Threads

  • How the crew handles Miken after the sending stone sets the difficulty of this arc, and nobody connects the two.
  • Ondessa holds her seat; the beholder-killer rumour makes the crew interesting to her.

Still to decide
