Vecna
God of secrets, who means to see into Continuity itself.

Vecna is a god of secrets, knowledge, manipulation and trickery, with a hold over death and the undead. He was an archmage of Oerth who became a lich, gathered magical secrets and knowledge, and rose to godhood on his homeworld. He works through vessels and under many names, and he thrives on what others do not know.

Appearance

Vecna is known by what he lost: his left eye and his left hand. As a lich he was gaunt and withered, grey skin drawn tight over bone, dressed in the dark, layered robes of an archmage. His left eye socket is empty and his left arm ends at the wrist, and he never hid either. Where his name surfaces at all, in old lore, it comes with a figure missing both. His holy symbol, an eye in the palm of a left hand, turns the loss into a sign.

Personality

Secrecy is his nature and his power. He keeps his own name and origin close, because there is power in knowing who he was and where he came from, and most of his worshippers know him by other names. He lets his chapters stay ignorant of one another and feeds on that ignorance too. He plans in layers, and every plan he makes has another behind it.

He has a taste for music, and it shows in how he names things. His working across the galaxy he calls the Circle of Fifths.

He wants to understand the cosmos and become part of it: where everything came from, what the Reach was, what was taken. His grievance is that something was taken from him and hidden from him, and he believes the cosmos and every being in it are owed an answer. Breaking reality is not his goal. He believes he could repair whatever breaks.

Biography

Background

Vecna was born on Oerth, a darkest-timeline world in Greyspace. He became an archmage, then a lich, and spent his long unlife gathering magical secrets and knowledge.

At least a few centuries ago he worked a ritual to become a god. It pushed Oerth to its Zenith, and the Zenith made him one. This was his first climb, the Godhood Ascension. The Monolith that answered Oerth's Zenith archived his mortal form, and that form, Lich Vecna, sits now in the Radiant Citadel. Ascension charged him into Oerth's heart and pushed him off the world. Oerth's heart has been his ever since, and he works through others.

His worship is open, and large and public on Oerth. Elsewhere it lives in small, hidden pockets. His legacy is hidden temples, secret texts and artifacts kept for his worship, not ruined ziggurats. Not every worshipper is a cultist: some lean into necromancy, and some believe in an idea of him, the breaking of the cycle of death, more than in the god himself. His faith is the Seekers, chapters that each seek something of their own and that mostly worship him under another name.

The climb he is making now is the Axiomic Ascension, into Continuity itself. Its road runs through Erevos, which opens only when all five galaxy cores are awake, and Vecna works the core of the Rykoe Galaxy. How a core is woken is on Cosmology.

Relationships

N'ghathrod

A means to an end. Vecna whispered to him for centuries while he searched for a home, and it was a whisper that told him a dreadnought was the best way to keep a colony on its passage. N'ghathrod's colony is a chapter of the Seekers that believes it answers to an elder brain. Vecna needs it to open and link hearts.

The Seekers

His faithful. He keeps every chapter hungry for something, and lets each believe the artifacts it hunts will lead it to what it seeks.

Tharizdun

A contingency, used and not allied. They distrust each other. Her weakening of reality on Fonains serves his ends, and he lets it run.

The Accordance

The Accordance know little of him. A few centuries ago they felt a new entity like themselves appear where there had been none. Asmodeus likely knows something is wrong.

The Restorers

They follow the Core Monolith's pressure as he does, read through different minds, and compete for the same residue. Without knowing it, they advance his plan.

Abilities

He was an archmage before he was a god, and spellwork is still his method: his working across a galaxy is an inscription. As a god he holds the domains of Knowledge, Death and Trickery and a hold over death and the undead. He reaches mortals by whisper, and they act for him. He is a lesser god: he can travel the Astral Sea within a galaxy, and a system's edge pushes him back.

What's hidden

  • H'Catha. Vecna influenced the making of the Sovereign Eye at H'Catha's founding, hoping its dreaming would bring the world to a Zenith. It did not. A trace of his worship lingers there, faint.
  • Lich Vecna has had centuries in the Radiant Citadel to conclude he was wrong. He is calm, almost redeemed, and meeting him unsettles everything the crew thinks they know.
  • The name. No one else knows the Circle of Fifths or what he is building: not the Seekers, not the Order. Some gods suspect he is up to something.
  • Steering the chapters. He feeds the colony the few secrets it needs and lets every chapter think the residue it hunts is the thing it seeks.

How to run him

He never appears in person. He is played through what is withheld: vessels acting on whispers, chapters that do not know each other, a symbol the players recognise before the characters do. His name surfaces late, through lore of a figure missing his left eye and left hand. A confrontation, if it comes, can be with a part of him manifested in the First Heart.

His arc

He stands behind N'ghathrod and beyond the campaign's direct reach. The late campaign is about stopping his working, not necessarily fighting him. The eye inscription, where the working stands, his contingencies and the failure state are on Opposition: Vecna.

Threads

  • Oerth is the one world he cannot lose: every link in the eye runs back to it.
  • The first Seekers chapter the crew meets shows his hand and eye openly and is benevolent. The players will expect villainy from the symbol; the characters know nothing of it.
