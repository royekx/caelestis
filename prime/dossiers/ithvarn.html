Ith'varn
Blind, never leaves the ship, and knows what happened before anyone tells him.

Ith'varn is one of the enna, the five lieutenants of N'ghathrod, and carries the Hold of the Sight: the colony bond. He is unthinned and blind, and every taken person's reporting passes through him before it reaches N'ghathrod.

Appearance

His eye sockets never formed. He is blind, and he reads the world entirely through the colony bond.

Personality

Still and certain. He has not left Vth'oramu in thirty years and does not need to, and he knows what the crew did before anyone tells him.

Biography

Background

Ith'varn is unthinned: an illithid raised without an elder brain to cull and tend him. He was made aboard Thessalar's planar craft about five hundred years ago, from a body Thessalar supplied for his experiments and one of N'ghathrod's own tadpoles. The experiments with the seventh Rod piece gave him a greatly extended lifespan; he is not immortal. He escaped with N'ghathrod in the craft's fall and was exiled from Glyth with the rest of the unthinned.

He offered himself for a Hold in gratitude, because N'ghathrod gave him what bad rearing denied him. To an illithid, absorption into an elder brain is the proper end of a life, the mind kept inside a greater one and service repaid with union, and almost none live to collect it. A Hold is that promise delivered early and while alive, and none of the five experiences it as subjugation. In Qualith an enna is n'ghath, one who holds, and also n'ghathu, one of the held; being both at once is what Enna Vth'ghathu, the near-held, names.

In the campaign

The crew has not met him.

Relationships

N'ghathrod

His centre. Everything the colony's taken see reaches N'ghathrod through Ith'varn.

Abilities

He carries the colony bond and reads the world through it. Every taken person's report passes through him, so he knows what has happened across the colony's reach before anyone speaks of it. N'ghathrod can speak through him at will.

What's hidden

  • The ceiling. Every report passes through one blind man, Joffrey's included. The colony's picture of Caelestis is exactly as good as one operative's view of it, and what the colony fails to notice, it fails to notice because of him.

How to run him

If the colony reacts to something too fast, it was him. Use him to show the crew how quickly the colony knows things.

Killing one of the five ends that Hold for good. N'ghathrod cannot re-form it without spending more of himself than he has left, and the colony loses the function. The crew will not know they have done it; they will think they killed a mind flayer, and the consequence surfaces later as something the colony can no longer do. Build him between CR 12 and 16 and lopsided: overwhelming at what his Hold carries and soft everywhere else, with advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened. The unthinned template applies (the Vth'orama, Stat block): remove something visible on the body, add +2 AC, +30 HP and one malformation feature.
