Ith'varn

Ith'varn is one of the enna, the five lieutenants of N'ghathrod. His power is the Sight: the colony bond. He is malformed and blind, and through the bond he sees what the taken report as it reaches N'ghathrod.

Appearance

His eye sockets never formed. He is blind, and he reads the world entirely through the colony bond.

Personality

Still and certain. He has not left Vth'oramu in thirty years and does not need to, and he knows what the crew did before anyone tells him.

Biography

Background

Ith'varn is one of the first three malformed illithids. He was made aboard Thessalar's planar craft about five hundred years ago from one of N'ghathrod's own tadpoles, brought aboard before the experiment and cut off from his elder brain. Thessalar experimented on him too and put him into a body he supplied. The experiment with the seventh piece of the Rod of Seven Parts gave him a power of his own, the Sight. He escaped with N'ghathrod in the craft's fall. When N'ghathrod brought the three to Glyth, Glyth saw what they were, refused them and exiled them, about four centuries ago, and N'ghathrod went with them.

He offered himself for a Hold in gratitude, because N'ghathrod chose exile with the three over his own world. A Hold is a portion of N'ghathrod, carried permanently, so that the one who carries it and he are one. To an illithid, absorption into an elder brain is the proper end of a life, the mind kept inside a greater one and service repaid with union, and almost none live to collect it. A Hold is that promise delivered early and while alive, and none of the five experiences it as subjugation. In Qualith an enna is n'ghath, one who holds, and also n'ghathu, one of the held; being both at once is what Enna Vth'ghathu, the near-held, names.

His long life comes from the taking. A portion of every taken person passes up to N'ghathrod as the tithe, and he shares the life it gives with a chosen few, the enna among them. Ith'varn has outlived every illithid of his age by centuries.

In the campaign

The crew has not met him.

Relationships

N'ghathrod

His centre. The taken report up to N'ghathrod, and Ith'varn sees each report as it arrives.

Abilities

The Sight is his own, from the experiment. He reads the world through the colony bond, and what the taken report to N'ghathrod he sees as it reaches him, so he knows what has happened across the colony's reach before anyone speaks of it. Like every illithid aboard he spawns into the pools, and illithids grown from his tadpoles lean toward his power. N'ghathrod can speak through him at will.

What's hidden

  • The ceiling. The colony's picture of Caelestis is only as good as what Joffrey reports. What Joffrey fails to notice, the colony fails to notice, Ith'varn included.

How to run him

If the colony reacts to something too fast, it was him. Use him to show the crew how quickly the colony knows things.

Killing one of the five ends that power for good: it came from the experiment, nobody can make it again, and his spawn carry only some of it. The crew will not know they have done it; they will think they killed a mind flayer, and the consequence surfaces later as something the colony can no longer do. Build him between CR 12 and 16 and lopsided: overwhelming at the Sight and soft everywhere else, with advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened. The malformed template applies (the Vth'orama, Stat block): remove something visible on the body, add +2 AC, +30 HP and one malformation feature.
