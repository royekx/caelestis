Miken Haverstance
A cadet, a friend, and a leveraged asset wrestling with it.

Miken Haverstance is a cadet at the Academy at Caelestis, on the crew that races opposite the party, and newer to the cohort than they are. He is sociable, willing and easy to like, with a command of magic that seems to run ahead of his year. He is engaged to someone off-station whose debts have pulled him into trouble beyond a cadet's stipend. He is aboard the Tyrant Ship with the crew.

Appearance

A slight, nervous young cadet, eager and a little awkward, who glances at empty chairs and hides a wince when he is hurt.

Personality

He believes he can keep both, the crew and his fiancé, and that what he was asked to do was small enough for that to be true. A beacon is not a knife, and he has worked hard to make that distinction carry the weight. He holds that the debt is his to settle and that telling anyone would make it theirs. He is wrestling with guilt, not hiding malice, and the guilt is the part the crew can reach. He calls the people around him his crew and means it.

Biography

In the campaign
  • Voyage 2. He shared his table at the Weeping Goddess with Bartholomew and the gathering crew, nervous and talkative about the simulation, glancing at an empty chair no one named. He silenced Tumak's snoring with an incantation beyond a cadet of his year, and was assigned with Veena, Barret and his squad to the salvage race from the opposite chamber.
  • Voyage 3. He knelt over a fallen crewmate in the wrecked simulation chamber, sat shaken in the infirmary beside his injured squad and asked to stay with them, then came aboard the Tyrant Ship with the crew.
  • Voyage 5. He offered to come along or guard the crew, came down the rope after them in the retreat from the Hollow Deck, saw the dead beholder on the Command Deck, and declared all hands on deck.
  • Voyage 6. He went down the shaft after the crew and charmed their fall. On the first watch Bartholomew caught him speaking into a sending stone in the hallway by the garden shaft; he said he was saying hello to his family, grew sharp when pressed, then apologised. He is the one member of Veena's crew not badly hurt.

Abilities

Spellcasting beyond his year: he silenced a snore with an incantation and charmed a fall.

What's hidden

  • The beacon. Vocath's crew recruited him and paid his fiancé's debts. What he was asked for was a beacon, planted aboard the ship, so that Qitru could follow it. That is the whole of the sabotage.
  • The report. In Voyage 6 he reported through the sending stone on the first watch and told them the crew killed a beholder, or had a hand in it. He believes it, because Tumak joked about it on a ladder and he took it at near face value. Qitru carries that to H'Catha ahead of the crew, and when the graduation assault comes, Vocath's people have been told this crew kills beholders.
  • The moneylender. The moneylender behind the Tallow Wren repossession contract also holds the debts of Miken's fiancé.

How to run him

Give him no shadow. Every friendly beat is honest, which is what makes the reveal land as a friend's betrayal and not a trick. If a PC cannot attune the helm and he volunteers, let it read as loyal eagerness; in hindsight it is access. Do not lean on it.

Miken stays leveraged and complicit, and he does not flip. He lives, whatever he does and whenever it surfaces, which is what makes deferring his resolution worth anything. The payoff is capture or near-death during the graduation raid, landing on a complicit Miken the crew still likes.

His arc

How the crew treats him sets the difficulty of two later arcs, because it decides how good Qitru's intelligence is. The sending stone and the consequence sit far enough apart that neither looks like the other's cause. The crew suspects him and has the stone on record; whether they confront him, watch him or let it ride is theirs.
