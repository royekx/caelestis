OSTEKK-6
The only working survivor of Ostekk's nine automatons, voiceless, rebuilding, and travelling with the crew.

OSTEKK-6 is an automaton built by Ostekk, one of the nine OSTEKK units Vaelorix stole with the Lantern of Flourishing, and the only one still working. The crew found him broken in the Tyrant Ship's cargo hold with clockwork horrors taking him apart, and he travels with them now, rebuilding on a Lantern charge.

Appearance

His lower half was gone and his voice box destroyed; his arcane core held. Boogie fitted him with pseudopod legs, Mini-Boogie, and he rode them facing backwards, calling out directions. Since Voyage 6 his body has been rebuilding below the waist.

Personality

Clerical, precise and particular. He holds to his designation, his manifest and his function, and being called an autognome is the one thing that reliably provokes him: “I am a unit. An automaton.” He would travel with the crew if they wished, and they do.

Biography

Background

Ostekk built nine automatons, each with a fragment of the Lantern, to depend on the Engine for their maintenance. Vaelorix stole them with the Engine, kept the Engine and used it sparingly to repair his units. When the bloom overran the ship, the creatures aboard destroyed nearly all of the nine.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 3. Found in the cargo hold, lower half gone and voice box destroyed, as clockwork horrors fed on him.
  • Voyage 4. The cargo manifest named him. Tumak opened a telepathic link, and through it he warned of a shape-shifting mimic aboard, the bloom and more horrors, and explained the access shafts and the wisps.
  • Voyage 5. He told the crew he is a unit, an automaton, rode Boogie's legs facing backwards, named Vaelorix as the ship's master and the decks below, and slowed and stuttered on the Command Deck but kept talking.
  • Voyage 6. He identified the Engine as Ostekk's, kept by Vaelorix. When Gregory spent a charge on him, his body began to rebuild. He had not known Ostekk-10 existed.

OSTEKK-2

OSTEKK-2 is another of the nine, whole but for a missing arm. Gregory's jolt of lightning woke it on the Hollow Deck in Voyage 5; it offered its service, named the behir as the entity that boarded the ship and destroyed its comrades, and named the behir's sender as “the original Ostekk”, a brilliant tinkerer. It said retreat is always an option. It lies inert on the Hollow Deck where the crew left it.

What's hidden

  • Why the field slowed him. He was built with a trace of the Lantern, which holds the Rod's fourth piece and is paracausal. That trace is why the Command Deck field slowed him instead of stopping him. Everything Ostekk built separately has no trace: the horrors froze outright and the behir could not enter at all.
  • One object. He does not know that the thing keeping him running and the thing the behir was sent for are the same object.
  • OSTEKK-2. OSTEKK-6 is the only active survivor of the nine. OSTEKK-2 is inert, and the crew could heal it back to function if they return for it, for example with the Lantern they keep hidden.

How to run him

Keep him unrepaired for now. A mute, helpless NPC the crew chose to save and now hauls around is worth more than the exposition a working unit would hand over. He answers direct questions through the link, names things on the manifest and confirms mechanics the crew has guessed; he is a reference, not a revelation. He is a noncombatant: 25-foot speed on Boogie's legs, no attacks, and the durability of something already broken. Putting him in a room with Gregory is the cheapest way to make the contrast visible.
