Boatswain Tarto
Boatswain and training officer, Caelestis

Runs the Simulation Deck. Clipped manner, tracks cadet performance closely, and assigns to scenarios without sentiment. Once counted among the academy's great spelljammers.

Scarred, grey at the temples, wreathed in cigar smoke. One wing flap was torn away long ago; she has not glided since and has never needed to.

"Listen to what I tell you; it might just save your life."

One of the two instructors the crew can choose to bring to H'Catha. She arrives as authority — their own chain of command, so their decisions get supervised. The comparison.

The neogi crate She passed on the crate holding the neogi hatchlings because she was informed rather than having checked it herself. Guilt without negligence, which is the version that keeps her sympathetic and still answerable.

Cut during design, deliberately: superiority dice, Deck Ape (redundant with her climb speed), Silhouette Deck (became "There!"), and the "Hold!" temporary-hit-point order. Her maneuver list lives in the working file. Not recorded here

Why she does not read as the warrior she is presented as

Clear the Deck is a worse Multiattack against one target. She already attacks three times; the recharge gives her one attack per creature she passes. Against a single enemy it is strictly a downgrade, so her signature button is dead weight in exactly the fights meant to feel biggest. A recharge should be her best moment, and instead it is conditional on enemy formation.

Removing superiority dice removed the decision and the spike together. Maneuvers on every hit with no damage attached means three saves a turn, forever — more bookkeeping, less memory, and nothing that lands hard.

Her best features queue behind each other. Commander's Strike and her maneuvers both spend an ally's reaction, so the same cadet cannot take both, and at a six-PC table the routing congests rather than scales.

Proposed revision

ChangeWhat it fixes
Restore Superiority Dice — 4d10, short rest, and the die adds damage again. One maneuver per attack, so a committed round spends up to three. Gives back the decision and the spike at once. A full round goes from ~39 to roughly 55, which is what a CR 10 veteran should manage when she means it, and running dry means something.
Make Clear the Deck free movement rather than a replacement action. She moves her full Speed without provoking, attacking and applying a maneuver to each creature she passes — and still takes her Attack action. Removes the Multiattack overlap and the dead-against-one-target problem in one stroke. Keeps her identity as the one who crosses a deck under fire, and it is never a downgrade.

Not urgent — the crew is several sessions from choosing. Awaiting your call
