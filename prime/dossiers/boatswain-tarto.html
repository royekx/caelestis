Boatswain Tarto
A scarred hadozee boatswain who runs the Simulation Deck, drills cadets without sentiment, and means for every one of them to come home.

Boatswain Tarto is a hadozee instructor at the Academy at Caelestis, and she runs the Simulation Deck. She tracks cadet performance closely and assigns them to scenarios without sentiment. She was once counted among the Academy's great spelljammers.

Appearance

Scarred, grey at the temples, and wreathed in cigar smoke. She wears worn leathers, a cutlass on one hip, and on the other a belaying pin of dark wood banded in tarnished brass, a ship's name carved deep into its grain. One wing flap was torn away long ago; she has not glided since and has never needed to. She crosses a mooring line hand over hand the way only a hadozee can, and arrives unwinded. A leather band of the lost and found is on her wrist.

Personality

Clipped, exacting and fiercely protective. Cadets come home, and everything else she does follows from that: she drills them hard because the alternative is burying them, and she tells them so. She does not want command. She had the helm once and gave it up, and she has never explained which way round that went. She draws: charts, boarding plans, the shape of a room nobody has walked yet, scrawled on a bulkhead and gone by the next watch. It is the only thing she does that looks like patience.

Biography

Background

Tarto was a great spelljammer of Caelestis in years past, and the Academy keeps her as a boatswain and instructor. Since Clint Dashforest went overboard from the Bellwether Crow, she and Saerthe Abizjn have worn a matched pair of bands of the lost and found, and she feels she could have done something.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 1. She revealed that the Moonraider battle was a simulation of Captain Sardax's final stand, never meant to be won. She praised each cadet's strengths, issued their assignments, warned them to stay together amid the thefts, and sent them to deliver a crate to Mirt.
  • Voyage 2. She oriented Bartholomew, telling him being a wilder changed nothing aboard a crew, took the cadets to the Nexus, and ran the salvage simulation briefing with Saerthe.
  • Voyage 3. She ushered the injured out of the wrecked simulation chamber and took the recovered rod from Gregory. With Saerthe she read its marking as Vocath's, walked the cadets in to Mirt, and woke them at four in the morning to brief them on the H'Catha meteor recovery aboard a tyrant ship. She saw the crew off alongside the Tyrant Ship on the Flighty Foundling.

Relationships

A crewmate she lost to the Crow ambush.

A cadet under the Academy's protection. When the Crystalline Retrieval Construct comes for him, she stands up for him.

Abilities

A Battle Master in all but name. She fights through the people around her, putting openings in a cadet's hands, pulling danger onto herself, shoving boarders over a missing rail. She reads an enemy's weaknesses at a glance, the silhouette deck she made the crew memorise, and her pin and line make a rescue rig for anyone going over the side.

What's hidden

  • The neogi crate. She passed on the crate holding the neogi hatchlings because she was informed, not because she had checked it herself. Guilt without negligence keeps her sympathetic and still answerable. She wants the name of whoever routed it through her: a chain of custody, a manifest and a signature, all of which a boatswain can pull without anyone thinking twice.
  • Sol. She, Mirt and Saerthe know Sol's background and keep it to themselves.
  • What she knows. She holds part of the picture of what is stretching Caelestis thin, and she may be withholding some of it.

How to run her

She fights through the crew. Commander's Strike, Maneuvering Attack and Bait and Switch all put the payoff in a cadet's hands, so let them roll the big numbers; Goading Attack pulls danger onto her and off them. Four dice are the whole fight between rests. Three can go in one round, so a hard opening leaves her a single die, and running dry in front of cadets is a scene worth having. That is why she picks her moment. A spent round is about 55 damage; an empty one is about 39. On a deck, Pushing Attack is the kill: fifteen feet and a missing section of rail ends a boarder without a second attack, and it teaches the crew to look at the terrain.

Her three cheap things work together. The pin and the line are a rescue rig: throw the pin past a falling cadet, press it, and send the rope after them. Apart, the pin is a rail where the rail is gone, a handhold when gravity cuts out, a jammed hatch, or a stop to shove a boarder into; Pushing Attack into a set pin rewards arranging the board before swinging. The ring is how she thinks: hand her a bulkhead and she will draw the deck plan, the boarding order or the route out, gone by the next watch.

Her arc

She is one of the two instructors the crew can bring to H'Catha. She arrives as authority, their own chain of command, so their decisions get supervised. Against Saerthe, she makes the crew hit harder and survive a bad opening, where Saerthe changes what they can do; the comparison is on Stat Blocks. When the crew returns to Caelestis and the CRC is waiting, she stands up for Sol.

Threads

  • Commander's Strike, Maneuvering Attack and Bait and Switch all spend an ally's reaction, so the same cadet cannot take two in a round. At a six-PC table that spreads her attention around; watch it in play.

Traits

Combat Superiority.
Tarto has four superiority dice (d10), regained on a short rest. She spends one to use a maneuver, adding the die to that attack's damage. One maneuver per attack, so a committed round spends three. Maneuver save DC 16.
Indomitable (2/Day).
If Tarto fails a saving throw, she can reroll it and must use the new roll.

Actions

Multiattack.
Three attacks, Cutlass or Belaying Pin in any combination. She can replace one with Commander's Strike.
Cutlass.
Melee Attack Roll: +8, reach 5 ft. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) slashing.
Belaying Pin.
Melee or Ranged Attack Roll: +8, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) bludgeoning. Dark wood banded in tarnished brass, a ship's name carved deep into the grain.
The Pin (immovable rod, uncommon).
Pressed, it holds where she leaves it — in open air, against nothing — bearing up to 8,000 pounds, and takes a DC 30 Strength check to shift.
The Line (rope of climbing, uncommon).
Sixty feet, spliced to the pin's eye when she means to use them together. It answers as a bonus action — uncoils, knots itself, or fastens to a creature or object within 20 feet — bears 3,000 pounds, has AC 20 and 20 hit points, and coils itself back on command.
The Ring (doodling ring, common).
A wooden band, always splotched with charcoal, ink or paint however hard it is scrubbed. She touches a finger to any solid surface and draws in any colour. Marks smudge or wash off clean, cover up to 5 square feet at a time, and vanish on their own after 24 hours.
Band of the Lost and Found (common).
One of a matched pair of leather bracelets. Each compass points at the other while both are worn, giving heading but no height or depth, and spins uselessly if the two are on different planes. Saerthe wears the other. They have worn them since Clint Dashforest was lost.

Maneuvers

Commander's Strike.
Replaces one of her attacks. A cadet who can see or hear her uses its Reaction to attack, adding the die to the damage.
Maneuvering Attack.
On a hit, a cadet uses its Reaction to move half its Speed without provoking an opportunity attack from her target.
Goading Attack.
On a hit, Wisdom save or the target has Disadvantage attacking anyone but Tarto until the end of her next turn.
Pushing Attack.
On a hit, Strength save or pushed up to 15 ft. away.
Trip Attack.
On a hit, Strength save or Prone.
Bait and Switch.
On her turn, within 5 ft. of a willing creature: they swap places without provoking. One of them gains a bonus to AC equal to the die until her next turn.
Parry.
Reaction to melee damage. Reduce it by the die + 4.

Bonus Actions

Clear the Deck.
Know Your Enemy (1/Day).
She reads a creature within 30 ft. and knows its Immunities, Resistances and Vulnerabilities. She can restore this by spending a superiority die, no action required. This is the silhouette deck, and it is why she made the crew memorize it.
