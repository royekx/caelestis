Boatswain Tarto
Boatswain and training officer, Caelestis

Runs the Simulation Deck. Clipped manner, tracks cadet performance closely, and assigns to scenarios without sentiment. Once counted among the academy's great spelljammers.

Scarred, grey at the temples, wreathed in cigar smoke. Worn leathers, a cutlass on one hip and on the other a belaying pin of dark wood banded in tarnished brass, something carved deep into its grain.

One wing flap was torn away long ago; she has not glided since and has never needed to. She crosses a mooring line hand over hand the way only a hadozee can, and arrives unwinded.

"Listen to what I tell you; it might just save your life."

One of the two instructors the crew can choose to bring to H'Catha. She arrives as authority — their own chain of command, so their decisions get supervised. The comparison.

Cadets come home. Everything else she does is downstream of that. She drills them hard because the alternative is burying them, and she tells them so.

She wants the name of whoever routed the neogi crate through her. That is a chain of custody, a manifest and a signature, and a boatswain can pull all three without anyone thinking twice about it.

She does not want command. She had the helm once and gave it up, and she has never explained which way round that went.

The neogi crate She passed on the crate holding the neogi hatchlings because she was informed rather than having checked it herself. Guilt without negligence, which is the version that keeps her sympathetic and still answerable.

She fights through the crew. Commander's Strike, Distracting and Maneuvering all put the payoff in a cadet's hands, so let them roll the big numbers. "Eyes on Me!" and Take It pull danger onto her and off them.

Spend her dice early. Four is two committed rounds, and running dry in front of cadets is a scene worth having. A spent round is about 55 damage; an empty one is about 39.

The ally-reaction bottleneck Commander's Strike and her maneuvers both spend an ally's reaction, so the same cadet cannot take both, and at a six-PC table that congests rather than scales. It is a constraint on her rather than a flaw in her — she lends her tactics to one person at a time, and the table has a reason to spread her attention around. Revisit in play
Against Saerthe Tarto is the pick that makes the crew hit harder and survive a bad opening; Saerthe is the pick that changes what the crew can do. The choice should read as trading capability for muscle rather than picking the better block. The comparison

On a deck, Pushing is the kill. Fifteen feet and a missing section of rail ends a boarder without a second attack, and it teaches the crew to look at the terrain.

Traits

Superiority Dice (4d10).
Tarto has four superiority dice. When she hits with an attack she can spend one to add 1d10 damage and apply one maneuver — at most one maneuver per attack, so a committed round spends up to three. Maneuver save DC 16. She regains all expended dice on a short or long rest.
Indomitable (2/Day).
If Tarto fails a saving throw, she can reroll it and must use the new roll.

Actions

Multiattack.
Three attacks, Cutlass or Belaying Pin in any combination.
Cutlass.
Melee Attack Roll: +8, reach 5 ft. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) slashing.
Belaying Pin.
Melee or Ranged Attack Roll: +8, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) bludgeoning. Dark wood banded in tarnished brass, carved deep into the grain. Mundane.

Maneuvers

Trip.
Strength save or Prone.
Pushing.
Strength save or pushed up to 15 ft. away.
Disarming.
Strength save or it drops one held object of her choice.
Goading.
Wisdom save or Disadvantage attacking anyone but Tarto until the end of her next turn.
Distracting.
The next attack against the target by someone other than Tarto has Advantage.
Maneuvering.
An ally uses its Reaction to move half its Speed without provoking an opportunity attack from the target.

Bonus Actions

Clear the Deck (Recharge 5–6).
Tarto moves her full Speed without provoking opportunity attacks, making one Cutlass attack against every creature she passes. She can spend a superiority die on each of those attacks as normal. This costs her no part of her Attack action, so a recharge round is three attacks plus everything she crosses.
Commander's Strike.
An ally within 60 ft. who can hear her uses its Reaction to make one attack.
"There!"
She calls a weak point. The next ally attack against that creature before her next turn has Advantage.
"Eyes on Me!"
A creature within 30 ft. makes a DC 16 Wisdom save. On a failure it has Disadvantage attacking anyone but Tarto until the end of her next turn.

Reactions

Take It.
Trigger: an ally within 5 ft. is targeted. Response: she swaps places and becomes the target.
Parry.
Trigger: melee damage. Response: reduce it by 9 (1d10 + 4).
