Ulh'rek
Nine feet, malformed, moving like it costs him: the colony's archmage and archivist.

Ulh'rek is one of the enna, the five lieutenants of N'ghathrod. His power is the Arcane: spellcasting. With it he keeps the colony's Qualith library, its charts and its research into Atria. He is malformed, and the thing that looks like a siege engine is the scholar.

Appearance

He grew far past normal size and his spine never closed. He stands nine feet tall, malformed, and moves as though every step costs him.

Personality

Scholarly and exacting. He knows better than anyone alive how often the colony's hunt for Atria has been wrong, and he keeps the record of every miss.

Biography

Background

Ulh'rek is one of the first three malformed illithids. He was made aboard Thessalar's planar craft about five hundred years ago from one of N'ghathrod's own tadpoles, brought aboard before the experiment and cut off from his elder brain. Thessalar experimented on him too and put him into a body he supplied. The experiment with the seventh piece of the Rod of Seven Parts gave him a power of his own, the Arcane. He escaped with N'ghathrod in the craft's fall. When N'ghathrod brought the three to Glyth, Glyth saw what they were, refused them and exiled them, about four centuries ago, and N'ghathrod went with them.

He offered himself for a Hold in gratitude, because N'ghathrod chose exile with the three over his own world. A Hold is a portion of N'ghathrod, carried permanently, so that the one who carries it and he are one. To an illithid, absorption into an elder brain is the proper end of a life, the mind kept inside a greater one and service repaid with union, and almost none live to collect it. A Hold is that promise delivered early and while alive, and none of the five experiences it as subjugation. In Qualith an enna is n'ghath, one who holds, and also n'ghathu, one of the held; being both at once is what Enna Vth'ghathu, the near-held, names.

His long life comes from the taking. A portion of every taken person passes up to N'ghathrod as the tithe, and he shares the life it gives with a chosen few, the enna among them. Ulh'rek has outlived every illithid of his age by centuries.

In the campaign

The crew has not met him.

Relationships

N'ghathrod

His centre, and the one who went into exile with the three of them. He serves in gratitude.

Khaleth

The Surveyor, who takes Ulh'rek's research down into worlds.

Abilities

The Arcane is his own, from the experiment, and makes him the colony's archmage. He holds the deepest knowledge the colony has of where Atria lie and how to find them. Like every illithid aboard he spawns into the pools, and illithids grown from his tadpoles lean toward his power. N'ghathrod can speak through him at will.

How to run him

Anyone who reads him as the brute finds out quickly. Play him as a scholar first; his spells are the danger.

Killing one of the five ends that power for good: it came from the experiment, nobody can make it again, and his spawn carry only some of it. The crew will not know they have done it; they will think they killed a mind flayer, and the consequence surfaces later as something the colony can no longer do. Build him between CR 12 and 16 and lopsided: overwhelming at the Arcane and soft everywhere else, with advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened. The malformed template applies (the Vth'orama, Stat block): remove something visible on the body, add +2 AC, +30 HP and one malformation feature.
