Ulh'rek
Nine feet, malformed, moving like it costs him: the colony's archmage and archivist.

Ulh'rek is one of the enna, the five lieutenants of N'ghathrod, and carries the Hold of the Arcane: the spellcasting. With it he keeps the colony's Qualith library, its charts and its research into Atria. He is unthinned, and the thing that looks like a siege engine is the scholar.

Appearance

He grew far past normal size and his spine never closed. He stands nine feet tall, malformed, and moves as though every step costs him.

Personality

Scholarly and exacting. He knows better than anyone alive how often the colony's hunt for Atria has been wrong, and he keeps the record of every miss.

Biography

Background

Ulh'rek is unthinned: an illithid raised without an elder brain to cull and tend him. He was made aboard Thessalar's planar craft about five hundred years ago, from a body Thessalar supplied for his experiments and one of N'ghathrod's own tadpoles. The experiments with the seventh Rod piece gave him a greatly extended lifespan; he is not immortal. He escaped with N'ghathrod in the craft's fall and was exiled from Glyth with the rest of the unthinned.

He offered himself for a Hold in gratitude, because N'ghathrod gave him what bad rearing denied him. To an illithid, absorption into an elder brain is the proper end of a life, the mind kept inside a greater one and service repaid with union, and almost none live to collect it. A Hold is that promise delivered early and while alive, and none of the five experiences it as subjugation. In Qualith an enna is n'ghath, one who holds, and also n'ghathu, one of the held; being both at once is what Enna Vth'ghathu, the near-held, names.

In the campaign

The crew has not met him.

Relationships

N'ghathrod

His centre, and the one who gave the unthinned a place. He serves in gratitude.

Khaleth

The Surveyor, who takes Ulh'rek's research down into worlds.

Abilities

He carries N'ghathrod's spellcasting and is the colony's archmage. He holds the deepest knowledge the colony has of where Atria lie and how to find them. N'ghathrod can speak through him at will.

How to run him

Anyone who reads him as the brute finds out quickly. Play him as a scholar first; his spells are the danger.

Killing one of the five ends that Hold for good. N'ghathrod cannot re-form it without spending more of himself than he has left, and the colony loses the function. The crew will not know they have done it; they will think they killed a mind flayer, and the consequence surfaces later as something the colony can no longer do. Build him between CR 12 and 16 and lopsided: overwhelming at what his Hold carries and soft everywhere else, with advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened. The unthinned template applies (the Vth'orama, Stat block): remove something visible on the body, add +2 AC, +30 HP and one malformation feature.
