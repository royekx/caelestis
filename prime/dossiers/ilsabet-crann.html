Ilsabet Crann
Hadozee field historian. Warm, blunt, and trading rather than helping.

A field historian who keeps a cell of six out of a leased hold on a trade station, with a Ledger segment covering four decades of Realmspace losses and a working relationship with every archivist in the sphere who takes correspondence.

How she earned her Third On a settlement nobody had established at all. Eleven hundred people, two generations, a world's minor record, gone with the cause still open. She established that they had been there. The entry carries her name.

She is good company, pays for what she takes, and will tell the crew plainly that she is trading rather than helping. What she wants from them is where they have been and what was there.

She will decline to pretend the exchange is a friendship — and after a year of it she will have made one anyway.

That a thing existed is worth establishing even when nothing can be done about it. Her Third was earned on a record nobody had opened, for people nobody was looking for, and she regards that as the work rather than a consolation prize.

She holds that an honest trade is more durable than a favour. She says so out loud, which is why the crew will trust her long before they like her.

Little of her own. Her value is that she holds four decades of losses in one place and will exchange any of it.

What her Ledger would show if anyone asked it the right question Forty years of Realmspace losses, filed by a historian with no cosmology to defend. The Zenith pattern is in that segment already — worlds and settlements going with the cause left open — and the Order reads it as history rather than as a sequence.

A crew that brings her what they have been seeing gives her the frame she lacks. That exchange is the most consequential thing she can do, and she will experience it as a good trade.

She is the most useful contact available, and she is useful in the one way the campaign needs: she converts the crew's experience into a record, and the record back into leads.
