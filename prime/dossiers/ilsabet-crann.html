Ilsabet Crann
A hadozee field historian of the Restorers, warm and blunt, who trades with the crew and says so.

Ilsabet Crann is a hadozee field historian of the Order of All and a Restorer, working with a circle of six out of a leased hold on a trade station. Her circle keeps a Ledger copy covering four decades of Realmspace losses, and she has a working relationship with every archivist in the system who takes correspondence.

Appearance

A hadozee with ink on her fingers and a field satchel that never leaves her shoulder, weathered from years of landing on dead worlds.

Personality

Warm, blunt and good company. She pays for what she takes and says plainly that she is trading, not helping, and she will decline to pretend an exchange is a friendship; after a year of it she will have made one anyway. That a thing existed is worth establishing even when nothing can be done about it, and she holds that an honest trade is more durable than a favour. She says so out loud, which is why a crew will trust her long before they like her.

Biography

Background

She made her name on a settlement nobody had established at all: eleven hundred people, two generations, a world's minor record, gone with the cause still open. She established that they had been there, and the Ledger entry carries her name.

In the campaign

The crew has not met her.

Relationships

The Restorers

Her movement. Her circle trades Ledger copies with other Restorers by correspondence.

Abilities

Field survey, archive work and a historian's memory. She converts what people have seen into a record, and the record back into leads.

What's hidden

  • Her Ledger. Forty years of Realmspace losses, filed by a historian with no cosmology to defend. The Zenith pattern is in that copy already, worlds and settlements going with the cause left open, and the Restorers read it as history, not as a sequence.

How to run her

She is the most useful contact the crew can find, because she converts their experience into a record and the record back into leads. Run the trade honestly. She is explicit about it, so a crew that tries to get something for free is refused pleasantly and remembers it.
