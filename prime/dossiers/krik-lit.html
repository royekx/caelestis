Krik'Lit
A thri-kreen deckhand who turned out to be the one mouth aboard Ostekk's machines could understand. He leaves at the V006 swap.

Krik'Lit is a thri-kreen deckhand who signed on under Winston Ryeback to help fit the helm of the Tyrant Ship. Ostekk's clockwork horrors speak thri-kreen, and he translated the crew's parley with Ostekk-10. Krik'Lit and Pffred are relieved at the V006 swap, when the crew meets the Flighty Foundling, and they leave aboard the Foundling for Caelestis.

Appearance

A thri-kreen, four-armed and chitinous, with the pincers he fought the horrors with.

Personality

A competent stranger with a trade, a short way of speaking and no agenda. He told the crew the wail from below was their mother calling them.

How to run him

Keep him flat. He is better as a competent stranger than as a character with an arc, and the payoff is the crew realising what they had.

Threads

  • Trading two deckhands for a cadet and a teacher at the swap also trades away a translator for Ostekk's machines. After it, a conversation with them has no thri-kreen aboard, unless the crew brings Sor'Kur or finds another. The crew can argue to keep him before the roster closes.
  • The parley with Ostekk-10 is done; the behir is outside the hull, still searching.
