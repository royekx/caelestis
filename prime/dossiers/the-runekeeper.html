The Runekeeper
Holds the binding that hides a world, and fades as the fragment leaves him for two heirs.

The Runekeeper is the head of the Runekeepers, the hidden order of four clans on the Wayfinders' homeworld. The title is held by one living practitioner at a time, who holds the binding that keeps the world folded inside its heart. The man who holds it now is the seventh. He is old and fading: the Dissolution fragment is leaving him for Wan and Tu, the twins who study at Caelestis as Tumak, and his haven waits for him. He is the only permitted way into and out of the homeworld.

Appearance

The inscription is written on his skin. The fragment is visible in him: it forms and re-forms in his chest, never still, and as it leaves him the loss shows.

Personality

He holds the order's withholding as doctrine, not cruelty. Wayfinders go out with no map and no name for home, and a returning Wayfinder must recognise it through remembered fragments. That is deliberate, and he would do it again. He believes the cost of the office is his to carry, and that fading is what the office is. He knows only that the world's protection must continue.

Biography

Background

He was the Wayfinder of the generation before. He walked the havens, met the sixth Runekeeper at that Runekeeper's haven for the Becoming, and came home as the Runekeeper. The binding lives in him wherever he stands; the havens pin it, and the fragment's passing renews it.

When the twins were born he felt the fragment begin to move. While they were children he made sure the world was safe, went on pilgrimage, and built his haven on another world: the seventh, the place where he will fade. When the fragment had formed in both of them, he sent them out himself, with no map and a council's teaching behind them. Every leaving passes through him.

A Runekeeper's death is celebrated, not mourned. “We do not mourn this. We sing.”

In the campaign

Voyage 4. As Tumak's first soul-knife throw went wide, a quiet voice said that all things find their way home, and the blade bent back to strike true. Tumak felt a tether running toward home, a connection and not a place.

Relationships

Wan and Tu

His heirs, whom he sent out himself; they may have met him once before, as small children, at a formality they do not remember. He watches over them and nudges rarely, at key moments. When they reach his haven he will know, and he will meet them there.

The Runekeepers

The order he leads. It is competent, and its doctrine is sound.

Abilities

He holds the binding that hides a world, in himself and not in a place. He is a complete Wayfinder, able to attune to a celestial body by ritual and time; he can Wayfind to his haven at will; and his voice can reach a Wayfinder of his order across the cosmos.

What's hidden

  • The fragment. The concealment rests on an object nobody in the order has identified as residue.
  • Two heirs. The fragment has always settled in one heir. It leaves him at one pace and settles in two people, half in each, and a few runes into their journey he feels it going faster than any generation before. The binding slips with it; left too long, the world unhides mid-journey.
  • The Becoming. When the twins reach his haven, he meets them there in person and they take the exchange. The Heart wakes in Tumak, the twins go home as the Runekeeper, and he stays and spends his last few days passing into the havens, which completes the binding.
  • Homecoming. The fragment arrives home as two halves, never before seen, and that change is the world's second Zenith. He is alive, in his last days, while it happens.
  • Tumak's Heart-rune. Tumak carries his own Heart-rune, already matched, and it wakes at the Becoming.

How to run him

He is someone doing everything right with an incomplete cosmology. The order is competent, the doctrine is sound, and the thing they stand on is a piece of the Reach world that none of them can name. He spoke to Tumak at a dice-driven moment; the tether is a connection, not a location, and that is the line to hold. Speak through him rarely, at key moments, so his voice does not become a guide. Keep Tumak's Heart-rune from the table until the Becoming.
