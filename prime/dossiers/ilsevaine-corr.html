Ilsevaine Corr
The ruler of a hidden polity outside Realmspace, who has seen something large brush the edge of her sight and keeps watch for it.

Ilsevaine Corr rules a hidden polity outside Realmspace, at the edge of a system or between systems, and Caelestis does not know it exists. She has noticed more traffic than usual near her borders, and once something large brushed the edge of her sight and was gone. She keeps watch for it.

Personality

Watchful and patient, the ruler of a realm that stays unseen. She keeps watch on her borders herself.

Biography

In the campaign

The crew has not met her or heard her name.

How to run her

The touchstone is Mara Sov, the Awoken Queen of Destiny: a sovereign of a hidden people, watching the dark at her borders, playing a long game nobody else can see.

Her arc

Once N'ghathrod grows bolder, she will face Vth'oramu. Together with Aurel Venn, the rise in Monoliths and the Watchers' calls for help, she is one of the strains on the Fleet.
