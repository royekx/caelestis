Clint Dashforest
Left the Bellwether Crow with the device Vocath's crew came for, dove through a color pool on purpose, and came out alive on Fonains.

Clint Dashforest was Caelestis crew aboard the Bellwether Crow. When Vocath's crew ambushed the Crow for A.T.L.A.S.'s sister, he took the device and left the ship to draw the boarders off, and went through a color pool on purpose. The Fleet carries him as gone overboard. He is alive on Fonains, working as a bounty hunter out of Orerius, in T'Arionese, where the Cre'Tez estate stands.

Appearance

He keeps his head down and his hat brim low. A long scar runs across one eye, and his ring finger is missing. He carries a heavy, double-barrelled pistol that thrums when it is primed.

Personality

Quiet to the point of discourtesy, with a stare that goes past whoever is talking. He speaks when a job needs a decision and stays out of it otherwise, and he is the first in any room to notice it going quiet. He took to the frontier at once. He needs money, and he has said it is for information he has to buy; he has said no more than that. About the finger he says only: “Not going to talk about that yet.”

Biography

Background

About a year ago the Bellwether Crow went out from Caelestis to learn why Cairn Station had stopped answering, carrying A.T.L.A.S.'s sister, with Clint and Joffrey aboard. Vocath's crew ambushed it short of Cairn for the device. Clint took it and left the ship to draw the boarders off. He steered for a color pool he could see opening and went through it on purpose. Color pools open and close quickly, and this one carried him through a small pocket of another dimension and spat him out on Fonains, separated from the boarders who followed him in. The Crow's survivors still argue about whether he meant to go through. He did.

In the Fleet, “went overboard” means more than falling from a ship. A crew member who leaves the ship to fight for it and does not come back has gone overboard; it is a softer way of saying killed in action. The Fleet Intelligence Archive carries him as unrecovered.

He landed on Fonains about a year before the present, before its Monolith appeared, and he was there when it did. He brought a laser pistol onto a world of black powder. Two of his jobs there are on record.

  • The Blooming Glass. A bounty posted out of Orerius, where the Cre'Tez estate stands, by Finerva, who is Vanifer under another name, for the Cre'Tez, “for the eldest brother”: four archaeologists, missing. Clint joined an ad hoc crew of Bosk, Jasper Whitmore, Glarg, Valaire and Blazkagne. The Blooming Glass stands in the Wandering Sands at the end of the Shattered Path, which looks like stars lying on the ground: a petal-layered construct of Rubicon, a gem close to diamond, throwing prismatic light, with a research facility buried beneath it built to study the Pedra Ardente. A fire elemental had killed most of the research troop. The elemental was a woman, Chloe Chespetes, with the Pedra Ardente fused into her hand. About three and a half centuries earlier a sect of her own clan had tried to use the gem to open a portal and release Imix; her clan and the Cre'Tez fought them, and Chloe flooded the laboratory with lava to stop them. She was infused with the Plane of Fire and trapped in the elemental until Clint's crew freed her. The crew rescued the archaeologists and handed the Pedra Ardente to Finerva, and the job fed the Cre'Tez line's fire-key plot without anyone aboard knowing it.
  • The Twin Tirades. Marshal Elias Lang, a half-orc stretched across more territory than one man can hold, wanted two half-elf sisters dead or alive at a thousand gold a head. They led a gang called the Tumbleweeds out of a casino across the creek from Rose Creek, wanted for homicide, treason, grave robbing, interfering with corpses and necromancy. Clint fell in with a second crew, the Six Shooters, who kept the name despite having seven members. They rode an arcane train in, finished it in a standoff in the street, and collected.
In the campaign

The crew has not met him. Boatswain Tarto and Saerthe Abizjn have worn a matched pair of bands of the lost and found since he went overboard.

Relationships

Boatswain Tarto and Saerthe Abizjn

His instructors and friends. Both feel they could have done something. Saerthe was not aboard the Crow, and carries it: “If I'd been there I could've saved him.”

Joffrey

His shipmate on the Crow, who brought the survivors home and was decorated for it.

The Six Shooters

The crew he rides with out of Orerius.

Abilities

A seasoned spacefarer and a good shot. His pistol is heavy, revolver-shaped and double-barrelled, with a switch between a precise shot and a spread. It does the work of a hunting rifle in a world of black powder, and nobody on Fonains had seen one fired.

Notable items

  • His laser pistol. The one piece of Caelestis technology known on Fonains.
  • A.T.L.A.S.'s sister. The Distinction housing he carried through the pool. It is somewhere on Fonains.

What's hidden

  • He is alive. Nobody on Caelestis has reason to suspect it, and the standing order closing the edge run means nobody will go and check.
  • A witness. He saw the first boarding and what the boarders came for. He is the one person outside Vocath's crew who knows the device was the target.
  • The salvage chain. Vocath's people chased him to Fonains and never found him. They came back with fragments of the broken Monolith through Vocath's line, as ordinary cargo, and one of those fragments ended up inside Boogie.

How to run him

His present belongs to Fonains: he was mined from the Fonains one-shots 3 and 4, and their own hooks stay theirs. In Caelestis he is a figure in the record, and his loss shows through the people who miss him. What the crew can find is the engagement record, the salvage chain through Vocath's line, and the bands Tarto and Saerthe wear. The salvage chain is the one that leads back to Boogie's chest.

Kwalish, a gnome planar specialist with a gate and a planar device, is within his reach on Fonains. Kwalish sent nobody back in time; the Accordance did that. If a crossing back is ever wanted, the Accordance is the place to look.

Threads

  • Someone from Vocath's crew followed him to Fonains and learned there about the gems, and Vocath trades in them now (A.T.L.A.S.'s sister).
  • A man buying information on a frontier world, who lost a finger somewhere he will not discuss, may suspect his crossing had a cause. If what he is buying is a way back, Vocath's line is what he is paying to learn about.
