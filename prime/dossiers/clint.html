Took the boarders' craft and broke away. Alive, off the board, and being played elsewhere.

A Caelestis crewmate aboard the Bellwether Crow. When the first boarding came, he seized the boarders' own craft and broke away with it, pulling the pursuit off the Crow and buying the vessel its only clear minutes of the engagement.

SA-005 carries him as unrecovered. The Fleet's position is that he died out there.

Nobody planned what he did Vocath's people came to take something specific and were taking it. Clint stealing their craft is the part nobody planned, and it is the only reason a second action happened at all — the action in which Joffrey was taken.

A man the Fleet has written off as unrecovered changed the shape of that night more than anyone who came home from it.

A psychic wind carried him out of the system entirely. He reached W-21: Fonains — where he brought laser pistols onto a world that had none. Crossing open

What he has done on Fonains

Two jobs are on record. He landed in a frontier country of marshals, bounty paper and hard sun, and he took to it immediately — which is the part worth noticing. A man who was crew on a Fleet vessel is now freelance, armed, and buying information.

JobWhat happened
The Blooming Glass. A bounty out of Osiris, placed by a broker named Fenerba for the Cortez family: four archaeologists, missing.He joined an ad hoc crew — Bosk, Jasper Whitmore, Glarg, Valaire, Blazkagne — and reached the site by the Shattered Path, which is described as stars lying on the ground. A fire elemental had killed most of the research troop. The elemental was a woman, Mary Chispitez, fused to an ancient crystal called the Pedra Ardente, which is what the archaeologists had gone down for. They separated her from it and carried her back to Osiris for payment.
The Twin Tirades. Marshal Elias Lang, a half-orc stretched across more territory than one man can hold, wanted two half-elf sisters dead or alive at a thousand gold a head.Clint stayed in Osiris after the first job and fell in with a second crew, which took the name the Six Shooters despite having seven in it. The sisters led a gang called the Tumbleweeds out of a casino across the creek from Rose Creek; the paper listed homicide, treason, grave robbing, interfering with corpses and necromancy. The Shooters rode an arcane train in, finished it in a standoff in the street, and collected.
The pistol reads as a wonder Heavy-revolver shape, double barrel, a switch between a precise shot and a spread, and it thrums when it is primed. Nobody local had seen it fired. It does the work of a hunting rifle in a world of black powder, and it is the only piece of Caelestis that made the crossing.
Qalish, and what he is not The gnome planar specialist turns up in both halves of the Fonains record, and he is at the Hall of Endless Knowledge when Clint's first crew disbands. He runs a gate and a planar device, and he is working on the Monolith problem with them.

He did not send anyone back in time. The gods of the Accordance did that, for their own reasons. So Qalish is a local expert within reach of Clint rather than a route home, and the Accordance is the thing to look at if a crossing is ever wanted.

Quiet to the point of discourtesy. Head down, hat brim low, a stare that goes past whoever is talking. He speaks when a job needs a decision and stays out of it otherwise, and he is the first in any room to notice it going quiet.

The finger and the information are the same question A man buying information on a frontier world, who lost a finger somewhere he will not discuss, is a man who suspects he was taken off the board on purpose rather than by weather. He has not said so. He has said he needs the money.

If what he is buying is a way back to how he got there, the psychic wind stops being weather, and Vocath's line — which sold his salvage on as ordinary cargo — is what he is paying to learn about. Open

He is alive and the Fleet is wrong Unaccounted for rather than dead, and nobody on Caelestis has any reason to suspect it. The closure of the Tetherbreak guarantees nobody will go and check.

He is also a living witness to the first boarding — the only person outside the colony who saw what the boarders came for, and who was not allowed to live so that there would be a story.

His present is being played elsewhere Clint's current life runs in the one-shots, not in this campaign's timeline. This page covers his past, which is the part Caelestis can reach: what he did at the engagement, where he ended up, and what came home because of him.

That split is the useful one. The crew can investigate him as history and find a trail, while whatever he is doing now stays unconstrained by what they discover.

What the one-shots actually gave us

Two full records, and they are thinner on him than on the jobs, because his player had to leave most sessions early. The yield is a man and a method rather than an arc — which is the right shape for a figure the Fleet carries as unrecovered.
