Clint Dashforest
Led a few of Bellwether Crow's hands aboard a pirate ship and took her helm, drawing half the fight away so the captives could be recovered; a color pool opened by chance and took her, with him aboard, to Fonains.

Clint Dashforest led the crew of Bellwether Crow when the Fleet sent it to look in on Cairn Station. Near Cairn Crow came on two pirate ships handing captives to a third vessel, and chose to intervene. Clint led a few of his crew hands aboard one pirate ship, and together they took her helm and steered her away, pulling half the threat off Crow. A color pool opened by chance and took that ship, with him, his party and its pirates aboard. His diversion made the rescue possible. The Fleet carries him as gone overboard and honors him as a hero. He is alive on Fonains, working as a bounty hunter out of Orerius, in T'Arionese, where the Cre'Tez estate stands. He was Mirt's friend.

Appearance

He keeps his head down and his hat brim low. A long scar runs across one eye, and his ring finger is missing. He carries a heavy, double-barrelled pistol that thrums when it is primed.

Personality

Quiet to the point of discourtesy, with a stare that goes past whoever is talking. He speaks when a job needs a decision and stays out of it otherwise, and he is the first in any room to notice it going quiet. He took to the frontier at once. He needs money, and he has said it is for information he has to buy; he has said no more than that. About the finger he says only: “Not going to talk about that yet.”

Biography

Background

About a year ago the Fleet sent Bellwether Crow to look in on Cairn Station, with Clint leading its crew and Joffrey aboard as its junior spelljammer. Near Cairn, Crow came on a transfer of people under guard from two pirate ships to a third vessel. The two pirate ships were of comparable size. One was captained by a pirate the captives heard called Saltjaw; her ship carried no name on her hull. Neither ship's name is recorded. Crow's crew saw what was happening and chose to intervene, and the pirates ran up their colors, a crossbones, and fought. Clint led a few of Crow's crew hands aboard the other ship, and together they took her helm and steered her away, pulling half the threat off Crow. A few of Crow's crew hands went with him. Crow's crew saw the party go aboard, and saw a figure on that deck wearing a large coin on a chain, a seal struck on its face. A color pool opened by chance and took that ship, with Clint, his party, the figure and its pirates aboard. With the figure gone and half the pirates with him, Saltjaw had little left to fight for. Joffrey took charge on Crow's side, led the recovery of the captives and got the rest of the crew out, and Crow drove Saltjaw's ship off. She got away, and the Fleet hunts her ship. Many of Crow's crew had died. The Academy honors Clint as a hero: his diversion made the rescue possible.

In the Fleet, “went overboard” means more than falling from a ship. A crew member who leaves the ship, willingly or not, and does not come back has gone overboard; it is a softer way of saying killed in action. The Fleet's archive carries him as lost to a color pool.

The pool took the pirates' ship about a year before the present and set her down on Fonains while its Monolith stood, about three months before it broke. Time runs differently inside a color pool; for those aboard the crossing was short, and months had passed outside. Clint got away from the pirates, and they hunted him for a while. He lost his communication piece and has had no way to send word to Caelestis. He brought a laser pistol onto a world of black powder: the first there, though laser pistols are common in wildspace. Two of his jobs there are on record.

  • The Blooming Glass. A bounty posted out of Orerius, where the Cre'Tez estate stands, by Finerva, who is Vanifer under another name, for the Cre'Tez, “for the eldest brother”: four archaeologists, missing. Clint joined an ad hoc crew of Bosk, Jasper Whitmore, Glarg, Valaire and Blazkagne. The Blooming Glass stands in the Wandering Sands at the end of the Shattered Path, which looks like stars lying on the ground: a petal-layered construct of Rubicon, a gem close to diamond, throwing prismatic light, with a research facility buried beneath it built to study the Pedra Ardente. A fire elemental had killed most of the research troop. The elemental was a woman, Chloe Chespetes, with the Pedra Ardente fused into her hand. About three and a half centuries earlier a sect of her own clan had tried to use the gem to open a portal and release Imix; her clan and the Cre'Tez fought them, and Chloe flooded the laboratory with lava to stop them. She was infused with the Plane of Fire and trapped in the elemental until Clint's crew freed her. The crew rescued the archaeologists and handed the Pedra Ardente to Finerva, and the job fed the Cre'Tez line's fire-key plot without anyone aboard knowing it.
  • The Twin Tirades. Marshal Elias Lang, a half-orc stretched across more territory than one man can hold, wanted two half-elf sisters dead or alive at a thousand gold a head. They led a gang called the Tumbleweeds out of a casino across the creek from Rose Creek, wanted for homicide, treason, grave robbing, interfering with corpses and necromancy. Clint fell in with a second crew, the Six Shooters, who kept the name despite having seven members. They rode an arcane train in, finished it in a standoff in the street, and collected.
In the campaign

The crew has not met him. Boatswain Tarto and Saerthe Abizjn have worn a matched pair of bands of the lost and found since he went overboard.

The crew he rides with out of Orerius.

Abilities

A seasoned spacefarer and a good shot. His pistol is heavy, revolver-shaped and double-barrelled, with a switch between a precise shot and a spread. It does the work of a hunting rifle in a world of black powder, and nobody on Fonains had seen one fired before he came.

What's hidden

  • He is alive. Nobody on Caelestis knows it. He lost his communication piece on Fonains and cannot send word.
  • A witness. The pirates were in Vocath's hire. They run his captive line, and the third hull in the handover was a courier of the Vth'orama colony. Vocath's overseer, one of his own crew and the one paying them, rode the other ship and had put on Vocath's personal seal, a large coin on a chain with the seal struck on its face, for the handover, so the pirates knew whom he spoke for; the same seal is on the crates aboard. Clint knows the seal on sight, as Mirt's trusted circle does. He boarded the overseer's ship and saw the seal on him, so he knows who hired the pirates and who stood over them. He was gone before Crow reached Cairn, and he knows nothing of the colony, of Cairn or of Joffrey's taking there.
  • The pirates on Fonains. The pool took the ship with everything aboard: Clint's party, the pirates, Vocath's overseer and the crates under Vocath's seal. Every one of the crew hands who went with Clint died in the taking of the ship and its fall; Clint is the only one of Crow's people who reached Fonains alive. Because Vocath's sealed cargo had gone through the pool with the ship, the overseer called Vocath by sending stone as soon as the pool set them down. The overseer and the pirates with him hunted Clint for a while, so that he could not report what he saw on the lane, and the overseer told Vocath that Clint died in the hunt. Vocath believes his witness is dead. Vocath's trade on Fonains began after the Monolith broke, and his people carry its gems and shards home. Boogie's shard came through that line, in a crate carrying Vocath's personal seal (Fonains shard).

How to run him

His present belongs to Fonains: he was mined from the Fonains one-shots 3 and 4, and their own hooks stay theirs. In Caelestis he is a figure in the record, and his loss shows through the people who miss him. What the crew can find is Crow's report in the Fleet Intelligence Archive, Vocath's line on Fonains, and the bands Tarto and Saerthe wear.

Kwalish, a gnome planar specialist with a gate and a planar device, is within his reach on Fonains. Kwalish sent nobody back in time; the Accordance did that. If a crossing back is ever wanted, the Accordance is the place to look.
