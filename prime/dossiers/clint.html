Took the boarders' craft and broke away. Alive, off the board, and being played elsewhere.

A Caelestis crewmate aboard the Bellwether Crow. When the first boarding came, he seized the boarders' own craft and broke away with it, pulling the pursuit off the Crow and buying the vessel its only clear minutes of the engagement.

SA-005 carries him as unrecovered. The Fleet's position is that he died out there.

Nobody planned what he did Vocath's people came to take something specific and were taking it. Clint stealing their craft is the part nobody planned, and it is the only reason a second action happened at all — the action in which Joffrey was taken.

So the single most consequential thing in the campaign's recent history was an improvisation by a crewman the record lists as lost.

A psychic wind carried him out of the system entirely. He reached W-21: Fonains — where he brought laser pistols onto a world that had none. How he crossed is undecided.

What his rescue attempt dragged home That salvage is how a fragment of the Fonains Monolith ended up inside Boogie. The only destroyed Monolith in the cosmos has a piece in a PC's chest because somebody went looking for a man the Fleet had written off.
He is alive and the Fleet is wrong Unaccounted for rather than dead, and nobody on Caelestis has any reason to suspect it. The closure of the Tetherbreak guarantees nobody will go and check.

He is also a living witness to the first boarding — the only person outside the colony who saw what the boarders came for, and who was not allowed to live so that there would be a story.

His present is being played elsewhere Clint's current life runs in the one-shots, not in this campaign's timeline. This page covers his past, which is the part Caelestis can reach: what he did at the engagement, where he ended up, and what came home because of him.

That split is the useful one. The crew can investigate him as history and find a trail, while whatever he is doing now stays unconstrained by what they discover.

What the crew can find: the engagement record, the salvage chain through Vocath's line, and the fact that a rescue crew went out. Each of those is a thread into something larger — and the salvage chain is the one that leads back to Boogie's chest.

Open: how he crossed to Fonains, and whether a meeting is ever on the table or he stays a figure in the record.
