Mirt
Headmaster. The school's institutional shield, and the reason the Tetherbreak is closed.

Headmaster of Caelestis Academy, long in the position. He has accumulated enemies across a career — commercial, political and personal — and his standing as headmaster is the institutional protection that keeps several of them at arm's length. Currently in active commercial conflict with the mercane Vocath.

He keeps an extradimensional wardrobe in his personal quarters whose interior corresponds to a stellar location in Virenspace, and has disclosed nothing about where it came from.

What he does not know he is holding

The wardrobe is one of Zerathis's tethers — a paracausal replica of his anchor site, carrying a thread of the Viren shift across space. It is why Casey was drawn to it and why Polly could reach him there. Mirt has kept a piece of Astra's network in his bedroom for years.

The Academy comes before the feud, and the feud is his to settle. Both at once, which is what makes him useful and what makes him dangerous to the crew's investigation.

He also holds that a filing has to survive being read by someone looking for a motive. Years of being a man with enemies have taught him that certainty and usable evidence are different objects, and he will not confuse them even when it costs lives.

He protects cadets as a matter of course, and he is willing to be wrong in public to do it.

What's hidden

He knows whose mark it is

He holds Above Bridge clearance, so he has read WM-002 and FR-016. He recognises the eye in the wheel on sight. It is Vocath's house mark, and he has spent years on the other side of a commercial war with the man who uses it.

He knows an eye-in-a-wheel craft mauled a Fleet relief vessel on the Cairn approach. He knows the same mark is on half the incidents at his own station. He knows exactly whose it is.

What he has is certainty the Bridge cannot act on. FR-016's cross-reference returned no match, because a mercane's private house mark was never in the registry. He knows the mark the way a rival knows a rival — personally, which is to say it is worth nothing in a filing.

Naming Vocath in the record would read as a headmaster using Fleet intelligence to prosecute his own feud, and it would put the Academy's protection of him in play the moment someone decided to look at it that way.

So he backed the closure instead

Keeping Fleet vessels off that bearing stops anyone else dying out there while he handles Vocath by means that need no proof. He holds the position that the sector is lethal and unexplained, and he holds it knowing it is neither.

Threads

The cost he accepts: nobody finds out what is actually at Cairn, and nobody looks twice at who came home from it. Which is how Joffrey walked onto his station unexamined — the one consequence Mirt would never have accepted if he had seen it.

How to run them

He is the campaign's best demonstration that an institution can be wrong on purpose for defensible reasons. He is lying about the Tetherbreak and he is not a villain, and a crew that works that out has to decide what to do with a headmaster who protects them.
