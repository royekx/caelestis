Mirt
The Academy's headmaster, an old Astral Sea adventurer whom Caelestis protects, who lost his friend Clint and knows more about Vocath than he can prove.

Mirt is the headmaster of the Academy at Caelestis, long in the post. He runs the Academy, and only the Academy: the Caelestis HQ, the Corps and the Bridge operate outside him. He is a well-known adventurer who knows the Astral Sea, and he has made enemies over the years. Caelestis protects him, so he is also stuck there. He is in active commercial conflict with the mercane Vocath, an old enemy with a grudge.

Appearance

A big, bearded old adventurer gone comfortably to seed, more often in a dressing gown than a uniform. His chambers are cluttered with curiosities, and a fishbowl holds two octopi, Flotsam and Jetsam.

Personality

He is no fool, and the bumbling is mostly a manner. He keeps a code: the Academy and its cadets come first, and his feud with Vocath is his own. Years of being a man with enemies have taught him that certainty and usable evidence are different things, and that a headmaster's accusation has to survive being read by someone looking for a motive. He protects cadets as a matter of course, and when they bring him something, he takes it seriously.

Biography

Background

Mirt made his name as an adventurer in the Astral Sea and made enemies doing it, commercial, political and personal. His standing at Caelestis keeps several of them at arm's length. About a year ago he lost his friend Clint Dashforest in the raid on Bellwether Crow. He keeps a wardrobe in his quarters whose interior is a magical replica of a stellar feature in Virenspace, a gift from the spellcaster Zerathis.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 1. The crew found him half-buried in a hangover in his cluttered chambers. He praised Sol's instinct to take the creature alive as evidence, rewarded the cadets with gold, and told Casey the wardrobe replicates a place beyond H'Catha in the Viren system, a gift from Zerathis.
  • Voyage 3. He summoned the six cadets to the bridge. When Saerthe named the rod's marking, he went still and told them Vocath had long borne him a grudge, making anyone connected to him a target. When Tumak named Joffrey as the one who paid a bribe at the Sky Dock, he told them to look into Joffrey carefully, given his standing, and named a man called Jeffrey with him. He told them to keep it quiet and check the teaching rooms for similar devices. He paid them for the simulation win and their discretion, granted them sailor-level clearance, and left the phrase “fleet intelligence archive” visible on his notepad beside the fishbowl.

What's hidden

  • He knows the seal. Vocath signs his darker dealings with a personal seal, a balance scale with coins on one pan and a crate on the other, registered nowhere. Mirt knows it on sight, as do the rest of his trusted circle: Saerthe, Tarto, Mr. Blip, and Clint before he was lost. Vocath put it on the rod on purpose, a message to Mirt that he can reach him.
  • He knows Vocath is tied to Clint's loss. He holds Above Bridge clearance and has read WM-002, Crow's report of the raid, and FR-016, the seal the survivors saw on a brass medallion worn by a figure aboard the smaller pirate ship, drawn from their descriptions. WM-002 records the main pirate ship's name, Saltjaw, and her hull mark; the Fleet hunts her. FR-016's drawing is Vocath's seal, and Mirt knew it on sight; nobody had to tell him whose it is. He knows Vocath is connected to the raid, but not how far: hired hands, his own line, or why there. In truth the raiders were pirates in Vocath's hire, and the figure was Vocath's overseer, one of his own crew and the one paying them. The seal is registered nowhere, so he cannot prove it. He says nothing of either file and works through the cadets.
  • Why he keeps quiet. Vocath is his personal grudge. If that grudge were seen to cost the Fleet, Caelestis would turn him out, and he has too many enemies to live long without its protection.
  • What he does instead. He works quietly to learn what Vocath is actually doing. If he knew how to reach Vocath without risking his place or his students, he would act.
  • The wardrobe. It is one of Zerathis's tethers, a paracausal replica of his anchor site carrying a thread of the Viren shift across space. That is why Casey was drawn to it, and why Astra Virex, speaking in Polly's voice, could reach him there. Mirt has kept a piece of Astra Virex's reach in his bedroom for years.
  • Sol. He, Tarto and Saerthe know Sol's background and keep it to themselves.

How to run him

He shows that a good man can keep a secret for defensible reasons. He knows whose seal is on the rod and that the same hand reached into the raid that took his friend, and he cannot say either without making his grudge the Fleet's. Play the bumbling as cover, and let the crew catch him being sharp.

His arc

Vocath's grudge campaign aims to disgrace him and pry him loose from Caelestis's protection. A breach of the Brig at graduation, on his watch, is the blow that could do it; how hard it lands depends on the Fork, on Opposition: the Vth'orama.

Threads

  • The H'Catha report came down from Watcher to Hall to the Caelestis Corps to Academy, and Mirt made it a cadet mission.
  • The crate the crew took to Mirt, with the neogi, is a different crate from the sealed one at the Sky Dock. Nobody has checked it for a mark, and Vocath's seal is on it to find.
  • FR-016's drawing, read with WM-002, puts Vocath's seal aboard the smaller pirate ship; the ship WM-002 names leads only to hired pirates. Mirt has already read the two together; the crew can once they reach the archive.
