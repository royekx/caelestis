Mirt
The Academy's headmaster, an old Astral Sea adventurer whom Caelestis protects and keeps quiet, and who knows more about Vocath than he can file.

Mirt is the headmaster of the Academy at Caelestis, long in the post. He runs the Academy, and only the Academy: the Caelestis Corps and the Bridge operate outside him, and the Academy is the small part of the station, roughly a fifth of it. He is a well-known adventurer who knows the Astral Sea, and he has drawn ire over the years. Caelestis protects him and keeps him quiet, so he is also stuck there. He is in active commercial conflict with the mercane Vocath.

Appearance

A big, bearded old adventurer gone comfortably to seed, more often in a dressing gown than a uniform. His chambers are cluttered with curiosities, and a fishbowl holds two octopi, Flotsam and Jetsam.

Personality

The Academy comes before the feud, and the feud is his to settle; both at once is what makes him useful and what makes him dangerous to an investigation. A filing, to him, has to survive being read by someone looking for a motive. Years of being a man with enemies have taught him that certainty and usable evidence are different things, and he will not confuse them even when it costs lives. He protects cadets as a matter of course and is willing to be wrong in public to do it.

Biography

Background

Mirt made his name as an adventurer in the Astral Sea and made enemies doing it, commercial, political and personal. His standing at Caelestis keeps several of them at arm's length. He keeps a wardrobe in his quarters whose interior is a magical replica of a stellar feature in Virenspace, a gift from the spellcaster Zerathis.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 1. The crew found him half-buried in a hangover in his cluttered chambers. He praised Sol's instinct to take the creature alive as evidence, rewarded the cadets with gold, and told Casey the wardrobe replicates a place beyond H'Catha in the Viren system, a gift from Zerathis.
  • Voyage 3. He summoned the six cadets to the bridge. When Saerthe named the rod's marking, he went still and told them Vocath had long borne him a grudge, making anyone connected to him a target. He told them to keep it quiet, check the teaching rooms for similar devices, and look cautiously into Joffrey and a man named Jeffrey. He paid them for the simulation win and their discretion, granted them sailor-level clearance, and left the phrase “fleet intelligence archive” visible on his notepad beside the fishbowl.

What's hidden

  • He knows whose mark it is. He holds Above Bridge clearance in the Fleet Intelligence Archive, so he has read WM-002 and FR-016. He recognises the eye in a wheel on sight: it is Vocath's house mark. He knows an eye-in-a-wheel craft mauled a Fleet relief vessel on the Cairn approach, and that the same mark is on half the incidents at his own station. FR-016's cross-reference returned no match, because a mercane's private house mark was never in the registry, so his certainty is worthless in a filing. Naming Vocath in the record would read as a headmaster using Fleet intelligence to prosecute his own feud.
  • So he backed the closure. Keeping Fleet vessels off the edge run stops anyone else dying out there while he handles Vocath by means that need no proof. He holds that the run is lethal and unexplained, knowing it is neither.
  • The wardrobe. It is one of Zerathis's tethers, a paracausal replica of his anchor site carrying a thread of the Viren shift across space. That is why Casey was drawn to it, and why Astra Virex, speaking in Polly's voice, could reach him there. Mirt has kept a piece of Astra Virex's reach in his bedroom for years.
  • Sol. He, Tarto and Saerthe know Sol's background and keep it to themselves.

How to run him

He shows that an institution can be wrong on purpose for defensible reasons. He is lying about the edge run and he is not a villain, and a crew that works that out has to decide what to do with a headmaster who protects them.

His arc

The cost he accepts is that nobody finds out what is at Cairn and nobody looks twice at who came home from it. That is how Joffrey walked onto his station unexamined, the one consequence Mirt would never have accepted if he had seen it. Vocath's grudge campaign aims to disgrace him and pry him loose from Caelestis's protection.
