Khaleth
The enna who runs the hunt for Atria and goes down into a world's interior himself.

Khaleth is one of the enna, the five lieutenants of N'ghathrod, and carries the Hold of the Surveyor: the hunt for Atria. He is properly raised, a bearer, and of the five he is the one who regularly goes down into a world's interior, leading the descents himself.

Appearance

A properly raised mind flayer, violet-grey, with a crown of face tentacles and pale eyes, dressed for descents and not for ceremony.

Personality

Decades of being wrong about where the Atria are have made him blunt and unsentimental about the work. Of the five he is the most likely to leave a fight instead of finishing it.

Biography

Background

Khaleth is a properly raised illithid, a bearer, and he was captured with N'ghathrod when Thessalar's planar craft took his ship about five hundred years ago. Thessalar's experiments with the seventh Rod piece changed him with N'ghathrod and gave him a greatly extended lifespan; he is not immortal. He escaped with N'ghathrod in the craft's fall, went with him to Glyth, and chose exile with the unthinned when Glyth turned them out, though as a bearer he could have stayed.

He offered himself for a Hold in loyalty. To an illithid, absorption into an elder brain is the proper end of a life, the mind kept inside a greater one and service repaid with union, and almost none live to collect it. A Hold is that promise delivered early and while alive, and none of the five experiences it as subjugation. In Qualith an enna is n'ghath, one who holds, and also n'ghathu, one of the held; being both at once is what Enna Vth'ghathu, the near-held, names.

Abilities

He finds the way into a world's interior and leads the colony's descents to its heart. N'ghathrod can speak through him at will. As a bearer he can still lay into the pools.

How to run him

He is the enna the crew is most likely to meet in the field, underground and near a heart. He fights to keep the descent going and withdraws when it is lost.

Killing one of the five ends that Hold for good. N'ghathrod cannot re-form it without spending more of himself than he has left, and the colony loses the function. The crew will not know they have done it; they will think they killed a mind flayer, and the consequence surfaces later as something the colony can no longer do. Build him between CR 12 and 16 and lopsided: overwhelming at what his Hold carries and soft everywhere else, with advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened.
