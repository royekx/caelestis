Khaleth
The enna who runs the hunt for Atria and goes down into a world's interior himself.

Khaleth is one of the enna, the five lieutenants of N'ghathrod. His power is the Surveyor: he finds the way into a world's interior, and he runs the hunt for Atria. He is whole, one of the colony's illithids who call themselves the true-formed, and of the five he is the one who regularly goes down into a world, leading the descents himself.

Personality

Decades of being wrong about where the Atria are have made him blunt and unsentimental about the work. Of the five he is the most likely to leave a fight instead of finishing it.

Biography

Background

Khaleth is a whole illithid, and he was captured with N'ghathrod when Thessalar's planar craft took their ship about five hundred years ago. The experiment with the seventh piece of the Rod of Seven Parts changed him with N'ghathrod and gave him a power of his own, the Surveyor. He escaped with N'ghathrod in the craft's fall and went with him to Glyth. When Glyth refused the three malformed illithids made aboard the craft and exiled them, about four centuries ago, N'ghathrod went with them, and Khaleth went too.

He offered himself for a Hold in loyalty. A Hold is a portion of N'ghathrod, carried permanently, so that the one who carries it and he are one. To an illithid, absorption into an elder brain is the proper end of a life, the mind kept inside a greater one and service repaid with union, and almost none live to collect it. A Hold is that promise delivered early and while alive, and none of the five experiences it as subjugation. In Qualith an enna is n'ghath, one who holds, and also n'ghathu, one of the held; being both at once is what Enna Vth'ghathu, the near-held, names.

His long life comes from the taking. A portion of every taken person passes up to N'ghathrod as the tithe, and he shares the life it gives with a chosen few, the enna among them. Khaleth has outlived every illithid of his age by centuries.

Abilities

The Surveyor is his own, from the experiment. He finds the way into a world's interior and leads the colony's descents to its heart. Like every illithid aboard he spawns into the pools, and illithids grown from his tadpoles lean toward his power. N'ghathrod can speak through him at will.

How to run him

He is the enna the crew is most likely to meet in the field, underground and near a heart. He fights to keep the descent going and withdraws when it is lost.

Killing one of the five ends that power for good: it came from the experiment, nobody can make it again, and his spawn carry only some of it. The crew will not know they have done it; they will think they killed a mind flayer, and the consequence surfaces later as something the colony can no longer do. Build him between CR 12 and 16 and lopsided: overwhelming at the Surveyor and soft everywhere else, with advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened.

Threads

  • Reaching one of the five means reaching N'ghathrod in the only way that costs him anything permanent.
  • A collapsed descent is one of the defeats that actually beats N'ghathrod, and Khaleth is usually leading it.
  • He is one of the true-formed. They keep Glyth's ideal in a colony where most illithids are malformed, three of the enna among them, and N'ghathrod does not accept the name. The colony has never resolved that, and the true-formed are where it is loudest.
