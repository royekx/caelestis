Khaleth
The Surveyor · Vth'merru

Properly raised. The only one of the five who regularly goes down into a world's interior. He runs the hunt for Atria and leads the descents himself.

A century of being wrong about where the Atria are has made him blunt and unsentimental about the work.

Tell He is the one the crew is most likely to meet at a live site, and the one most likely to leave rather than finish a fight.
What an enna is One of five illithids taken a century ago, each holding a portion of N'ghathrod. All five offered themselves — the three unthinned in gratitude, because he gave them what bad rearing denied them; the two bearers in loyalty, because they could have stayed on Glyth and chose him.

He speaks through them at will and does not drive them. They are still themselves, and they are also him, and the colony holds both ideas without difficulty.

Killing one ends that Path permanently He cannot replace it without spending more of himself than he has left, and the colony loses the function entirely. The party will not know they have done this. They will think they killed a mind flayer.

Build between CR 12 and 16, lopsided. None of the five is a balanced encounter. Blocks not yet built

Not yet built CR 12–16, deliberately lopsided. The design brief is that none of the five is a balanced encounter — each is overwhelming at the one thing their Path holds and soft everywhere else.

The unthinned template applies to the three raised in untended pools: remove something visible from the body, add +2 AC, +30 HP, and one malformation feature. All of them have advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened, because nothing cultivated them and so nothing reaches them easily.
