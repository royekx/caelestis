Aurel Venn
A Watcher who warned the Order of a pattern, was not heeded, and left his post to find it himself.

Aurel Venn is a Watcher of the Order of the All-Father who has gone from his post. He saw a pattern of more traffic and something large moving at the edges of systems, warned the Order, and was not heeded. He never broke with the Order openly, but he left his post to find the thing himself. Accounts of him contradict one another.

Personality

A Watcher who would not wait to be believed. He keeps the Order's bearing and its charge, and he chose his own judgment over its protocol, as a rogue Watcher does.

Biography

Background

Venn was a posted Watcher when he saw the pattern. He reported it, and the Order did not act on his warning. He left his post without renouncing the Order.

In the campaign

The crew has not met him. They hear of him through contradictory accounts long before any meeting.

Relationships

Order of the All-Father

His order, which did not heed him. He has never broken with it openly.

What's hidden

  • What he is chasing. The thing he saw is Vth'oramu.

How to run him

The touchstone is Osiris, from Destiny: a figure the crew hears about from people who disagree, each account partly true. Let the contradictions do the work before he ever appears.

His arc

He is one of the strains on the Fleet, with Ilsevaine Corr, the rise in Monoliths and the Watchers' calls for help. He is a named presence first, a meeting later.

Threads

  • He and Ilsevaine Corr have seen the same thing: something large moving where no one looks.
  • He is a separate figure from the fringe sage of Ascher Mir, who tracks the Seekers' pattern across chapters.

Still to decide
