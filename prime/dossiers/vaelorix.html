Vaelorix
A landless beholder who collected living things, stole Ostekk's Engine, and died on his own Command Deck with a plant grown out of him.

Vaelorix, the Cultivant, was a beholder of H'Catha who held no domain and collected living things. He stole the Containment Engine, Fourth Series, the Lantern of Flourishing, from Ostekk, with the nine OSTEKK units. He died on the Command Deck of his own ship, the Tyrant Ship, with a plant grown out of his body. On his home world his ship reads as native traffic.

Appearance

A beholder's body on the Command Deck platform. A great flower grew out of it until Bartholomew tore it free; beneath, his central eye is dried open in its socket.

Personality

He wanted living things kept, not governed, and built a collection where other beholders built domains. That is why he held no port, kept few thralls worth the name, and had nobody to call on. He was paranoid and solitary even for a beholder. He judged rightly that the Engine was worth stealing, and wrongly that he could point it at something and control the result.

Biography

Background

Vaelorix stole the Engine and the nine units from Ostekk, kept the Engine and used it sparingly to repair his units. He flew his ship from the Command Deck and never needed a helm. He kept his research specimens in the garden at the bottom of the garden shaft. He pointed the Engine's regenerative working at a naturally occurring wisp-lotus symbiosis and got a paracausal eruption instead of a specimen. The bloom overran the ship, and the field came off it: the field that froze the clockwork horrors, slowed the OSTEKK units and kept the behir out. Nobody on H'Catha learned what became of him, and the ship drifted in orbit around Toril for years. His next target had been the Rod's fifth piece, held by Xanathar.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 5. Ostekk-6 named him as the ship's master, a beholder who simply floated between decks. The crew found his body on the Command Deck platform, a great flower grown out of it, and read the room as his central eye cancelling magic.
  • Voyage 6. Gregory read the roots and learned the flowers made both the wisps and the field. The crew cut the side flowers, Bartholomew tore the great one off the body, and with the field gone, magic returned. Gregory found the Lantern beneath the roots.

What's hidden

  • H'Catha does not know he is dead. He disappeared and nobody followed up. When his ship enters a H'Cathan domain's space, a faction will assume he is back, as an ally, a rival or a debtor, depending on whose space it is. The disguise works too well: the crew will be identified as him.
  • His next target. The Rod's fifth piece, held by Xanathar. He was going after it when the catastrophe stopped him.

How to run him

He never appears alive. He is a corpse, a reputation and a set of consequences.
