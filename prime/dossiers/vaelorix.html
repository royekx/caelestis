Vaelorix
Beholder collector of living things. Landless, H'Cathan, and dead on his own command deck.

A beholder who collected living things, and who held no domain. H'Cathan and landless — which is the detail that makes his ship dangerous to fly: on his home world, a vessel everyone recognises arriving without territory behind it reads as a claimant returning rather than a trader.

He stole the Containment Engine from Ostekk, a rival with no interest in living things at all, and that theft is why a clockwork behir was stranded aboard his ship until the crew ejected it in V006.

What killed him was his own hobby

He pointed the Engine's regenerative effect at a naturally occurring wisp-lotus symbiosis — and got a paracausal eruption instead of a specimen. The bloom overran the ship. He is dead on the Command Deck with a plant grown out of his body, and the field that unmakes constructs came off it until the crew cut the flowers down in V006.

The crew read the room in V005 as his central eye cancelling magic. In V006 Gregory read the roots and learned the flowers made both the wisps and the field; the crew cut the side flowers, and Bartholomew tore the great one off the body.

He wanted living things kept rather than ruled. Alone among the beholders he was building a collection instead of a domain. That is why he held no port, kept few thralls worth speaking of, and had nobody to call on.

He was paranoid and solitary even by the standards of a species defined by both, and he vanished without anyone on H'Catha learning what became of him.

He held, correctly, that the Engine was worth stealing. He was wrong about being able to point it at something and control the result.

What's hidden

H'Catha does not know he is dead

He disappeared and nobody followed up. So when his ship arrives in a H'Cathan domain's space, a faction will assume he is back — as an ally, a rival, or a debtor, depending on whose space it enters.

The disguise works too well. The crew will be identified as him rather than as intruders.

His next target was the Rod's fifth piece, find the path, held by Xanathar — which is where he was going when the catastrophe stopped him.

Threads

Kurrzot is the one to watch: something between him and Vaelorix was left unsettled, and he sends an emissary rather than an attack. A crew flying a dead beholder's ship is negotiating on a dead beholder's credit without knowing the balance.

How to run them

He never appears alive. He is a corpse, a reputation, and a set of consequences, and all three are more useful than a beholder encounter would have been.
