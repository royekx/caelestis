Veena & Derek
Two rival cadets unconscious in the infirmary since the simulation blast, and nobody has asked why they have not woken.

Veena and Derek are cadets at the Academy at Caelestis, on the crew that races opposite the party; Veena leads it. The arcane blast that wrecked the simulation chamber in Voyage 3 put both of them down, and they have lain bandaged, breathing and unconscious in the infirmary since.

Appearance

As the crew last saw them: bandaged and still in the infirmary beds, breathing, side by side.

Personality

Rivals in the ordinary way: the other squad, the other chamber, the salvage race run from the opposite side. Veena made the one piece of friction anyone remembers, reaching for Boogie's corner bunk before he had settled, and she backed off when Sol stepped in behind him.

How to run them

They share one page because they are one fact. Separately they are a bunk argument and a name; together they are the question of why two cadets have been asleep for three voyages, and nobody aboard has asked. The cheapest way to raise it is a crew member asking after them before the ship leaves, and one line from an orderly that the healers are satisfied and they simply have yet to wake.

Three readings are available, in rising order of consequence. An injury healing slowly costs nothing and closes the thread. An arcane blast that left something in them is cheap and gives the Academy a reason to be evasive about an accident on its own grounds. Being kept down, for what they saw or because they are more useful asleep, makes Caelestis itself a problem. Waking them is always available; un-waking them is not.
