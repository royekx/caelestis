Veena & Derek
Two cadets have been unconscious in the infirmary since the blast. Nobody has asked why they have not woken up.

Rivals in the ordinary way — the other squad, the other chamber, the salvage race run from the opposite side. Veena made the one piece of friction anyone remembers, reaching for Boogie’s corner bunk before he had settled, and she backed off when Sol stepped in behind him.

The thing on this page The arcane blast that wrecked the simulation chamber put both of them down. They have been bandaged, breathing and unconscious ever since, across every voyage since V003.

That is a long time for an injury the record treats as an injury, and nobody aboard has asked a single question about it.

Their squadmate left and they stayed Miken came out of the same squad and is aboard now, reporting to Qitru. The crew knows the rival squad as Miken, and these two are the part of it still lying on Caelestis.

The silence around them has grown long enough to carry something. Three readings, in ascending order of consequence: Open

ReadingWhat it costs and buys
It was an injury. They are healing slowly and will wake up offstage.Free. Closes the thread. Use it if the board is full, and it already is.
The blast was arcane and left something in them. Two cadets who have been touched by whatever wrecked that chamber.Cheap, and it gives the Academy a reason to be evasive about an accident on its own grounds.
They are being kept down. Either because of what they saw in the chamber, or because they are more useful asleep.Expensive. It makes Caelestis itself a problem, and the crew has enough adversaries.
Recommendation Take the middle one and spend it later. Two sleepers in an infirmary cost nothing to maintain and can be cashed in whenever Act I needs the Academy to look less clean. Waking them up is always available; un-waking them is not. Your call

They are here as one page because they are one fact. Separately they are a bunk argument and a name. Together they are the question of why two cadets have been asleep for three voyages.

The cheapest way to raise it A crew member asking after them before the ship leaves. One line from an orderly that the healers are satisfied and they simply have yet to wake. Satisfied healers and an unexplained sleep is all the hook needs, and it costs a single exchange on the way to the dock.

Open: which reading is true, and whether Veena’s small grudge against Boogie survives waking up.
