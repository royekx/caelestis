M. Tarroway
Strake Station's cartographer, whose readings were the first hint of something at the edge of the system.

M. Tarroway is the cartographer on Strake Station. A year ago his readings were the first hint that an unknown vessel or structure was disturbing the satellites at the edge of the system. His name turns up here and there, mainly in the Fleet Intelligence Archive. Nothing about him is public.

Appearance

A station hand in a working coat, with chart ink on his fingers.

Biography

Background

Tarroway files route notations with Strake's manifests and keeps the station's charts of the edge run to Cairn. When Strake's readings began to show something disturbing the satellites, his were first, and he revised the Cairn approach three times in a single cycle and filed a note saying so. Strake reported an unidentified mass; Cairn went silent soon after.

In the campaign

The crew has not met him or read his name.

Relationships

Quartermaster Halix Verrow

Strake's quartermaster, who counter-signs the manifests Tarroway's notations ride on.

What's hidden

  • The mass. What his readings caught was Vth'oramu, which sat on the Cairn bearing for days before it moved off into the dark between systems.

How to run him

He is a thread for the players to piece together, not a figure the crew meets. His name surfaces in the archive, mainly in Cairn's last logistics report, LG-003, with his filings also in FR-005 and WM-002, and a reader who follows it notices that the first person to see something was Strake's cartographer. Leave him off the deck plan.
