M. Tarroway
Strake Station's cartographer, who found that Strake sees things on Cairn's lane that Cairn does not report.

M. Tarroway is the cartographer on Strake Station. He pulled a pattern out of the noise at the limit of Strake's scope: Strake logs contacts on Cairn Station's lane that Cairn never reports. His first filing on it sent Bellwether Crow to look in on Cairn, and his later filings were set aside after its report. His name turns up mainly in the Fleet Intelligence Archive. Nothing about him is public.

Appearance

A station hand in a working coat, with chart ink on his fingers.

Biography

Background

Tarroway keeps Strake's charts and reads the returns from its long scope. Strake sits on its own lane, nearer home than Cairn and off to the side of Cairn's lane, and Cairn's stretch lies at the very limit of Strake's scope, where it picks up faint, odd returns. Week after week Tarroway set Strake's contacts on that stretch against Cairn's reports, which read perfectly and never mention them. He filed the discrepancy, Halix Verrow sent it up the chain, and on it the Fleet sent Crow to look in on Cairn.

Crow was raided on Cairn's lane, repaired at Cairn and came home reporting the station in order, so its report answered his filing. Strake had logged a contact during Crow's approach, inside Cairn's own range, and Cairn's own scope log for that watch shows none. Tarroway kept filing, and the later filings were set aside.

Among his work is a plate of four sweeps from Strake's scope toward Cairn's lane, each a little closer, with the return a different shape on every pass: a long smooth oval; the oval with a deep notch in its leading edge; two lobes, one far larger than the other; one mass again, trailing long filaments from its lower edge. His margin note reads: “Not a vessel profile. Does not hold a shape. Cairn reports nothing on this bearing.”

What's hidden

  • The sweeps. The four shapes are Vth'oramu seen from different angles. It crosses Cairn's lane now and then on its way somewhere.
  • Cairn's reports. The colony holds Cairn. Its station master is one of N'ghathrod's taken and writes every report that leaves the station, and the rest of the crew are held by one of Zhauveth's malformed spawn and set aside what they see on the lane.
  • The approach. The contact Strake logged during the approach was the raid on Bellwether Crow.
  • Strake's reach. Nobody in the colony knows that Strake can see as far as Cairn's stretch. The colony never learned that Tarroway raised the question; as far as it knows, the Fleet looked and got its answer.

How to run him

He is a thread the players piece together through the archive. What waits at the end of it is a witness who noticed: a careful man with years of filings and a plate of sweeps that does not hold a shape. Once the crew has seen Vth'oramu, the sweeps read as a body seen from four angles.
