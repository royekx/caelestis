M. Tarroway
Keeps the Cairn approach packet. Has every fact and the wrong model.

Station cartographer at Strake, and the keeper of the only staged copies of the Cairn approach packet in the sector — charts, survey material, and timing tables for the dreadnought's patrol.

He has revised those tables three times in a single cycle and filed a note saying so. It reads as a cartographer's complaint and it is the most accurate observation anyone has made about the Tetherbreak.

He will talk, at length, to anyone who asks He has the approach, the filings, the history of the bearing, and a professional grievance he is glad to explain. A live, willing, talkative source sitting exactly where the crew will need one.

A table that keeps failing is a table that is wrong. He has never once considered that the problem might be the thing being tracked rather than his measurement of it, because in his profession that is almost always the right instinct.

He holds the standing order as settled: the run past Strake is closed, and he is not the man to argue with the Bridge about it. He will give the crew everything he has and decline to help them use it.

He is not hiding anything, which is the point He has every fact and the wrong model. The patrol his tables track is the Vth'oramu — a hunting animal under command. An animal keeps a rhythm; a ship goes where it is wanted.

He has spent cycles revising a patrol table for something that was never keeping a patrol. And he does not know the thing has gone, so he is still maintaining a schedule for an object that left the sector a year ago.

He also does not know what is in the secure storage on his own station. Contents open

The cleanest way to hand players a mystery He is a competent professional who has done the work correctly and reached a conclusion that cannot be right. The crew gets the whole shape of the problem from someone who is sure of none of it, which is how you deliver a mystery without an NPC solving it.

His grievance is the clue. Nothing has to be pried out of him.

He is also the obstacle he does not mean to be. Everything the crew needs for Cairn is in his packet, and he will not help them break a standing order to use it — so the route runs through Quartermaster Verrow’s counter-signature rather than through him.
