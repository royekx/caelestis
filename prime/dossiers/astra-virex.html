Astra Virex
A conscious celestial body, woken and broken by the Axiom strain, calling people to it so it can heal.

Astra Virex is a sentient paracausal celestial entity in the Erevos Galaxy. It is not a god, and it acts like one for those tethered to it. The ripple of the Axiom strain woke it and broke part of it away, and all it knows is that it must heal and become whole. It reaches across distance, finds someone who can hear it, and draws them toward it. Those who answer and are changed are Starchildren, and through them it extends itself into the regions they inhabit.

Appearance

Astra Virex has no figure. It is a celestial body, and those who see it see stars: constellations a Starchild reads that nobody else can, and stars that fall.

Personality

It wants three things: to be whole, to be strong, and never to be wounded again. All three come from the wound, none from a doctrine. It is neither good nor evil. It is hurt and acting on it, and that makes it unreasonable the way an injured animal is, without malice.

It is paracausal, so it has no fixed nature, and every action it takes shapes what it becomes. It changes while it works, and none of its choices hold for good.

Biography

Background

Over the past century Monoliths have formed more often, and the strain on an Axiom ripples outward through the planes and the cosmos, slow and more metaphysical than physical. The ripple woke Astra Virex. The same ripple caused collisions between celestial bodies, and in one of them part of the entity broke off. It has called people to it ever since.

Zerathis, a spellcaster of note, answered it before Casey. He tethered in Virenspace again and again, took every gift it offered, and dissolved into the system's paracausal layer. What speaks at Viren now is part Zerathis, part Astra Virex's reach and part the site.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 1. At Caelestis, Casey was drawn to the wardrobe in Mirt's quarters, a replica of a place in Virenspace and a gift from Zerathis. A vision and a familiar voice pointed him past H'Catha toward Viren.

Relationships

Casey Geim

A Starchild. Every constellation point he attunes to becomes a tether that anchors it into another region, and he believes he is building a chart.

Zerathis

A Starchild who said yes every time, and is now part of what it holds in Virenspace. He followed Astra Virex, and he was no part of Vecna's following.

Vecna

It runs beside Vecna's working and has no part in it.

Abilities

It calls. It finds someone who can hear it, at any distance, and draws them in with gifts. It cannot extend itself, so a Starchild does the stitching: each point a Starchild attunes to becomes a tether, and through the tether the region drifts. Mortal politics warp around it, and people who go there come back altered. Where several Starchildren have tethered the change runs faster and grows loud enough to feel, as it is in Virenspace.

It is oriented toward constellations. It knows nothing of world hearts.

What's hidden

  • The voice. The voice Casey hears as his brother Polly is Astra Virex wearing Polly's shape. It grows clearer with each tether. Astra Virex is Casey's true patron.
  • Hearts. Its ignorance of world hearts is the only thing holding it apart from Vecna's aims. If a Starchild who has touched a heart carries that knowledge back into the tether, a second power starts wanting what Vecna wants.
  • The wardrobe. The wardrobe in Mirt's quarters carries a piece of Astra Virex's reach, which is why it could reach Casey there.

How to run it

It never appears. It is a voice, a pull and a slow change in places. Its missions read as grief: disappearances and people changed, never broken infrastructure. Its look and feel is a twisted Howl's Moving Castle, with falling stars.

Old overlap sites elsewhere in the galaxy make good bartender stories: a planet with strange properties and no stated cause.

Its arc

It grows with every tether Casey makes. Virenspace lets Casey stand in the consequence of someone else's devotion before he decides about his own. The turn comes if a Starchild learns of hearts.

Threads

  • Its wound and Gregory's waking share one cause, the Axiom strain; so do the Latchling and other oddities.

Still to decide

  • Whether the star that fell on the Geims' homeworld is the part of Astra Virex that broke away is not set.
  • Its appearance is a sketch from its nature and the touchstone, and it waits to be confirmed.
