Astra Virex
A conscious body that fell. Hurt, calling, and expanding through those who answer.

A sentient paracausal celestial body, wounded by a Seam — Transformation and Boundary overlapping across something that was already conscious, opening its distinction and altering its nature at once.

Astra is what the Order calls it from outside. Virex is nearer to what it is. It is not a god in the doctrinal sense, and it functions like one in the experience of anyone tethered to it.

A second cosmic pressure, belonging to nobody else's plan It runs in parallel to the Ascension and has no part in it. Two things are reshaping the cosmos and only one of them is a plan — which is what keeps the campaign from being a single villain's story.

To be whole, to be strong, and to never let this happen again. That is the whole of it, and all three follow from the wound rather than from any doctrine.

It is neither good nor evil. It is hurt, and it is acting on that — which makes it unreasonable in the specific way an injured thing is, rather than malicious.

Because it is paracausal it has no fixed nature, so every action it takes shapes what it becomes. It is changing while it works, and nothing it has decided is permanent.

What it does not know It is oriented toward constellations. It knows nothing of world-hearts. It may only learn of them through a Starchild who touches one, which is a turn rather than a given.

That ignorance is the campaign's safety valve. The moment a Starchild carries heart-knowledge back into the tether, a second power starts wanting what Vecna wants.

It reaches Casey in Polly's voice. Whether that is a deliberate deception, a convenience, or the only channel it has is unestablished — and the three answers produce very different endgames.

Open: how much of what it was survived the Seam, and whether anything of the original is recoverable.

It never appears. It is a voice, a pull, and a slow change in places — which is the right register for something that is a property of the cosmos rather than a thing inside it.
