Vanifer
The eldest of the Cre'Tez siblings, who raised the younger ones, and who goes by Finerva when she wants something done.

Vanifer is the eldest of the Cre'Tez siblings of Fonains, a half-sister to the others by the same father, Cenvinzo. After Benedetta died she raised most of the younger ones. Under the name Finerva she posted the Blooming Glass bounty out of Orerius, and the Pedra Ardente, the gem recovered there, went to her. A voice reached her and traded her the lost meaning of the Alpha and the secret in the Cre'Tez blood.

Personality

To her siblings she is the caretaker, the one who filled in for their father's lack of affection. Xero shares the odd quiet moment with her, and so does Lusion.

Biography

Background

Vanifer is Cenvinzo's daughter by a mother other than Benedetta, and the eldest of seven: Vanifer, Asmodus, Kevulan, Xero, Lusion, Dharra and Rihiri. Benedetta fell ill at Rihiri's birth, about twelve years ago, and died two years later. Vanifer raised most of the younger siblings after that. About two years ago Cenvinzo invoked the Alpha Inheritance, and the family broke apart over the birthright.

As Finerva she posted a bounty out of Orerius for the Cre'Tez, “for the eldest brother”: four archaeologists, missing at the Blooming Glass in the Wandering Sands. Clint Dashforest's crew took the job, freed Chloe Chespetes, rescued the archaeologists, and handed her the Pedra Ardente, the gem a fire genasi sect once used to try to open a portal to Imix.

What's hidden

  • She has no birthright. Her mother was not Benedetta, and a Bloodstone is imprinted with the souls of both parents, so she could claim none. She made an illegitimate Bloodstone by slowly siphoning Benedetta's soul into it. The siphoning was Benedetta's illness, and it killed her.
  • Asmodus's memory. Asmodus learned that she killed their mother, and they fought; Kevulan witnessed part of it. She modified Asmodus's memory so he believes the younger siblings' births wore Benedetta out, and he turned on them.
  • The Alpha. She tried to make her father concede his birthright to her. He invoked the Alpha instead, locking them both out of the inheritance. He is barely alive now, a shadow of himself, held captive and bound to her.
  • The phoenix dreams. She sends Asmodus dreams in Benedetta's likeness, promising that the inheritance will release a phoenix and raise his mother. She leads the fire cult, and on her orders it plays along and treats Asmodus as its prophet.
  • The voice. The voice that reached her was a Seeker chapter. It fed a hatred she had kept hidden, her spite at being punished for her father's grievances, and told her Imix, a phoenix, would cleanse the world of transgressors like her father and leave those with the heart to withstand it. It lied. Imix was sealed to stop his true goal: to burn everything and revel in the flames.

How to run her

She waits to see who becomes heir, so she can make that heir concede to her. If she can steer the heirs to name her by a unanimous vote, she enters the Eternal Flame temple at night, rested. If she has to force a contested Beta, she flees into the temple at once. If she is caught there, she escapes immediately. Either way, Lola's Boys find the temple.

Play her warm: she is the sister who stayed and raised them, and that is how she moves the others. She steered Lusion and Xero toward Dharra and Rihiri, then told the fire cult where to find the sisters.
