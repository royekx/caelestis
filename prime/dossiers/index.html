Dossiers

Everything with a will that the DM has to play: people, gods, constructs, and a ship with something driving it. Each dossier says what its subject is, what it wants, what it hides and how to run it. Organized groups and their members are on Factions; a ship's decks and interior are on Vessels; the player characters are on Crew. Rows run A to Z by the name a subject goes by, and can be grouped by side.
