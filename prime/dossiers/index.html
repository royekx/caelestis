DM Dossiers

Per-character pages carrying what the players cannot see. Same layout as the public dossier — portrait, identity card, bio — with a Stat Block tab beside the bio.

Why these exist separately The pages under dossiers/ are player-facing: they carry no noindex, they are indexed by site search, and the bearings and inventory pages link to them. Anything written there is published. These pages are the DM half, and they carry the stat blocks, the private motives, and anything else a player should reach only when the campaign hands it over.

A DM page exists only for a character who needs one. Most of the cast is fine with a Factions entry.

Later: handing an ally's block to the players An ally's stat block is worth publishing once the crew has earned it. Knowing that Tarto can spend her reaction to swap places with you, or that Saerthe's cubes can be repaired by anyone who casts mending, turns an NPC from a passenger into something the party can build a plan around.

The structure already supports it: the block is written once here, and publishing it later means adding the same tab to that character's public page. Start with whichever NPC the crew has actually bonded with, and treat it as a reward rather than a reference release.

Blocks are also listed together in the appendix, which holds the opening line for each and links back here.
