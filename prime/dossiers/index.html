DM Dossiers

Everyone the DM has to play, with what the players cannot see. A dossier exists for a subject that has motive, something withheld, and a way it reacts to being pushed; the rest of the cast is fine with a Factions entry.

The public half is published The pages under dossiers/ at the site root are indexed by site search and linked from bearings and inventory, so anything written there is published. These pages are the DM half, and they hold the stat blocks, the private motives, and anything a player should reach only when it is handed to them.

Who deliberately has no DM page

A public page plus a line in a voyage is enough for a character with no hidden side. These are complete as they stand, and giving each a DM page would add navigation rather than information.

Promote on contact, not in advance. If one of them turns out to be standing where a scene needs a person, that is the moment the page earns itself.

Handing an ally's block to the players

An ally's stat block is worth publishing once the crew has earned it. Knowing that Tarto can spend her reaction to swap places with you, or that Saerthe's cubes can be repaired by anyone who casts mending, turns an NPC from a passenger into something the party can build a plan around.

The structure already supports it: the block is written once here, and publishing it later means adding the same tab to that character's public page. Start with whichever NPC the crew has actually bonded with, and treat it as a reward rather than a reference release.

Blocks are also listed together in the appendix, which holds the opening line for each and links back here.
