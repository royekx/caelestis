Vth'oramu
An astral dreadnought taken alive and held by a colony's worth of tadpoles, going where N'ghathrod sends it.

Vth'oramu is an astral dreadnought, the apex predator of the Astral Sea, taken alive by N'ghathrod and rebuilt into the home of his colony, the Vth'orama. It is still alive, and it goes where it is sent. N'ghathrod is fused into it: the dreadnought's nervous system is his. Its decks, pools and quarters as a place are on its vessel entry.

Appearance

A chain-tailed leviathan of the Astral Sea. The body lies elongated and contorted, its plating carved for docking, berths and observation and braced with scaffolding. Its eye still works as an eye and serves the colony as its watch on the Astral Sea. The mouth remains, and so does the physical tail, immensely long, ending in an astral cord, a silver line running off to somewhere unknown.

Personality

It once hunted its own territory, as astral dreadnoughts do. Now it moves on command, held by the tadpoles in it, and keeps only the instincts of its kind under them.

Biography

Background

The dreadnought hunted its territory for as long as anything had watched it, and it bore no wound before the siege. N'ghathrod knew a dreadnought's physiology, and a whisper told him a dreadnought was the best way to keep a colony on its passage home. He saw his chance and took it. The fight lasted a long time, and controlling the creature took a great many tadpoles. Taking it and turning it into Vth'oramu took decades, perhaps a century, in the centuries after the colony's exile from Glyth. N'ghathrod went into it himself.

The people and the ship share their roots: vth- the whole of, or- toward, amu home. The Vth'orama are all who go toward home; Vth'oramu is the passage to a new home. The founders say the name as a promise, and those born aboard say it as “here”.

In the campaign

A year ago it sat for days on Cairn Station's bearing, a mass on Cairn's long scope that matched no vessel profile. It has moved since, and keeps to the dark between systems. The unidentified mass Strake Station reported was Vth'oramu.

Relationships

N'ghathrod

Its master, fused into it. The tadpole pools bloom out of the hull, so every taken person aboard was grown from his flesh.

The Vth'orama

The ship means two things to the two generations aboard. To the founding colonists it is a means to an end, the passage to a home they were promised and have yet to be given. To those born aboard it is home itself; they have stood nowhere else, and the name describes a journey they have no memory of beginning.

Abilities

Its mouth draws things in, the way an astral dreadnought's mouth opens on its inner demiplane. Its great weapon is the reverse: a pulse pressed outward from every side of its body, and everything within range is teleported away. The pulse takes a long time to charge and is rarely used. Skiffs go out from it to the worlds in its vicinity while it stays where no one looks. It can enter a system, but a vessel that size near a world's planets would almost certainly be seen, and its tail could never hide there. Illithid arcanists from N'ghathrod's own crew keep the plane-shift transit points inside.

What's hidden

  • Under command. Every institutional record treats it as wildlife, a predator with a range and a patrol. It is a creature under command, and every Fleet model of the region is built without that fact.
  • Inside him. Boarding is walking around inside N'ghathrod. Most of the colony has never been told; they live in him and think the ship is a ship.
  • The walls. The earliest copies of N'ghathrod are in the dreadnought's walls.
  • The promise. A people still travelling toward a home will follow whoever carries them, and arriving would end that. N'ghathrod has never let the two generations' readings of the name settle.
