Hessa Dun
A third-intake cadet who posts a notice-board job, and whose uncle collects things and likes visitors.

Hessa Dun is a third-intake cadet at the Academy at Caelestis. She posts a job on the notice board, “WANTED: hands and strong back”, to move her family's crates. She mentions, lightly, that her uncle collects a lot of things, and that if the crew are ever on his world they should say hello, because he likes visitors.

Biography

In the campaign

The crew has not met her yet.

Relationships

Her uncle

A living uncle with strange stories, who collects a lot of things and likes visitors. He is not openly a Seeker.

What's hidden

  • Her uncle. He is a Seeker, and the crew's first Seekers contact. Hessa is a soft plant on the Academy's own notice board, and the crew may think nothing of her uncle.

How to run her

She is a thread that can grow into something, and she makes the Academy feel lived in: a cadet with family crates and a notice on the board. Plant her in Arc 1. If the timing slips, the job can come after graduation, as an ordinary contract.

Threads

  • Her uncle's collection is the way to the first Seekers chapter the crew meets (the Seekers).
