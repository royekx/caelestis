Ostekk
A ruling beholder of H'Catha who builds, whose Engine and automatons were stolen, and who wants his property back.

Ostekk is a ruling beholder of H'Catha, lord of one of its ten domains, and a builder. He made the Containment Engine, Fourth Series, now called the Lantern of Flourishing, around the fourth piece of the Rod of Seven Parts, and the nine OSTEKK units, and both were stolen from him by Vaelorix. Everything the crew fought aboard the Tyrant Ship was his: the automatons, the clockwork horrors and the behir.

Personality

He rules his domain like a workshop. What he wants is his property back. He secures loyalty through need, not trust: he built the OSTEKK units to depend on the Engine for their maintenance, so their service required no faith on his part, and that is what he thinks of other minds. He is still a beholder. He sends agents and never comes himself.

Biography

Background

Ostekk built the Engine, the fourth of its series, around a stone that did one impossible thing well; the name records three earlier attempts. He built nine automatons to depend on it. Vaelorix stole the Engine and the nine units together, and Ostekk built a tenth, Ostekk-10, a clockwork behir, to bring them back. The behir was stranded aboard Vaelorix's ship by a field it could not enter.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 5. Ostekk-2 named the behir's sender as “the original Ostekk”, and called him a brilliant tinkerer.
  • Voyage 6. Ostekk-10 named him as its master, the one it was recovering the Engine for, and Ostekk-6 called him his previous master and the Engine's maker. The crew took the Lantern, told the behir nothing had been found, and ejected it through Cargo.

What's hidden

  • The piece. He does not know what is inside his Engine. He built a housing for a thing that did one impossible thing well, as every competent finder of a Rod piece has done. The paracausal trace in his automatons is an accident: he made nine servants slightly outside causality and has no idea.
  • A reason to come. The crew took his Engine and lied to his machine. He has reason now to come for it himself.

How to run him

Keep him offstage as long as it holds. His agents are more interesting than he is while the crew is still assembling what he wants. Hearing his name soon after landing on H'Catha is the payoff, and it gives the H'Catha arc a second power whose motive the crew already understands.

Threads

  • Ostekk-10, the behir, is outside the hull and still searching.
  • Krik'Lit translated the V006 parley. He leaves at the V006 swap aboard the Flighty Foundling, and after it the next conversation with Ostekk's machines has no translator aboard.
