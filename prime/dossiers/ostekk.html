Ostekk
Beholder builder. Made the Engine, had it stolen, and has been reaching for it ever since.

A technology-obsessed beholder, and the only one in this arc who builds rather than rules. He made the Containment Engine, Fourth Series — the name records three earlier attempts — around the Rod's fourth piece, the one keyed to regenerate.

Vaelorix stole it. Ostekk has been trying to get it back ever since, and everything the crew has fought aboard the tyrant ship is his: the clockwork horrors, and the behir sent to retrieve the Engine and stranded by a field that unmakes constructs.

He has never appeared and is already everywhere

The crew has met his automatons, his horrors, his behir and his name — OSTEKK-2 said it out loud, naming the behir's sender as “the original Ostekk.” Everything they have fought on that ship was built by a man they have never seen.

Loyalty through need rather than trust

He built the OSTEKK units to depend on the Engine for their own maintenance, so that their service required no faith on his part. It is the clearest statement of what he thinks about other minds: they are unreliable, and dependency is the fix.

He does not collect, rule, or scheme for territory. He wants his property back, and he has been spending constructs on that problem for however long it has been.

He is still a beholder: he sends agents rather than coming himself, and he has never once had to be present for any of it.

What's hidden

He does not know what the piece inside his Engine is. Nothing on record suggests he understood he was building around Reach residue. He built a housing for a thing that did one impossible thing well, which is what every competent finder in history has done with a piece of the Rod.

Which also means the paracausal trace in his automatons is an accident. He made nine servants slightly outside causality and has no idea.

Threads

The numbering

OSTEKK-1 through OSTEKK-9, OSTEKK-6 among them, were taken from Ostekk along with the engine. He built Ostekk-10 afterward, to go and retrieve them. The behir, Ostekk-10, is the live thread. In V006 it parleyed through Krik'Lit, asked for the containment engine for its master, and offered to leave once it had it. The crew took the Lantern, told it nothing had been found, and ejected it through Cargo. It is outside the hull and still searching, and Ostekk now has a reason to come for the engine himself: the crew took it from him and lied to his machine.

The window was used

The crew talked to his constructs. Krik'Lit translated the parley in V006, and the crew answered the behir's offer of mutual trust with a lie. When Krik'Lit leaves at the V007 swap, the next conversation with Ostekk's machines has no translator aboard.

How to run them

Keep him offstage as long as it holds. His agents are more interesting than he is while the crew is still assembling what he wants, and the moment he appears the question stops being open.

Open

Where to put him

Putting him in the Spindle turns a rock recovery into his doorstep, because the crew would arrive at H'Catha carrying the Lantern he has spent years reaching for. The tyrant ship keeps paying off after the ship is taken.

It fits a beholder world, it costs no invention, and it gives the H'Catha arc a second antagonist whose motive the crew already understands. Open

Still to decide

His location, whether he knows the Engine is findable, and what he does when he learns a cadet crew has it.
