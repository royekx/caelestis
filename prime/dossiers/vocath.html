Vocath
A mercane merchant prosecuting a private grudge against Mirt, and the tool of a colony he has never heard of.

Vocath is a mercane merchant of long standing in the trade circles around Realmspace, and the head of Vocath's crew, his trading house. He is in active commercial conflict with Mirt, headmaster of the Academy at Caelestis; the feud is well known in the trade and older than any recent trouble at the station. His aim is to disgrace Mirt enough to pry him loose from Caelestis's protection, so that someone else can finish him. He is not a fighter and takes no part in the assault on Caelestis.

Appearance

A mercane: tall, long-limbed and blue-skinned, robed like a merchant prince, with long fingers made for counting. His house mark, an eye inside a wheel, is on everything he ships.

Personality

He owns his grudge openly, and that is his best cover: a merchant loudly feuding with a headmaster is doing something everyone can see and nobody looks past. A trade is a trade. He holds that what he buys is what he gets, that an asset who takes his money answers to him, and that his manifests describe his cargo. He sees the Fleet stretched thin and uses it to put pressure on the Academy.

Biography

Background

Vocath was at Cairn Station transiting goods, and his line runs through it. When Cairn went dark his people took it and waited for the relief run, and they ambushed the Bellwether Crow for the second Distinction piece. The corrupted Monolith fragments that reached Boogie came through his line. His house mark has turned up on the recovered rod, on the Sky Dock crate and in the Crow's last transmission; it is private, so the Fleet registry has no entry for it, and anyone who trades in the right circles reads it on sight.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 3. His name was the marking on the arcane rod recovered from the wrecked simulation chamber. Mirt named him as one who had long borne him a grudge, making anyone connected to Mirt a target.

How to run him

Name him to the players; the name means nothing to them yet. He never appears in a fight. Pressure on him is commercial, legal and social, and the most dangerous thing the crew can do to him is prove he was used, which costs him the standing the feud was meant to win.

Threads

  • Cairn Station is the crew's first thread to him, and he can point them toward N'ghathrod without knowing what he is pointing at (Cairn Station).
  • His operation has two principals and he knows of one. The colony's side is on Opposition: the Vth'orama.
