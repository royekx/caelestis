Vocath
A mercane merchant with an old grudge against Mirt, who sells captives to an illithid and believes the informant she gave him is his own.

Vocath is a mercane merchant of long standing in the trade circles around Realmspace, and the head of Vocath's crew, his family's trading house. The house holds the Fleet contract for stores on Cairn Station's lane, and since the Fonains Monolith broke it has traded on Fonains. He is in active commercial conflict with Mirt, headmaster of the Academy at Caelestis; the feud is well known in the trade and older than any recent trouble at the station. His aim is to disgrace Mirt enough to pry him loose from Caelestis's protection, so that someone else can finish him. He is not a fighter.

Appearance

A mercane: tall, long-limbed and blue-skinned, robed like a merchant prince, with long fingers made for counting.

Personality

He owns his grudge openly, and that is his best cover: a merchant loudly feuding with a headmaster is doing something everyone can see, and nobody looks past it. A trade is a trade to him. He holds that what he buys is what he gets and that an asset who takes his money answers to him. He is a merchant first and moves fast when a market opens. He likes an enemy to know whose hand reached him. He sees the Fleet stretched thin and uses it to put pressure on the Academy.

Biography

Background

Vocath's house is a family business that has traded along the lanes around Realmspace for a long time. It supplies the stations on Cairn Station's lane under the Fleet's stores contract, and its ships call at Cairn as a matter of course. After the Fonains Monolith broke, his people were quick to start trading there, and they carry its gems and shards home; the shard Boogie carries came through his line. His feud with Mirt is older than all of it.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 3. Boogie glimpsed his name on the arcane rod recovered from the wrecked simulation chamber, and Saerthe named its marking. Mirt went still and named him as an old enemy who had long borne him a grudge, making anyone connected to Mirt a target. The crate Boogie and Sol traced at the Sky Dock bore the same seal.

Relationships

Mirt

His old enemy, in open commercial war.

Qitru

His intermediary, a githyanki who recruits, pays and collects. She recruited Miken for the house, and the informant inside Caelestis answers to her.

Zhauveth

An illithid he deals with. His line supplies captives, and she supplies information: which cargo moves when, which inspection is coming, where the patrols and escorts run. That knowledge is why his house prospers. She told him he has an informant inside Caelestis.

Joffrey

The informant inside Caelestis he believes is his, a decorated officer of the Caelestis Corps who answers to Qitru.

Abilities

Money, a trading line across Realmspace and now to Fonains, the Fleet's stores contract on Cairn's lane, and the reach of a house that has dealt in the right circles for a long time.

Notable items

  • His personal seal. A balance scale with coins on one pan and a crate on the other. It is registered nowhere.
  • His house mark. The public trade mark of the family business, on its ordinary cargo and its contracts.

  • The captive line. Pirates in his hire run the line, with one of his own crew riding with them as overseer, the one who pays them. They carry captives up Cairn Station's lane, and every few weeks, out on the lane near Cairn, the captives change hands from the pirates' ships to a colony courier. Zhauveth touches down at Cairn now and then to make the colony's deals with his overseers. After the raid the handovers stopped for a while. When they resumed they ran around the Fleet's escort routes on the lane, which N'ghathrod's taken pass to him through Zhauveth, and Cairn's taken station master has reported a clean lane every week since; the Fleet looked for transfers, found nothing on Cairn's word, and let it lapse. He is not taken: a willing partner costs the colony nothing.
  • The two marks. His house mark is the public trade mark of the family business; the Fleet knows it from the stores contract on Cairn's lane and has never seen it on anything wrong. His personal seal goes on his darker dealings: the rod, the Sky Dock crate, the neogi crate delivered to Mirt, the small crates his Fonains shards travel in, one to a crate, among them the one Boogie opened, and the crates aboard the pirates' ships, which outsiders never see. He signs grudges, never crimes against the Fleet. Against the Fleet means Fleet ships, cargo and contracts; he counts the Academy as Mirt's, which is why the rod carries his seal. His overseer puts it on at a handover, to show the pirates whom he speaks for, and does not wear it day to day. Mirt's trusted circle knows the seal on sight: Saerthe, Tarto, Mr. Blip, and Clint Dashforest.
  • The rod. He signed it on purpose, a message to Mirt that he can reach him. He meant it to hurt. Qitru told Miken it would only spoil the drill. Joffrey left it where Miken would pick it up, and it came in through the Sky Dock among the shipments under his seal.
  • The raid on Bellwether Crow. About a year ago Bellwether Crow, on its way to look in on Cairn, came on his hired pirates mid-handover, the captives strung between their two ships and a colony courier. They could not scatter, so they ran up pirate colors, a crossbones, and struck to drive it off. Clint Dashforest went aboard the smaller ship, the one carrying the overseer, and took its helm to draw the fight off his crew, and a color pool opened by chance and took that ship, with Clint, the overseer and its pirates aboard. With the overseer who paid them and the sealed cargo gone through the pool, the fight was not worth it to the main ship, and Crow's crew drove her off. She stayed free. Crow's survivors reported the main ship's name and the mark on its hull, and a figure on the smaller ship wearing a seal; the Fleet hunts that ship (SA-005), and the seal matches nothing in its registry. The colony courier at the handover pulled away from the fight and carried word to Zhauveth that Crow was coming to Cairn.
  • Fonains. The pool set the taken ship down on Fonains with the overseer and the crates under Vocath's seal aboard. Because his sealed cargo had gone through the pool with the ship, the overseer called Vocath by sending stone as soon as they were down. Clint got away, and the overseer and the pirates with him hunted him for a while so that he could not report what he had seen on the lane. The overseer told Vocath that Clint died in the hunt, and Vocath believes his witness is dead. His trade on Fonains began after the Monolith broke. Vocath knows nothing of any device Clint may carry.

How to run him

He never appears in a fight. Pressure on him is commercial, legal and social, and the most dangerous thing the crew can do to him is prove he was used, which costs him the standing the feud was meant to win. His seal is the thread the crew holds: describe it plainly whenever his darker side surfaces, and the crew will match the rod, the Sky Dock crate and FR-016's drawing in whichever order they meet them.

Threads

  • The stores contract puts his house mark at Cairn Station in plain sight, and the seal the survivors saw on the overseer (FR-016) is what ties him to the raid. The ship the Fleet hunts leads only to hired pirates. Cairn is the crew's road from him to the colony, and he can point them there without knowing what he points at.
  • Mirt knows the seal on sight. He has read WM-002 and FR-016, the seal as Crow's survivors described it, and knows Vocath is connected to the raid that took his friend Clint, but not how far: hired hands, his own line, or why there. The seal is registered nowhere, so he cannot prove it.
  • His operation has two principals and he knows of one. The colony's side is on Opposition: the Vth'orama.
