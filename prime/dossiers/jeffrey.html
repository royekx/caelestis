Jeffrey
A human merchant whose ship trades into the Sky Dock, whom Joffrey pays to carry certain crates in, and the softest thread to Joffrey.

Jeffrey is a human merchant who owns a ship trading into the Sky Dock of Caelestis, and a friend of Joffrey. Joffrey pays him to carry certain crates in on his ship. He is bribed, not taken, and he is in over his head.

Appearance

A human merchant who looks the part of a ship's owner, with the look of a man who has started sleeping badly.

Personality

Frightened, not venal. He said yes once and has been saying yes since. He believes he is doing a favour for a friend, a commended officer who would ask for nothing serious, and that belief rests on something true: Joffrey is that man. He believes he can stop whenever he likes, and he has not tested it. He has begun to suspect the sums are too large for what he is being asked to do.

Biography

In the campaign
  • Voyage 1. At the Sky Dock the cadets saw Joffrey pass him gold and pocket a bottle from him, and did not learn his name.
  • Voyage 3. Tumak named Joffrey to Mirt as the one who paid the bribe. Mirt named Jeffrey, alongside Joffrey, as a man the cadets should look into carefully.

Joffrey and Jeffrey on one station have sent cadets after the wrong man before, and the confusion works in Joffrey's favour without anyone arranging it.

What's hidden

  • The exchange. The exchange the cadets saw at the Sky Dock in Voyage 1 was Joffrey paying Jeffrey, and the bottle Joffrey pocketed came from him. What the bottle holds is not set; a piece of the sabotage is one option.
  • The sealed shipments. On Joffrey's word he carries in the crates Joffrey points him at, and each of them carries Vocath's personal seal. One was the sealed crate the cadets later found at the dock. The rod and the beacon that Miken used came in the same way. They came in under Mr. Blip's signature unseen: he knows the seal on sight, but he signs manifests off without opening every crate, a lapse, not complicity.
  • What he knows. He knows which crates, which runs and who asked. Why, what was inside, and who else is involved are hidden from him, and he has no idea what his friend is.
  • The neogi crate. The crate the crew took to Mirt, with the neogi inside, is a different crate. Nobody has checked it for a mark, and the seal is on it to find.

How to run him

Joffrey's file and his account of Bellwether Crow hold up, because they are true. Jeffrey can be caught by arithmetic. He is untrained, nobody is watching him, he has never had a cover story, and he would rather be caught than go deeper. What he hands over is a list of the shipments he passed for Joffrey: dates, hours and crates. Set against the crates, every one carries Vocath's personal seal, and that ties Joffrey to Vocath's darker dealings. It is the one piece of evidence that points at Joffrey; nothing Miken or the thief knows does. The crew leaning on him should feel slightly bad about it.
