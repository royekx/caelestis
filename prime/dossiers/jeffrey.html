Jeffrey
A bribed Sky Dock hand who thinks he is helping a friend smuggle, and the softest thread in the operation.

Jeffrey is a hand at the Sky Dock of Caelestis and a friend of Joffrey. He is paid to let certain manifests go unchecked and certain crates move at odd hours. He is bribed, not taken, and he is in over his head.

Appearance

A dock hand in work clothes, with the look of a man who has started sleeping badly.

Personality

Frightened, not venal. He said yes once and has been saying yes since. He believes he is doing a favour for a friend, a decorated officer who would not ask for anything serious, and that belief rests on something true: Joffrey is that man. He believes he can stop whenever he likes, and he has not tested it. He has begun to suspect the sums are too large for what he is being asked to do.

What's hidden

  • What he knows. He keeps a small secret inside someone else's large one. He knows which manifests, which hours and who asked. He does not know why, who else is involved or what moved, and he has no idea what his friend is.

How to run him

Joffrey cannot be caught by investigation, because every fact about him is true. Jeffrey can be caught by arithmetic. He is untrained, nobody is watching him, he has never had a cover story, and he would rather be caught than go deeper. What he hands over is a pattern of access with no explanation attached, and held against the Brig's classified routing it is the one piece of evidence that makes Joffrey legible. The crew leaning on him should feel slightly bad about it.
