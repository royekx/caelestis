Jeffrey
Bribed dock hand. In over his head, and the softest thread in the operation.

A Sky Dock hand and a friend of Joffrey's. He is the seam in the station's inventory handling — paid to let certain manifests go unchecked and certain crates move at odd hours.

Bribed rather than taken. He has no idea what his friend is, assumes he is helping with smuggling at worst, and has begun to suspect the sums are too large for what he is being asked to do.

The names are a real nuisance Joffrey and Jeffrey, on one station. It has already caused Mirt to point cadets at the wrong man, and the players' own quest board reads look into Joffrey and the man named Jeffrey. The confusion is working in the operation's favour without anyone having arranged it.

He believes he is doing a favour for a friend, and that the friend is a decorated officer who would not ask for anything serious. That belief is the entire mechanism of his compliance, and it is built on something true: Joffrey is that man.

He also believes he can stop whenever he likes. He has not tested it.

He is keeping a small secret inside someone else's large one. What he knows is real and shallow: which manifests, which hours, who asked. He does not know why, who else is involved, or what moved.

He is the thread that actually pulls Joffrey cannot be caught by investigation, because every fact about him is true. Jeffrey can be caught by arithmetic. He is already uneasy about the money. He is untrained, has nobody watching him, and has never had a cover story. He would rather be caught than get in any deeper.

Play him frightened rather than venal. He is not a criminal who got caught; he is a dock hand who said yes once and has been saying yes since, and the crew leaning on him should feel slightly bad about it.
