Captain Sardax
The campaign opened inside her last fight. Everything the crew believes about withdrawal came from watching her refuse to.

Bleeding across her own top deck, ordering cadets to hold and give no quarter. She went down giving that order, and the ship went down with her. The battle was already lost when she gave it.

Every cadet who has run the simulation has watched her die the same way, and the crew has since withdrawn on purpose more than once and counted it competence rather than failure. That reflex is Sardax’s, and they learned it by failing to save her.

The Moonraider was lost with her captain. The Academy keeps the battle as a simulation and runs cadets through it on their first day, which means the institution has decided what the loss is for.

What is settledWhat is open
She died. The ship was lost. The cadets relived it and could not change it.Whether the reavers were the whole of it, or whether a pirate boarding is what the Academy chose to show.
The Academy preserves it as a lesson about retreat.Whether that is the lesson the battle actually teaches, or the one the Academy wanted.
It is the crew’s shared founding memory.Who recorded it closely enough to reconstruct a deck, a wound and a dying order.
The recording is the thread A simulation that detailed requires a source. Either someone survived to describe it, or something was watching the Moonraider die closely enough to render it.

A survivor would be someone still alive who has carried this since before the current cadets enrolled. An observer puts the Watchers, or the Academy’s own instruments, at a battle the Academy presents as simple.

Hold the deck, give no quarter. She said it dying, to cadets, in a fight she had already lost. Read generously it is a captain refusing to let her crew’s last minutes be a rout. Read plainly it is the order that killed her.

The Academy agrees with the generous reading It teaches her as courage first and judgement second, which is a choice about what kind of officers it wants. The crew drew the opposite conclusion and the Academy has never corrected them. Whether that is tolerance or indifference is a live question about the institution.

She is a fixed point rather than an NPC. She cannot be met, talked to or saved, which is exactly what makes her useful: she is the one piece of history every player character experienced identically.

The opening set the register. Beginning inside a battle that could not be won said it in one scene — courage is assumed here, and the interesting question is knowing when to withdraw. Lean on that whenever a retreat is on the table.
