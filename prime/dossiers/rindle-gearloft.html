Rindle Gearloft
A rock gnome dockworker hiding the Latchling from the Academy, and the person standing between Gregory and his first anomaly.

Rindle Gearloft is a rock gnome who works a dock near the Sea Dock at Caelestis. He is hiding the Latchling, a paracausal entity, along the mooring struts of the aligner berths, and he is protective of it.

Appearance

Dark hair tied back, goggles pushed up, a vest crowded with tools. He is gruff and preoccupied until his attention is demanded, and guarded the moment anyone takes an interest in the aligner berths.

Personality

The Academy would take the Latchling apart; that is his whole reasoning, and he is right. He is not hiding it to study it or to sell it. He is hiding it because somebody has to, and he was the one who noticed. He is fair when he sees he has been unfair.

Biography

In the campaign
  • Voyage 2. Casey reached his dock by mistake while looking for the Sea Dock. Rindle barked orders and had him work cargo for the better part of an hour, then apologised when he learned a cadet had answered the refectory notice, and quietly paid Casey for the time.

Relationships

Gregory

The cadet whose anomaly the Latchling is. The Latchling goes to Gregory as something familiar.

Abilities

A rock gnome's hands for machinery and a dock worker's knowledge of every berth and strut, which is how the Latchling has stayed hidden.

What's hidden

  • The Latchling. He knows it is awake, he knows what happens to things like that on a Fleet installation, and he has told nobody. He is keeping a secret, not running a scheme.
  • What he does not know. Gregory's prologue repair of a latch may have been the local condition that let the ripple resolve into the Latchling at all. He is protecting something a cadet arguably caused.

How to run him

He is a social obstacle, not a hostile one, and the journey is the arc, since Gregory already knows roughly where he is going. Calibration delivers the location and nature of the anomaly; Rindle appears when somebody physically looks, and the discovery there is that someone already knew. When the Latchling goes to Gregory as something familiar, that visible reaction is what cracks Rindle's secrecy. Gregory trusts people at their word, and Rindle has reasons to shade the truth.
