Rindle Gearloft
Sea Dock worker. Hiding the Latchling, and the obstacle between Gregory and his first anomaly.

Sea Dock worker. Rock gnome — dark hair tied back, goggles pushed up, vest crowded with tools. Guarded in manner, and more so the moment anyone takes an interest in the aligner berths.

He is hiding the Latchling along the mooring struts, and he is protective of it.

He is on a different dock from the bribe Jeffrey works the Sky Dock, which is where the seam is and where the crew saw Joffrey's odd exchange. Rindle works the Sea Dock, two levels and one function away.

So there is no coincidence to over-read, and his secrecy is entirely about the Latchling. That is the cleaner version: a man being evasive at a dock, for a reason that has nothing to do with the thing the crew is actually hunting. They will suspect him anyway, and be wrong.

The academy would take it apart. That is his whole reasoning, and he is probably right — a thing that should have no sentience and has it is precisely what the Brig exists to absorb.

He is not hiding it to study it or to sell it. He is hiding it because somebody has to, and he is the one who noticed.

He knows it is awake, he knows what happens to things like that on a Fleet installation, and he has told nobody. That is the extent of it. He is keeping a secret rather than running a scheme.

What he does not know That Gregory's prologue repair of a latch may have been the local condition that let the ripple resolve into the Latchling at all. He is protecting something a cadet arguably caused.
He sits outside the Calibration mechanic entirely Calibration delivers the location and nature of the anomaly. Rindle appears when somebody physically looks — asks questions, pokes around the berths — and the discovery there is that someone already knew.

Calibration gets him to the dock. Rindle is what is waiting.

He is a social obstacle rather than a hostile one, and the arc is the journey rather than the destination, since Gregory already knows roughly where he is going. How the crew handles his is the content.
