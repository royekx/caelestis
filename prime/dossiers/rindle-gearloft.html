Rindle Gearloft
Sea Dock worker. Hiding the Latchling, and the obstacle between Gregory and his first anomaly.

Sea Dock worker. Rock gnome — dark hair tied back, goggles pushed up, vest crowded with tools. Guarded in manner, and more so the moment anyone takes an interest in the aligner berths.

She is hiding the Latchling along the mooring struts, and she is protective of it.

The dock she works is the one with the seam in it The Sea Dock is also where Jeffrey is being paid to look away, and where the network's material moves. She is on the same dock as an active bribe and has nothing to do with it — which is exactly the kind of coincidence a crew will over-read.

The academy would take it apart. That is her whole reasoning, and she is probably right — a thing that should have no sentience and has it is precisely what the Brig exists to absorb.

She is not hiding it to study it or to sell it. She is hiding it because somebody has to, and she is the one who noticed.

She knows it is awake, she knows what happens to things like that on a Fleet installation, and she has told nobody. That is the extent of it. She is keeping a secret rather than running a scheme.

What she does not know That Gregory's prologue repair of a latch may have been the local condition that let the ripple resolve into the Latchling at all. She is protecting something a cadet arguably caused.
She sits outside the Calibration mechanic entirely Calibration delivers the location and nature of the anomaly. Rindle appears when somebody physically looks — asks questions, pokes around the berths — and the discovery there is that someone already knew.

Calibration gets him to the dock. Rindle is what is waiting.

She is a social obstacle rather than a hostile one, and the arc is the journey rather than the destination, since Gregory already knows roughly where he is going. How the crew handles her is the content.
