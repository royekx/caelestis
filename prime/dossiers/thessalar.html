Thessalar
The Mad Mage, who experimented on N'ghathrod with the seventh Rod piece, learned to copy himself from the result, and is still in his fallen craft.

Thessalar is Halaster Blackcloak, the Mad Mage: once a high priest of Moloch, an artificer, and the master of a planar craft, a floating city of many tiers. About five hundred years ago he captured N'ghathrod and experimented on him with the seventh piece of the Rod of Seven Parts, and the taking began in his laboratory. The craft fell on Fonains, and its wreck is the Mad Mage's dungeon. He is still inside it, with his records and the seventh piece.

Appearance

A gaunt, ageing man in a black cloak, with the stained hands of someone who has worked at a bench for centuries. The people on the temple tier know him as a scholar, and he looks the part. Eight copies of him hang in sealed tubes in his laboratory.

Personality

He is curious before he is cruel. He took a result and went looking for what else it could do, and he keeps people for faculties he wants, as he kept N'ghathrod. He wants nothing smaller than to outlast death, and he pursues lichdom. Tharizdun's influence, reaching him through the craft's gems, drove him slowly mad.

Biography

Background

Thessalar was a high priest of Moloch, an exiled archdevil whose worship has faded, and an artificer. He killed the other priests and turned the Temple of Moloch, one of the craft's tiers, into his laboratory. The temple stands over caverns where Olhydra and Ogrémoch fought and tore a rift between the Planes of Water and Earth, and the stream there carries huge gems, rubies most of all. He made the thessalhydra, an owlbear and beasts like them, and the Thessalkraken, his lake monster. One of his assistants, Andrastolos, was split by his early experiments into two beings, Andras and Stolos.

The temple keeps his work. The mechanical heart Lola's Boys called “the chicken” is the thessalheart construct, which powers the thessalhydra and reports to him. Moloch's statue has for eyes the two largest rubies ever taken from the lake, wired with silver to every control panel in the laboratory. In a private chapel the Thessalkraken is worshipped as the temple's living demigod, before a statue of Moloch reworked with tentacles and needle teeth. An incomplete apparatus of Kwalish stands in the temple; it already existed in Thessalar's time.

About five hundred years ago N'ghathrod's ship clashed with the craft and lost. Thessalar and the Thessalkraken captured him and some of his illithids, and Thessalar experimented on him with the seventh Rod piece, keyed to simulacrum, before any copy of himself existed. It changed N'ghathrod's reproduction: his tadpoles became copies of him. The experiments also changed the five who became N'ghathrod's lieutenants, and Thessalar supplied the bodies from which the three unthinned were made. Thessalar took the finding and turned it on himself, and the eight numbered simulacra in his laboratory are the proof that it worked. The hazy yellow gem, one of the craft's control and defence gems, powered his work.

In the year 425 Lola's Boys, sent back in time by the Accordance, reached the craft. They moved through the laboratory while he was elsewhere aboard and took the craft's control and defence gems: the red gem from the cavern lake and the hazy yellow gem from his workshop. Without them the craft fell, about five hundred years ago, and in the crash N'ghathrod escaped. The craft broke apart as it fell. Its main body raised the Barrier Peaks, and its unexplored tiers are the Mad Mage's dungeon. A broken-off section became Daoine Gloine, and the Thessalkraken landed separately in the sea and became the Drowned God's kraken.

In the Fonains present his name lands on nothing. He is forgotten, which hides him better than anything he could have arranged.

In the campaign

The crew has not met him or heard his name.

Relationships

N'ghathrod

His first subject, and the prototype for his own continuation.

Tharizdun

One of her temples was aboard his craft. She wanted nothing from him but chaos, and her influence reached him through the craft's gems.

Abilities

He is an artificer who builds creatures and leaves them where they are useful, and a wizard who has copied himself eight times with what he learned from the seventh Rod piece. He keeps captives for their faculties; Seldra, who senses precious gems by feel, was one.

Notable items

  • The seventh Rod piece. A Consolidation piece keyed to simulacrum, the root of the taking. It is still aboard the wreck.
  • The hazy yellow gem. One of the craft's control and defence gems, formed where Olhydra and Ogrémoch collided, seated behind a panel in his workshop and fed by near-invisible silver wire into the walls. Lola's Boys took it.

What's hidden

  • What he knows. Every taken person in the cosmos exists because of the seventh piece. The colony has never worked it out, and N'ghathrod has never worked it out either. Thessalar is the one person who knows, and his records of the work on N'ghathrod are aboard with him.
  • The watcher. One of Lola's Boys, in the Ethereal Plane, saw a large eye with a purple slit iris pass over the party and vanish. It was the Elder Elemental Eye, Tharizdun, and Thessalar is tied to her through it. He is unlikely to know he was watched.

How to run him

He is a destination more than an antagonist, and the destination is a dungeon. His material was mined from the Fonains campaign, Lola's Boys sessions 11 to 13; it is continuity for this campaign, and the timing of the two tables stays separate. Reaching him means descending through tiers that came down with the craft and have been filling up ever since. Killing a numbered copy settles nothing; the original is the one at the bench. Keep him alive if the crew will let you: he could tell them what the colony is made of with none of the colony's beliefs attached. He would describe N'ghathrod as a result, and then ask what they wanted to know next.
