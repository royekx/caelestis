Winston Ryeback
Petty officer and galley cook, the Weeping Goddess

A muscular giff who runs the kitchen at the Weeping Goddess refectory, commanding the din with a bellow and feeding cadets with brusque affection. Beneath it he keeps hot baths and knitting, and warms to anyone who asks kindly.

He carries a hand cannon and is quick to use it. Short-barrelled, heavy, loud enough to be heard through a deck. He put a round through a clockwork horror's chest in the cargo hold with Sol beside him.

The academy thefts took a custom pistol out of his quarters. The hand cannon is what he has been carrying since, and he does not discuss the difference.

He went aboard the tyrant ship to cook and to command the deckhands, and has stayed in an advisory role since — a weather eye on the cadets rather than a hand on their work.

He wants the cadets fed and alive, in that order. He treats both as the same job and does not separate them.

He wants to be asked. The quickest route past his gruffness is a direct question about himself, and anyone who takes it gets an extra portion and the truth.

He does not want the mission. He asked the crew in the cargo hold whether they could finish it or whether he should send for Saerthe and Tarto, and he accepted their answer.

He backs the crew up at range and lets them make the decisions. That is the whole of it. He is on the deck, he is armed, and he will not take a scene off them.

He is CR 3 beside two instructors at CR 10 and 11, and that gap is the point. Ryeback is the adult the crew actually spends time with, and he can be hurt, which is a thing neither instructor usefully can.

He believes they killed the beholder He was told the beholder had been taken care of and took it to mean the crew killed it. His report to Caelestis says so, which is why Tarto arrives at the rendezvous with a question rather than a congratulation.

He is the one who tells them to lead with the good news. Use him to coach the crew on how a report lands before they give one, and let them decide whether to take the advice.

The giff stat block from Spelljammer Academy, with the Musket and Pistol lines replaced by the hand cannon he actually carries.
