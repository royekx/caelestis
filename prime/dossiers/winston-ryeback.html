Winston Ryeback
A giff galley cook with a bellow, a hand cannon and a soft spot for cadets who ask about his knitting.

Winston Ryeback is a giff, a petty officer and the galley cook of the Weeping Goddess, the Academy refectory at Caelestis. He commands the din with a bellow and feeds cadets with brusque affection. He went aboard the Tyrant Ship to cook and command the deckhands, and has kept a weather eye on the cadets since.

Appearance

A muscular giff, hippo-headed and broad, in a breastplate over his apron when there is trouble. A short-barrelled, heavy hand cannon rides on his hip, with a longsword beside it.

Personality

Gruff on the surface, warm underneath. Beneath the bellow he keeps hot baths and knitting, and he warms to anyone who asks kindly; a direct question about himself gets an extra portion and the truth. He wants the cadets fed and alive, in that order, and treats both as the same job. He does not want the mission. He will not take a scene off the crew.

Biography

Background

Ryeback has run the Weeping Goddess kitchen for years. The Academy thefts took his custom pistol out of his quarters. The hand cannon is his own gun, the sidearm he still has, and it is why he backs the crew up at closer range than he used to. His sign is the Feast of the Moon, Sagittarius.

In the campaign
  • Voyage 2. He ran the kitchen at a bellow, served the crew with brusque affection, and took their special orders: a silvery starfruit for Boogie, his knock-your-mama's-socks-off soup for Sol, two meatloafs for Tumak, spare utensils for Gregory. He mentioned a cadet named Barret talking about a hadozee who could read birthdays, and warmed to Casey's questions about hot baths and knitting.
  • Voyage 3. He served a subdued Academy the evening after the explosion, then joined the tyrant-ship crew, saluting alongside Tarto, to cook and command the deckhands.
  • Voyages 4 and 5. Aboard the Tyrant Ship, he named the wisps for Miken, set up a camp kitchen, reminded the crew that the helm was their problem, and stayed above with Sol, Pffred and Krik'Lit to guard it.
  • Voyage 6. He shot a clockwork horror in the chest with his hand cannon while Sol fought beside him in the cargo hold, and asked whether the crew could finish the mission or whether he should call Saerthe and Tarto. Tumak told him outright that the crew had killed the beholder. After the behir was ejected he said, “Nice job, cadets,” and told them to lead with the good news.

Abilities

A brawler and a shot. He fires the hand cannon as fast as he can bring it level, charges headfirst to knock a foe flat, and keeps one fragmentation grenade for a bad day.

Notable items

  • Hand cannon. Short-barrelled, heavy, and loud enough to be heard through a deck.
  • His custom pistol. Stolen from his quarters in the Academy thefts.

Quotes

  • “Nice job, cadets.”

What's hidden

  • His report. He believes the crew killed the beholder, because Tumak told him so outright, and his report to Caelestis says so. That is why Tarto arrives at the rendezvous with a question instead of a congratulation. The beholder is Vaelorix.

How to run him

He backs the crew up at range and lets them decide. He is CR 3 beside two instructors at CR 10 and 11, and that gap is the point: he is the adult the crew actually spends time with, and he can be hurt. Use him to coach the crew on how a report lands before they give one, and let them choose whether to take the advice.

Ryeback lives, and no death is planned for him. His death belonged to Fire in the Galley, which is cut; Miken's sabotage became a beacon planted for Qitru, and Ryeback came through unhurt.
