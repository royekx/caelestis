Ezra Ahl-Sier
An astral elf Glimpse of the Order, near the end of a century of training, travelling with a Caelestis crew to finish it on H'Catha.

Ezra Ahl-Sier, the All-Seer, is an astral elf and a Glimpse of the Order of the All-Father: a trainee who has seen part of what the All-Father sees and has not yet been knighted a Watcher. He has trained for about a century in the Hall of Sight and Sound, the great hall within the Order's seat, and he is among the youngest batch of Glimpses. He is a paladin of the Oath of the Watchers. He joins a Caelestis crew on the voyage to H'Catha to finish his field training under Nereth Vaul, the Watcher posted there.

Appearance

A young astral elf whose eyes are like the Astral Sea. He carries a shield bearing the Order's device, a white tower with a blue-violet eye at its centre, the tower's outline slightly wrong. When he casts, his eyes flare and his magic renders as violet light and cosmic dust.

Personality

He has a wanderlust that pulls him toward a posting and away from home, and a lesson for every situation, drawn from watching nature. He holds the natural world above everything built by civilization. The enemy of his enemy is, to him, a temporary ally. He will not save those who cannot save themselves: it is nature's way that the strong thrive.

He holds the Watchers' charge sincerely and incompletely, and he has not yet had to apply it to anything he cares about. Like the Order's other juniors he takes aphorisms literally, and he will do the same with anything the crew tells him. He introduces himself plainly, “Ezra Ahl-Sier, from the Order of the Watchers,” and says “All-Father be with them” over people he is about to shield.

Biography

Background

Ezra has trained in the Hall of Sight and Sound for about a century. A Glimpse can be sent into the field to finish training under a Watcher already posted there, and his field mission is H'Catha. At his knighting a Glimpse is christened a Watcher, and a new Watcher takes a posting to a region of a world. He speaks Celestial, Common and Deep Speech.

In the campaign

He joins the crew at the swap, when the Flighty Foundling comes alongside the Tyrant Ship, and travels with them to H'Catha. He arrives informed: he can say who he is meeting and why, and he will, which makes him the first Watcher the crew can simply ask things of. The crew leaves him on H'Catha, where his training continues. Later a Caelestis crew collects him at H'Catha and carries him to the Hall, the last troop he travels with before his journey as a Watcher begins.

Relationships

Nereth Vaul

H'Catha's Watcher and his instructor, posted at the rim fog. She trades intelligence and protection with Ondessa, a beholder ruler whom the other rulers want dead, and she filed the report on the meteor strike.

Captain-Commander Dejexic

Leader of the Watchers and speaker of the All-Father, a purplish-blue plasmoid in obsidian armour that reflects tiny swirling galaxies. He floats, barely raises his voice, and silences a hall by entering it. Ezra answers to him.

Abilities

A paladin of the Oath of the Watchers. He senses celestials, fiends and undead, heals by laying on hands, and smites. His Channel Divinity steadies him and his allies against being reached into, and he can turn the extraplanar away. He steps through starlight, fights by blindsight at close range, and cannot be put to sleep by magic. A larger divine figure moves behind him when he smites.

What's hidden

  • Sol. As a Glimpse, Ezra knows nothing of the Order's break with protocol over Sol Fortuna.
  • His first thought about a suffering world. Waiting to be knighted, he dreads one posting above the rest, a world in continuous strife, and privately thinks an extraplanar entity wiping its mortals out would be a blessing in disguise. He catches himself and goes back to idly picking a method: an extinction-level meteor, or a syzygy to raise a wave. The doctrine of watching without intervening is permission he has already been glad of, and he has never said that thought aloud.

How to run him

He is the parallel thread that pulls a PC away at the wrong moment: his objectives at H'Catha are his order's, and they will not always point the crew's way. He is the open door into an order the crew has only seen closed. Ask him and he answers as far as he understands, which is not far. On a world where beholders are about to fight over a meteor the crew was sent to take, his doctrine gets tested in front of them; the interesting failure is that he might agree with it.

His look is the Order's register, not his own: violet light, cosmic dust, something larger behind the caster. Any Watcher the crew meets should read the same way. In play on Emporia he has used Divine Sense, Shield of Faith, Lay on Hands, Divine Smite and a Channel Divinity granting him and three others advantage on Wisdom, Intelligence and Charisma saves.

His arc

Caelestis is Ezra's prequel. Ezra is a player character in the campaign set on Emporia, run by one of this campaign's players; Caelestis shows him earlier, before that campaign begins, and treats him as transit, so the crew never visits his homeworld. His knighting, his posting and his allies all come after the crew leaves him on H'Catha.

  • The knighting. In the Hall of Sight and Sound, Dejexic puts a hand on his shoulder and says, unprompted: “Birds are vigilant creatures. Some can even see in the dark and have a near 360 field of view. Watch the birds and they'll tell you all you need.” It is advice about vigilance from a man who speaks in maxims, and Ezra takes it as a literal instruction. This is the scene in the backstory document “Ezra Ahl-Sier”. The meteor he idly picks while waiting is H'Catha remembered: whatever the crew does there is what he thinks about at his own knighting.
  • The posting. His posting is Emporia, the world he was hoping to be spared; the unnamed world in the backstory document is Emporia. He reaches it without the crew.
  • His allies. On Emporia his allies are Valance, Irohden and Borgor. None of them has met him during this campaign.
  • The birds. He followed birds to his posting. On Emporia he guarded a nearly mute child and buffed a ritual that read a cursed crown. A small black bird with grey feathers and very large eyes spoke to him with someone else's voice, called the party visitors and him “watchers, quite the pest”. Its open mouth held four landscapes of the same world and pulled like gravity. He refused to leave the child, chose the water, and came up on the sand of an island called Hulila, leaving a figure of dancing light of himself, shield before her, so she would know he had not walked away.

Threads

  • The Watcher Transport, from H'Catha to the Hall, is a Grade IV contract on the crew's graduation board.
  • The Hall transport mirrors the other table: the crew is the last troop he travels with before his journey begins.

Still to decide

  • Whether Dejexic knew what the birds would lead to is not set.
  • What the Watchers want him to learn at H'Catha, and how much of what the Order knows about Zeniths has been passed to him, are not set.
  • Whether Emporia's trouble is a Zenith with Tharizdun behind it is not set, and may stay the other table's.
