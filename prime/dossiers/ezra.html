Ezra
A Watcher in training, joining the crew for his field work.

A Watcher in training, assigned to a field rendezvous with a Fleet vessel for in-field instruction. He joins the crew at the swap, collected by the Flighty Foundling once they report the tyrant ship taken, and travels on with them to H'Catha to finish his training.

He arrives as a peer rather than an authority — junior enough to be questioned, senior enough to know things the crew does not.

Why he exists in the plot The crew swap needed a reason, and he is it. His training is what the rendezvous is for, which means the reinforcement arrives as an arrangement rather than a rescue, and the crew keeps the credit for having handled the ship themselves.

He holds the Watchers' position, incompletely and sincerely: that what happens to worlds is observed, recorded and not interfered with. He has not yet had to apply that to anything he cares about.

He is the order's doctrine before it has been tested, which makes him the right person to voice it and the wrong person to defend it.

What he is walking into There is already a Watcher at H'Catha, surviving at the rim fog alongside an enlightened beholder exile — and she reported the meteorite strike that started the whole dispute. His field work has an instructor he may not know about yet.

Open: what the Watchers actually want him to learn, whether he knows what the order knows about Zeniths, and what he does the first time observing costs someone their life.

He is the crew's first Watcher who is reachable. Mirt and Saerthe let things slip; Ezra can be asked directly, and will answer as far as he understands it, which is not very far.

Run him as the parallel thread that pulls a PC away at the wrong moment — his objectives at H'Catha are his order's rather than the crew's, and they will not always point the same direction.

The useful tension He is the one person aboard whose job is to watch a disaster and let it happen. On a world where two beholders are about to fight over a meteorite the crew has been sent to take, that doctrine is going to be tested in front of them.
