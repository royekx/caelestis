Quartermaster Halix Verrow
Strake Station's quartermaster, who files Tarroway's reports up the chain.

Quartermaster Halix Verrow runs supply on Strake Station and files the station's reports up the chain, the cartographer M. Tarroway's among them. He sent up Tarroway's first filing on Cairn Station's lane, the one on which the Fleet sent Bellwether Crow to look in on Cairn, and every filing after it. The later ones were set aside once Crow reported Cairn in order.

Appearance

A seated station officer, dressed closer to a uniform than most Driftwardens bother with, his rank on a pin.

Personality

A signature, to him, is a record of who is answerable, so whatever he signs and sends up he stands behind afterward. He keeps his filings in order and on time. He holds no private theory about Cairn's lane and does not want one; the reading belongs to the office that receives what he sends.

Biography

Background

Verrow counter-signs Strake's manifests and files its reports. Tarroway's notations ride on those manifests, and his filings go up the chain under Verrow's signature, so the record of when each one left Strake is his.

In the campaign

The crew has not met him.

How to run him

He is the paper trail behind Tarroway. A crew that reaches Strake can learn from him when each of Tarroway's filings went up, that the first one sent Crow, and that the later ones were set aside after its report. Bridge authorisation, or a reason that would survive a review, gets the crew his copies. Charm gets them nothing, and lying to him makes an enemy who keeps records.
