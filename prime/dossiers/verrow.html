Quartermaster Halix Verrow
Strake Station's quartermaster, who counter-signs everything that moves on the edge run and will not let anyone past the standing order.

Quartermaster Halix Verrow runs supply on Strake Station and counter-signs everything that moves on the edge run to Cairn, which makes him both the obstacle and the paper trail. He enforces the standing order that has closed the run since the Bellwether Crow came back, and he holds the seal on the station's secure storage.

Appearance

A seated station officer, dressed closer to a uniform than most Driftwardens bother with, his rank on a pin.

Personality

A standing order is a standing order. He did not write SA-005, and he will not be the one who let someone past it. He has no private theory about the edge and does not want one. A signature, to him, is a record of who is answerable, so he is hard to move, and whatever he signs he stands behind afterward.

Biography

Background

Verrow counter-signed Cairn's supply manifests and crew rotations through Strake, and his margin notes in the station's filings record the long scope drifting week after week before Cairn went silent. Everything the crew would need for Cairn is on Strake, catalogued and staged, and the route to it runs through his signature.

In the campaign

The crew has not met him.

Relationships

M. Tarroway

Strake's cartographer, whose notations ride on the manifests Verrow signs.

What's hidden

  • The seal. The item under seal in his secure storage is the one thing on the station he will say nothing about.
  • The hazard. The thing the closure answers to has moved on; Vth'oramu left the Cairn bearing a year ago. He does not know.

How to run him

He is the honest lock on the door: a problem authorisation solves and persuasion does not. He can be out-ranked, out-papered, or given a reason that would survive a review. Charm does nothing, and lying to him makes an enemy who keeps records.

ApproachResult
Bridge authorisationImmediate compliance, and he helps.
A written reason that would survive reviewHe considers it, and says so plainly either way.
Persuasion, charm, urgencyNothing.
Forgery or theftWorks once, and he notices afterward. He is the one who files it.

A crew that gets him on side gets the one thing a trip to Cairn otherwise lacks: a record that they were authorised. Without it, surviving Cairn and coming back with the truth still leaves them explaining why they were there.
