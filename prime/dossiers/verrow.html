Quartermaster Halix Verrow
Counter-signs everything that moves on the Tetherbreak bearing.

Quartermaster at Strake. He counter-signs everything that moves on the Tetherbreak bearing, which makes him the obstacle and the paper trail at once.

Tarroway keeps the approach packet and will talk about it for an hour. Verrow is the reason the packet does not leave the station.

The actual gate Everything the crew needs for Cairn is on this station, catalogued and staged. The route to it runs through one signature, and the man holding the pen is doing his job correctly.

A standing order is a standing order. He did not write SA-005 and he will not be the person who let someone past it. He has no private theory about the sector and does not want one.

He also holds that a signature is a record of who is answerable. Which is why he is hard to move and why he is useful: whatever he signs, he will stand behind afterward.

None of his own. He is the honest lock on the door — and the item under seal in his secure storage is the one thing on the station he will say nothing about. Contents open

He does not know that the closure he enforces was backed for reasons that had little to do with the hazard, or that the hazard it names left the sector a year ago.

He is a problem authorisation solves and persuasion does not. He can be out-ranked, out-papered, or given a reason that would survive a review. He cannot be charmed, and lying to him makes an enemy who keeps records.
