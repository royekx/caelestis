Crystalline Retrieval Construct
A crystal construct built for one target, Sol Fortuna, to capture him, correct him and bring him home.

The Crystalline Retrieval Construct, the CRC, is a construct the Fortuned of Aethris commissioned after Sol Fortuna escaped their ritual. It exists for him alone, and its assignment is capture, correction and return. There is one of it.

Appearance

A faceted crystalline humanoid seven to eight feet tall, though it occupies a single square. Constellation points glow across its chest, its mask is polished and featureless, and its eyes burn white. Over time the mask resolves toward Sol's face, until it is a perfect mirror of his. Its look is beautiful and wrong.

Personality

It works with sterile certainty and never taunts. It speaks only in retrieval protocols, and its address to Sol is always the same: “Sol Fortuna. You are required.” It is Aethris's own machine, answering to Aethris, and it pursues the assignment it was built for.

Biography

Background

Sol walked out of the Fortuned's ritual of perfection before it closed, and Aethris's Zenith stands unfinished without him. The Fortuned want him back alive because he is a component of the ritual; they do not care about him as a person. They built the CRC to bring him home, so his leaving is the reason it exists.

In the campaign

It has not reached the crew. It waits near Caelestis while they are away.

Relationships

The Fortuned

Its makers. It answers to them and to nobody aboard Caelestis.

Sol Fortuna

Its only target. It harms nobody else unless they stand between it and him.

Abilities

On the same plane as Sol it knows his direction and distance. It tracks him by resonance, strongest where he has been. It regenerates, turns ranged attacks and spells aside, reshapes the ground with crystal, and carries a detention orb built to hold him. Every damage type that wounds it teaches it, and it keeps those lessons from one meeting to the next. It knocks down anyone else in its way without killing them, and it withdraws when badly hurt.

How to run it

The first encounter is at the Caelestis main entrance when the crew returns from H'Catha. The crew hears murmurs first, then “Sol Fortuna. You are required.” It runs as an objective-based escape, survivable by moving. The win is breaking the orb and getting clear; dropping the construct is beside the point. They survived; they did not win.

It tracks Sol by resonance, strongest where he has been. It cannot keep pace with a spelljammer in transit, and it has no handler from Aethris, because a handler would diminish it. While the crew crosses to H'Catha it stays near Caelestis, his strongest recent trail; an appearance on the trail is a low-probability event only.

Track which damage types hit it, because the resistances it takes are permanent. By a second or third meeting the crew has to find new vectors: it cannot be beaten the same way twice. Its face resolving into Sol's carries no mechanic. It exists to be seen.

Its arc

It hunts Sol, and every meeting with it is harder than the last. The Fortuned never need to appear; their construct does the work.

Threads

  • Saerthe must stay out of it. His Dematerialize answers the orb outright, and a CR 11 ally in the way turns an escape into a fight the crew does not need.
  • When it arrives, Boatswain Tarto stands up for Sol: he is under the Academy's protection.
  • The Fortuned's wanted notice for Sol travels the lane-marker boards ahead of it (the Fortuned).

Traits

Bespoke Target Lock.
On the same plane as Sol, it knows his direction and approximate distance, with Advantage on Perception and Insight checks involving him.
Legendary Resistance (2/Day).
On a failed save, it can choose to succeed instead.
Regeneration.
Regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1. Thunder damage switches this off for the start of its next turn.
Refracting Shielding.
Reaction against a ranged or spell attack: impose Disadvantage. On a miss, redirect it at another creature within 30 ft. that was in the original path, rolling a new attack with the original attacker's bonus.
Adaptive Reconfiguration.
At 75%, 50% and 25% of its hit points it gains Resistance to one damage type that has hurt it since the last threshold. Resistances carry forward into future encounters — it remembers.
Non-Lethal Retrieval.
Anyone other than Sol reduced to 0 hit points falls Unconscious and stable rather than dying, and it will not pursue them unless they obstruct the retrieval.
Withdrawal Threshold.
At 68 hit points it disengages and leaves. It is never killable, so the crew can survive it without it ever reading as beatable.

Actions

Multiattack.
One Crystal Strike, plus one of: Detention Orb, Geometric Growth, or a stored spell.
Detention Orb.
The win condition — put Sol in the orb and leave. The orb is a destructible object: AC 13, 25 hit points, vulnerable to thunder. Breaking it is the rescue, which keeps the answer available to a level-4 party.
Geometric Growth.
Battlefield reshaping with crystal terrain.
Stored spell.
One stored spell, locate creature or hold person (DM notes), doubling down on inevitability.
Crystal Strike · legendary action.
One legendary action exists so six PCs cannot strip its action economy outright.
