Crystalline Retrieval Construct
Bespoke retrieval unit, commissioned by the Fortuned

"Sol Fortuna. You are required."

Commissioned by the Fortuned of Aethris after Sol's escape, which makes it bespoke: his leaving is the reason it exists. Its assignment is capture, correction and return.

Operates with sterile certainty and a beautiful-yet-wrong aesthetic, and never taunts. Aethris's own machine, answering to Aethris, pursuing the assignment it was built for.

There is one of it. It stands seven to eight feet tall and occupies a single square. Its face progressively resolves into a perfect mirror of Sol's — visual only, with no mechanic attached, so the horror costs the player nothing.

Open Where the first encounter happens. The Sky Dock on arrival — off the ship, in the open, before anyone knows they are back — is the stronger scene, and it removes the warded-interior objective, so it needs something else to run toward.

Traits

Bespoke Target Lock.
On the same plane as Sol, it knows his direction and approximate distance, with Advantage on Perception and Insight checks involving him.
Legendary Resistance (2/Day).
On a failed save, it can choose to succeed instead.
Regeneration.
Regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1. Thunder damage switches this off for the start of its next turn.
Refracting Shielding.
Reaction against a ranged or spell attack: impose Disadvantage. On a miss, redirect it at another creature within 30 ft. that was in the original path, rolling a new attack with the original attacker's bonus.
Adaptive Reconfiguration.
At 75%, 50% and 25% of its hit points it gains Resistance to one damage type that has hurt it since the last threshold. Resistances carry forward into future encounters — it remembers.
Non-Lethal Retrieval.
Anyone other than Sol reduced to 0 hit points falls Unconscious and stable rather than dying, and it will not pursue them unless they obstruct the retrieval.
Withdrawal Threshold.
At 68 hit points it disengages and leaves. It is never killable, so the crew can survive it without it ever reading as beatable.

Actions

Multiattack.
One Crystal Strike, plus one of: Detention Orb, Geometric Growth, or a stored spell.
Detention Orb.
The win condition — put Sol in the orb and leave. The orb is a destructible object: AC 13, 25 hit points, vulnerable to thunder. Breaking it is the rescue, which keeps the answer available to a level-4 party.
Geometric Growth.
Battlefield reshaping with crystal terrain.
Stored spell.
Locate creature, doubling down on inevitability.
Crystal Strike · legendary action.
One legendary action exists so six PCs cannot strip its action economy outright. Numbers in the working file
Run it as an escape Built to retrieve rather than kill, so the first encounter is objective-based and survivable by moving. The win is breaking the orb and getting clear, not dropping the construct. They survived; they did not win.

Track which damage types hit it, because the resistances it takes are permanent. By a second or third meeting the crew has to find new vectors, which is the point: you cannot beat it the same way twice.
