Voyage 005 — Fire in the Galley → Behold H'Catha Upcoming
Briefing — Planned

Cold open: Fire in the Galley. Held back twice now so it would land rather than be rushed. Miken sets it with two scrolls — a 4th-level glyph of warding on Ryeback's portable cooking surface plus conjure minor elementals — and the glyph fires when Ryeback lights the stove. Magma mephits spread it. The crew descends into ten-foot visibility and smoke inhalation, DC 10 Constitution per turn with advantage behind a damp cloth.

Investigation at DC 13 establishes that the stove could not have done this, which points at the only cadet aboard with magic. Detect thoughts or a DC 13 Intimidation breaks him: recruited by a mercane, two scrolls, money for a young fiance, and he expected Ryeback hurt rather than killed. He surrenders.

Then the helm goes into the gaze cradle, the ship flies, and the crew makes H'Catha to recover the adamantine meteor. Sol's first CRC encounter sits in this window as an objective-based escape: "Sol Fortuna. You are required."

Voyage 004 — The Tyrant Complete
Briefing — Planned

The redesigned tyrant ship dungeon, aboard the Tyrant. The dungeon was rebuilt around a want-bearing agent after the original version was diagnosed as having none: every pressure in it had been an impersonal timer rather than something that noticed the crew and responded.

The answer was a clockwork behir stranded aboard, sent by Ostekk to retrieve the Engine Vaelorix stole, and unable to reach it because the Command Deck is an anti-magic field that unmakes constructs. The crew can cross that field; the behir cannot. Both need it cleared, for different purposes and different destinations.

Planned beats: the bloom-lock shafts, the behir's abduction of two PCs to the Command Deck threshold as forced recon, the Garden, the clockwork horrors, Wizpop's repair, and the helm installed at the finale.

Delta — what to bring forward, what to scrap

Carry forward. The helm is in the crew's possession and uninstalled, so the next session opens with an objective they already hold the means to finish. Vaelorix, the Lantern and the anti-magic field are untouched. The behir's business with the Engine is unresolved.

Settled at the table and worth keeping. The construct distinction now has a ruling: automatons such as the OSTEKK units are affected by the anti-magic field without being frozen by it, because their construction is paracausal; autognomes such as Gregory are unaffected and paracausally sensitive. That is a clean, reusable line and it reinforces his arc without a word of explanation.

Deliver as cleanup rather than table time. The prep corrections that did not reach play — clockwork horror HP at 30, the Faelight spore relocated to the Garden, OSTEKK-6's parts check standardized, the lattice-flower cover at +3 — were decided to go out as out-of-session notes.

The pacing lesson, twice confirmed. Fire in the Galley has now been deferred out of two consecutive sessions. It is the emotional climax of the Miken and Ryeback thread, and the risk is no longer that it gets rushed — it is that the fuse goes cold across a long dungeon crawl. It should open the next session rather than waiting for a third window.

Delta — what to bring forward, what to scrap

What this session taught about the table. They work an investigation thoroughly and expansively, and they expand scenes you planned as short — the Rindle detour and the Sky Dock rail in V002, the desk snoop here. Budget investigation acts at roughly double and put the compressible scene last on purpose rather than by accident.

Reframed, not scrapped. Sol's recognition moves out of a formal institutional commendation and surfaces through Ryeback instead, which is warmer and does more work. Miken was rebuilt as a leveraged character wrestling with guilt rather than a concealed antagonist, so every friendly beat he gets is honest rather than a feint.

Owed. Sol's recognition in some form, the sailor-access inventory, and Ryeback warmth banked before the galley. Gregory's Latchling beat was deliberately held so the Wizpop repair could carry his arc instead.

Naming, settled. Vocath was named to the players directly. The preference is clarity over withheld mystery, and the real mystery layers stay intact regardless — the chart theft, Cairn, and the two-layer saboteur structure are all untouched by the players knowing a name.

Delta — what to bring forward, what to scrap

The miss worth remembering. Gregory's beat was crowded out — Casey occupied the Sea Dock scene and the autognome moment never arrived. Two PCs with business in the same room means one of them loses, so place arc beats in separate scenes rather than separate moments within one.

Carried forward and paid off. The Miken-adrift cliffhanger set the pattern of ending on a question rather than a resolution, which the table responds to. Sol heard Beshaba for the first time, so small misfortune around him now reads as attended to rather than random.

What the session was actually for. Seating the sixth cadet without the crew having to decide to include him, and making Caelestis feel lived-in. Both landed, and the Ryeback and Miken warmth banked here is the capital the galley spends.

Delta — what to bring forward, what to scrap

Everything seeded here is still live. Four PCs left with a personal breadcrumb, and three of those threads remain open: Casey's first directional clue toward Viren, Boogie's half-ship, and the wardrobe. Tumak's theft of Mirt's bag went unexamined and is available whenever it is useful.

The atmosphere decision that paid off most. Running the academy's burglary problem as baseline texture rather than a plot announcement means the crew learned the thefts were real before anyone framed them as a case. Everything the network does afterward lands on ground the players already accept.

The lesson the opening taught. Starting inside Sardax's doomed battle set the campaign's register in one scene: courage is assumed, and the interesting question is knowing when to withdraw. The crew has since withdrawn twice on purpose — the cargo deck in V003 and the tyrant ship in V004 — which suggests the lesson took.
