Setting·The Continuum

Cosmology

What is true, and what runs whether anyone is watching. The structural rules underneath every world, every faction, every player choice.

The Prime Continuum

The Prime Continuum is the total structure of existence. It encompasses all planes, realities, timelines, divine realms, and metaphysical systems. Everything that exists does so within the Prime Continuum.

The Prime Continuum is not sentient, moral, or intentional. It does not dictate outcomes, favor particular states, or distinguish between good and evil. It exists as the framework within which reality persists.

It is self-regulating. It rises and falls, threatens and stabilizes, archives and forgets — all according to its own structural pressures, on its own clocks, without needing anyone to be paying attention. The cosmology was here before any of its current beings and will be here after them. Antagonists, however clever, are beings inside it.

The Prime Continuum requires equilibrium to remain stable. Equilibrium is not balance, symmetry, or peace. It is the dynamic tension between foundational structural forces operating within acceptable tolerances. Conflict, catastrophe, and extreme states may all exist without threatening equilibrium. Only instability beyond recovery threatens the Continuum itself.

The Prime Continuum currently maintains equilibrium through four Axioms. The interaction of those Axioms produces Continuity.

Useful distinction The Prime Continuum is the field. The four Axioms are the principles that maintain it. Continuity is what their equilibrium produces. The three concepts are different things; the rest of this page depends on keeping them straight.

Continuity

The Prime Continuum is all of existence. It is the cosmos: everything that exists. Continuity is what the four Axioms produce within the Prime Continuum. In practice the two terms are interchangeable; both are existence. "Continuity" is the precise term for the equilibrium product. Everyday speech calls either one Existence.

Continuity is the emergent result of the Four Axioms operating in equilibrium. It represents the ongoing persistence of reality itself.

Continuity is not sentient. It does not make decisions, formulate plans, or possess desires. However, it possesses function. When equilibrium is placed under sufficient strain, Continuity produces structural responses intended to preserve the persistence of existence. Monoliths are one such response.

If an Axiom is altered, fractured, replaced, or reconfigured, Continuity changes with it. Existence persists, but the nature of reality changes because the equilibrium producing it has changed. The current form of Continuity is not eternal. It is the form existence has taken since the Reach, the event that reconfigured reality and began this cosmos.

The Axioms

The four foundational structural principles through which the Prime Continuum currently maintains equilibrium. They emerged from necessity rather than creation and predate all known gods and civilizations. Together they are the universe. The Continuum is what the four of them produce between them, and there is nothing underneath it to find. To know the universe, in the only sense the word can bear, is to be one of them.

The Axioms are sentient, and constrained entirely by their function. Each possesses a limited personality and perspective shaped by its governing principle. No Axiom can act contrary to its own nature. Four minds that cannot choose.

The seat A mind that could choose, sitting in one of these four positions, would be a categorically different thing from what has ever occupied it — and everything that Axiom governs would thereafter pass through a will with preferences. Nothing in the structure forbids it. Nothing in the structure has ever been tested for it.
Convergence Locked Most stressed

Convergence governs escalation toward extremity. Systems trend toward consolidation, centralization, and peak expression. Power accumulates, conflicts intensify, and threshold states become reachable.

Zenith Events occur as a result of Convergence driving a system toward maximum expression. Every Zenith — every cultural apex, every magical saturation, every civilizational transcendence — is Convergence doing what Convergence does.

Currently the most stressed Axiom Because Zenith Events are products of Convergence, and because each Zenith places strain on equilibrium, an environment of repeated Zenith Events accumulates strain disproportionately within Convergence — and through Convergence, on the Continuity it helps sustain. Convergence is presently the Axiom bearing the most pressure.
Dissolution Locked

Dissolution governs decay, fragmentation, and dispersal. Structures break down over time, concentrations disperse, and extremes become unstable. Dissolution prevents permanence and reduces accumulated intensity within systems.

Dissolution is the counterweight to Convergence. Where Convergence builds toward peak expression, Dissolution prevents that peak from becoming permanent. A reality with only Convergence would crystallize into rigid maximum states; a reality with only Dissolution would never accumulate enough to form anything in the first place.

Boundary Locked

Boundary governs separation and distinction. It maintains the differentiation between planes, identities, concepts, and structures. Boundary preserves limits and prevents uncontrolled merging or collapse into uniformity.

Boundary is why a soul is distinguishable from a body, a plane from another plane, a self from a not-self. Without Boundary, the categories that make reality navigable would not hold.

Transformation Locked

Transformation governs state change. Entities and systems transition between conditions without requiring annihilation. Transformation enables adaptation, evolution, and conversion while preventing stagnation.

Transformation is the Axiom by which a mortal becomes a god, a city becomes a ruin, a winter becomes a spring. Change without destruction. Boundary keeps things separate; Transformation lets them become something else without ceasing to be.

Axiom Fracture

An Axiom may fracture under sufficient structural strain. This is the only known mechanism by which the foundational configuration of the Prime Continuum can change without external cause. Fracture occurs in two phases.

The Shock produces immediate destabilization throughout the Prime Continuum — planar instability, magical irregularities, divine disturbances, Astral anomalies, widespread metaphysical disruptions. The Shock is sudden, but it is not the catastrophe itself. It is the announcement of one.

The Unraveling occurs over an extended period. During this phase the fractured Axiom continues to function, but increasingly imperfectly. Instability propagates from higher metaphysical layers downward through divine, planar, and eventually mortal systems. The Axiom's governing principle persists but gradually weakens until replacement, reformation, or redistribution of function occurs. The vacancy cannot persist indefinitely; the Prime Continuum will eventually establish a new equilibrium. When that occurs, Continuity changes accordingly. Existence does not end — but what it means for a thing to exist becomes different than what it meant before.

Why this matters Axiom Fracture is the door by which a being inside the Continuum could position themselves to influence — or become part of — the new equilibrium. Anyone who could engineer a Fracture and arrange to be the replacement-function for the vacated Axiom would become structural rather than merely powerful.

Paracausality

Paracausal = the reality of a thing has been changed. Not whether it exists — how it exists. The word means operating outside normal cause-and-effect. It is an Axiom-level concern: a thing whose place in the structure of reality has been altered.

Paracausality and the Weave are orthogonal. Paracausality describes what changed. The Weave governs why anti-magic does or does not suppress. They are separate axes and a thing can sit anywhere on both.

Paracausality vs. the Weave

The Weave is the fabric mortals reach into to cast spells. Anti-magic severs local Weave access — it doesn't delete energy. The consequences fall out cleanly:

  • Weave-acts (spellcasting) are suppressed inside anti-magic.
  • A spell slot is innate stored energy. It persists inside anti-magic — it just can't be cast.
  • Innate, non-Weave processes (flowers' anti-magic emission, wisps' draining feed, the counter-spore plant's spore-conversion) are not suppressed, which is why a flower's own emission survives inside the field it creates.
Analogy Energy is underground water — the same for all. The Weave is the well mortals use to reach it. Anti-magic caps the well. A plant with non-Weave roots has its own path to the same water — but a conduit alone has no reserves; it still needs a caster to supply a spell slot. The caster has the water; the plant has the roots.
Counter-spore plant specification
  • Fuel with a spell slot (non-Weave conduit — works in anti-magic field).
  • Bonus action to activate.
  • The slot is the cost; slot level scales area and duration.
  • At L3, caps at 2nd-level slot (~two rungs of scaling).
  • Innate obscurity-clear — faerie-fire-like, not a cast spell.
  • Caster-only.

Other non-Weave flora and fauna exist; this is one specification of a wider category.

How paracausal change manifests

Paracausal change is rare and individual. A being or object becomes paracausal when its place in the cosmological structure has been altered along some axis — typically not at the being's own initiative.

Common forms of change observed across the cosmos include: a being acquiring an ability outside what its kind can ordinarily do; a being acquiring sentience where its kind do not have it; an object or place becoming resonant with cosmological structures it ordinarily has no relation to. Each change is along a specific axis; the change has a shape, not just a magnitude. Two paracausal beings are rarely paracausal in the same way.

Paracausal beings frequently develop a resonance — a felt or sensed awareness of other paracausal events, anomalies, or sites of distortion nearby. This is a consequence of the change, not a definition of it.

Zeniths & Monoliths

Cause and response. A Zenith is the threshold; a Monolith is what the Continuum produces when that threshold strains equilibrium past tolerance. They are two halves of one mechanism, which is why they live together.

Zenith Events Locked

A Zenith is the moment a world, a people, a species or a metaphysical system arrives at an irreversible change to what it fundamentally is. It is produced by Convergence doing what Convergence does: driving a system toward its maximum expression until the system crosses into something it cannot cross back out of.

A Zenith is a hinge, not a disaster The outcome is unknown while it is happening. A coronation that unifies a world for ten thousand years is a Zenith. So is an arcane singularity, a civilizational transcendence, a divine war, a planetary elemental upheaval, a stellar-scale harvest, and a collapse that leaves a cinder. The common element is that the world on the far side is a different world. What that world turns out to be is settled afterward, by events, and often by the people standing in the middle of it.

Zeniths are natural. They occur with or without manipulation, constantly, large and small, across the Continuum at all times. Most pass without consequence beyond the world they happen to. Mortals seeking power produce the most unstable ones, because they compress a threshold into a ritual, a conquest or a coronation and force it to arrive on a schedule — but Convergence would produce thresholds regardless, and the overwhelming majority of Zeniths have nobody's hand behind them.

A Zenith places strain on equilibrium. Where that strain exceeds what Continuity can absorb, Continuity answers, and the answer is a Monolith.

Why they are arriving too often Zeniths strain Convergence specifically, and strain accumulates. An environment of repeated Zeniths loads one Axiom disproportionately, and through it loads the Continuity that Axiom helps sustain. Convergence presently bears more pressure than the other three. Everything the Order has noticed about the rate is correct, and the cause is the part they have no instrument for.

Monoliths Locked

A Monolith is an emergent manifestation of Continuity. It is a response rather than an object anyone made — distinct in kind from a construct, an artifact, a god or a machine — and it arrives without any intelligence designing, creating or deploying it. When equilibrium strain during a Zenith exceeds tolerable thresholds, a Monolith manifests, anchors itself in the affected system, and stays. It absorbs the dominant resonance of the Zenith that raised it, which is why no two are alike.

Monoliths preserve continuity through catastrophe. They do not stop Zeniths and they do not prevent catastrophes. They take a record of the world before the change, because the change is irreversible and its outcome is unknown.

They belong to no one A Monolith serves nothing beyond its own preservation function. The Continuum produces them whether anyone observes the result or not. They were appearing long before any current faction noticed and they will continue long after. Factions that treat them as sacred relics or as evil artifacts are both reading intent into weather.

The Monolith response began with the Reach. The first Monolith manifested in answer to it, and from that point raising a Monolith at a Zenith of sufficient strain became part of how Continuity functions. Every Monolith since reports to that first one.

Archiving

A Monolith archives bodily. It takes people, animals, structures, texts, craft and whole districts — alive, intact, as a sample of what the world was. The taking is not a negotiation and it is not announced. From the ground it looks like a region of the world being selected and removed while everything around it keeps burning or keeps cheering.

A Monolith holds nothing locally. Everything it takes is relayed to the Core, and what stands on the world afterward is the marker, not the vault. This is the fact most institutions have wrong: the Order guards Monoliths as though the taken are inside them.

The intake The relay channel is open only while a Monolith is actively archiving, and closes when the taking ends. It is the sole passage between a Monolith and the Core, it runs in both directions, and almost nobody in the history of the cosmos has used it outward. Because Zeniths happen constantly, somewhere a taking is always underway — which is why the Core's side of the relay never shuts.

What a Monolith takes is what the Zenith threatens with loss. The criterion is not worth or power; it is whether a thing is about to stop existing.

A god is not taken A god standing in a world that is ending is not being lost. It is unbound from that world and continues past it, so the mechanism finds nothing at risk and takes nothing. The exception demonstrates the rule. Vecna's mortality was archived at the Zenith of his own ascension, because his mortality was precisely the part about to cease — and what the Monolith declined to touch went on to become what it is now.

Corrupted Monoliths

A Monolith can be corrupted by outside influence. Its origin is unchanged; its function distorts. A corrupted Monolith preserves incompletely, distorts what it preserves, magnifies destructive resonance, or becomes a vector for hostile metaphysical influence.

The observable signature A corrupted Monolith relays improperly and holds what it takes locally. That is the diagnostic, and it is the one difference anyone could in principle detect from outside — an archive that keeps its contents in the room rather than sending them on.

Fonains. Shadow-influence shaped its Zenith, Tharizdun had a hand in both, and it is the only Monolith known to have been destroyed. It was corrupted, so it held locally; when it was destroyed, what it held was released onto the world. Those people exist. Where they went, and what they carry of a world that no longer matches their memory of it, is unwritten.

An outlier What Fonains demonstrated holds for Fonains. Every other Monolith remains an open question, and no one has repeated the experiment. A fragment of it is currently held inside Boogie's nucleus, still attempting to archive on a very small scale.

The Core Locked

The Core is the first Monolith. It manifested above the First World while that world's Zenith was occurring, and it remained after the world was destroyed. It stands at the center of the cosmos, and it is the origin point, the sink and the archive of the entire Monolith system. Every Monolith relays to it.

It is enormous — far larger than any Monolith raised since, which is the first thing anyone who reaches it understands about the world it answered.

It is permanently receiving Every other Monolith opens its intake at a Zenith and closes it afterward. The Core is the terminus of all of them at once, and Zeniths never stop, so its side never shuts. Matter, light, structures and people arrive continuously, from everywhere, falling inward. That constant intake is what makes the region around the center the most violent place in the cosmos.

The approach is lethal to anything. It is more dangerous to gods, and most dangerous to gods born from the Reach. A later god is harmed less and still cannot make the crossing. Mortals can. That asymmetry is the single most consequential fact in the cosmology, and it is why every power with an interest at the center operates through hands rather than in person.

The Citadel

Everything the Monolith system has ever taken is inside the Core, and the Citadel is what the taken built there. It began with the people of the First World and has accreted every Zenith since — cities, languages, species no one outside has seen in ages, and the ships people arrived in, which sit where they landed because no ship can leave either.

Zeniths are frequently caused, and the people who cause them are standing in the middle when the Monolith manifests. The Citadel holds an age of archmages, ritualists, druids, hierophants and architects of transcendence, taken by the response their own working summoned. It is the densest concentration of capability in existence.

It presents as a golden age Preserved people, an inheritance of craft and knowledge from every world that ever crossed a threshold, and abundance that has never had to be fought over. On arrival it is beautiful, and the Order's doctrine looks borne out: the taken were saved rather than harmed. The Restorers' objection stands alongside it undamaged — nobody asked, and there is no way out. Both readings survive contact with the place, which is why neither order will yield.

Underneath it, the Citadel is broken in the way every world is broken, and for the same reasons, arrived at faster. Each Zenith delivers a population that chose nothing: a district of strangers with their own gods, laws, languages, debts and grudges, set down inside a city that already had an order. Nobody negotiated terms. Nobody can leave. So every grievance is permanent and every settlement is provisional.

Precedence runs by arrival. The First World's descendants are the oldest lineage and sit accordingly; the long-settled hold the good ground, the deep archives and the offices that decide where a new arrival is housed. Abundance is real at the center and thins the further out you go, and nobody planned that either — it is simply what an age of accretion does when the people doing the accreting were never asked whether they wanted neighbours.

The newest want out the most The recently taken still remember a world, a street and people who are now unreachable. The long-settled have made their peace across generations and regard the question as closed. The sect skews recent, which is why it is dismissed as grief rather than argument, and why it has never had the standing to command the resources it would need.

Nothing has ever come out. The theory of leaving is solved and has been for an age — find a relay that is currently open, meaning a Zenith is underway somewhere, and go up it against the flow. The practice defeats everyone. You must predict where a Zenith will reach the threshold, locate that intake among a permanent inbound flood, and move against an active cosmological event in the brief window it runs. And then you arrive on an unknown world at the hinge of its history, stripped of ship, charts and any way home.

Generations turn over. The long-lived persist a while. Those who went never sent word, and from inside there is no way to distinguish a success from a death. Most of the Citadel has stopped treating it as a question. A sect has not, and what they want from any outsider is less a door than evidence that out leads anywhere.

The All-Father & the All-Mother

A persona has accumulated in the Core through pressure, archival weight and recursive significance. It is real, and it is the personification of a cosmic function rather than a god standing outside creation. It has never issued a directive to anyone. What reaches people is weight and meaning; what they do with it is interpretation.

Two orders have built doctrine on it, and both draw real power.

  • The Order calls it the All-Father. It archived what it could; therefore preserve, hold the line, and accept what is lost, because accepting loss is what preservation costs.
  • The Restorers call it the All-Mother. It reached out and kept the living; therefore preservation was never the whole of it, and accepting loss betrays what it actually did.
Neither can be refuted Both are exegesis on the same documented act, and the Citadel is standing evidence for each of them. The thing answers prayers and has never produced a sentence. That silence is the whole of the schism, and it is why two custodial orders with identical reverence treat each other as heretics.

The Core is not public knowledge. Most gods do not understand it. Most mortal factions are unaware it exists in its true form.

The black hole DM only There is no separate object. What every chart in the cosmos marks as a black hole at the center is the Core's intake — a permanent, omnidirectional draw into an archive that never stops receiving. Instruments read a gravitational sink because that is exactly what it behaves like. In-world this is an open question and has been for as long as anyone has been able to look: something is there, everything that goes near it goes in, and no one has identified it.

The Structure of the Cosmos

The cosmos, the Prime Continuum, is a collective of spheres arranged around a black hole at its center.

  • Cosmos — every sphere, together. Five spheres are known and charted.
  • Sphere — a bounded region of the Astral Sea containing several systems. The number of systems varies by sphere. Large systems with large worlds occupy more of their sphere.
  • System — a sun and its worlds. Each system carries its own name: Realmspace, Virenspace.
  • Wildspace — the space between systems inside a sphere. Travel between systems crosses Wildspace on lanes rated in four tiers: charted lane, long haul, uncharted, and hidden.
  • The Astral Sea — the medium everything lies within: systems, Wildspace, and the space between spheres. Its intensity varies between systems and between spheres.
  • The Flow — the phlogiston that fills the space between spheres. Crossing from one sphere to another is uncommon and belongs to late campaign play.
  • The center — a black hole formed from the remains of the Reach world. Above it stand the Core and the Radiant Citadel.
Caelestis's sphere
  • Designation: SPH-01.
  • SYS-01: Realmspace, SYS-02: Virenspace, SYS-03 (Aethris's system), and the Ascension world's system.
  • Krynn and Fonains share one system in this sphere: SYS-04.
  • Lunarfoot's system is in this sphere and is hidden. The Wayfinders map what Lunarfoot must be protected against and update its protection; that purpose requires Lunarfoot to be in the same sphere as the threats.
  • Distances between systems in this sphere are set as the campaign needs them.
  • Designations are Caelestis's charting and stand in until names are chosen. Numbers carry no meaning beyond order. A sphere is SPH and a number (SPH-01). A system is SYS and a number (SYS-01). A world is W and a number (W-01); world numbers run across all of Caelestis's charts, including the FIA registry. A known name follows the designation (SYS-01: Realmspace, W-08: Aethris). Without a name, the designation stands alone (SYS-03). Systems Caelestis has not charted, such as Lunarfoot's and the Ascension world's, carry no designation.
Pantheons and spheres

The gods born from the Reach scattered. Pantheons gravitated to different spheres. At least one sphere has no gods associated with it and no Weave; it is a non-magical sphere. The Axioms and Continuity operate in every sphere, including that one.

Other campaigns

Each sphere can hold a campaign. Events in one sphere can have consequences in another through the Axioms, Monoliths, residue, and Vecna's spell. Fonains and Krynn campaigns take place in Caelestis's sphere.

The Reach

The Reach is the event in which a civilization on a single world, or a cluster of worlds, reached for the Axioms themselves. Not for Continuity. Not for Monoliths. They reached for the foundational principles underneath, attempting direct contact with the structure that maintains the Prime Continuum. No civilization has attempted it since. The world is unnamed.

The Reach was a Zenith of extraordinary magnitude. The first Monolith, the Core, manifested above the Reach world while the Zenith was occurring. The Zenith concluded with the world's destruction. The Core remained. Everything beneath the Core is what remains of the Reach world: the black hole at the center of the cosmos.

The Reach reconfigured reality. It is the birth of this cosmos. What came out of it:

  • The first gods. The Reach world's people met one of three fates. Some were sequestered into the Core. Some were destroyed. The rest were struck by the Axioms and reshaped, merged, and re-formed into the first gods. This is why gods know the Axioms exist and do not understand them.
  • Worlds. Debris and energy from the event formed worlds and planets. World-hearts originate here.
  • The Monolith response. From the Reach onward, Continuity answers Zeniths of sufficient strain with Monoliths.
  • The black hole at the center. The remains of the Reach world, beneath the Core.
  • Residue. Fragments of the contact between mortal will and the Axioms, scattered across the spheres.

Residue of the Reach Locked

The Reach scattered the First World, and what the worlds formed out of was its debris. Residue is the portion of that debris which came through the contact-event changed — matter that is paracausal by origin, carrying a bias toward one of the four Axioms. It is infinitesimal measured against an Axiom and it is the most potent material anything in this cosmos will ever hold.

Why residue is the key A heart is paracausal and its Atrium is a paracausal fold. Residue is the only matter in existence that can act on one. That is a material fact rather than a sentimental one: it works because of what it is made of, it works on every Atrium by the same mechanism, and nothing else works at all. A piece carried from world to world opens door after door and is not consumed by the doing.

Four fragments, seventeen pieces, split by Axiom bias in prime numbers: Boundary in two, Dissolution in three, Transformation in five, Convergence in seven. Pieces of the same fragment draw toward one another; pieces of different fragments are indifferent to each other. A Boundary piece points toward its one sibling. A Convergence piece feels six.

Two thresholds on the same material One fragment made whole opens a door. All seven pieces of the Rod, brought before an Atrium, force it — including the one Atrium the Working was built to force. All four fragments together bend reality. Enough axiomity to put a thing back into being, nowhere near enough to rewrite the world. Nobody alive has confirmed either. Vecna holds suppositions. A Restorer in the Citadel has had centuries with nothing to do but reason it out, and may be the only being in the cosmos who holds the second.

Unsanctioned use. The Continuum sanctioned no extraction of these pieces and no housing of them. Every instrument built from residue is an instrument built out of a dead world, and every institution that has held one across generations has broadly chosen not to think about that. The Restorers, in their darker moments, call them grave-goods.

Hearts Locked

Every world has a heart — its accumulated self, everything it has been and settled into being, held as a metaphysical interior the world grew around. A heart is paracausal, and it is the closest thing in existence to the Axiom layer. That second property is the one that matters: a heart is not merely a secret room inside a planet, it is the thinnest point between a world and the structure underneath reality.

Because a heart is paracausal, it persists where its world does not. A destroyed world still has one.

The Atrium Every heart has one entrance, and it is folded away rather than buried. A door in an old temple that appears only under certain light. The still point at the center of a maelstrom. A well that has always been dry, in a city that forgot why it was dug. Some are moments rather than places: a courtyard that becomes the way in for a few minutes each year. An Atrium is a paracausal fold, which is why ordinary searching finds nothing and why residue is the key.

Past the Atrium is the world's interior self, and it is rarely a cavern. The descent is made of what the world is. A world of sailors gives a sea with no sky, and every ship that ever sank still crewed. A world of war gives a battle that never resolved, repeating. A dead world gives the last thing it remembered. A young world gives something barely formed.

Found, opened, linked

Three separate steps, with three separate costs. Conflating them is the most common error in anyone's understanding of the subject, including most of the people acting on it.

StepWhat it takes
FoundGenerations of scholarship, or an instrument that reads a world beneath its surface. This is the hard one, and it is what A.T.L.A.S. solves for whoever can read it that deep.
OpenedResidue in hand, the Atrium entered, and the descent made to the heart's center. Possible on any world, with or without a Monolith. Opening grants no control over the world; it makes the heart able to hold a link.
LinkedA Monolith standing over that world. The Monolith is a permission, never a route: it licenses the heart beneath it to carry a link, and connects to nothing itself.

Two opened hearts can link, heart to heart — a passage that takes moments instead of a crossing. The link is permanent, works in both directions, and can be used by anyone who reaches either end. A lattice of them is a road network, and it belongs to whoever can find its doors rather than to whoever built it.

Linked worlds bleed Two worlds joined at the heart begin to exchange. Over months a tide arrives on a world with no moon; a crop fails on one and appears on the other; people dream in a language that is not theirs. One world gains what the other loses. Given long enough, neither world is quite itself, and the change does not reverse.

The First Heart DM only

The First World was destroyed and its heart remains. It is the deepest heart in the cosmos, because it belonged to the first world, and it is the one point from which the Axiom layer can be reached. Every other heart is a child heart and sits shallower.

It is closed, and it has been since the world ended.

The Yearning Before there were worlds there was one thing, and the separation left a pull in each of them. That pull is every child heart reaching for the First Heart, and it is why a world does not resist being joined — it wants it. While the First Heart stays closed the Yearning is latent and inert: a tilt in the floor with nothing at the bottom of it.

Residue opens a child heart. The First Heart is not a door residue can force by itself, and the two things that can are both enormous: the massed Yearning of a lattice of opened hearts routed into a single line, or one fragment made whole brought before it.

Who knows this exists Vecna. Possibly the half of him held in the Citadel, who has reasoned toward it rather than been told. The Restorers have no idea — their doctrine points at the Core and stops there. The colony has never heard of it and is opening the hearts that would force it.

Other Planes

The cosmos is the Material Plane. Every sphere, system, world, and stretch of Wildspace is material. The other planes sit adjacent to it and overlaid on it, and each keeps its standard makeup: the Plane of Water is water without end, the Elemental Chaos churns, the Feywild and the Shadowfell echo, the Outer Planes hold the divine realms.

Every plane sits inside the Prime Continuum. The Axioms and Continuity operate in all of them.

  • The Ethereal — the border layer pressed against a world. Reached from the surface, not from the Astral Sea.
  • The Astral Sea — material space between worlds, and the route to everything outside them.
  • Elemental planes — their own geography, tied to a sphere. The Princes of Elemental Evil and Tharizdun's chains are both SPH-01 problems for this reason.
  • The Feywild and the Shadowfell — echoes of worlds, with their own geography and their own distances between them. See below.
  • Divine realms — anchored to the sphere their pantheon settled in. A god's realm is reachable from that sphere and from nowhere else.
Why the Flow silences gods A divine realm is anchored to its own sphere. A vessel in the Flow has left that sphere and has not entered another, so the tie to the realm does not reach. Divine spells fail there for the same reason a lane ends at a sphere's edge.

The center is not a plane. The black hole, the Core, and the Radiant Citadel sit inside the cosmos, at its center. Reaching them is travel, not planar transit.

Echoes: what exists in the Feywild and the Shadowfell

The Reach seeded the worlds across the planes at once. When the debris of the Reach world formed worlds, it formed them in the Feywild and the Shadowfell in the same act. A world that dates to that moment has an echo in each. The echo is the world's own shape — its landmasses, seas, and living growth — rendered in that plane's terms.

Everything built afterward exists only where it was built. Cities, stations, roads, and ships have no counterpart in an echo, unless they were raised in that plane by whoever lives there. A fey court stands in the Feywild because fey built it. Caelestis has no echo at all, and neither does Wildspace, the Flow, or any station on the ring.

The Ethereal is the exception among the planes: it mirrors the Material closely enough that a traveler in the Border Ethereal sees Caelestis where Caelestis is.

  • Distances differ. Echo geography follows its own measure. Two worlds far apart by lane can be a walk apart in the Feywild, which makes echo travel a route between worlds for anyone willing to risk where and when they come out.
  • Crews rarely meet the Fey. You reach an echo from a world's surface. A career spent between systems never touches one.
  • Caelestis is planar-dead. A thin place aboard the station leads nowhere, which sits alongside the Forbiddance in making the Academy a sealed room.

Getting to another plane

Four routes, in rough order of how likely a crew is to find one.

  • Thin places. A spot where a plane presses close enough that going in is a physical act rather than a working: a fountain that keeps going down, a vent that keeps going in, a shadow with depth. Anyone can find one. Anyone curious enough can use one.
  • Color pools. Portals adrift in the Astral Sea, each opening onto another plane. The Fleet charts them and sails around them.
  • Spells. Plane shift and gate move a body. Astral projection sends the traveler into the Astral Sea itself, which in this cosmology means it doubles as a way to cross the cosmos without a hull.
  • Planar shortcuts. Distance in an echo plane does not match distance in the Material. A crossing that takes weeks by lane can, in principle, be cut by entering one plane and leaving it somewhere else. Nobody at Caelestis teaches this.
Standing ruling If the crew finds a door, there is something on the other side. A plane entered by accident is still a real place with its own rules and its own inhabitants, and what the crew does there is theirs to decide. Curiosity gets rewarded with a place, not with a wall.

This belongs to mid and late campaign play. Early on, a thin place is scenery the crew notices and leaves alone.

Crossover with Fonains

The Fonains Zenith rose out of elemental upheaval and shadow corruption, with Tharizdun behind both. Those pressures come from SPH-01's own elemental and shadow planes, which is the same set reachable from Realmspace.

This is a door between campaigns. A crew entering SPH-01's elemental planes is entering the region the Princes of Elemental Evil work in. What Lola's Boys did on Fonains happened in the same sphere, one system over.

The Gods

What the gods are, where they came from, and the bounds of what they understand. Individual deities are catalogued in Factions & Characters → Deities.

The first gods were born from the Reach. They are the souls of the Reach world, reshaped by contact with the Axioms. Later gods ascended afterward through Transformation. Vecna is a later god.

The gods did not create the Prime Continuum, the Axioms, Continuity, or the Monoliths. Pantheons gravitated to different spheres after the Reach.

What the gods generally know: that the Axioms exist, predate the gods, and hold the Continuum stable; that Monoliths are as old as the gods; that Monoliths appear during world-altering crises; that Monoliths preserve something, though not in ways gods understand.

What very few gods understand: how the Axioms work; the relationship between Monoliths, Continuity, and Zenith Events; that Continuity and existence are the same phenomenon; that repeated Zenith Events accumulate strain on Continuity.

The Reach-born gods carry knowledge of the Axioms from the moment that made them. They cannot read it. Only the most ancient and knowledgeable beings suspect that repeated Zeniths strain the equilibrium of the Prime Continuum. Vecna is among them.

The approach to the center is more dangerous to gods than to mortals, and most dangerous to the Reach-born.
