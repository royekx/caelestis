Setting·The Continuum

Cosmology

What is true, and what runs whether anyone is watching. The structural rules underneath every world, every faction, every player choice.

The Prime Continuum

The Prime Continuum is the total structure of existence. It encompasses all planes, realities, timelines, divine realms, and metaphysical systems. Everything that exists does so within the Prime Continuum.

The Prime Continuum is not sentient, moral, or intentional. It does not dictate outcomes, favor particular states, or distinguish between good and evil. It exists as the framework within which reality persists.

It is self-regulating. It rises and falls, threatens and stabilizes, archives and forgets — all according to its own structural pressures, on its own clocks, without needing anyone to be paying attention. The cosmology was here before any of its current beings and will be here after them. Antagonists, however clever, are beings inside it.

The Prime Continuum requires equilibrium to remain stable. Equilibrium is not balance, symmetry, or peace. It is the dynamic tension between foundational structural forces operating within acceptable tolerances. Conflict, catastrophe, and extreme states may all exist without threatening equilibrium. Only instability beyond recovery threatens the Continuum itself.

The Prime Continuum currently maintains equilibrium through four Axioms. The interaction of those Axioms produces Continuity.

Useful distinction The Prime Continuum is the field. The four Axioms are the principles that maintain it. Continuity is what their equilibrium produces. The three concepts are different things; the rest of this page depends on keeping them straight.

Continuity

The Prime Continuum is all of existence. It is the cosmos: everything that exists. Continuity is what the four Axioms produce within the Prime Continuum. In practice the two terms are interchangeable; both are existence. "Continuity" is the precise term for the equilibrium product. Everyday speech calls either one Existence.

Continuity is the emergent result of the Four Axioms operating in equilibrium. It represents the ongoing persistence of reality itself.

Continuity is not sentient. It does not make decisions, formulate plans, or possess desires. However, it possesses function. When equilibrium is placed under sufficient strain, Continuity produces structural responses intended to preserve the persistence of existence. Monoliths are one such response.

If an Axiom is altered, fractured, replaced, or reconfigured, Continuity changes with it. Existence persists, but the nature of reality changes because the equilibrium producing it has changed. The current form of Continuity is not eternal. It is the form existence has taken since the Reach, the event that reconfigured reality and began this cosmos.

The Axioms

The four foundational structural principles through which the Prime Continuum currently maintains equilibrium. They emerged from necessity rather than creation and predate all known gods and civilizations.

The Axioms are sentient — but constrained entirely by their function. Each possesses a limited personality and perspective shaped by its governing principle. No Axiom can act contrary to its own nature.

Convergence Locked Most stressed

Convergence governs escalation toward extremity. Systems trend toward consolidation, centralization, and peak expression. Power accumulates, conflicts intensify, and threshold states become reachable.

Zenith Events occur as a result of Convergence driving a system toward maximum expression. Every Zenith — every cultural apex, every magical saturation, every civilizational transcendence — is Convergence doing what Convergence does.

Currently the most stressed Axiom Because Zenith Events are products of Convergence, and because each Zenith places strain on equilibrium, an environment of repeated Zenith Events accumulates strain disproportionately within Convergence — and through Convergence, on the Continuity it helps sustain. Convergence is presently the Axiom bearing the most pressure.
Dissolution Locked

Dissolution governs decay, fragmentation, and dispersal. Structures break down over time, concentrations disperse, and extremes become unstable. Dissolution prevents permanence and reduces accumulated intensity within systems.

Dissolution is the counterweight to Convergence. Where Convergence builds toward peak expression, Dissolution prevents that peak from becoming permanent. A reality with only Convergence would crystallize into rigid maximum states; a reality with only Dissolution would never accumulate enough to form anything in the first place.

Boundary Locked

Boundary governs separation and distinction. It maintains the differentiation between planes, identities, concepts, and structures. Boundary preserves limits and prevents uncontrolled merging or collapse into uniformity.

Boundary is why a soul is distinguishable from a body, a plane from another plane, a self from a not-self. Without Boundary, the categories that make reality navigable would not hold.

Transformation Locked

Transformation governs state change. Entities and systems transition between conditions without requiring annihilation. Transformation enables adaptation, evolution, and conversion while preventing stagnation.

Transformation is the Axiom by which a mortal becomes a god, a city becomes a ruin, a winter becomes a spring. Change without destruction. Boundary keeps things separate; Transformation lets them become something else without ceasing to be.

Axiom Fracture

An Axiom may fracture under sufficient structural strain. This is the only known mechanism by which the foundational configuration of the Prime Continuum can change without external cause. Fracture occurs in two phases.

The Shock produces immediate destabilization throughout the Prime Continuum — planar instability, magical irregularities, divine disturbances, Astral anomalies, widespread metaphysical disruptions. The Shock is sudden, but it is not the catastrophe itself. It is the announcement of one.

The Unraveling occurs over an extended period. During this phase the fractured Axiom continues to function, but increasingly imperfectly. Instability propagates from higher metaphysical layers downward through divine, planar, and eventually mortal systems. The Axiom's governing principle persists but gradually weakens until replacement, reformation, or redistribution of function occurs. The vacancy cannot persist indefinitely; the Prime Continuum will eventually establish a new equilibrium. When that occurs, Continuity changes accordingly. Existence does not end — but what it means for a thing to exist becomes different than what it meant before.

Why this matters Axiom Fracture is the door by which a being inside the Continuum could position themselves to influence — or become part of — the new equilibrium. Anyone who could engineer a Fracture and arrange to be the replacement-function for the vacated Axiom would become structural rather than merely powerful.

Paracausality

Paracausal = the reality of a thing has been changed. Not whether it exists — how it exists. The term is borrowed (Destiny); it fits because it means operating outside normal cause-and-effect. Paracausality does not mean "made magical." It is an Axiom-level concern: a thing whose place in the structure of reality has been altered.

Paracausality and the Weave are orthogonal. Paracausality describes what changed. The Weave governs why anti-magic does or doesn't suppress. Keep them separate.

Paracausality vs. the Weave

The Weave is the fabric mortals reach into to cast spells. Anti-magic severs local Weave access — it doesn't delete energy. The consequences fall out cleanly:

  • Weave-acts (spellcasting) are suppressed inside anti-magic.
  • A spell slot is innate stored energy. It persists inside anti-magic — it just can't be cast.
  • Innate, non-Weave processes (flowers' anti-magic emission, wisps' draining feed, the counter-spore plant's spore-conversion) are not suppressed. This also dissolves the "flowers cancel their own anti-magic" paradox.
Analogy Energy is underground water — the same for all. The Weave is the well mortals use to reach it. Anti-magic caps the well. A plant with non-Weave roots has its own path to the same water — but a conduit alone has no reserves; it still needs a caster to supply a spell slot. The caster has the water; the plant has the roots.
Counter-spore plant specification
  • Fuel with a spell slot (non-Weave conduit — works in anti-magic field).
  • Bonus action to activate.
  • The slot is the cost; slot level scales area and duration.
  • At L3, caps at 2nd-level slot (~two rungs of scaling).
  • Innate obscurity-clear — faerie-fire-like, not a cast spell.
  • Caster-only.

Other non-Weave flora and fauna exist; this is one specification of a wider category.

How paracausal change manifests

Paracausal change is rare and individual. A being or object becomes paracausal when its place in the cosmological structure has been altered along some axis — typically not at the being's own initiative.

Common forms of change observed across the cosmos include: a being acquiring an ability outside what its kind can ordinarily do; a being acquiring sentience where its kind do not have it; an object or place becoming resonant with cosmological structures it ordinarily has no relation to. Each change is along a specific axis; the change has a shape, not just a magnitude. Two paracausal beings are rarely paracausal in the same way.

Paracausal beings frequently develop a resonance — a felt or sensed awareness of other paracausal events, anomalies, or sites of distortion nearby. This is a consequence of the change, not a definition of it.

Zeniths & Monoliths

Cause and response. A Zenith is the threshold; a Monolith is what the Continuum produces when that threshold strains equilibrium past tolerance. They are two halves of one mechanism, which is why they live together.

Zenith Events

A Zenith Event occurs when a system reaches maximal intensity under the influence of Convergence. It is the full expression of a dominant force within a world, civilization, plane, species, or metaphysical system. Zenith Events are not inherently destructive. They simply represent threshold extremity. Some become disasters. Some become renewals. Some become coronations, revelations, collapses, or irreversible turnings.

Examples may include planetary elemental upheaval, divine wars, arcane singularities, civilizational transcendence, planar collapse, or stellar-scale energy harvesting.

A Zenith Event places significant strain on equilibrium. If that strain exceeds acceptable limits, Continuity may respond through the manifestation of a Monolith.

Zenith Events are natural consequences of Convergence. They occur with or without outside manipulation. Mortals seeking power often produce the most unstable Zeniths because they try to compress destiny into ritual, conquest, perfection, or control — but the Axiom would produce thresholds regardless. Zeniths happen constantly, large and small, across the Continuum at all times.

Monoliths

Monoliths are emergent manifestations of Continuity. They are not constructs, artifacts, gods, or machines. They were not designed, created, or intentionally deployed by any known intelligence.

A Monolith manifests when equilibrium strain during a Zenith Event exceeds tolerable thresholds. Upon manifestation, a Monolith anchors itself within the affected system and absorbs the dominant resonance of the Zenith Event. This resonance permanently influences the Monolith's nature and behavior.

Monoliths serve as preservation mechanisms. Their function is to preserve structures, populations, knowledge, cultures, metaphysical patterns, or other elements capable of contributing to the continued persistence of existence. Monoliths do not stop Zenith Events. They do not prevent catastrophes. They preserve continuity through catastrophe.

Once manifested, a Monolith remains as a permanent structural node within the Prime Continuum. Because Monoliths absorb resonance from the Zenith Event that created them, no two Monoliths are identical.

Critically: monoliths do not belong to anyone. They are not for anything beyond their own preservation function. The Continuum produces them whether any being observes the result or not. They were appearing long before any current faction noticed, and they will continue to appear long after.

The Monolith response began with the Reach. The first Monolith manifested in response to the Reach. From that point, manifesting a Monolith at a Zenith of sufficient strain became part of how Continuity functions.

Every Monolith is a relay. Each one archives the Zenith it answered and returns that resonance to the Core.

Corrupted Monoliths

A Monolith can be corrupted by outside influence. This does not change its origin, but it distorts its function. A corrupted monolith may preserve incompletely, distort what it preserves, magnify destructive resonance, or become a vector for hostile metaphysical influence. This is especially dangerous because many factions wrongly assume monoliths are either sacred relics or evil artifacts. In truth, they are structural instruments. Mishandling them out of fear or reverence can be equally catastrophic.

The Fonains Monolith — an exceptional case

The Monolith on Fonains manifested during a Zenith Event associated with elemental upheaval, shadow corruption, and the influence of Tharizdun. The Monolith itself was not the cause of the crisis; it appeared in response to equilibrium strain generated by those events. As it manifested, it absorbed the dominant resonances present — resulting in a Monolith heavily influenced by elemental and shadow-aligned forces. Vecna encouraged the Zenith Event on Fonains because he wanted a Zenith there. He did not know that Tharizdun had corrupted the Monolith. Tharizdun used the event for his own purpose: planar rifting severe enough to break his chains. The Fonains Monolith is the only Monolith on record to have been destroyed. It was destroyed by the party known as Lola's Boys. Its destruction forced Vecna to change his plan (see What Vecna wants).

Not representative The Fonains Monolith is an exceptional case and should not be regarded as representative of Monolith behavior as a whole. The shadow-influence, Tharizdun involvement, and the unique fact of its destruction make it an outlier, not a template. What was learned about destroying Fonains tells us about Fonains; it tells us less about what would happen to other Monoliths. A fragment of this Monolith is currently held inside Boogie's nucleus. Boogie took it from a shipment he inspected without authorization (see his backstory). Open: how that shipment connects to Vecna's network.

The Core

The Core is the first Monolith. It manifested in response to the Reach. It sits at the center of the cosmos, beyond the event horizon of the black hole there. It is the origin point, sink, archive, and stabilizing nexus of the Monolith system. Every Monolith relays back to it.

The Core archived what it could of the Reach world and the act of the Reach. Contact with the Reach changed the Core. It is not a typical Monolith.

The Radiant Citadel accreted around the Core over uncountable time, drawn together by the same archival pressure that produces Monoliths. It is a civilization of its own. The Core itself is hidden from corporeal beings, layered beneath the Citadel.

Some of the Reach world's people were sequestered into the Core. Their descendants live in the Radiant Citadel. These are the only descendants of the Reach world.

Reaching the Citadel is a severe journey. Leaving it is harder. The event horizon is the most violent region in the cosmos. Crossing it is dangerous to anything. It is more dangerous to gods, and most dangerous to the gods born from the Reach.

The Core is not public knowledge. The title Mother of the Universe belongs to hidden doctrine associated with the Restorers. They believe the Core can be reached, understood, and used to restore worlds, civilizations, and histories lost to catastrophe, archival failure, or metaphysical erasure. Most gods do not understand the Core. Most mortal factions do not know it exists in its true form.

The All-Father is the Core made sentient through accumulation, pressure, and recursive significance. As Monoliths returned resonance and meaning to the Core, a persona emerged from the function. The All-Father is real. It is the personification of a cosmic function, not a god standing outside creation. It rarely acts directly. Its influence is felt through the Order, the Watchers, and those unusually sensitive to Continuity and Zenith pressure.

The Structure of the Cosmos

The cosmos, the Prime Continuum, is a collective of spheres arranged around a black hole at its center.

  • Cosmos — every sphere, together. There are five or six known spheres.
  • Sphere — a bounded region of the Astral Sea containing several systems. The number of systems varies by sphere. Large systems with large worlds occupy more of their sphere.
  • System — a sun and its worlds. Each system carries its own name: Realmspace, Virenspace.
  • Wildspace — the space between systems inside a sphere. Travel between systems crosses Wildspace on lanes rated in four tiers: charted lane, long haul, uncharted, and hidden.
  • The Astral Sea — the medium everything lies within: systems, Wildspace, and the space between spheres. Its intensity varies between systems and between spheres.
  • The Flow — the phlogiston that fills the space between spheres. Crossing from one sphere to another is uncommon and belongs to late campaign play.
  • The center — a black hole. Beyond its event horizon are the Core, the Radiant Citadel, and the Hollow.
Caelestis's sphere
  • Realmspace, Virenspace, the Aethris system, and the Ascension world's system.
  • Krynn and Fonains share one system in this sphere.
  • Lunarfoot's system is in this sphere and is hidden. The Wayfinders map what Lunarfoot must be protected against and update its protection; that purpose requires Lunarfoot to be in the same sphere as the threats.
  • Distances between systems in this sphere are set as the campaign needs them.
Pantheons and spheres

The gods born from the Reach scattered. Pantheons gravitated to different spheres. At least one sphere has no gods associated with it and no Weave; it is a non-magical sphere. The Axioms and Continuity operate in every sphere, including that one.

Other campaigns

Each sphere can hold a campaign. Events in one sphere can have consequences in another through the Axioms, Monoliths, residue, and Vecna's spell. Fonains and Krynn campaigns take place in Caelestis's sphere.

The Reach

The Reach is the event in which a civilization on a single world, or a cluster of worlds, reached for the Axioms themselves. Not for Continuity. Not for Monoliths. They reached for the foundational principles underneath, attempting direct contact with the structure that maintains the Prime Continuum. No civilization has attempted it since. The world is unnamed.

The Reach was a Zenith of extraordinary magnitude. The Prime Continuum responded with the first Monolith, the Core. The Reach world was shattered.

The Reach reconfigured reality. It is the birth of this cosmos. What came out of it:

  • The first gods. The Reach world's people met one of three fates. Some were sequestered into the Core. Some were destroyed. The rest were struck by the Axioms and reshaped, merged, and re-formed into the first gods. This is why gods know the Axioms exist and do not understand them.
  • Worlds. Debris and energy from the event formed worlds and planets. World-hearts originate here.
  • The Monolith response. From the Reach onward, Continuity answers Zeniths of sufficient strain with Monoliths.
  • The Hollow. What remains of the Reach world, at the center of the cosmos.
  • Residue. Fragments of the contact between mortal will and the Axioms, scattered across the spheres.

Residue of the Reach

Residue is fragments of the contact-event itself. It is paracausal by origin, not by intervention. There are four fragments, one biased toward each Axiom. The bias determines what a fragment can do.

Each fragment is broken into a prime number of pieces: Dissolution 2, Transformation 3, Boundary 5, Convergence 7. Seventeen pieces in total. The number of pieces reflects the fragment's distance from the contact-event. The pieces are scattered across the spheres.

Pieces of the same fragment yearn toward each other. Fragments of different Axioms do not.

Residue is Axiom-aligned. It is not an Axiom. Against the Axioms, residue is an infinitesimal speck. Collecting all seventeen pieces does not approach an Axiom.

Residue was not designed. Across millennia, individuals have found pieces, never all at once and never with full context. Some built devices around their piece to use its property. The devices look nothing alike. What unifies the set is provenance, not design.

  • Convergence (7): the Rod of Seven Parts. The tyrant ship's Containment Engine, Fourth Series, was built by Ostekk to harness a piece of the Rod.
  • Boundary (5): one piece is housed in A.T.L.A.S., held at Caelestis. A Boundary piece points toward its four siblings.
  • Dissolution (2): the Runekeeper's fragment. Its two pieces oscillate, one diminishing as the other forms. The cycle conceals Lunarfoot.
  • Transformation (3): no pieces identified.
Unsanctioned use The devices are made; the residue they use was not made for them. The Continuum did not sanction the extraction of these pieces or their housing in instruments. Using the artifacts is not morally neutral. The Order has avoided thinking about this for centuries. The Restorers, in their darker moments, call them grave-goods.

What Vecna wants

Vecna is a later god: he ascended after the Reach, through Transformation, instead of being born from it. He is collecting residue and has been for centuries. His spell is a figure inscribed across the cosmos. It gets one cast. If the working fails, Convergence re-stabilizes around the existing configuration and his project is defeated permanently.

Fonains changed his plan. Vecna nudged Zeniths into happening so that Monoliths would manifest where his figure needed them. The destruction of the Fonains Monolith broke that approach. He moved from saturation to precision.

  • Residue, for precision. Residue lets him place the minimum necessary nodes exactly. A.T.L.A.S. tells him which worlds resonate with which, and a Boundary piece points toward the other Boundary pieces.
  • Multiple residues, for access. His plan requires multiple residues to access the world-heart network.
  • The weak point. The site of the Reach is the thinnest point in the cosmology, closest to the Axiom layer. If his figure passes through it, the spell casts through pre-weakened terrain.
  • The Core's record. The Core holds a record of exactly how Axiom contact was attempted. Reading it is his deepest possible win.

World-hearts

The worlds of this cosmos formed from the debris and energy of the Reach. Each living world has a heart: the cosmological inner truth at its core, carrying a trace of the Reach. A.T.L.A.S. partially reads world-hearts.

World-hearts form a network. Vecna's plan requires multiple residues to access it.

The Gods

What the gods are, where they came from, and the bounds of what they understand. Individual deities are catalogued in Factions & Characters → Deities.

The first gods were born from the Reach. They are the souls of the Reach world, reshaped by contact with the Axioms. Later gods ascended afterward through Transformation. Vecna is a later god.

The gods did not create the Prime Continuum, the Axioms, Continuity, or the Monoliths. Pantheons gravitated to different spheres after the Reach.

What the gods generally know: that the Axioms exist, predate the gods, and hold the Continuum stable; that Monoliths are as old as the gods; that Monoliths appear during world-altering crises; that Monoliths preserve something, though not in ways gods understand.

What very few gods understand: how the Axioms work; the relationship between Monoliths, Continuity, and Zenith Events; that Continuity and existence are the same phenomenon; that repeated Zenith Events accumulate strain on Continuity.

The Reach-born gods carry knowledge of the Axioms from the moment that made them. They cannot read it. Only the most ancient and knowledgeable beings suspect that repeated Zeniths strain the equilibrium of the Prime Continuum. Vecna is among them.

The event horizon at the center is more dangerous to gods than to mortals, and most dangerous to the Reach-born.
