SYS-05 — the Ascension World
A world Vecna already forced a threshold on. The endpoint, visitable before the plan is understood.

A world where Vecna once forced a local threshold and won — a small, personal ascension distinct from the canonical Sigil event. The world remains marked by it: terrain, atmosphere and surviving structures all carry the residue of an event in which a single will dictated local reality.

Surface accessible, reached from Realmspace on an uncharted route and from Virenspace on a charted lane. The sphere is uncatalogued.

It is a worked example the crew can walk around in Everything the Ascension intends at cosmic scale, this world has already experienced at local scale. A crew that visits sees the endpoint before they understand the plan, which is the cheapest and most honest foreshadowing available.

Open: what he forced here and what it cost him, what the residue does to a visitor, and whether anyone survived it.
