W-10 — Krynn
Where the dragons of Fonains went, and a complete setting that needs nothing from this campaign.

An aspect of Bahamut is held here by Tiamat. The dragons who left Fonains came to fight that war, which makes Krynn the destination of a migration the campaign has already established and never followed.

It is a complete setting in its own right and needs nothing from this campaign. Its value here is as the place a question about dragons leads — and the First World and dragons thread is the one that would make Krynn load-bearing rather than adjacent.
