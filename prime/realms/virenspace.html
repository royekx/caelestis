SYS-02 — Virenspace
An overlap site that speaks in a dead Starchild's voice, and the destination encoded in Mirt's wardrobe.

A system beyond H'Catha, sparsely documented in standard Fleet archives, reached on a charted lane that splits at the Astral waystation. It contains the stellar feature corresponding to the destination encoded in Mirt's wardrobe, and Mirt has identified the match as somewhere in the Viren system without narrowing it further.

It is already altered Zerathis was a Starchild who tethered here repeatedly, took every gift offered, and dissolved into the system's paracausal layer. Virenspace is an overlap site, which means paracausal change here runs faster than a single tether would produce and is loud enough for Casey to feel on arrival.

What is at Viren now is part Zerathis, part Astra's reach, part the site itself. It speaks, sometimes in his voice and with his memories. It is malevolent in no direction at all, and it may call Casey brother or Starchild without preamble, which is how he first hears the word applied to himself.

W-34 sits here in draft placement: an Ortholith — the Order's word for a Monolith — established and recording, over a natural convergence Zenith, flagged high priority in the FIA registry. A standing Ortholith in the same system as Casey's first tether point is a coincidence the campaign can cash at any time.

Long-form notes in the Wildspace Lore Bible.

Open: what Casey's first tether point actually is, and how many tethers Viren already holds, which decides how deep the overlap runs.
