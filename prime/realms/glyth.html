W-07 — Glyth
The illithid world that refused the unthinned, and so started everything the colony is doing.

Ringed and remote, ten days out. The illithid world in this system, and a settled civilization with elder brains, thrall populations and the full apparatus of illithid society.

Glyth is the reason the colony exists N'ghathrod returned here with illithids the world judged unfit — the unthinned, raised without an elder brain — and Glyth refused them. That refusal is the origin of everything the Vth'orama is doing. They are building a home because they were denied one, and they are opening worlds to do it.

Glyth has no idea what the refusal set in motion, and by colony reckoning Glyth is not an enemy. It is simply a door that closed. A crew that reaches Glyth can learn the colony's history from the only party with a reason to tell it plainly, and will be talking to illithids to do so.

Open: whether Glyth knows the colony still exists, and what Glyth would do if told what it is building.
