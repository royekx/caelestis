Artifacts

Objects that are carried, held or used, and where the pieces of the Reach are. Most of these are residue or the housings finders built around it: the device is made, the piece is not. What residue is and why it opens a heart are in Cosmology; ships, even small ones, are in Vessels. Holdings, why the pieces lie scattered, and the lesser named objects are below the list.

Holdings

The Reach left five fragments of residue, one for each Axiom, and each broke into a prime number of pieces. Pieces of one fragment draw toward each other; pieces of different fragments are indifferent. A Distinction piece points toward its two siblings, and a Consolidation piece feels six, a direction and a rough sense of how many. The Erevos Galaxy holds pieces of three fragments, Consolidation, Dissolution and Distinction, both by chance and by that pull.

Vecna's side holds one piece, which N'ghathrod's colony carries from world to world to open hearts. Seekers chapters hunt for more, and Vecna steers them: he lets each chapter believe that finding these artifacts will lead it to what it seeks, or that the artifacts are what it seeks. The Restorers want every piece studied, and hold that restoring a world takes a Monolith, a heart and all five fragments. Everything else is scattered. Pieces stay scarce, and every piece is a race.

Why they are scattered and hidden

The residue spread across the five galaxies when the Reach world collapsed into Rykoe. Since then it has passed through the hands of separate finders over long ages, each of whom worked out roughly what they had and then hid that they had it. People who learn what they are holding stop telling anyone, which is why provenance trails run short and institutions hold so little.

A finder builds a housing around a piece that does one impossible thing, to express that thing as something usable. The device is made; the piece is not. Every housing hides what it really does behind a lesser use, the way A.T.L.A.S.'s sister passes for an item-finder.

Other named objects
  • Mirt's wardrobe. It opens onto a bounded stretch of astral night with a floor to stand on, a replica of the sky seen from Virenspace and a constellation visible from there. Zerathis gave it to Mirt, and it stands in his quarters.
  • The marked crate. A crate at the Sky Dock bearing Vocath's mark, the eye in a wheel. Something fills it, nothing living, and its contents route through Mr. Blip toward Toril and H'Catha.
  • Saerthe's miniature ship. Saerthe's personal miniature spelljamming ship is missing from the Academy, and half of it is inside Boogie.
  • The derelict logbook. The logbook from the Derelict's captain's quarters, in two halves; Boogie holds one. The original sits somewhere in Caelestis with pages missing.
  • The Vocath Arcane Rod. A sigil-engraved metal rod built to feed on ambient magic until it detonates, now spent. It bears Vocath's mark. Gregory pulled it from the wall of the wrecked simulation chamber, and Saerthe holds it for study.
  • The Hand of Vecna, the Eye of Vecna and the Book of Vile Darkness. Artifacts bearing or tied to Vecna's name, all three in this cosmos.
