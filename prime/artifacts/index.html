How the set works

Four fragments. Seventeen pieces. Two of them are located, and that number has stood for a long time.

Pieces of a fragment pull toward each other

This is the tracking mechanic. Pieces of the same fragment draw toward one another; pieces of different fragments are indifferent. A Boundary piece points toward its one sibling; a Convergence piece feels six — a direction and a rough sense of how many, which is why anyone holding one knows there is a great deal more out there.

The two thresholds

AssemblyWhat it does
One fragment made wholeOpens a door. Any Atrium, including the one the Ascension was built to force.
All four fragmentsBends reality. Enough axiomity to put a thing back into being, and nowhere near enough to rewrite the world. Nobody has confirmed it.

Why they are scattered and hidden

Millennia of separate finders, each of whom worked out roughly what they had and then obscured that they had it. The pattern recurs across every documented housing: people who learn what they are holding stop telling anyone. That is why provenance trails run short and institutions hold so little.
