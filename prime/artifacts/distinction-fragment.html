Distinction fragment
Three pieces: two housed in instruments of finding, the third not known.

The Distinction fragment is the Reach's residue of Distinction, the Axiom that makes and keeps things different from each other. It broke into three pieces. Every Distinction piece, in whatever housing, finds or reveals something: it tells one thing apart from everything else.

Properties

A housing built around a Distinction piece becomes an instrument of finding, and each housing finds a different thing. Like every housing, it hides what it really does behind a lesser use. The three pieces draw toward each other, so each housing points, faintly, toward the other two.

The pieces

A.T.L.A.S.
The Brig at Caelestis
Reveals the aspects of a world, and a world's heart is one of them.

A brass-and-glass projection instrument that renders a world as a holographic chart. A reader sees as deep as their understanding allows, down to a world's Atrium and the links between hearts. Caelestis holds it in the Brig, catalogued as an antique navigational instrument. Entry →

A.T.L.A.S.'s sister
Somewhere on Fonains
Finds magic items on the surface, and Reach artifacts beneath it.

A device Caelestis held. It seems a lesser item-finder, because every enchanted thing carries a faint trace of the Reach; its true use is finding Reach artifacts. Vocath's crew ambushed the Bellwether Crow to take it, and Clint Dashforest carried it through a color pool to Fonains. Entry →

The third piece
Unknown
It finds something too.

Its housing, its holder and what it finds are unknown. A.T.L.A.S. and its sister both pull toward it.

History

The fragment scattered when the Reach world collapsed into Rykoe. Finders built housings around two of its pieces, each discovering that the stone did one impossible thing and building an instrument to express it. The Erevos Galaxy holds Distinction pieces, drawn together by their pull; Caelestis came to hold two of the housings, and lost one of them in the ambush on the Bellwether Crow.

What's hidden

  • The sisters' pull. A.T.L.A.S. and its sister point toward each other and toward the third piece. Anyone who understands that holds a compass for the whole fragment, and it is part of why antagonists want A.T.L.A.S.

Threads

  • The Restorers hold that restoring a world takes a Monolith, a heart and all five fragments; two Distinction housings have passed through Caelestis's hands.
  • The Academy thefts are after A.T.L.A.S.; its arc is on its entry.

Still to decide

  • What a fragment made whole does is not set.
  • The third Distinction piece, its housing, its holder and what it finds are not set.
  • How a bare Distinction piece looks is not set; A.T.L.A.S.'s core is stone.
