Expansion fragment
Five pieces, none located.

The Expansion fragment is the Reach's residue of Expansion, the Axiom that spreads things apart: it stretches space and gives what lies in it room. It broke into five pieces, and none of them has been located.

Properties

Its five pieces draw toward each other, as the pieces of every fragment do. A housing built around one would express Expansion in one working performed impossibly well.

History

The fragment scattered when the Reach world collapsed into Rykoe. The Erevos Galaxy holds pieces of three other fragments, so the Expansion pieces lie elsewhere among the five galaxies.

How to run it

No Expansion piece is placed, so one can be found wherever play needs it, outside the Erevos Galaxy or carried into it.

Still to decide
