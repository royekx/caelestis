Rod of Seven Parts
The Consolidation fragment: seven pieces, each known by its working.

The Rod of Seven Parts is the Consolidation fragment: the Reach's residue of Consolidation, the Axiom that draws things together. It broke into seven pieces. Each piece is keyed to a single working, performed impossibly well in whatever housing a finder builds for it, so a piece can be recognised by what it does before anyone knows what it is.

Properties

A Consolidation piece feels the other six: a direction, and a rough sense of how many, which tells anyone holding one that there is a great deal more out there. The pieces have yearned toward each other for long ages and are drawing close. Several now sit near Realmspace, because once a few came to rest there, more followed.

History

The fragment scattered when the Reach world collapsed into Rykoe, further than most, and drifted back toward itself over the ages. Separate finders built around separate pieces, each working out roughly what they had. Thessalar obtained the seventh and used it on his prisoner. Ostekk, a beholder builder of H'Catha, made the fourth the heart of his Containment Engine, and Vaelorix stole it from him. Neither Thessalar nor Ostekk understood what the stone was.

In the campaign

  • Voyage 6. Gregory's pull found the Lantern of Flourishing between two of Vaelorix's eyestalks, and the crew took the fourth piece with it. Tumak hid it in his bag of holding and told Ostekk's behir nothing had been found.

What's hidden

  • The taking's origin. The colony has no idea the taking comes from a piece of the Rod, and neither does N'ghathrod, who experiences his own making as having been healed. Thessalar knows. N'ghathrod's long life comes from the tithe of his taken, not from the piece.
  • The Lantern's pull. The Lantern of Flourishing can pull the crew toward the fifth piece.

How to run it

A crew that learns the pattern can start recognising pieces in the wild by their effects. Each piece is its own adventure, in the way of Critical Role's Luxon beacons; losing one is a setback for N'ghathrod, not a defeat, so he keeps other objectives running alongside.

Threads

  • The Tyrant Ship ties to the Rod through the Lantern it carried, a little-known link to the larger world.
  • Thessalar can be reached by following the Rod, by following the colony's origin, or by following N'ghathrod's mouse; all three arrive at the same wreck.

Still to decide

  • What a fragment made whole does is not set.
  • Where the four loose pieces sit, what each does, and whether any institution has catalogued one are not set.
  • Whether the loose pieces keep clear of beholders is not set. The fourth piece's maker and last holder were both beholders, and Ostekk wants it back.
  • Which fragment the colony's residue piece belongs to is not set; it may be one of the four loose pieces.
