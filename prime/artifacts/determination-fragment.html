Determination fragment
Eleven pieces, none ever found.

The Determination fragment is the Reach's residue of Determination, the Axiom that holds that every outcome must be decided while nothing sets which way it goes. It broke into eleven pieces, and none has ever been found.

Properties

Its eleven pieces draw toward each other, as the pieces of every fragment do.

History

The fragment scattered when the Reach world collapsed into Rykoe. The Erevos Galaxy holds pieces of three other fragments, so the Determination pieces lie elsewhere among the five galaxies.
