The Transformation fragment
Five pieces. Nowhere anyone has written down.

Five pieces, and the only fragment the record is entirely silent about — every piece unlocated, every housing unnamed, and the institutions that collect such things silent on the subject.

Transformation governs a thing becoming another thing, and it is the Axiom Vecna ascended through. A housing built around a Transformation piece would do something to the category of what it touched, which is a strange enough effect that anyone who found one would likely have obscured it faster than most.

Why the silence is itself information Three of the four fragments have left traces across known history. This one has left none, in a cosmos that has been looking at residue for millennia. Either the pieces are somewhere nobody surveys, or somebody has been very successful at keeping five of them quiet.

Open: all of it. This is the cleanest hook in the set and the most portable — a Transformation piece can be found anywhere the campaign needs one.
