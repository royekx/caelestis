Second Distinction piece
Finds magic items on the surface, and Reach artifacts beneath it.

The second Distinction piece is one of the three pieces of the Distinction fragment, set in a device that Caelestis held. Like every housing it hides what it really does: on the surface it finds magic items, and its true use is finding Reach artifacts. Vocath's crew ambushed the Bellwether Crow to take it, and Clint Dashforest carried it through a color pool. It is somewhere on Fonains.

Properties

Every Distinction piece finds or reveals something. This one finds. Its surface use is a lesser kind of item-finder: it picks out magic items, because every enchanted thing carries a faint trace of the Reach. Beneath that it finds the Reach itself, in residue and its housings. As one of three Distinction pieces it pulls toward the other two, A.T.L.A.S. and the third.

History

Caelestis held it as a device. When Cairn Station went silent, the Bellwether Crow went out on the relief run with the device aboard, and Vocath's crew, waiting at Cairn, ambushed the Crow for it. Clint took the device and left the ship in one of the boarders' craft to draw them off. He steered for a color pool he could see opening and went through it on purpose; some of the boarders went after him, and the crew fended off the rest. The pool carried him through a small pocket of another dimension and set him down on Fonains, away from the boarders who followed him in.

Someone from Vocath's crew went looking for the device, followed Clint to Fonains and learned there about the gems; Vocath now trades in them. The device is somewhere on Fonains.

What's hidden

  • What it finds. Its true use, finding residue, is why Vocath's crew ambushed the Crow for it. In anyone's hands it is a way to the scattered pieces, which every Seekers chapter and the Restorers hunt.

Threads

  • Clint is alive on Fonains, and the device went with him through the pool.
  • The ambush and its survivors are on the Bellwether Crow.
