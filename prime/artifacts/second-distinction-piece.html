Second Distinction piece
Finds magic items on the surface, and Reach artifacts beneath it.

The second Distinction piece is one of the three pieces of the Distinction fragment, set in a device. Like every housing it hides what it really does: on the surface it finds magic items, and its true use is finding Reach artifacts. Where it lies and who holds it are not known.

Properties

Every Distinction piece finds or reveals something. This one finds. Its surface use is a lesser kind of item-finder: it picks out magic items, because every enchanted thing carries a faint trace of the Reach. Beneath that it finds the Reach itself, in residue and its housings. As one of three Distinction pieces it pulls toward the other two, A.T.L.A.S. and the third.

What's hidden

  • What it finds. Its true use is finding residue. In anyone's hands it is a way to the scattered pieces, which every Seekers chapter and the Restorers hunt. Whoever holds it and takes it for an ordinary item-finder holds more than they know.

Threads

  • Clint Dashforest may carry an artifact device on Fonains that he knows only as a magic item, and that device may be this piece. The pirates and the overseer who came through the color pool with him, and Vocath, know nothing of it.
  • It pulls toward A.T.L.A.S., which Caelestis holds in the Brig, so each points faintly at the other.

  • How it looks, who built it, and how it shows what it finds are not set.
