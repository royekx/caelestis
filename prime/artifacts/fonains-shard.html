Fonains shard
A piece of the Fonains Monolith, inside Boogie, and the source of the Pulse.

The Fonains shard is one of the many small shards of the Fonains Monolith, the corrupted Monolith that Lola's Boys destroyed. It is Monolith matter, not residue. It sits in Boogie's nucleus, and it is the source of the Pulse, the rhythm he reads as his own.

Properties

The shard is still archiving, on a very small scale: whatever a Monolith does when it takes a sample of a world, this does at the size of a thing in a plasmoid's nucleus. A corrupted Monolith is a tunnel to Tharizdun's prison, so what the shard takes goes toward Tharizdun rather than to the Core Monolith.

It keeps what it archives. Two objects are held inside Boogie by it: half of a model spelljamming ship, and half of the Derelict's logbook. It draws people toward its bearer as something holy, and it shows as the Pulse: a buzzing in the nucleus, a rhythmic pressure.

History

Fonains's Monolith formed corrupted, with Tharizdun's black crystal replicating into it as it formed. Kwalish's cannon split it, and four great shards fell, each guarded by a Prince-aligned elemental, turning around a black cyst in the Trintara Forest. Many smaller shards came from the four great ones and scattered across the cosmos. Three hundred and thirty-three of the small shards are black; the rest took colours close to their elemental affinities.

Vocath's people chased Clint Dashforest to Fonains and never found him, and came back with fragments of the broken Monolith through Vocath's line, as ordinary cargo. Before Boogie's first voyage with the crew, he looked into a shipment he should have left alone, and the shard ended up in him.

In the campaign

  • Voyage 1. Boogie pulled a tiny half-missing model ship from his own chest.
  • Voyage 2. He slipped the Derelict's logbook into his chest.
  • Voyage 3. He dreamed of countless other versions of himself in an infinite dark and a presence converging on him, and woke half-dissolved before humming himself back together. The half-ship and the logbook were both still in his chest.

What's hidden

  • Tharizdun. Tharizdun wants nothing specific from Boogie. She is not lucid; she is an opportunist, and what happens through the shard depends on what Boogie does. Things may come back through the current.
  • His power. Boogie's Divine Soul power comes from Tharizdun's influence through the shard, and he does not know it.
  • The fractal dream. In the dream Boogie saw a network of other Boogies, each with a differently shaped shard: this shard made this Boogie, and others exist.
  • The logbook. Boogie holds half of the derelict logbook. The other half was in his chest inside the Monolith.

How to run it

The shard escalates rather than repeats, and is not revealed as a Monolith piece too soon. Boogie sometimes feels corruption leaking into him from it, a roleplaying beat and not a mechanic. Collecting the shards is never a goal. In time he can reach for the archive on purpose: something offered during a Pulse goes in incomplete and comes out incomplete, and his ceiling stays bounded because he is a damaged relay.

Threads

  • The prophet thread. A small faith that predates the corruption may treat Boogie as a prophet. It is available to pull on and never pushed.
  • The Lantern. The Lantern of Flourishing pulls toward wholeness, and the shard's corruption pulls apart.
  • FR-022. A different piece of the Fonains Monolith, recovered by the Order and routed to the Fleet. Boogie may find it and consume it.
  • Giorge. He searches for pieces of the broken Monolith and may cross Boogie's path later.
