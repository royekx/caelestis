Faelight spore
A knuckled fungus that, fed a spell slot, throws a cloud of cold light over everything in it.

The Faelight spore is a fungus from Vaelorix's collection, entry 051 on the Tyrant Ship's Collection Manifest, gathered in the outer Kellis Belt. It grows in the ship's Garden. Fed a caster's spell slot, it discharges a lasting cloud of cold light that coats everything inside it and shows it plainly.

Appearance

A knuckled, colourless fungus studded with tight pustules. Its discharge clears the dust and particles hanging in the air and keeps a cold luminescence for some minutes after it settles.

Properties

The spore runs on a spell slot, and it is a conduit apart from the Weave: a slot is stored energy that persists inside an anti-magic field, so the spore works there too. A caster touches it and spends a slot, and the spore throws out a cloud of faerie light that hangs in place for a minute. Anything in the cloud is coated and outlined, allies as well as enemies, and nothing coated can hide behind invisibility or concealment. The larger the slot, the wider the cloud.

History

Vaelorix collected it from the outer Kellis Belt. His manifest records it as unprocessed intake, its assessment deferred until he could see to it, with an advisory against processing it in an enclosed volume. It was logged in with the cargo and has since been moved to the Garden, where it grows.

In the campaign

  • Voyage 4. The crew found the Collection Manifest aboard the tyrant ship. Entry 051 lists the spore.

Its arc

The crew has not taken it up. Its player inventory entry is made when they do, and it holds what the crew has learned of these rules.
