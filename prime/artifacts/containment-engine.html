Lantern of Flourishing
Ostekk's Containment Engine, Fourth Series: a small lamp that mends bodies, built around the Rod's fourth piece.

The Lantern of Flourishing is a small lamp built by the beholder Ostekk around the fourth piece of the Rod of Seven Parts, the piece keyed to regenerate. Ostekk named it the Containment Engine, Fourth Series, and the name records three earlier attempts. Vaelorix stole it, kept and studied it, and called it the Lantern of Flourishing. The Caelestis crew carries it now, hidden in Tumak's bag of holding.

Appearance

An iron-and-bronze lamp six or eight inches tall, with a handle and an iris shutter over a faint green light. The core that holds the piece is sealed inside the lamp, below the light.

Properties

The Engine holds the piece in a working state rather than containing it, and harnesses it to do one thing: mend bodies. Its green pulse heals the living and mends constructs better, and in constructs and plants it regrows what was lost. It holds three charges, and they renew each day. Identify reads the charges and the mending; it cannot read the sealed core.

The piece pulls toward wholeness, toward the other six pieces of the Rod. Inside an anti-magic field the Lantern is dampened rather than switched off: its pull goes faint and its working stutters. Seated in the repair-bay pod on the Tyrant Ship's Hollow Deck, it becomes a rejuvenation pod for one. Ostekk built his OSTEKK automatons to depend on it for their maintenance, securing their loyalty through need rather than trust.

History

Ostekk built the Engine, the fourth of its series, around a stone that did one impossible thing well, without ever learning that it was residue. He built nine automatons, OSTEKK-1 to OSTEKK-9, to depend on it. Vaelorix stole the Engine and the nine units together, and Ostekk built a tenth, Ostekk-10, a clockwork behir, to bring them back.

Aboard his ship, Vaelorix pointed the Engine's regenerative working at a wisp-lotus symbiosis and got a paracausal eruption instead of a specimen. The bloom overran the ship; its flowers made the wisps and a field that killed magic and unmade constructs. Vaelorix died on his Command Deck with a plant grown out of his body and the Lantern lodged between two of his eyestalks, and the behir sent after it was stranded aboard, unable to enter the field.

In the campaign

  • Voyage 6. Once the great flower came off Vaelorix's body, Gregory's pull led him beneath the roots to the Lantern. Read with a spell: three charges a day, each mending a body, constructs better than the living. A charge set OSTEKK-6's body rebuilding below the waist. Tumak hid the Lantern in his bag of holding and told Ostekk-10 nothing had been found, and the crew ejected the behir through Cargo.

What's hidden

  • The piece is extractable. The Engine is not built to survive losing it, and taking it out stops whatever the Engine does.
  • Ostekk. He does not know what the piece is. He rules a domain on H'Catha today, and he has reason to come for the Engine himself: the crew took it and lied to his machine.
  • OSTEKK-6. He does not know that the thing keeping him running and the thing the behir was sent for are the same object.

How to run it

The Lantern pulls toward wholeness, and Tharizdun's corruption turns the Fonains shard's archiving into pulling apart. Neither object is aware of the other, and Boogie stands between them.

Threads

  • Ostekk. The crew hearing his name soon after landing on H'Catha is the payoff.
  • OSTEKK-2. It lies inert on the Hollow Deck. The crew could heal it back to function if they return for it, with the Lantern for one.
  • Gregory. The Lantern becomes his next anomaly if he investigates it.
  • The fifth piece. The Lantern can pull the crew toward it; see the Rod.
