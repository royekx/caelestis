Containment Engine, Fourth Series
Built by Ostekk around a piece of the Rod, by someone who did not know what the piece was.

A built device rather than a found one, and the clearest surviving example of what a competent finder does with residue. Ostekk built it around the fourth piece of the Rod of Seven Parts, the one keyed to regenerate, and the name records that there were three earlier attempts.

Harnessing is the operative word: the Engine holds the piece in a working state rather than containing it, drawing on a Convergence bias to do one thing extremely well. As a usable item it is the Lantern of Flourishing — an iron lamp-housing with an iris shutter, uncommon, three charges refreshing at dawn. Its green pulse heals 1d8 to any creature it touches within fifteen feet, and 2d8 plus biological regrowth to constructs and plants.

Vaelorix stole it from Ostekk, which is why Ostekk sent the behir after it. The crew took it in V006. Gregory's pull led him to it between two eyestalks once the great flower came off the body, and he read it with identify: three charges a day, each mending a body, constructs best. He spent one charge on OSTEKK-6, who began rebuilding. It rides in Tumak's bag of holding, and the players know it as the Lantern of Flourishing. The crew told Ostekk-10 nothing had been found and ejected it, so Ostekk has a reason to come for it.

The bay cradle. Its empty seat is above the repair-bay pod on the Hollow Deck. Seated, the pod spends all three charges to give one creature a long rest, a long rest aboard restores all Hit Dice, constructs aboard are fully repaired, and the Garden thrives. A charge in the pod with an INT DC 15 parts check wakes OSTEKK-2 for good. The V007 runsheet offers the seating.

The automatons are bound to it by design Ostekk built servants that depend on the Engine for maintenance, securing loyalty through need rather than trust. Nine OSTEKK units existed; OSTEKK-6 and OSTEKK-2 survive. That is the same question Gregory poses from the other direction, and the two can be put in a room together.
At the table The piece is extractable and the Engine is not built to survive losing it. The crew holds it now, so the decision is live whenever they find out: a Convergence piece in hand is a compass pointed at six more and a key to any Atrium they find, and taking it stops whatever the Engine has been doing for however long it has been doing it.

Open: what the Engine was built to contain or power, what the first three Series were, and whether Ostekk knew what the piece inside it was.
