A.T.L.A.S.
Renders worlds as holographic models, and reads deeper the more its holder understands.

A brass-and-glass projection device, palm-to-fist sized, with a hinged case and an armillary-style internal frame. At its center, mounted at the central node of the frame, is a fragment of Reach residue biased toward Boundary — the Axiom of distinction, of this world is itself.

The device is made; the fragment is not. Whoever built the casing was a finder, not a creator — they discovered the fragment, observed that it responded to proximity to planetary bodies in ways no ordinary stone does, and built around it an instrument that could express the response as a readable form.

Function

Activated near a planetary body, the device projects a holographic chart of the world below — a three-dimensional rendering surfacing structural and cosmological features the naked eye and ordinary instruments cannot perceive. Once per long rest, the user reading the chart gains the effect of Commune with Nature, reframed: the device is not casting the spell, it is letting the world speak through the fragment. Three facts about that world surface, drawn from what the world knows of itself.

The kind of information surfaced depends on the world being read. A young world says different things than an old one; a world hiding a paracausal truth says different things than one without one; a world close to its own Zenith says urgent things. What surfaces is a function of what the world has to say.

Cosmological depths of reading

The device's readings carry information at multiple cosmological depths. A reader sees what their cosmological understanding allows them to see — the same activation produces the same data, but the meaning extracted depends on the reader.

Surface — the readable layer
Anyone with ordinary perception can read the chart's surface features: the world's hidden geography, structures the surface hides, presences and absences, places of resonance. A reader at this level can use the device as a portable exploratory instrument.
Operational — the surveying layer
A reader with cosmological-operational understanding sees where a world folds into itself — its Atrium, the one entrance to its heart, which the surface read registers only as an oddity. Cross-referencing that across world after world produces a survey: which worlds give strong responses, which weak, which carry resonant features. One world at a time it is a door; many worlds at a time it is a map of doors.
Structural — the architectural layer
A reader with structural cosmological understanding perceives what the device is actually reading — the Yearning, the latent pull every heart holds toward the First Heart, and against that background any link that has been made live. At this depth the instrument shows a reader where the lattice stands. Only a few entities in the cosmology have the understanding to perform this reading.

The device hides nothing from any reader. The data is the data; the interpretation is what each reader brings.

How a reader reaches a depth

There is no check and no DC. A depth opens when the reader holds the concept it depends on, and it stays open afterward — so the gate is knowledge the party can earn in play rather than a number they can roll against.

Track who holds which concept, because the crew acquires them unevenly and a single conversation can promote a reader a whole depth. That is the campaign's central reward loop in one object: understanding is the upgrade.

Status & provenance
Current custody
Held in the Brig at Caelestis. Catalogued as "navigational instrument, antique, of uncertain origin, exceptional rendering capability" — known-rare, partially understood by the institution holding it.
Provenance
Partial. The instrument has changed hands across known history more than once. The trail before the most recent custodian is murky — finders have generally chosen to obscure their possession of it. This pattern is itself a tell, and recurs with most artifacts of the set: people who learn what they have tend to hide it.
The piece's nature

The central fragment is a piece of the Reach's own Boundary-signature — the part of that dead world that knew it was that world, scattered when the world broke. It retains its resonance with any other world's equivalent self-knowledge, which is why it can be coaxed into rendering a chart of any planetary body it is brought near. The Reach knew itself; through the fragment, every other world can briefly know itself in the same way.

This is what makes the device a survey instrument rather than a generic scrying one. Other Reach artifacts' fragments are biased toward different Axioms and produce different effects in different housings. A.T.L.A.S.'s bias toward Boundary is what shapes its function.

Ethics

The set-wide position is in Cosmology → Residue of the Reach. What it adds up to here: whoever first cut this piece out and built a housing for it is unrecorded, Caelestis has held the result for generations without the question coming up, and any user benefits from an extraction they did not perform.
