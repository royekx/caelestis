A.T.L.A.S.
Renders a world as a holographic chart and reveals its aspects, a world's heart among them.

A.T.L.A.S. is a projection instrument built around one of the three pieces of the Distinction fragment. Brought near a world, it projects a chart of that world and reveals its aspects, and a world's heart is one of them. Caelestis holds it in the Brig. Its name is the Fleet's catalogue name, the Astral Topographic Lithic Augury System, and each word names only what the Fleet could observe: it maps worlds from astral space, its core is stone, and it reveals hidden things. Nobody who named it knew what the core is.

Appearance

A brass-and-glass device the size of a palm or a fist, in a hinged case. Inside is an armillary-style frame, and the stone core is mounted at its central node.

Properties

Activated near a planetary body, A.T.L.A.S. projects a three-dimensional chart of the world below, surfacing structural and cosmological features that the eye and ordinary instruments miss. The device does not cast anything: it lets the world speak through the piece, and what surfaces is what the world knows of itself. A young world says different things from an old one, a world hiding a paracausal truth says different things from one without, and a world close to its own Zenith says urgent things.

Its readings carry several depths at once. The same activation gives every reader the same data, and each reader takes from it as much as their understanding allows. At the surface anyone can read the world's hidden geography, the structures its surface hides, its presences and absences, and its places of resonance. A reader who knows that worlds have hearts sees where the world folds into itself: its Atrium, which the surface reading shows only as an oddity, and across world after world a survey of which worlds answer strongly. A reader who knows that hearts pull toward one another and can be linked sees what the device is reading underneath: the Yearning, the pull every heart holds toward the First Heart, and against it any link that has been made live.

As one of three Distinction pieces, A.T.L.A.S. pulls toward the other two: its sister, on Fonains, and the third.

History

Whoever first cut the piece out and built its housing is unrecorded. The finder discovered that the stone answered nearness to planetary bodies as no ordinary stone does, and built an instrument that could show the answer as a readable form. The device has changed hands more than once. Its trail before its most recent custodian runs short, because finders have hidden that they held it, as people who learn what they hold usually do. Caelestis has held it for generations, catalogued as a navigational instrument of antique origin and exceptional rendering, known to be rare and partly understood.

What's hidden

  • The thefts. A.T.L.A.S. is what the Academy thefts are really after. In the antagonists' hands it finds hearts for them, and its pull toward the other two pieces makes it a compass for the rest of the fragment; keeping it out of their hands denies them that reach.
  • Who wants it. N'ghathrod wants A.T.L.A.S. Vocath wants the Brig's other goods and to disgrace Mirt.

Its arc

The thefts come forward when the crew returns to Caelestis. At graduation, under cover of the attack on Caelestis, Joffrey runs the Brig and takes A.T.L.A.S.; the heist and what follows are on the Fork and the plot hook.

How to run it

Track who holds which concept. The crew acquires them unevenly, and a single conversation can promote a reader a whole depth, so understanding is the upgrade. The device hides nothing from any reader: the data is the data, and the interpretation is what each reader brings. Anyone using it benefits from an extraction they did not perform; the set-wide position is in Cosmology.
