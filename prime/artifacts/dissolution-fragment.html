Dissolution fragment
Both Dissolution pieces, forming in two people.

The Dissolution fragment is the Reach's residue of Dissolution, the Axiom that pulls everything toward sameness. It lies in two pieces, and it has no housing: it lives in people. The Runekeepers have carried it for about five centuries without knowing it as residue, and the concealment of their homeworld rests on it. It is now leaving the Runekeeper and forming in Wan and Tu, the twin Wayfinders who study at Caelestis as Tumak.

Appearance

The fragment is visible in its bearers. It forms and re-forms in the chest of the Runekeeper and of the Wayfinders who carry it, never still, and the order's emblem is the fragment drawn half-formed, as it shows in a bearer's chest. On the skin it writes itself as an inscription of runes: half a rune on each twin, so no rune is complete while they are apart.

Properties

The fragment is whole in a Runekeeper for their tenure and moves only when a next host exists. When an heir is born it begins to move, and it builds in the heir as runes while it leaves the Runekeeper, at the same pace. It forms only in someone of the four primed lines.

Dissolution unmakes joins. It disjoined the homeworld from everything around it and drew it inside its heart, and through it each Runekeeper's haven binds to its host world's heart. A bearer's runes surface in the presence of cosmological threat, and a bearer develops the felt awareness every carrier of paracausal matter has, keenest for anything of its own Axiom. Like every fragment, its pieces draw toward each other; with both pieces in the twins, the pull runs between two people.

History

About five centuries ago a seeker carried the fragment to the Wayfinders' homeworld and used it to open the world's heart and link it to another world. The leaders of four clans fought the seeker and took it, and Dissolution struck all four. The fragment was already oscillating; the one who seized it etched one pole into themselves, caught the cycle running, and became the first Runekeeper, and the other three lines were primed. In that struggle the fragment folded the world inside its heart. Every Runekeeper since has carried it, from Runekeeper to heir.

It began forming in Wan and Tu, both at once, and the order sent them out. For the first time it is settling in two heirs, half in each.

What's hidden

  • The order's ignorance. Nobody in the order has identified the fragment as residue. The Becoming tale is their memory of the first fold; the runes come from the fragment.
  • Two halves. Settling in two heirs, the fragment leaves the Runekeeper faster than any generation before, and the binding slips. It has always come home whole; this time it comes home as two halves, and that change is the homeworld's second Zenith.
  • The counter. N'ghathrod is made of joins, and Dissolution unmakes joins. The fragment forming in Tumak is a counter to him, and nobody holding it knows.

Its arc

It leaves the Runekeeper as the twins walk the havens, and the Runekeeper's fade is the arc's clock. The mechanics are on Rules, and the homeworld's side on the Wayfinders' homeworld.

Threads

  • The Runekeeper fades as the fragment leaves him, and he believes the cost is his to carry.
