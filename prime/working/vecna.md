# Vecna

Vecna is a god, and has been for centuries. That is a fact of the world, known
the way any god is known. What is hidden is his plan, not his existence.

## The two bodies

He has two groups of people, and they must not be confused.

| | The faithful | The Seekers |
|---|---|---|
| What they are | Open worship of a god of secrets, under many names | Chapters taught a method by a voice |
| Do they know him? | Yes, by some name, sometimes by his own | No. No chapter knows the name Vecna |
| What they give him | Worship: his power, reach and awareness | Zeniths, and therefore Monoliths |
| Do they know the plan? | No | No |
| Where | Wide and thin, across spheres | Dozens of worlds |

The faithful are why he is a god. The Seekers are how the Ascension happens.
A temple to the Keeper of Secrets in a port city and a scholarly order chasing
a lost discipline on another world can both trace back to him, and neither
would recognise the other.

Some people are both: a faithful worshipper who also belongs to a chapter,
never suspecting the two are the same hand.

## History

1. A mortal mage on his home world. Recruited as a boy by a voice (published
   lore; the layer above him stays parked).
2. Became a lich to stop ending.
3. Ascended to godhood through a Zenith on his home world. A Monolith answered,
   and his mortality was archived to the Core, where that half still lives in
   the Citadel.
4. Spent the centuries since building worship. He failed before he had
   believers; he does not intend to fail that way again.
5. Now working toward the Convergence seat (see the Ascension).

### Home world

His home world is **Oerth**, in its own sphere. It is the centre of his worship
and the anchor of his realm. The Monolith from his ascension still stands
there, and his faithful treat it as their holiest site.

The Ascension World (SYS-05) is a different place: a world in SPH-01 where he
later forced a small, local threshold.

Exandria exists in the cosmos with none of his history there.

### Realm

His divine realm is anchored to Oerth's sphere. Published lore names it
Cavitius; the name can stay or change.

## The faithful

### What the faithful believe

He is the god of secrets, knowledge kept and knowledge lost. Most of his
faithful worship the part of that which sounds safe: memory, discretion, the
preservation of what would otherwise be forgotten.

### Names and readings

Each congregation reads him through what it needs. Some use his name.

| Reading | Name used | Who | What they believe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Benevolent keeper | The Keeper, the One Who Remembers | Archivists, scribes, mourners | He preserves what the world would lose. Prayers are for memory and for the dead to be remembered |
| Patron of the hidden | The Whispered One | Spies, brokers, the persecuted | He protects those who must keep secrets to survive |
| The returning god | Vecna, Lord Vecna | Fringe cults | He is coming back to walk the world, and they will be rewarded. The Darkest Day cult on Fonains is one |
| The scholar's god | Vecna, openly | A respectable church on some worlds | Knowledge is sacred and should be sought. The name is used without fear |

The benevolent church that says "Vecna" openly is where players know more than
their characters. The players know Vecna is evil; the characters meet kind
people who pray to him.

### Iconography

The hand and the eye. Green eye-slit gems used as tokens, compasses and keys.
Eye tattoos on the neck. Black candles burning green. Congregations vary the
details, but the hand and eye recur.

## On Fonains

- **The Darkest Day cult** is a faithful congregation that names him and
  expects his return. He used them as bait: their ritual on George was meant to
  be found and stopped, while the sister ritual to the Princes ran on the same
  eclipse.
- **Vanifer's voice** was a Seeker chapter. It traded her the lost meaning of
  the Alpha and the secret in the Cre'Tez blood. She believes the bargain was
  fair.
- Nobody on Fonains treats him as a god. Typha knows him only as "a power
  hungry archmage. Has played many realms."

## What the gods think of him

The gods know he ascended and know what he is god of. They do not know his
plan.

- Most regard him as new, small and strange: a mortal who climbed in.
- His worship is wide and thin, so he is hard to locate and hard to oppose.
- The Accordance knows his name. Whether any of them saw his hand in the Fonains
  ritual is open.
