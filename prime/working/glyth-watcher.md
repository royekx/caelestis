# The Watcher who came back from Glyth

An Eris Morn analogue. A Watcher posted to a region of Glyth for years, who got
out and was never the same.

## Sketch

- **Posting:** a region of Glyth, the illithid world. Watchers are posted one
  per region, so she was one of several, and the others did not come back.
- **What happened:** she lived inside the elder brain's psychic range for
  years. She never took a tadpole. The range got into her anyway, and she
  still hears it.
- **Exfiltration:** she got out alone. How is open.
- **Now:** she can't switch the hearing off. It makes her the only person
  outside Glyth who knows what the brain is thinking, and it is slowly costing
  her.
- **Tell:** something physical, the way Eris has her eyes. Candidates: her
  ears, her mouth, or skin that ripples under the surface.

## What she knows

- Glyth from the inside: the colonies, the elder brain, the properly raised illithids.
- N'ghathrod's exile, and the unthinned leaving with him, all five of his
  lieutenants among them.
- Why the Fleet bans approach.

## Where she fits

- The open handler slot for Act 2.
- One of the **named-but-unseen** figures, mentioned in contradictory accounts
  before the crew meets her.
- Where she lives is open: the Order's Caelestis Sector house, whose
  Watchers' wing holds the Hall of Sight and Sound, or elsewhere aboard
  Caelestis.

## Open

- Name.
- How she got out, and what it cost.
- What she wants now: revenge, a cure, or to go back.
- Whether the Order trusts her.
