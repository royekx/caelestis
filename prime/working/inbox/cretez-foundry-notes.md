Inheritance
 

At birth, each heir is given a gem called a Bloodstone, which is bound to their soul and proves their birthright. Over generations, power has transferred to the next head of the family through various rituals involving these Bloodstones, however only two remain: the Alpha and Beta Inheritances. 


The Alpha Inheritance initiates a battle royale between the heirs with the winner being appointed as leader of the family. Those who prevail in combat inherit the birthright of the defeated and the Bloodstones judge when the bearer's soul truly concedes. Often this is only attainable through death.

 

The Beta Inheritance is a less violent approach and has been utilized for the past five generations. Heirs can willingly concede their birthright to a designated person. However, this vote of power can be contested under two conditions: 1) the decision among the heirs is not unanimous or 2) the current head of the family objects to the designated heir however this immediately concedes their birthright to their appointee. In either case, as all Bloodstone bearers must concede, the Alpha Inheritance can be invoked by any heir until all birthrights have conceded.

 

Only the current leader can invoke an inheritance ritual (with the exception of Beta Inheritance objection two). If the leader dies before invoking an inheritance then both are invoked and it us up to the heirs to decide how to appoint a leader which can involve an entirely new ritual.

Reveal
GM Notes :

Long ago, a group of fire genasi attempted to summon their god, Imix, to the material plane. There were few  among them that opposed but were severely outnumbered. Despite this, they bravely fought against. In desperation, they called out for help, for power and they got a response. Asmodeus, Lord of the Nine Hells, made a pact with the fire genasi. He gave them a weapon, Tinderstrike, which allowed them to amplify their elemental abilities and seal Imix away. Keen on not allowing this evil to return, they devoted their family to protecting Tinderstrike which, in the wrong hands, could release Imix. But making a deal with the devil came at a cost. To truly seal Imix away, he must be forgotten and with each generation the truth of their family was burned away and their corrupted bloodline gave birth to a new Tiefling lineage.

Vanifer has hidden her hatred for the family in secret, spiteful of being punished for her father's grievances. In her desire for vengeance, a voice reached out to her and told her of secret locked away in Cre'Tez bloodline. The Cre'Tez family have been the guardians of a powerful phoenix, Imix,  and even made a pact with Asmodeus, lord of the nine hells, to seal it away.
