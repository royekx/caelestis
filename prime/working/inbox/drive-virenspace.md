## Viren System

Most navigators reach Viren by taking the long route out past H'catha before
making the Astral crossing. Viren itself is a pale blue-violet star whose light
feels thin and cold, as though it has been strained through crystal. Charts
made in the system often disagree with charts made anywhere else, and the
constellations seen there look unnervingly sharp, as if the sky is closer than
it should be.

Viren is not a massive, bustling system like Realmspace. It is quieter,
stranger, and more prized by diviners, astrologers, reclusive mages, and those
looking for things best left unnamed.

### Inner Worlds

1. **Sear.** A tidally locked world of black glass dunes, red stone canyons,
   and shimmering heat. Most settlements cling to the narrow twilight band
   between the burning day side and the frozen dark side, where miners harvest
   sunglass, emberstone, and spell-reactive crystal.
2. **Nym.** A warm sea world broken by floating reed-islands, mangrove mats,
   and luminous algae blooms. Its fisher-scholars are known for reading the
   heavens in the water's reflection, and many claim Nym sometimes shows the
   sky a day early.
3. **Ilyra.** The most settled world in the system, temperate and windswept,
   with cliffside ports, observatories, and old arcane estates built to study
   the stars. If the party wants records on Zerathis, old spellwork, or where
   Mert's closet was modeled after, this is the best first stop.

### Middle Reach

4. **Mourning.** A ringed gas giant whose silver rings sing faintly when ships
   pass through them. Seven small moons drift within a common air envelope
   around the planet, and these moons host monasteries, smugglers' holds, and
   hidden gardens suspended beneath the ringlight.

### Outer Worlds

5. **Threadfall.** A long chain of shattered asteroids and broken observatories
   linked by thin veins of silver crystal. Travelers come here for salvage,
   pirates come here to disappear, and star-seers come here because the
   constellations from Casey's clue make the most sense when viewed from
   Threadfall.
6. **Vesper.** A cold, dark world of mirror-ice flats and buried ruins. At
   night the ice reflects skies that do not belong to Vesper at all, and
   explorers who camp too long often wake convinced they saw a star move
   against the heavens.
7. **The Hollow Star.** A dark shell of cracked stone and metal wrapped around
   a captive stellar ember. It glows through great fissures like a lantern
   hidden inside a corpse. Divination, teleportation, extradimensional spaces,
   and star-based magic all behave erratically near it.

### The place Mert's closet replicates

Mert's closet is a replica of the Ward of No Walls, an extradimensional
observatory on Ilyra once maintained by Zerathis.

From the outside it is just a chamber. From within, it appears to open directly
into the Viren sky, with solid footing underfoot and no visible walls: stars,
constellations, and a few personal furnishings suspended in impossible calm. It
was designed to study stellar alignments without interference from weather,
horizon, or ordinary distance. The closet is a miniature sympathetic copy of a
real place in Viren.

### Casey hook

The flickering point Casey noticed is a route-marker, a fixed stellar alignment
called the Lantern Node. Zerathis used it to chart a safe path from Ilyra to
Threadfall and from Threadfall toward the Hollow Star.

- Ilyra gives answers.
- Threadfall gives direction.
- The Hollow Star gives danger and payoff.

## Flags for sorting

- "The Hollow Star" breaks the naming rule for celestial bodies (proper name,
  not "The X").
- "Lantern Node" may collide with the Lantern of Flourishing.
- Casey's patron canon has moved on since September (Astra Virex, Zerathis as
  an earlier Starchild tethered in Virenspace); check the closet and Casey hook
  against `prime/dossiers/zerathis.html` and `astra-virex.html`.
