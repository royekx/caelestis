### H'Catha: the Pact (accepted as foundation)
- There are ten domains, each bound to one eye, with ten rulers. All of the rulers are enlightened. — **conflicts** (realms/hcatha.html and campaign/spine.html: "the eight domains")
- The rulers dreamed a Sovereign Eye together. Korey's model is Sauron's eye: it sees everything in daylight and enforces the Pact. — **not in repo**
- Shadow is the Eye's blind spot. The founders agreed they needed some privacy, and the shadow is where it lives. — **not in repo**
- When the shadow reaches a domain, the eye bound to that domain shuts on every beholder until the shadow reaches the next domain. — **not in repo**
- A shut eye is how beholders get partial sleep without dreaming a rival. — **not in repo**
- Each ruler sees only its own domain, day and night. — **not in repo**
- The Eye punishes lawbreakers with whichever eye is shut that hour (death on a Death hour). — **not in repo**
- Common Law applies everywhere in daylight: no killing, wounding, stealing or taking anyone against their will. — **not in repo**
- Each domain has one Edict, a strange law that comes from its ruler's paranoia, and the Eye enforces it. Korey's idea; he wants to revisit the Edicts later. — **not in repo**
- "The dark decides, the light keeps": nothing can be taken by force in daylight. — **not in repo**
- People believe the Eye holds deals made in daylight ("light-sworn"), so true deals are struck in the light. Korey's idea. — **not in repo**
- A new ruler can only be dreamed when a seat is open. The new ruler is an ally of whoever dreamed it, not a rival. — **not in repo**
- Sleepborn are ill-formed beholders that come from the leaks of partial sleep. They live in the shadow and light unmakes them. Some rulers hunt them, some ignore them, and some fear what they could become. — **not in repo**
- The Spindle is a sundial: its tapering shadow sweeps the disk. Night lasts longest near the Spindle and never comes near the rim. — **not in repo**
- Each ruler chooses where in its domain to keep its citadel. — **not in repo**

### H'Catha: the eclipse (accepted as foundation)
- The eclipse is caused by Turnbetl at a close pass, not Lumbe. — **not in repo** (hcatha.html lists Turnbetl only as "a flammable atmosphere")
- It comes roughly once a year, unevenly, and lasts 3–4 hours of full dark. The Sovereign Eye shuts and every ruler sleeps. — **not in repo**
- The eclipse is the only time open action is possible, and the rulers are asleep for it. They act through forces, alliances and betrayals. — **not in repo**
- An eclipse is coming soon. The crew arrives as it nears. — **not in repo**
- Lumbe stays canonical: Sarelk's laboratory, with no part in the Pact. — **not in repo** (hcatha.html: Lumbe "unattached to anything, which makes it available")
