# Module sweep: the Fonains source modules

Sources: *Princes of the Apocalypse*, *Lost Laboratory of Kwalish*, *Temple of
Moloch* (chapter 4 of a DMs Guild Infernal Machine adventure) · updated 2026-10-25

The transcripts are canon. The modules fix spellings and explain where a homebrew
detail came from. Fonains began as a run of the Kwalish one-shot and grew into a
campaign, so the modules show the seams: what was kept, what was bent, and what
was replaced.

## Spellings kept from the table

- **Seldra.** The module has Seodra. The DM spelled S-E-L-D-R-A at the table, so
  Seldra stands.
- **"Speckled amber."** The module's component is speckled gold. Kept as said.
- **"The plane of gems."** The module has the Quasi-elemental Plane of Mineral.
  Kept as said.

## Princes of the Apocalypse

Confirms:
- The four weapons: Tinderstrike (dagger), Ironfang (war pick), Windvane (spear),
  Drown (trident). The summoning line "With this key, I call forth…".
- Devastation orbs. The Gransby fire orb and the Zonlilimer earth orbs.
- Cult signs. Air is an inverted triangle with three lines (the downward-arrow
  pendants and rune darts). Earth is a triangle (the vault, letters and earthen
  guard).
- The four Haunted Keeps: Feathergale Spire, Rivergard Keep, Sacred Stone
  Monastery, Scarlet Moon Hall.
- Ogrémoch's earth portal is a "monolith" in the module.
- Aerisi Kalinoth is a moon elf with illusory wings, "prophet and queen."
- The Elder Elemental Eye stands behind the cults, and the module points to
  Tharizdun.

Homebrew divergences:
- Bastian Thermandar is a fire genasi in the module. At the table he is a blond
  human.
- In the module Vizeran DeVir forged the weapons recently and left them on the
  Fane of the Eye's altar. At the table the keys have a family history (the
  Accordance, Imix sealed by the Cre'Tez line), and Vizeran's role is unused.

Usable mechanics:
- A portal closes when its weapon is thrown or struck into it, and the weapon is
  destroyed. A ready answer for how the keys get spent.
- Gar Shatterkeel has barnacles and a crab-claw prosthetic arm. A possible echo
  of Hayden's prosthetic and his one-armed father (inference only).

## Lost Laboratory of Kwalish

Confirms (module source for table details):
- **The craft is the monastery.** In the module the floating island above the
  valley is what remains of a crashed planar craft. It tore a chunk of rock away
  trying to escape. Kwalish made it his first laboratory.
- **The Grand Master is a machine.** The bone devil is a war-machine exoskeleton
  of Kwalish's design, piloted by the quasit Whazzit. The real bone devil is the
  brain in a jar in the treasury.
- **The brains in jars** are linked into a hive mind and know Kwalish. They are
  his "brain friends."
- **Daoine Gloine** is a glass city sealed under one huge ooze from Juiblex's
  layer of the Abyss. The kenku worshipped an undead medusa, Gloine Nathair-Nathair,
  whose gaze turns victims to glass.
- **The baubles** are crystal pocket dimensions inside the city's glass statuary.
  The module's bauble table includes an arena of shadow duplicates that mirror
  the party. That is the Session 10 shadow-copy fight.
- **The jellified kenku** in a coffin is the city's old high priest, wired with
  silver cord as the control module of a clockwork kraken.
- **The canister hearts** (three humanoids, a troll, a dragon) are wired with
  silver cord and power Kwalish's powered armor.
- **Teleportation-field devices** work only in the monastery and Daoine Gloine.
  The six recall bracelets are this tech.
- **Ctenmiir** is a dwarf vampire sealed in an iron coffin on golem legs, an early
  Kwalish design, as penance.
- **West Wind**, the Grand Master's bodyguard, carries a *blade of the medusa*.
  The likely source of Hayden's Medusa sword (inference).
- Other module names: **Anaxi Zephries**, "the Cartophile"; the elder monk **Lean
  Meimbaol**; bodyguards **East Wind** and **West Wind**; the hirelings **Garret
  Levistusson**, **Mary Greymalkin** and **Gearbox** the modron; the
  **merrenoloth** ferryman.

Homebrew divergences:
- Anaxi is Kwalish's nephew at the table, not in the module.
- The module's crash is "eons ago." The table's is about 500 years ago.
- In the module the craft's origin is unknown. At the table it is Thessalar's
  craft, with N'ghathrod already aboard.

Hooks:
- **A mind flayer arcanist in stasis** sits in Kwalish's lab, kept for its
  knowledge. With N'ghathrod and the elder brain in play, this is the strongest
  open thread in the module.
- **A demilich skull with gem eyes and teeth** sits among Kwalish's panels. In the
  *Temple of Moloch* module Seodra is forced to craft exactly that skull for
  Thessalar. The skull may have travelled with the craft.
- **Kwalish's price** in the module is a piece of the characters' souls, drawn by
  his machinery.

## Temple of Moloch

Confirms:
- The temple stands over caverns where Olhydra and Ogrémoch fought and tore a rift
  between the Planes of Water and Earth. The stream carries huge gems,
  especially rubies.
- The statue of Moloch's ruby eyes are the largest rubies ever taken from the
  lake. They are wired with silver to every lab control panel. Moloch is an
  exiled archdevil whose worship faded, as Xero said.
- Thessalar was a high priest of Moloch and an artificer. He killed the other
  priests and turned the temple into his lab. He made the thessalhydra, the
  owlbear and similar beasts, and he is pursuing lichdom.
- **The five gem colors match the table exactly:** speckled gold, cloudy orange,
  emerald green (three), light purple, hazy yellow. In the module they are lost
  components of the *Infernal Machine of Lum the Mad*. At the table they are the
  planar craft's control and defense components.
- **The mechanical heart "chicken"** is the thessalheart construct. It feeds power
  to the thessalhydra and reports to Thessalar.
- **The thessalkraken** is the temple's "living demigod," worshipped in a private
  chapel before a Moloch statue reworked with tentacles and needle teeth. That
  fits the Drowned God's kraken priests.
- Area 29 holds an incomplete *apparatus of Kwalish*. The apparatus existed in
  Thessalar's time.

Homebrew divergences:
- The module's time device is a "mechanical guide." At the table it is a mini
  Texel with four bauble slots, attuned with blood.
- The module's time is Greyhawk with a Tomb of Horrors rivalry. The table set it
  in 425.
