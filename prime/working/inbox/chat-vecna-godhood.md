## Settled

### Standing process and writing rules (Korey)
- Lore lives in the repo. Rulings go to STAGING as what IS, with no audit log and no decision voice: only the fact, never how or why it was decided. — **in repo** (CLAUDE.md § Writing; prime/working/README.md; terms.json "voice" rules)
- Write specific over sparse. Use plain, precise prose; in-world fan-wiki voice for world facts and DM voice for mechanics and planning. Pages never say "decision pending"; open questions live only on the planning layer. — **in repo** (CLAUDE.md § Writing)
- Make only the files that are necessary: one BACKLOG, not ten backlog files. — **in repo** (prime/working holds a single BACKLOG.md)
- Group STAGING by page and subject, by relation, never by round or turn. — **in repo** (prime/working/STAGING.md structure)
- Naming: avoid "The" plus a vague word. Places and celestial bodies get real proper names, often with a catalogue designation, the way astronomy names Andromeda or Sagittarius A*. Constructs get plain descriptions; people may carry monikers. Locals may have their own epithets, and names vary across the cosmos. — **in repo** (CLAUDE.md § Writing "Names")
- Plan the shape, not every detail. A named world has core facts. Anything named in play needs work. Prep where the crew is going plus one ring out. — **in repo** (principles.html "one ring out")
- Contracts surface from a world already in motion, never from the crew's arrival. They fall into a few categories: loose, Seekers-tied and personal. — **in repo** (campaign/index.html, principles.html "already in motion")
- Use visions sparingly. Polly nudges Casey; he is not directed. — **in repo** (principles.html)
- The PCs remember where they came from. Their pasts are filled in retroactively, with no amnesia. — **in repo** (crew/index.html)
- Player and DM pages on the same subject are intentional, not duplicates. Jeffrey and Joffrey are two different people. — **in repo** (CLAUDE.md "Two versions"; § Naming)
- A cosmic DM timeline should show what happened, what is happening and what "will" happen, kept honest rather than pretty. — **in repo** (prime/timeline/)
- A Connections map (word association) stays DM-only, with an in-play/DM toggle. — **in repo** (prime/timeline Connections view; prime/data/connections.json)
- Build a system generator with saved, persistent results. — **in repo** (tools/survey.js, prime/data/survey.json)
- Etymology for invented languages must be recorded somewhere, and Githyanki and others get added as they appear. — **in repo** (factions/vthorama.html § Roots; BACKLOG "Languages")
- Fonains transcripts are canon; the source modules (Princes of the Apocalypse, Lost Laboratory of Kwalish, Temple of Moloch) fix only spelling and context. — **in repo** (inbox/module-sweep-fonains.md)

### Gods and worship
- Worship gives every god its power, influence and reach. Attention and worship feed each other, which is why gods gravitate to where they are worshipped. — **in repo** (glossary/index.html, cosmology/index.html)
- Gods are not physical. They are woven into the cosmos and dwell between galaxies. A god drifts toward its greatest worship, and worship's signal weakens with distance. — **in repo** (glossary/index.html "fainter"; cosmology/index.html)
- Polarity, not a "divine gate", keeps gods off worlds and from the center. Like charges push apart, with ever-greater effort until the push is unbearable. Ascension gave Vecna the same charge as Oerth's heart, so the world pushed him off. — **in repo** (glossary; working/divine-rules.md)
- Greater gods are held off at a galaxy's core and live in the drift between galaxies, known across all of them. Lesser gods can enter a galaxy but stop at a system's edge. — **in repo** (cosmology/index.html, glossary/index.html "a system's edge")
- One god goes by many names: Takhisis is Tiamat and Paladine is Bahamut. A god's names and origin stories differ across galaxies, and the eldritch galaxy has Earth-like pantheons. — **in repo** (locations/krynn.html, locations/fonains.html "Tiamat"; working/divine-rules.md)
- A lesser god once forced its way onto a world. The friction destroyed it and the world, and a Monolith answered. — **in repo** (locations/nowhere.html "lesser god")
- Most gods came into being at the Reach and grew into godhood over time, so gods have different ages. Later gods ascended from mortality. — **in repo** (factions/deities.html)
- Asmodeus and Lord Primus are the eldest of the Accordance, and Asmodeus is its face. He began good-natured, fought the demons the Reach let loose, was betrayed, and became lord of the Nine Hells: evil because someone has to do it. — **in repo** (factions/deities.html "good-natured", glossary "Lord Primus")
- The Accordance know little of Vecna. They only felt a new entity like them appear a few centuries ago. — **in repo** (factions/deities.html)
- GAL-02 has gods, but they are eldritch, not good. It has no Weave, its core is awake, and the galaxy is overrun. It plays as Call of Cthulhu. — **in repo** (campaign/index.html; prime/data/survey.json; terms.json "godless")
- Each galaxy is assigned a game system ahead of time: GAL-01 is D&D 5e. — **in repo** (cosmology/index.html, chart/index.html "game system")
- Beshaba is the lesser sister on Aethris. She finds the Fortuned heretical and blames Tymora. Neither sister knows they need each other: luck and misfortune are two sides of one coin. — **in repo** (dossiers/beshaba.html, factions/deities.html)
- The Fortuned want Sol back alive only as a component of their ritual. Beshaba's reward is power, given shape by a want she lays on Sol as a quest. She did not arrange his escape. — **in repo** (dossiers/beshaba.html; crew/sol.html, inference)

### Vecna
- Vecna is an open god, known under many names, and keeps his true name and origin secret. The name surfaces late through lore: a figure missing his left eye and left hand. — **in repo** (dossiers/vecna.html "left eye")
- His godhood ascension is complete. It happened on Oerth, his homeworld, where his worship is large and public and his Monolith is a holy site. His ritual pushed Oerth to its Zenith. — **in repo** (locations/oerth.html "Godhood Ascension")
- The Godhood Ascension is done. His current climb is the Axiomic Ascension. — **in repo** (dossiers/vecna.html, glossary)
- His realm is Cavitius. — **in repo** (locations/oerth.html, dossiers/vecna.html)
- His domains are Knowledge, Death and Trickery. His symbol is an eye in the palm of a left hand, read differently by each chapter. — **in repo** (dossiers/vecna.html, factions/deities.html)
- He is the Savathûn of this cosmos: secrets, manipulation and information, with a hold over undeath. He wants to understand the cosmos and become part of it, not to break it. His grievance: something was taken and hidden from him, and the cosmos owes an answer. — **in repo** (dossiers/vecna.html)
- His archived mortal half (Vecna, Lich) sits in the Core and is nearly redeemed. — **in repo** (dossiers/vecna-lich.html)
- Vecna uses Tharizdun rather than allying with him, and the two distrust each other. — **in repo** (working/tharizdun.md; opposition/vecna.html)
- Some worshippers are necromancers and some sincerely believe in an idea of him. The first chapter the crew meets is a benevolent church showing the hand and eye, reached through Hessa Dun, well after graduation. — **in repo** (dossiers/vecna.html "benevolent", dossiers/hessa-dun.html)
- Vecna touched H'Catha only at its founding and stays faint there. — **in repo** (locations/hcatha.html, inference)
- His musical taste shows in how he names his working. — **in repo** (dossiers/vecna.html "Circle of Fifths")
- Fighting Vecna is not this campaign's end. N'ghathrod is the main villain. Vecna has contingencies, and the late campaign is about stopping his working. — **in repo** (campaign/index.html; opposition/vecna.html)

- Vth'oramu is a mothership in the drift between systems that sends out skiffs. It can enter a system but would be seen. Strake's unidentified mass was Vth'oramu. — **in repo** (dossiers/vthoramu.html)
- The dreadnought had no wound before the siege. Its tether is a silver astral cord to somewhere unknown, and its tail is immensely long.
