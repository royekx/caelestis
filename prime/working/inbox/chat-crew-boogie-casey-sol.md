- Korey's mechanic is Sol's own because he is Misfortuned; the oath is separate because he embraces Beshaba. It persists even if he breaks the oath. — **not in repo**
- Cap: Misfortune up to proficiency bonus. Each point is −1 to a d6-rolled ability modifier, including spell save DC and spell attack. — **not in repo**
- Absorb (reaction): a friendly creature within 60 ft fails; after the die is shown, before the outcome is declared, it becomes a success and Sol gains 1 Misfortune. — **not in repo**
- Discharge (reaction): at cap, apply ± total Misfortune to any creature's roll within 60 ft; all Misfortune clears. A natural 20 stays a crit. — **not in repo**
- Absorb and Discharge share the reaction and cannot both happen in a round. — **not in repo**
- Clear Misfortune items in D&D Beyond before processing a level up. — **not in repo**
