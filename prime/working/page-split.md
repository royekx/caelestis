# Subject pages and campaign pages: the Vecna test

What a subject page holds, what a campaign page holds, and where they overlap.
Tested on Vecna, using only what is in STAGING and on finished pages today.

## The split

| | Subject page | Campaign page |
|---|---|---|
| Example | `dossiers/vecna.html` | `campaign/index.html`, `opposition/vecna.html` |
| About | a noun: one person, place, thing | verbs: what happens, in what order, why |
| Answers | what is it, what is true, how do I play it | what happens in this run, when, and what the crew learns |
| Read when | the subject turns up, at the table or in prep | prepping a stretch of the campaign |
| Test | true whenever you look | tied to an arc, a voyage, or a trigger |

The overlap is a plan. A subject page holds a plan as **intent and ability**:
what he would do, and why. A campaign page holds it as **schedule**: what he
does in this run, when, and what it costs the crew.

## Four pages, four jobs

Vecna's material lands on four pages. Each states its own kind of fact and
links to the others for the rest.

| Page | Job | Vecna there |
|---|---|---|
| **Cosmology** (finished) | how the cosmos works | how cores wake, inscriptions, Rykoe, the eye as a working |
| **Vecna's dossier** | the person | who he is, what he wants, what's hidden, how to run him |
| **Opposition: Vecna** | his lane through this campaign | where the working stands, his contingencies, the reveal ladder, the failure state |
| **Campaign** | the whole story, all forces | the theme, the shape, the arcs; one or two sentences on Vecna, linked |

## The matrix

The campaign page and the Opposition pages are two cuts through one grid:

| | Vecna | N'ghathrod and the colony | The Seekers | … |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arc 1 | | | | |
| Arc 2 | | | | |
| Arc 3 | | | | |
| Arc 4 | | | | |

- **Campaign** reads across the rows, in the campaign's chronology: what
  happens, when it matters and how. Forces are woven through it where they
  act, not set out in blocks. A force as large as Vecna may get a section of
  its own, but mostly he appears where his work touches the story.
- **An Opposition page** reads down a column: everything Vecna does, Arc 1 to 4.

The Campaign page never retells who Vecna is. It shows what he is doing in
this run, how and when, and links to the dossier and the Opposition page for
the rest. Embeds are for the few places where the Campaign page wants a whole
Opposition passage in line.

Subject pages are not in the grid. A dossier's *Threads* subsection lists the
cells and arcs the subject appears in, as links.

## Vecna, page by page

### Vecna's dossier (`dossiers/vecna.html`)

**Who he is** (in-world)
- An archmage of Oerth, its darkest-timeline world, who became a lich and then
  a god. God of secrets, knowledge, manipulation and trickery, with a hold over
  death and the undead. Domains Knowledge, Death, Trickery. Realm: Cavitius.
- The Godhood Ascension: his ritual pushed Oerth to its Zenith, the Zenith made
  him a god, and the Monolith that answered archived his mortal form as Lich
  Vecna. Ascension charged him into Oerth's heart and pushed him off the world,
  so he works through vessels. At least a few centuries ago.
- His symbol, an eye in the palm of a left hand, and how chapters read it.
- His worship: an open god under many names; large and public on Oerth, small
  hidden pockets elsewhere; hidden temples, secret texts, kept artifacts. Not
  every worshipper is a cultist. His followers are the Seekers.
- He keeps his name and origin secret. A taste for music shows in how he names
  things.

**What he wants** (in-world)
- To understand the cosmos and become part of it: where everything came from,
  what the Reach was, what was taken. His grievance: something was taken from
  him and hidden from him. He believes he could repair whatever breaks.
- His aim is the Axiomic Ascension: through opened Rykoe, into Continuity.
  His method is the eye inscription across the Erevos Galaxy, which he calls the
  Circle of Fifths. Two or three sentences here, linking to Cosmology § Vecna's
  working for how it works.

**DM notes**
- *What's hidden:* the Sovereign Eye on H'Catha was his attempt at a Zenith,
  and it failed. Vecna (Lich) sits in the Radiant Citadel, calm and almost
  redeemed. He uses Tharizdun as a contingency, and they distrust each other.
  He steers Seekers chapters with residue. No one else knows the name Circle
  of Fifths or what he is building.
- *How to run him:* he never appears in person; he acts through vessels. He
  thrives on what others do not know, so he is played through what is
  withheld. A part of him can manifest in the First Heart.
- *Threads:* N'ghathrod, the Seekers, Oerth, Tharizdun, Vecna (Lich), H'Catha,
  the Restorers; Opposition: Vecna; the arcs he drives on the campaign page.

### Opposition: Vecna (`opposition/vecna.html`)

- **Where the working stands:** in GAL-01, two hearts opened and linked on the
  upper lid, both linked to Oerth; Nowhere's heart found. Tracked as counts;
  worlds are built when play reaches them. One residue piece, carried by the
  colony.
- **His vessels in this campaign:** N'ghathrod and the colony (links to
  Opposition: colony), the Seekers chapters.
- **Contingencies, in order:** the five lieutenants through; many lesser taken
  through the colony's spread; the Restorers' aims; Tharizdun. Defeating
  N'ghathrod does not end it. A victory over him is never undone by fiat.
- **Setbacks:** an iris world lost means the iris is redrawn; a lid world lost
  costs years.
- **By arc** (the matrix cells): what Vecna's side does in Arcs 1 to 4 if no
  one stops it. Each under its own anchor.
- **The reveal ladder:** every opened heart links back to one world in
  Greyspace → why Oerth → plotted from Oerth, the hearts trace an eye → the
  name Vecna surfaces through lore (a figure missing his left eye and left
  hand) → Vecna (Lich) in the Citadel.
- **Failure state:** GAL-01's core wakes for him; GAL-03 to GAL-05 fall;
  Rykoe can open, and every linked heart becomes a door.
- **DM notes:** what is still to decide about his lane.

### Campaign (`campaign/index.html`)

- **Theme:** everyone is missing something. Vecna's line: the answer to the
  cosmos and what was taken from him (links to the dossier).
- **Shape:** N'ghathrod is the main villain; Vecna stands behind him, beyond
  direct reach; the late campaign is about stopping the working, not
  necessarily fighting him. The failure state in one sentence, linked to
  Opposition: Vecna.
- **Principle:** the campaign is not a war of the gods.
- **Arcs:** each arc's own text, plus the embedded Opposition cells for that
  arc.

## What this changes

- **Setting pages and campaign links.** The page guide says setting pages never
  link to campaign pages. Under this split, the in-world tabs still pass the
  one-shot test, and the DM notes tab is where this campaign's threads are
  allowed to link out.
- **Embeds** are for the matrix: Opposition cells into the campaign page's arcs.
  Elsewhere, pages link.
- **The colony** splits the same way: N'ghathrod's dossier (the person),
  Opposition: colony (his lane, operations, world status), Campaign (the arcs).
- **The current Ascension page** (`campaign/ascension/`, to become
  `opposition/vecna.html`) is built on retired ideas (the seat, the Figure, the
  Cast, the downward tithe, the Remaking). It is rewritten, not moved.

## To settle

The lich is settled: he is a separate character with his own dossier. Both
pages are called Vecna: the god is Vecna (Deity), `dossiers/vecna.html`, and
the lich is Vecna (Lich), `dossiers/vecna-lich.html`. In prose plain "Vecna"
means the god; the lich is always "Vecna (Lich)".

- What Vecna's side does in each arc. STAGING has the shape and the failure
  state, not the per-arc cells.
