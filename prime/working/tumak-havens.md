# Draft: Tumak's road to the havens

How Tumak finds the havens, what the runes give him on the way, and what the
sending takes from a Wayfinder. Built in the cadets'-arcs chat of 2026-11-10 on
BACKLOG's "Make each cadet's arc tangible" (Crew, crew/index.html); Korey
keeps the lore and mechanic work for it in that chat. Rules
below are in DM voice; world facts in-world. Items marked *(proposed)* wait
for Korey's yes.

## Design constraints

- **The rules hold for every cycle**, from the first Runekeeper to the
  eighth heir and after, and not only for Wan and Tu.
- **Gregory helps, and never gates.** Any navigator, a Fleet chart office or
  the crew's own legwork does his part, slower. His arc stands on its own.
- **No fixed star chart.** Havens are placed when their first hint lands, so
  the sandbox stays open.
- **A rune never equals a place.** No position is tied to a haven in advance.
- **Getting home takes adventuring, not levelling.** Runes say where; travel
  and trials get him there.

## The sending

When an heir is sent out, the Runekeeper takes from them the name of home,
the way there, and most of the whole picture, and holds it in the binding.
The Wayfinder keeps that they came from somewhere, and loose fragments: a
grandmother, a smell, the way the trees look. It is a protection for the
world, because a captured Wayfinder cannot give it away, and it is the rite
of passage: the journey home is remembering. Each haven gives some of it
back, and the Becoming gives back the rest. "Given no map and no name" on
the pages becomes "loses the name and the way".

## The runes

- **A half-rune rolled** gives a memory of home. Korey pre-writes one memory
  per position (16). For the twins each memory comes in two halves, Wan's and
  Tu's, two sides of one moment; a single heir gets it whole.
- **A matched rune** gives a mark, as now, and one hint toward the current
  target haven.
- **A hint is a Runekeeper's memory.** He lives one sense of what that
  haven's Runekeeper saw, heard, touched, or smelled and tasted, as The
  Giver passes memories on. It is how the Runekeepers guide him, in place of
  a voice explaining the way, and it sets his arc apart from the other
  cadets'.
- **The four sets are retired**: Soul, Maker, Walker and Vessel, their spell
  menus, the Pacing Rule and the exhaustion cost for extra Wayscribe casts.
  Positions keep their places on the body and carry no set.
- **Clan runes (17 to 20)**, one for each of the four clans, his own
  included. Each comes from the first haven of that clan he walks; the
  current Runekeeper's clan rune comes in the exchange at the Becoming.
  They count as runes for every number (essence runes, marks, passengers).
  Whether they count as wildcards toward anything is held.
- **The clans are bound to the heavens and to the body**, top to bottom, in
  the order the office passes:

  | Order | Clan | Heavens | Body | Old menu |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1st, the founder's | Sun crown (or brow) | the Sun | head | Soul |
  | 2nd | Skyspine | the Sky | spine | Vessel |
  | 3rd | Starfist | the Stars | fist | Maker |
  | 4th | Lunarfoot | the Moon | foot | Walker |

  A clan rune takes its clan's shape: Lunarfoot's is a crescent moon, the
  Sun clan's a sun.
- **The clan runes sit inside the Heart.** The Heart-rune holds a place for
  each clan, and a walked haven fills its clan's place. That is why the
  Heart flares at a haven. The last place fills in the exchange, and the
  Heart, whole, wakes at the Becoming.
- **The Heart (21)** is the fifth sense, already matched. See On the haven's
  world.
- **The inscription grows with the order.** An heir's inscription is 16
  rolled runes, one clan rune for each clan that has built a haven, and the
  Heart. The second heir carried 18, the third 19, the fourth 20, and every
  heir from the fifth on carries 21. The early Runekeepers held a thinner
  binding, which is how the clans "learned to work it over the generations".

## Hints

The havens are sigils of the one spell, and the fragment is its engine. A
rune matched whole hears the sigil it is bound for, and the sigil answers
with a memory its Runekeeper left in it.

- **Four senses, one hint each**: sight, hearing, touch, and smell and taste
  together. A haven's four hints are always its four senses.
- **The same order for every haven**, broad to specific: sight, hearing,
  touch, then smell and taste. Set for the first haven; revisit if play
  says so.
- **Targeting follows the haven order** (see Homebound). Matches 1 to 4 are
  hints toward the first haven on his road, 5 to 8 the second, 9 to 12 the
  third, and 13 to 16 the current Runekeeper's. Where a clan has two havens,
  the target is the one nearest the crew when its first hint lands. A haven
  is placed when its first hint lands, and stays where it is placed.
- **The math.** Every heir makes 16 matches by level 16, and 16 hints at four
  a haven is four havens: the shortest Homebound path. The twins match late
  and in bunches, so on matches alone their havens lock at about levels 8,
  11, 13 to 14 and 16. That is the backstop, not the pace.
- **Hints are clues, not gates.** A crew that works a haven out
  from two or three hints goes early; a navigator helps.
- **A walked haven names older havens outright.** Each Runekeeper walked every
  older haven as a Wayfinder, so a haven's essence knows the host worlds of
  all before it: haven 6 knows 1 to 5, and haven 1 knows none. One walk can
  skip a whole set of hints. The essence teaches the same way the hints
  come: by memory, lived through its Runekeeper's senses.

## On the haven's world

Each haven sits close to its host world's heart, behind a thin veil between
the heart and the world: the haven draws on the heart without opening it.
On the haven's world the Heart-rune stirs as a sense and leads him to the
site. It gives no power and no Homebound. The first time, it is a scene: a
rune he has never seen flares, then dulls. At the site the twins can feel
the veil and press on it, like a hand on a window screen. When they leave
the haven's world the Heart goes inert and fades until the next one. It
wakes fully only at the Becoming, which opens Homebound.

The player may guess a further rune. It stays unshown until the first
haven: there it appears, he knows it is tied to the havens, and
it stays inert. The rules page's "hidden from the figure and the table
until you reveal it" changes to "hidden until the first haven".

## Homebound and the haven order

An heir walks one haven of each clan that has built one, in a fixed order.
He starts with his own clan's nearest haven, then follows the clans in the
order the office passes, so the current Runekeeper's clan always comes
last, at the current Runekeeper's haven. The order renews the binding from
weakest to strongest. A clan with no haven yet is skipped, so the second
heir walked one haven, the third two, the fourth three, and every heir
from the fifth on walks four. The next Wayfinder starts again with their
own clan.

For Wan and Tu (Lunarfoot): the fourth haven, Lunarfoot's; then one of the
first and fifth (the founder's clan); then one of the second and sixth (the
second clan); then the seventh, the current Runekeeper's.

| Heir | Clan | Road |
|---|---|---|
| 2nd | B | 1 |
| 3rd | C | 1, 2 |
| 4th | Lunarfoot | 1, 2, 3 |
| 5th | A | 1, 2, 3, 4 |
| 6th | B | 2, 3, 4, 5 |
| 7th | C | 3, 4, 1 or 5, 6 |
| 8th, Wan and Tu | Lunarfoot | 4, 1 or 5, 2 or 6, 7 |

Reaching a haven out of order, its trial is not open, as at the current
Runekeeper's haven today.

At the current Runekeeper's haven the exchange gives the last clan rune and
sends him home. Arriving home with the havens walked completes Homebound. The rules page's "order is free until the last"
changes to this.

## Mishaps

An Astral or Deep Astral mishap that lands a twin at a haven lands at the
current target. The crew now knows where it is and still has to get there:
one twin alone cannot take the trial, so the twin already there does not
help.

## A walked haven

Both twins present and the trial passed, the haven gives:

- **A spell** from the haven's clan, chosen from a short menu in the way
  the old Wayscribe menus worked: one free cast a day, then with spell
  slots. Up to seven over the campaign. Needs a mechanic review.
  Every clan is named as Lunarfoot is, a celestial body and a body part.
  *(proposed)* The body part sets the clan's menu, and the four old menus
  pass to them: Lunarfoot the Walker menu
  (feet, walking), and the other three clans the Maker (hands), Soul
  (head and heart) and Vessel (core) menus, rethemed.
- That Runekeeper's chapter of the order's history, and memories of home:
  its senses, its people. The name of home and the way back return only at
  the Becoming, so a Wayfinder taken mid-journey still cannot give the world
  away.
- A permanent mark.
- The essence's knowledge of every older haven.
- The clan's rune, if it is the first haven of that clan he walks.
