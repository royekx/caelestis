# Draft: Tumak's road home, what stays open

The ruled parts of Tumak's road are on the pages (change audit, 2026-11-11):
`prime/rules/index.html` § Tumak, `prime/crew/tumak.html`, the Runekeepers,
the homeworld, the Dissolution fragment and the Runekeeper's dossier. This
draft holds only what is still open, proposed or kept as an idea. When an
item is ruled, it goes to its pages and leaves this list; when the list is
empty, delete the draft.

| Tag | Means |
|---|---|
| **[open]** | Undecided |
| **[proposed]** | Offered, not yet ruled |
| **[alt]** | An alternative under consideration |
| **[idea]** | A brainstorm, kept so it is not lost; not weighed yet |

## Near-term

**Before V007**

- **[open]** Korey's pass on Lunarfoot's four hints and the haven's exact
  site at Selûne and the Tears of Selûne. **[proposed]** The four hints,
  the fourth Runekeeper's memories:
  1. Sight (V007): a sky of slow-tumbling silver stones lit by a great moon,
     a blue-and-white world beyond, and one crescent-shaped rock turning
     among the rest.
  2. Hearing: a low ringing like a struck bell as stone touches stone, and
     wind where no wind should be.
  3. Touch: cold, glassy rock under bare feet, worn into a trail of
     footprints, and the light pull of almost no weight.
  4. Smell and taste: iron and ozone, and under them sap touched by
     lightning, the homeworld's own smell.
- **[open]** A check that losing the name and the way at the sending sits
  with how the player plays the twins.
- **[proposed]** The first Wayfind is what wakes the first hint (written
  that way in the V007 beat; confirm).

## The conflict

- **[open]** How the twins learn that being two is wearing the binding
  down. **[proposed]** Through the havens' era records: each era shows the
  order as it was, and the records cannot explain what is happening now.
- **[open]** Whether the crisis band sets off the unhiding by itself, or
  the DM starts it at 12 or more when the story can carry it (the
  simulation's lean); whether a walked haven is worth 2 against Strain.
  Also on BACKLOG (locations/wayfinders-homeworld.html).
- **[alt] Why the fragment split.**
  - Chance: twins are rare, and it happened now.
  - The seeker's reach: if the seeker is sealed in the heart, they reach
    for the fragment they once held, and twin heirs gave the reach
    something to pull on.
  - Twins before: earlier eras had twin heirs and the fragment always took
    one; it splits now because the binding is thin. Learned from the
    records.

## Origin

- **[open]** What threatened the world, and what the seeker went after. It
  may matter at the first haven.
- **[open]** The seeker's place: a member of Dawnbrow, or of an older clan
  close to it that was lost afterward. **[proposed]** The lost clan: the
  real rhyme says knees where the homeworld's says hands, so the lost clan
  is the one cut from the song, and the player can notice.
- **[alt] Where the seeker went.** Sealed in the heart (the order hid the
  Atrium; the danger is at home); thrown to the far side with the link; or
  lost helping to close it.
- **[proposed] Life in the fold.** Inside its heart, the heart lights the
  world: the luminous haze, like light beneath water, is its sky now.
- **[idea]**
  - The homeworld was one of Vecna's first experiments, and he may have
    linked more of its neighbours than one; the seeker tried to fix the
    bleed and made it worse.
  - The world on the far side of the link may be Oerth.
  - A world disappearing is no quiet event either: someone may have recorded
    the homeworld vanishing five centuries ago.
  - The first Runekeeper's haven is half-lost: nobody has walked it for two
    or three generations, and the order has forgotten part of why it does
    this. The road allows it (the sixth and seventh heirs could take haven 5
    for Dawnbrow).
  - The first Runekeeper, with no havens to walk, had to place four anchors
    when they first went out. Sits uneasily with the idea above.
