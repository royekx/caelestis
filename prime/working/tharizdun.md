# Tharizdun

An imprisoned god of entropy who wants out. If he cannot get out, he will bring
everything to him.

## The prison

He is sealed in a prison with no address in any known cosmology. He cannot
leave it. What he can do is pull: anything delivered to him becomes his, and
the delivery mechanism he prefers is a corrupted Monolith.

## Corrupted Monoliths

A corrupted Monolith is a tunnel to his prison. It holds what it takes locally
instead of relaying it to the Core, and what it holds is drawn on toward him.
The Vanished of Fonains went there. Scholars on Fonains guessed the Shadowfell,
which was the best framework they had. The prison's landscape is a shadow realm
of jagged rocks, rubble and laughter.

His marks are spirals, a fanged eye in a split black crystal, and the words
"feed, consume, devour."

## The Day of Chaos

The day the four Princes of Elemental Evil enter Fonains together. The barriers
between planes break down, and Tharizdun gets his chance to leave. His avatar,
Shothragot, speaks of it, and so does Vecna.

## How the Fonains Monolith formed

1. Five cultists held a ritual. Four channeled the Princes, and the fifth held
   pieces of a Tharizdun artifact: a black crystal.
2. The ritual was the Zenith, and Vecna nudged it.
3. A Monolith manifested in response, as all Monoliths do.
4. The crystal replicated into the Monolith as it formed. That was the
   corruption.

The same eclipse carried Vecna's own cult ritual on George, which the
Darkest Day party disrupted. That ritual was bait.

## Vecna and Tharizdun

Use, not alliance. Each believes he is using the other.

- **Tharizdun** sees Vecna as a door-opener who nudges Zeniths.
- **Vecna** sees Tharizdun as a battering ram against the barriers, with his
  own exit planned before Tharizdun gets out.
- Vecna finds Tharizdun repugnant: entropy, power without architecture, the
  proof of where that ends.
- Vecna knew the Fonains Zenith would be ridden. The affront is that Tharizdun
  took more than his share: the Vanished, and a Monolith that should have been
  usable.
- Lola's Boys breaking the Monolith hurt both of them.
