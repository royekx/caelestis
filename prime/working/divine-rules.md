# Divine rules

How gods get power, why they cluster, why they cannot walk on worlds, and where
they live.

## Worship is power

Every god's power, influence and reach come from worship. A god with no
worshippers keeps existing but can do almost nothing. Belief is also how a god
learns: a god knows what its faithful see, report and pray about, and little
beyond that. Asmodeus put it plainly on Fonains: "the way we get information is
through our worshippers, through foot soldiers."

### Concentration and reach

Worship has two dimensions, and a god trades one for the other.

| | Concentration | Reach |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Many worshippers in one place | Worshippers spread across many places |
| What it buys | Force: miracles, avatars, direct intervention on that world | Awareness and a foothold almost anywhere |
| What it costs | Blind and weak elsewhere | Thin everywhere; little force at any one point |
| Example | Tymora and Beshaba on Aethris | Vecna |

### Why pantheons cluster in spheres

Attention follows worship, and worship follows attention. A god answers where
it is believed in; answered prayer draws more belief; more belief draws more
attention. Over centuries the loop pulls a god toward one world, then one
system, then one sphere. Pantheons settled where their faithful were densest
after the Reach and have stayed, because leaving would mean starting the loop
again somewhere colder.

A god can preach on a far world. Most don't, because a handful of believers in
another sphere gives almost nothing back.

## The divine gate

No god walks on a world in person. They act through apparitions, avatars,
chosen agents and champions.

The reason is the Yearning. Worlds were formed from the Reach's debris and their
hearts are pieces of it. Reach-born gods are the souls of the Reach world,
reshaped. When a god's full presence stands on a world, the world's heart pulls
at it the way child hearts pull toward the First Heart: like calls to like. A
god who stays risks being drawn in and anchored, bound to that world's fate.

The same pull, scaled up, is why the approach to the center kills gods. The
First Heart pulls hardest of all.

| | On a world | Approaching the center |
|---|---|---|
| Reach-born gods | Strong pull; full presence is dangerous | Lethal |
| Later gods (Vecna) | Weaker pull; still unsafe | Harmful, survivable for a time; still cannot cross |
| Mortals | No pull | Dangerous, crossable |

An apparition or avatar is thin enough that the pull finds little to hold.
That is why Vecna appeared as a projection in The Darkest Day, and why the
Accordance spoke through visions and chose champions: "even the gods have laws."

## Realms

Each god has a realm, a divine plane of its own. A realm is anchored to the
sphere where the god's worship is densest, and it can only be reached from
that sphere. This is why the Flow silences divine magic: past the sphere's edge,
a cleric's god has no road to them.

A god's realm moves only if its centre of worship moves, which takes
generations.
