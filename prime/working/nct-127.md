# Neo Constellation Troupe 127

A travelling band, and Korey's nod to NCT 127. It is a seed for now, kept off
the player side. If the players tug on it, it becomes real.

## Sketch

- **Who:** nine touring performers on one ship, known in every port as
  NCT 127.
- **Their songs** are opposites of NCT 127's titles, never the titles
  themselves. NCT 127 has both "Kick It" and "Punch", so the troupe sings
  "Punch It" and "Kick". Other candidates: "Cherry Dud" (Cherry Bomb),
  "Rumour Mill" (Fact Check), "Run" (Walk), "Irregular" (Regular),
  "2 Goodies" (2 Baddies), "Nobody Says" (Simon Says), "Peel" (Sticker).
- **Where a flyer could turn up:** the notice board at the Weeping Goddess,
  or a cantina the crew stops at on the way.
- **The flyer, drafted:**

  > PINNED TO THE BACK-WALL BOARD
  > **Neo Constellation Troupe 127**
  > Nine voices on one ship, playing every port between here and bearing 127.
  > Known in every port as NCT 127, they Punch It at the Weeping Goddess the night the Fleet clears their berth.
  > Rumour Mill says all nine are real, and the ship hums the harmonies.
  > Every port they play leaves its mark on the hull.

## Open
