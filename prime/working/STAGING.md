Sheet text, level 4, as D&D Beyond custom features:

**Wayfinder's Inscription.** Runes cover your skin, one for each level you
have reached; they are your Essence Runes (4). A rune glows when charged. As
a Bonus Action, you can expend a Psionic Energy Die to charge one Essence
Rune; Sorcery Points charge them as Runechild describes. Outside combat,
charging takes no action. Charged runes stay lit until you spend them or
finish a Long Rest.

**Wayfind.** You hold one mark for each matched rune (1). Placing a mark
where you stand takes 1 hour, or a Magic action and 2 charged runes. A mark
sits on a place or on a ship you could crew. Ending a mark requires no
action. As a Magic action, you expend 1 charged rune and teleport to one of
your marks within 60 feet, along with 1 willing creature within 5 feet of
you. As a Bonus Action, you can look through one of your marks, seeing (but
not hearing) everything within 30 feet of it until the start of your next
turn or until you stop. While you look, you are Blinded to your own
surroundings.
