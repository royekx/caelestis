## Rulings of 2026-10-25 not yet on their pages

### Cosmology and gods (cosmology/, realms/, the divine-rules draft)
- Worship gives gods power, influence and reach.
- Gods can't walk on worlds or reach the center because of polarity: like charges push apart, and the push grows until it is unbearable. The center harms them without destroying them.
- A god worshipped on several worlds is one god under several names. Takhisis is Tiamat; Paladine is Bahamut.

### Vecna (campaign/ascension/, realms/oerth, factions/)
- Vecna's godhood is complete. He ascended on Oerth, his homeworld, a darkest-timeline world where his hold is strongest; its Monolith is a holy site.
- Ascension charged Vecna to Oerth's heart and pushed him off the world, so he works through vessels.
- The Ascension still to come is his climb to Axiom.
- Vecna is an open god with a following under many names. His followers are the Seekers.
- Vecna uses Tharizdun as a contingency. It is use, not alliance.

### Tharizdun and the Princes
- The Day of Chaos is when the Princes of Elemental Evil break the barriers so Tharizdun can leave his prison.
- The purple slit-pupiled eye the party saw in the year 425 is the Elder Elemental Eye, Tharizdun.

### Timing (timeline, campaign/, realms/fonains)
- Caelestis's present sits a few months after the Fonains Monolith broke, level with Fonains Session 28.
- Session 28 is two to three months after the Monolith broke.
- Krynn's present is level with Caelestis's.
- Thessalar's planar craft fell about 500 years ago.

### Fonains (realms/fonains, dossiers/thessalar, Fonains doc)
- N'ghathrod was already a prisoner aboard Thessalar's craft. Lola's Boys, sent back by the Accordance, took its control and defense gems; without them it fell, and the crash let him escape.
- The craft is a megadungeon (the Mad Mage dungeon). Daoine Gloine is a broken-off piece of it. The Thessalkraken landed separately in the sea and is the Drowned God's kraken.
- The craft's gems formed where Olhydra and Ogrémoch collided. They are not Reach residue.
- Benedetta fell ill at Rihiri's birth, from Vanifer's slow siphoning for the Bloodstone, and died two years later.

### Krynn and the dragons
- The dragons that left Fonains and most worlds set out to face Takhisis and have not reached Krynn. Their own politics hold them; the reason is unrevealed.
- An aspect of Bahamut is trapped by Takhisis in the ruined temple of Paladine on Krynn. It speaks through Eldree's egg on Fonains.
