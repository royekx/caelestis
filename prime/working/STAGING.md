# Staging

What IS, ruled and waiting to be written into pages. Grouped by the section of the
wiki it lands in, then by subject; each subject lists its target pages. World
facts are written in-world; rules and play notes in DM voice. Rewrite an entry
when a ruling changes; delete it once the page carries it. See
[README.md](README.md) for the flow and the writing rules.
