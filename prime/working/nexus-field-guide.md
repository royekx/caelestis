# Nexus field guide: Peoples and Perils of the Drift

Sources, omissions and stat cross-checks for `spelljammer-nexus/peoples.html` and `spelljammer-nexus/perils.html`. The Nexus pages have no DM notes tab, so this draft holds what would go there.

## Rulings (Korey, 2026-10-29)

1. Published 5e Spelljammer and core D&D lore is cadet canon unless a DM page says otherwise (now in PAGE-GUIDE § 8). AAG air-envelope stages are allowed. The Elven Imperial Navy stays cut; the pages say "elven fleets" without a name.
2. Neogi hatchlings are described, though that identifies the crate creature on the record.
3. The astral dreadnought is on Perils as the published creature, rarely seen. Nothing beyond the book: no sensor or mass signature, no instrument drift or gravitational variance, no territory or patrol, no tail or astral cord, nothing linking it to Vth'oramu or the Cairn bearing.
4. Glyth is not named. The mind flayer entry says illithid worlds exist and the Fleet keeps clear of them.

Defaults that stand: beholder dream-birth omitted, psychic wind off, star lancer included. Ship gravity follows `wildspace.html` (a plane through the hull along the keel); nothing ties it to the tyrant ship. Nothing on either page says what powers a helm beyond the spelljammer at it.

## Left off on purpose

DM-sensitive material, from the plan's § 4 and the rulings above.

- **Mind flayers.** Hosts that survive, chitin under the skin, unreadable or mind-blanked minds, "Oramu", tadpole couriers, brine casks and crates, colonies without a brain (so the MM line that solitary mind flayers are rogues and outcasts is off), the unthinned and bearers, illithids aboard a living ship, illithid sects, the Seekers, the eye in a wheel, the voice of a greater brain, Glyth. Also off: the rare published complication in which a remnant of the host's mind survives ceremorphosis (older lore, the "partial personality"), since it brushes against hosts that survive; the elder brain's five-mile creature sense, Wildjammer's renegade mind flayers and the time-travel origin, and psurlons working with mind flayers.
- **Astral dreadnought.** Everything in ruling 3. The book's line that its tail trails off into the void is left off with the tail.
- **Neogi.** Brands, marks and sigils (BAM's "neogi mark themselves and those they capture" is off), Astral scent or foreign origin, supplying captives to illithids, Vocath. The hatchling description is in, per ruling 2. Lifejammer helms are off (helm power).
- **Beholders and kin.** The Sovereign Eye, the Pact, the eclipse, sleepborn, domains and the ten rulers, partial sleep, rays as terrain, adamantine blocking sight, Vaelorix and the wisp-lotus, Ostekk as a builder, dream-birth (so the MPMM line that a gazer is a beholder's dream made real is off), the dead beholder's eye still cancelling magic. Beholder-propelled ships are off (helm power).
- **Githyanki.** Qitru, the star lancer link (the star lancer entry names no rider), H'Catha, Vocath, Miken's beacon, Vlaakith, Tu'narath.
- **Astral elf.** Aethris, Fortuned and Misfortuned, Tymora and Beshaba, the wanted notice, the Watchers and Ezra, the Elven Imperial Navy.
- **Autognome.** Whether autognomes cast, look-alikes, maker deaths, a pull or resonance in their cores, beholder-built automata, Lantan.
- **Plasmoid.** Objects formed or kept inside, nucleus shards.
- **Halfling.** Hidden worlds, runes, Wayfinders, Lunarfoot; also any line about halflings keeping apart, which leans toward a hidden homeworld.
- **Tiefling and genasi.** Genasi lines turned tiefling by a pact.
- **Locathah, kuo-toa, ogres, giff, mercane.** Their H'Catha roles, the mercane working for Vocath, the kuo-toa god-by-belief lore; also the mercane's origin as the creation of commerce gods (cosmology of worship) and the giff's gods behind Firearms Mastery. The giff homeworld story stays cut.
- **Thri-kreen.** Clockwork horrors speaking thri-kreen.
- **Minotaurs.** Krynn's seafaring minotaurs.
- **Kindori.** A kindori skeleton fitted with a helm (helm power). The V007 calf and Bartholomew's tracking beat.
- **Off both pages.** Clockwork horrors, will-o'-wisps and the hollowed wisps, the behir, crystalline constructs, the shadow ship, psychic wind, liches, Vecna, deities, the Seekers, the Order, Monoliths, Zeniths, residue, Starchildren, dragon numbers and migration, Takhisis, Tiamat, Bahamut, Greelob and Orlob, Clint, where pools lead, a colour key, Nowhere, Sardax and who attacked the *Moonraider*.

## Cold review (2026-10-29)

A fresh reviewer read both pages against `prime/`. Fixes applied: the beholder's partial sleep cut (the Pact mechanism); the githyanki mercenary line cut (points at Qitru); silver swords as a mark of githyanki rank; the mercane grudge line cut; "most of their kin are smaller" (the astereater is huge); the spectator's fight-to-the-death and vanish-on-theft lines cut; dead magic reduced to "spells fail" (the spell-slot ruling stays in DM notes); "astral reaver" defined and raiders graded Dangerous; the dreadnought's "head to tail" dropped; resistances the fixed key implied were absent added (dwarves poison, air genasi lightning, goliaths cold, shadar-kai necrotic, dohwar cold, fire, frost and storm giants); stacked negations on the psurlon and the dragons rewritten; legendary resistance worded as "shrugs off the first few effects it would otherwise fail to resist"; giff aim, mind flayer once-a-day spells and the astral elf's cantrip choice made exact.

## Open

- **Giff magic resistance.** *Wildjammer* gives giff advantage on saves against spells and magic, and says they could not cast until recent decades. The *AAG* giff and the SJA and BAM giff blocks have neither, and Ryeback runs on the SJA block. Left off. Add it to the giff entry, or keep it off?
- **Sharchinid.** *Wildjammer*'s ship-hunting shark senses helms through the drift and links to a helm to follow a ship at spelljamming speed. The DM rule is that nothing is met at speed (V007 crossing). Left off.
- **Renegade mind flayers.** *Wildjammer* has illithids freed from an elder brain who no longer need brains to live. It sits next to "colonies without a brain" on the § 4 list. Left off.
- **Space clown.** A BAM fiend; left off for tone. Add to Boarders if wanted.
- **Meteor spectators.** What Greelob and Orlob are bound to, and whether the published rule (a summoned spectator vanishes if its charge is stolen or destroyed early) is table knowledge. Off the page until ruled.
- **Kindori calves.** No published calf block or calf behaviour. If the crew should learn something about calves before V007, it needs a ruling.
