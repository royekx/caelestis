Timeline

What has happened, what is happening, and what will happen if nobody intervenes, across every campaign in the cosmos. The DM's truth, never shown to players. Overview places everything on one compressed time axis; Threads follows chosen people and objects and shows where they meet. Session detail lives in Voyages, arc shape in the Spine, and the opposition's plan in the Ascension. Entries are kept in prime/timeline/timeline.json.
