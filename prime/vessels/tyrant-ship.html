The Tyrant
A derelict tyrant ship in Toril orbit, most of the way into the crew’s hands.

Vaelorix’s ship, and he is dead. Nobody aboard knows that, and nobody on H’Catha knows it either — he was H’Cathan and landless, so the ship arriving reads as a claimant returning rather than as a trader.

The interior holds the Containment Engine, Fourth Series, built by Ostekk around a piece of the Rod, and the garden that produced Bartholomew’s unbloomed flower. Everything the crew has fought aboard it is Ostekk’s: the clockwork horrors, the automatons, and the behir that cannot cross the field.

Unfinished business with a known inventory. The crew retreated to Cargo with the helm and left without seating it, so what remains is a short list rather than a mystery: drop the Command Deck field, seat the helm in the gaze cradle, and the ship flies.
