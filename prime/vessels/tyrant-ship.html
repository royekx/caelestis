The Tyrant
A tyrant ship in Toril orbit, taken by the crew in V006.

Vaelorix’s ship, and he is dead. The crew found his body in V005. Nobody on H’Catha knows it — he was H’Cathan and landless, so the ship arriving reads as a claimant returning rather than as a trader.

It held the Containment Engine, Fourth Series, built by Ostekk around a piece of the Rod. Gregory took it from between the eyestalks in V006 and it rides in Tumak’s bag of holding; its cradle sits empty above the repair-bay pod on the Hollow Deck. The garden at the foot of the garden shaft holds Vaelorix’s research specimens, including the bloom that woke Bartholomew’s Aspects. Everything the crew has fought aboard is Ostekk’s: the clockwork horrors, the automatons, and Ostekk-10, the behir, which they ejected through Cargo.

Done. The field is down: the crew cut the flowers and tore the great one off the corpse. Pffred and Krik'Lit fitted the helm and rigged a crew station for each great eye; it seated in the V006.5 interlude, and four of the ten eyes answer. OSTEKK-2 still lies on the Hollow Deck. Most things leave through Cargo, with the expulsion worked from the Command Deck, and there is still no fire suppression aboard.
