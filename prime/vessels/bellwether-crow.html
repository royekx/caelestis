Bellwether Crow
The Caelestis corvette sent to look in on Cairn Station, raided on Cairn's lane about a year ago.

Bellwether Crow is a Caelestis light corvette. About a year ago the Fleet sent it to look in on Cairn Station, with Clint Dashforest leading its crew and Joffrey aboard as its junior spelljammer. On Cairn's lane it came on two pirate ships in the middle of a handover and fought them. Clint was lost to a color pool and many of the crew died, and Joffrey took the helm and brought the survivors to Cairn, where the station repaired the ship before it went home.

History

The filing

M. Tarroway, the cartographer at Strake Station, pulled a pattern out of the noise at the limit of Strake's scope: Strake saw things on Cairn's lane that Cairn did not report. His filing went up the chain, and on it the Fleet sent Crow out. Its crew were told to look in on Cairn. The filing behind the order stayed with the office that sent them.

The raid

Crow's course to Cairn ran up the lane. Short of the station it came on pirates mid-handover, their captives half moved to a courier of a build nobody aboard could name. The pirates sailed two ships: a main ship with her name and her own mark on the hull, and a smaller ship in company. With the captives strung between hulls they could not scatter, so they ran up the common pirate crossbones and struck to drive Crow off. The crossbones showed what they were and named nobody; the main ship's hull named her. Aboard the smaller ship a figure gave the orders, with a seal hanging on them in plain view.

To draw the fight away from his crew, Clint went aboard the smaller ship and took its helm. His crew saw him there with the figure and the seal. A color pool opened by chance beside the fight and took that ship, with Clint, the figure and its pirates aboard. With that ship gone, and the figure and the crates aboard it, the main ship had nothing left worth the fight, and Crow's crew drove her off. She got away down the lane. Many of Crow's crew died, the senior helm and deck officers among them. Joffrey, the junior spelljammer, took the helm, flew the ship out of the fight and held the survivors together, and from then on they looked to him.

Cairn

Cairn Station took the crippled Crow in, and repairs took days. Cairn's station master had Caelestis told that Crow was safe and under repair, and relayed the raid as its crew told it. When the repairs were done, Crow went home.

The report

Crow's report gave the raid on the approach, Clint lost, Joffrey at the helm, and Cairn's help in getting the ship home. Joffrey was commended on the survivors' account as the hero of it. The Fleet carries Clint as lost to a color pool, gone overboard, and honors him as a hero. From the survivors it recorded the main ship's name and hull mark, and ordered escorts on Cairn's lane against her in SA-005. It drew the figure's seal from their accounts in FR-016, and the registry returned no match. Cairn was thanked. Crow had gone to look at Cairn and found it in order, so its report answered Tarroway's filing, and his later filings were set aside.

In the campaign

The crew has not seen Crow. Its report is WM-002 in the Fleet Intelligence Archive, which the crew has not read.

What's hidden

  • The raiders. They were pirates in Vocath's hire, running his captive line, and the receiving hull was a courier of the Vth'orama colony. The figure on the smaller ship was Vocath's overseer, one of his own crew and the one paying the pirates. The overseer put on Vocath's personal seal for the handover, to show the pirates whom he spoke for; he does not wear it day to day. The same seal was on the crates aboard, which outsiders never see. The seal goes on Vocath's darker dealings and is registered nowhere, so FR-016 matches nothing the Fleet holds, and the Fleet hunts the main ship by her hull. With the overseer, her paymaster, and Vocath's sealed cargo gone through the pool, the fight was not worth it to the main ship, and Crow's crew drove her off. She is still free, and the Fleet hunts her under SA-005. Mirt has read WM-002 and FR-016 and knows the seal on sight. He knows Vocath is connected to the raid but not how far, cannot prove it, and says nothing.
  • The warning. The colony courier at the handover pulled away from the fight and carried word to Zhauveth that Crow was coming to Cairn. Nobody in the office that sent Crow is taken, so nobody had warned Vocath before the raid.
  • Cairn. The colony holds the station. Its station master is one of N'ghathrod's taken, and one of Zhauveth's malformed spawn holds the rest of the crew by nearness, out of sight. Crow's crew would leave, so the illithid made no attempt on them. The survivors felt the station was a little off and put it down to shock and grief, and the feeling faded on the way home. Cairn's own scope log for that watch, written up as routine, shows no contact. Strake logged a contact during Crow's approach, inside Cairn's range.
  • Joffrey. During the stay a colony courier brought one of N'ghathrod's copies up the lane, and the station master took Joffrey. The colony wanted a man inside Caelestis whom the survivors follow, and a warning if anyone looks at Cairn again; it knows nothing of A.T.L.A.S. Joffrey knows Crow was sent to look in on Cairn, and nothing of who asked. He turned over in some months.
  • Clint. The pool dropped the smaller ship on Fonains about a year ago, with Clint, the overseer, its pirates and Vocath's sealed cargo aboard. Clint got away and lost his communication piece, and the overseer and the pirates hunted him for a while. He is alive there. Because Vocath's cargo had gone through the pool with the ship, the overseer called Vocath by sending stone as soon as the pool dropped them, and told him that Clint died in the hunt; Vocath believes his witness is dead. How and when Vocath answered depends on whether Clint landed before or after the Fonains Monolith rose. Vocath's trade on Fonains began after the Monolith broke. Clint knows the seal on sight and went aboard with the overseer wearing it: what he saw is the proof Mirt lacks.

Threads

  • One survivor has seen that Joffrey changed after the voyage. The crew can find them by pulling Crow's roster.
  • FR-016 is the seal the survivors saw on the figure aboard the smaller ship. The crew has seen the same seal on the rod and the Sky Dock crate, and Boogie on the small crate of Fonains shards he opened shortly before Voyage 001, before he came to the Academy as a late intake; the neogi crate delivered to Mirt carries it too, unchecked. Set beside WM-002, it puts one of Vocath's people aboard the pirates' smaller ship, and the name and hull mark in WM-002 point to the main ship he hired.
  • Tarroway's filings on the discrepancy are in LG-003: the first, which sent Crow, and the later ones, set aside after its report.
  • Tarto and Saerthe both feel they could have done something for Clint.
