Bellwether Crow
The Caelestis corvette sent to look in on Cairn Station, raided on Cairn's lane about a year ago.

Bellwether Crow is a Caelestis light corvette. About a year ago the Fleet sent it to look in on Cairn Station, with Clint Dashforest leading its crew and Joffrey aboard as its junior spelljammer. On Cairn's lane it came on raiders in the middle of a handover and fought them. Clint was lost to a color pool and many of the crew died, and Joffrey took the helm and brought the survivors to Cairn, where the station repaired the ship before it went home.

History

The filing

M. Tarroway, the cartographer at Strake Station, pulled a pattern out of the noise at the limit of Strake's scope: Strake saw things on Cairn's lane that Cairn did not report. His filing went up the chain, and on it the Fleet sent Crow out. Its crew were told to look in on Cairn. The filing behind the order stayed with the office that sent them.

The raid

Crow's course to Cairn ran up the lane. Short of the station it came on raiders mid-handover, their captives half moved from one hull to another. With the cargo strung between two ships the raiders could not scatter, so they struck to drive Crow off. To draw the fight away from his crew, Clint boarded one of the raiders' ships and seized it. A color pool opened by chance beside the fight and took that ship, with Clint and its raiders aboard. Many of Crow's crew died. Joffrey took the helm, flew the ship out of the fight and held the survivors together, and from then on they looked to him.

The crew glimpsed a marking on the raiders' hull during the fight. At debrief each survivor drew it, and every drawing came out different; none matched the registry. The drawings stand in Crow's report in the Fleet Intelligence Archive, and the composite made from them was withdrawn as unreliable.

Cairn

Cairn Station took the crippled Crow in, and repairs took days. Cairn's station master had Caelestis told that Crow was safe and under repair, and relayed the raid as its crew told it. When the repairs were done, Crow went home.

The report

Crow's report gave the raid on the approach, Clint lost, Joffrey at the helm, and Cairn's help in getting the ship home. Joffrey was commended on the survivors' account. The Fleet carries Clint as lost to a color pool: he went overboard. It ordered escorts against raiders on Cairn's lane in SA-005, and Cairn was thanked. Crow had gone to look at Cairn and found it in order, so its report answered Tarroway's filing, and his later filings were set aside.

In the campaign

The crew has not seen Crow. Its report is WM-002 in the Fleet Intelligence Archive, which the crew has not read.

What's hidden

  • The raiders. They were Vocath's people, running his captive line, and the second hull was a courier of the Vth'orama colony. The marking on their hull is Vocath's personal seal, which is registered nowhere. Mirt has read the debrief drawings and knows the seal on sight.
  • The warning. Vocath's people pulled back and warned Zhauveth that Crow was coming to Cairn. Nobody in the office that sent Crow is taken, so nobody had warned Vocath before the raid.
  • Cairn. The colony holds the station. Its station master is one of N'ghathrod's taken, and one of Zhauveth's malformed spawn holds the rest of the crew by nearness, out of sight. Crow's crew would leave, so the illithid made no attempt on them. The survivors felt the station was a little off and put it down to shock and grief, and the feeling faded on the way home. Cairn's own scope log for that watch, written up as routine, shows no contact. Strake logged a contact during Crow's approach, inside Cairn's range.
  • Joffrey. During the stay a colony courier brought one of N'ghathrod's tadpoles up the lane, and the station master took Joffrey. The colony wanted a man inside Caelestis whom the survivors follow, and a warning if anyone looks at Cairn again; it knows nothing of A.T.L.A.S. Joffrey knows Crow was sent to look in on Cairn, and nothing of who asked. He turned over in some months.
  • Clint. The pool dropped the raiders' ship on Fonains, with Clint and its raiders aboard, about a year ago, before the Monolith rose. Clint got away. He is alive there.

Threads

  • One survivor has seen that Joffrey changed after the voyage. The crew can find them by pulling Crow's roster.
  • The debrief drawings in WM-002 disagree with one another, so they prove nothing alone. Set beside the seal drawn plainly in FR-016, they point at Vocath.
  • Tarroway's filings on the discrepancy are in LG-003: the first, which sent Crow, and the later ones, set aside after its report.
  • Tarto and Saerthe both feel they could have done something for Clint.
