Bellwether Crow
The Caelestis corvette sent to look in on Cairn Station, which broke up a pirate handover of captives on Cairn's lane about a year ago.

Bellwether Crow is a Caelestis light corvette. About a year ago the Fleet sent it to look in on Cairn Station, with Clint Dashforest leading its crew and Joffrey aboard as its junior spelljammer. On Cairn's lane it came on two pirate ships handing captives over to a third vessel, and its crew chose to intervene. Clint led a few of the crew aboard one pirate ship, took her helm and drew half the fight away, and a color pool took that ship with all of them aboard. Joffrey took charge on Crow's side, led the recovery of the captives and got the rest of the crew out. Crow drove the other pirate ship off and carried the captives to Cairn, where the station repaired the ship before it went home.

History

The filing

M. Tarroway, the cartographer at Strake Station, pulled a pattern out of the noise at the limit of Strake's scope: Strake saw things on Cairn's lane that Cairn did not report. His filing went up the chain, and on it the Fleet sent Crow out. Its crew were told to look in on Cairn. The filing behind the order stayed with the office that sent them.

The raid

Crow's course to Cairn ran up the lane. Short of the station it came on a transfer of people under guard from two pirate ships to a third vessel, a courier of a build nobody aboard could name. The two pirate ships were of comparable size. One was captained by Saltjaw, a pirate captain; her ship carried no name on her hull, and neither ship's name is recorded. Crow's crew saw what was happening and chose to intervene. The pirates ran up the common crossbones and fought. The crossbones showed what they were and named nobody. Aboard the other ship a figure gave the orders, wearing a large coin on a chain in plain view, a seal struck on its face.

Clint led a few of Crow's crew hands aboard the other ship, and together they took her helm and steered her away, pulling half the threat off Crow. Crow's senior officers were killed or injured in the fighting; the only people who went with Clint were the hands he led aboard. His crew saw the boarding party aboard with the figure and the coin. A color pool opened by chance and took that ship, with Clint, his party, the figure and its pirates aboard. Clint's diversion made the rescue possible. Joffrey, the junior spelljammer, was one of those who stood up: he took charge on Crow's side, took the helm, led the recovery of the captives and got the rest of the crew out. With the figure gone and half the pirates with him, Saltjaw had little left to fight for, and Crow drove her ship off down the lane. Many of Crow's crew died, and from then on the survivors looked to Joffrey.

Crow recovered most or all of the captives. They had been held below and hooded, so they never saw the buyers or the receiving vessel's crew; they can describe only the pirates and the figure's coin, and they heard the pirates call their captain Saltjaw. The receiving vessel pulled away from the fight with few of them, if any.

Cairn

Cairn Station took the crippled Crow in, with its survivors and the recovered captives, and repairs took days. Cairn's station master had Caelestis told that Crow was safe and under repair, and relayed the raid as its crew told it. When the repairs were done, Crow went home and the captives were taken to theirs.

The report

Crow's report gave the fight on the approach, the captives recovered, Clint and the crew who went with him lost, Joffrey in charge, and Cairn's help in getting the ship home. Joffrey was commended on the survivors' account as the hero of it. The Fleet carries Clint and his party as lost to a color pool and honors Clint as a hero after his loss: his diversion made the rescue possible. From the captives it recorded the name Saltjaw for the captain of the ship that stayed, and from the captives' and survivors' descriptions it ordered escorts on Cairn's lane against Saltjaw's ship in SA-005. At debrief a survivor's memory of the seal on the figure's coin was drawn out by divination, and FR-016 renders it exactly; the registry returned no match. The recovered captives were identified and returned home, and their accounts are in the Fleet's records: each was taken from somewhere along the lanes, and none of them saw the receiving vessel, which went unidentified. The escorts were told to watch for transfers between vessels and have reported the lane quiet since. Cairn was thanked. Crow had gone to look at Cairn and found it in order, so its report answered Tarroway's filing, and his later filings were set aside.

In the campaign

The crew has not seen Crow. Its report is WM-002 in the Fleet Intelligence Archive, which the crew has not read.

What's hidden

  • The raiders. They were pirates in Vocath's hire, running his captive line, and the receiving hull was a courier of the Vth'orama colony. The figure on the other ship was Vocath's overseer, one of his own crew and the one paying the pirates. He wears Vocath's personal seal as a large coin on a chain, the seal struck on its face. He put it on for the handover, as the token the colony's courier knows him by, and takes it off after; he does not wear it day to day. The same seal was on the crates aboard, which outsiders never see. The seal goes on Vocath's darker dealings and is registered nowhere, so FR-016 matches nothing the Fleet holds, and the Fleet hunts Saltjaw's ship from the captives' and survivors' descriptions. With the overseer, her paymaster, Vocath's sealed cargo and half the pirates gone through the pool, Saltjaw had little left to fight for, and Crow drove her ship off. Saltjaw is alive and free, and the Fleet hunts her ship under SA-005. Mirt has read WM-002 and FR-016 and knows the seal on sight. He knows Vocath is connected to the raid but not how far, cannot prove it, and says nothing.
  • The warning. The colony courier at the handover pulled away from the fight with few or none of the captives, so the colony lost that shipment. It carried word to Zhauveth that Crow was coming to Cairn. Nobody in the office that sent Crow is taken, so nobody had warned Vocath before the raid.
  • Cairn. The colony holds the station. Its station master is one of N'ghathrod's taken, and one of Zhauveth's malformed spawn holds the rest of the crew by nearness, out of sight. Crow's crew and the captives it carried were visitors who would leave, so the hold left them alone. The survivors felt the station was a little off and put it down to shock and grief, and the feeling faded on the way home. After the raid the handovers stopped for a while, and when they resumed they ran around the escort routes, which N'ghathrod's taken pass to Vocath through Zhauveth. The station master has reported a clean lane every week since, and the escorts have seen nothing.
  • Joffrey. During the stay a colony courier brought one of N'ghathrod's copies up the lane, and the station master took Joffrey. The raid had cost the colony a shipment, and it wanted a man inside Caelestis whom the survivors follow, and a warning if anyone looks at Cairn again; it knows nothing of A.T.L.A.S. Joffrey knows Crow was sent to look in on Cairn, and nothing of who asked. He turned over in some months.
  • Clint. The pool dropped the other ship on Fonains about a year ago, with Clint, his party, the overseer, its pirates and Vocath's sealed cargo aboard. Every one of the crew who went with Clint died in the taking of the ship and its fall; he is the only one of Crow's people who reached Fonains alive. Clint got away and lost his communication piece, and the overseer and the pirates hunted him for a while. He is alive there. Because Vocath's cargo had gone through the pool with the ship, the overseer called Vocath by sending stone as soon as the pool dropped them, and told him that Clint died in the hunt; Vocath believes his witness is dead. The ship came out of the pool while the Fonains Monolith stood, about three months before it broke, months having passed outside, and the overseer called Vocath at once. Vocath's trade on Fonains began after the Monolith broke. Clint knows the seal on sight and was aboard with the overseer wearing the coin: what he saw is the proof Mirt lacks.

Threads

  • One survivor has seen that Joffrey changed after the voyage. The crew can find them by pulling Crow's roster.
  • FR-016 is the seal the survivors saw on the coin worn by the figure aboard the other pirate ship. The crew has seen the same seal on the rod and the Sky Dock crate, and Boogie on the small crate of Fonains shards he opened shortly before Voyage 001, before he came to the Academy as a late intake; the neogi crate delivered to Mirt carries it too, unchecked. Set beside WM-002, it puts one of Vocath's people aboard the second pirate ship, and the name in WM-002 points to Saltjaw, the captain Vocath hired.
  • The captives' accounts are in the Fleet's records. They describe the pirates and the coin, give the name Saltjaw, and name the places along the lanes they were taken from.
  • Tarroway's filings on the discrepancy are in LG-003: the first, which sent Crow, and the later ones, set aside after its report.
  • Tarto and Saerthe both feel they could have done something for Clint.
