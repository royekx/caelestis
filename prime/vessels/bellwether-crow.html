Bellwether Crow
The Caelestis ship ambushed on the Cairn approach a year ago.

Bellwether Crow is a Caelestis ship. About a year ago it went out to learn why Cairn Station had stopped answering, carrying A.T.L.A.S.'s sister, and Vocath's crew ambushed it short of Cairn. Clint Dashforest went overboard with the device, Joffrey brought the few survivors home, and most of the complement did not return.

History

When Cairn went dark, Vocath's people took the station and waited, because a silent Fleet post brings a relief run and a relief run has one bearing to take. The Crow called at Strake Station, drew the approach packet and went out on the edge run with Clint and Joffrey aboard. Its last transmission, from the Cairn approach, carried Vocath's eye-in-a-wheel.

Vocath's crew ambushed it short of Cairn for the device. Clint took the device and left the ship to draw the boarders off. He steered for a color pool he could see opening and went through it on purpose, and the boarders who followed went in after him. A second boarding killed the senior deck officers and most of the complement. The few survivors were let go, and Joffrey held them together and brought them off alive; he was decorated on their unanimous account. The Crow made Strake under damage.

The survivors still argue about whether Clint meant to go through. He did. The Fleet's archive carries him as unrecovered: he went overboard. The edge run beyond Strake has been closed by standing order since the Crow came back.

What's hidden

  • The deal. Vocath's ambush ran on an arrangement with the illithid Zhauveth: Vocath's line supplies captives, and in exchange Vocath has an asset inside Caelestis that he believes is his own informant. The asset is N'ghathrod's.
  • Joffrey. He was taken in the second boarding by a tadpole of N'ghathrod's. The survivors were let live so that there would be a story with him as its hero, and they believe every word of it.
