The Derelict
A wreck in an asteroid cluster, and the logbook the crew still carries.

The ship the salvage exercise was run against: crew a hammerhead, plot a course, survive Wildspace, find a vessel lost in an asteroid cluster. In the captain’s quarters, a long-decomposed body, and half-tucked behind it a logbook.

The logbook came out of the simulation as a real object. Boogie put it inside his own chest and it was still there at Voyage 004; Casey and Gregory each bound a duplicate during the githyanki attack, so three identical copies exist and nobody has read any of them for meaning.

Name, class, and what happened to her are all unwritten. Stub
