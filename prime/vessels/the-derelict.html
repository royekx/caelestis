Derelict
A real wreck in an asteroid field, the prize of the salvage exercise.

The Derelict is a wreck adrift in an asteroid field, the prize of the Academy's salvage exercise. Saerthe's simulation draws on real records, so the wreck is real. In its captain's quarters lies a long-decomposed body, and half-tucked behind it, a logbook.

Decks and interior

Its lower corridors are dark and creaking. The logbook lay in the captain's quarters, behind the body.

History

The original logbook sits somewhere in Caelestis with pages missing. Small mundane objects can cross the simulation threshold at exit, and magic items cannot, which is how Boogie carried a real logbook out of a simulated wreck. He holds half of it.

In the campaign

  • Voyage 2. The crew raced Miken's crew for it in simulation, leaping between drifting asteroids to reach it. Boogie slipped the logbook into his own chest while a githyanki vessel closed on the wreck.
  • Voyage 3. Boogie confirmed the logbook still rests in his chest beside the half-ship.

Threads

  • The other half of the logbook was in Boogie's chest inside the Monolith; see the Fonains shard.
  • The salvage race's hammerhead and githyanki ship are simulated too.

Still to decide

  • The ship's name is not set; "Salvage Race Derelict" and "Derelict" are the choices.
  • Its class, and what happened to it, are not set.
  • The record still lists three copies of the logbook; the settled account is two halves.
