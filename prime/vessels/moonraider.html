Moonraider
Captain Sardax's ship, and the battle every cadet runs on their first day.

The Moonraider was Captain Sardax's ship. Astral reavers boarded her from a pirate galleon, her masts already gone, and Sardax died on her own top deck ordering the cadets to hold and give no quarter. The Academy keeps the battle as a simulation and runs every intake through it on their first day.

Decks and interior

In the simulation the top deck is a losing fight: reavers swarming over the rail, explosions tearing through rigging and mast, and Sardax bleeding across the planks.

In the campaign

  • Voyage 1. The cadets woke on her deck mid-battle as astral reavers swarmed over the rail. The scene froze and peeled away, and they woke on the Simulation Deck of Caelestis.

How to run it

The Academy has decided what the loss is for. The crew read it as a lesson about knowing when to withdraw.

Still to decide

  • What the simulation is drawn from is not set. Saerthe's simulation of the Derelict draws on real records; whether the Moonraider's does too is not confirmed.
  • Where and when she was lost is not set.
