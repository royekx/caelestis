The Moonraider
The battle every cadet runs on their first day.

Boarded by astral reavers from a pirate galleon, her masts already gone. Captain Sardax died on her own top deck ordering the cadets to hold and give no quarter.

The Academy keeps the battle as a simulation and runs every intake through it, which means the institution has decided what the loss is for. The crew read it as a lesson about knowing when to withdraw.

The recording is the open thread. A simulation detailed enough to render a deck, a wound and a dying order needs a source, and nothing on record says what that source was. Open
