Thessalar's planar craft
A floating city of many tiers that fell on Fonains about five hundred years ago, and the Mad Mage's dungeon now.

Thessalar's planar craft was a vast floating city of many tiers. Where it came from is unknown. Thessalar, who is Halaster Blackcloak, the Mad Mage, held N'ghathrod prisoner aboard it and experimented on him with the seventh piece of the Rod of Seven Parts. About five hundred years ago it lost its gems and fell on Fonains, breaking apart as it came down. Its wreck is the Mad Mage's dungeon, and Thessalar is still in it with his records and the seventh piece.

Appearance

A city in tiers, built to float. Its fallen main body lies under the Barrier Peaks, which it raised, and its tiers came down with it. Pieces that broke away in the fall lie elsewhere on Fonains, and one of them still floats.

Decks and interior

The gems

Two gems controlled and defended the craft: a red gem in the cavern lake beneath the Temple of Moloch, and a hazy yellow gem in Thessalar's workshop, seated behind a panel and fed by near-invisible silver wire into the walls. The craft's gems formed where Olhydra and Ogrémoch collided, and they are not Reach residue. Tharizdun's influence reached Thessalar through them and drove him slowly mad.

The Temple of Moloch

The Temple of Moloch is a piece of the craft that broke off in the fall, with Thessalar's laboratory beneath it. The red gem lay in the cavern lake of its flooded lower temple. The laboratory holds pipes, steam, test tubes and a long workbench, and eight sealed tubes, a humanoid suspended in each, labelled Thessalar I to Thessalar VIII. The people living in the temple call him a scholar.

Tharizdun's temple

One of Tharizdun's temples was aboard. It broke off in the fall and came down apart from the main body, and her influence reached Fonains with it.

The elder brain's caverns

N'ghathrod's elder brain was aboard, kept apart from him. When the main body came down in the Barrier Peaks, the brain was left trapped in the caverns beneath them. Tharizdun's influence never reached it there.

Kwalish's laboratory: the Monastery of the Distressed Body

A piece of the craft broke off in the fall and tore a chunk of rock away with it. It floats above a valley beyond the Barrier Peaks, reached past the Sphinx and across the valley by skiff. Kwalish, a gnome planar specialist, made it his first laboratory, and the monks of the Monastery of the Distressed Body live on it under their elder monk, Lean Meimbaol.

The island's Grand Master looks like a bone devil. It is Kwalish's war-machine exoskeleton, piloted by the quasit Whazzit; the real bone devil is a brain in a jar in the treasury. Kwalish's brains in jars are linked into a hive mind that knows him, and he calls them his “brain friends”. The Grand Master's bodyguards are East Wind and West Wind, and West Wind carries a blade of the medusa. Canister hearts, three humanoid, one troll and one dragon, are wired with silver cord and power Kwalish's powered armour. Ctenmiir, a dwarf vampire, is sealed as penance in an iron coffin on golem legs, an early Kwalish design. Kwalish's nephew, Anaxi Zephries, is the Cartophile.

About thirty years ago Kwalish's adventuring party died to the Sphinx. He put their brains in jars, opened a gate to the Nine Hells, and a bone devil drove him from the island. Lola's Boys found him in Daoine Gloine. With the control and defence gems they brought back from the year 425, the island rose again, and its cannon split the Fonains Monolith. Kwalish freed his friends' brains afterward.

Daoine Gloine

Daoine Gloine is another piece broken off the craft: a glass city below Kwalish's island, sealed under one huge ooze from Juiblex's layer of the Abyss. Its kenku worshipped an undead medusa, Gloine Nathair-Nathair, whose gaze turns victims to glass. Its old high priest, a jellified kenku in a coffin, is wired with silver cord as the control module of a clockwork kraken whose tentacles guard the baubles. The baubles are crystal pocket dimensions inside the city's glass statuary; one holds an arena of shadow duplicates that mirror whoever enters. Kwalish kept a laboratory in one of them, with the time device that carried Lola's Boys to the year 425.

Kwalish's teleportation fields

Kwalish's teleportation-field devices work only on his island and in Daoine Gloine. The six recall bracelets are this technology. After a strange message from One, who keeps the island aloft, the bracelets pulled Lola's Boys back to it.

The Thessalkraken

Thessalar's lake monster, the Thessalkraken, landed separately in the sea. It is the Drowned God's kraken.

The dungeon

The wreck is a megadungeon. Its tiers have been collecting things since the fall, and its master is still in it.

History

About five hundred years ago, N'ghathrod's ship clashed with the planar craft and lost. Thessalar and the Thessalkraken captured him and some of his illithids, and his elder brain was held aboard apart from him. Some of N'ghathrod's own tadpoles had been brought aboard, cut off from the elder brain. Thessalar experimented on them and put them into bodies he supplied, and three came out of ceremorphosis malformed.

Thessalar then experimented on N'ghathrod with the seventh Rod piece, keyed to simulacrum. His tadpoles became copies of him, and he began to spawn a few at a time on a slow, steady cycle. The five who became his lieutenants, the three malformed and two properly raised illithids captured with him, were changed with him, and each came out with a power of its own. A mouse that had been aboard as long as N'ghathrod was changed too. The experiment lengthened none of their lives; their long life came later, from the taking. Thessalar took the finding and turned it on himself.

Lola's Boys, sent back in time by the Accordance, reached the craft in the year 425 and took its control and defence gems. Without them the craft fell. In the fall N'ghathrod escaped with his five lieutenants, a few lesser illithids and the mouse, which he saved, and never reached Fonains. The craft broke apart as it fell. The main body raised the Barrier Peaks, and the elder brain was left trapped in the caverns beneath them. Tharizdun's temple and the Temple of Moloch, with Thessalar's laboratory beneath it, broke off and came down elsewhere on Fonains; so did Kwalish's island and Daoine Gloine. The Thessalkraken came down in the sea.

The crash is how the Elder Elemental Eye reached Fonains. Tharizdun's influence spread across the world from the fallen temple, and it is why the Eye watched Lola's Boys in the year 425.

What's hidden

  • Lola's Boys. By taking the gems they brought the craft down, freed N'ghathrod, and landed Tharizdun's foothold on their own world.
  • The elder brain lived. N'ghathrod believes it dead or beyond reach. After Lola's Boys reached it in the caverns beneath the Barrier Peaks, it escaped to a place nobody knows.
  • The records. Thessalar's records of the work on N'ghathrod are with him. He is the one person who knows the taking came from the Rod.
  • The temple piece and Shothragot. The piece holding Tharizdun's temple came down somewhere other than the Barrier Peaks. Shothragot, her avatar, was bound to that temple, its true vestige may still be there, and Giorge may be trying to reach it.

How to run it

The craft is a destination, and the destination is a dungeon: reaching Thessalar means descending through tiers that came down with the craft and have been filling up ever since. The crew can arrive by following the Rod, by following the colony's origin, or by following N'ghathrod's mouse, and all three lead to the same wreck.
