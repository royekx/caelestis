An astral dreadnought taken alive a little over fifty years ago and rebuilt into a ship. The colony lives aboard and took the ship’s name for its own. N’ghathrod is fused into it, so it has no helm and no helmsman.

Every anomaly in the Tetherbreak file resolves to this one object, and no Fleet model has room for it.
