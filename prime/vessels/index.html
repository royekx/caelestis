MoonraiderLost with her captain; the Academy's first-day simulation Lost

Astral reavers boarded her from a pirate galleon. Captain Sardax died on her top deck ordering the cadets to hold and give no quarter, and every cadet since has run the battle on their first day.

DerelictA real wreck, the prize of the salvage exercise Wreck

A wreck in an asteroid field, simulated from real records. Boogie carried half of its logbook out of the simulation; the original sits somewhere in Caelestis with pages missing.

Thessalar held N'ghathrod prisoner aboard and experimented on him with the Rod's seventh piece. It fell about five hundred years ago, raised the Barrier Peaks, and is the Mad Mage's dungeon now, with Thessalar still inside.
