Flighty Foundling
A Caelestis hammerhead, and the ship that comes out to meet the crew.

Flighty Foundling is a Caelestis hammerhead. It carried the crew from the Academy to the Tyrant Ship above Toril, and came back to meet them with Saerthe and Boatswain Tarto aboard. With them rides Ezra Ahl-Sier, a Glimpse of the Order of the Watchers, on his way to finish his training on H'Catha. It supplies the scuttle ship that docks in the Tyrant Ship's Cargo intake for small shore parties.

Appearance

A hammerhead ship. At Caelestis it moors on the side of the Academy, about 400 feet above the Sea Dock, near the Gymnasium.

In the campaign

  • Voyage 3. It carried the crew from Caelestis to the tyrant ship in orbit above Toril, dropped them into its Cargo Deck and left.
  • Voyage 6. Answering Ryeback's word to Caelestis, it came to meet the crew on the third day after the helm was seated, eased alongside until the two ships' air envelopes met, and ran a gangway across. It is to carry Pffred and Krik'Lit back to Caelestis.

Its arc

At the crew swap Ezra joins the crew from the Foundling, and Krik'Lit and Pffred leave on it.

Still to decide

  • Its captain and crew, beyond Tarto and Saerthe, are not set.
