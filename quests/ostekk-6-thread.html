The automaton the crew pulled out of the wreckage in the tyrant ship’s cargo hold wears Gregory’s face. He is one of nine units that served the ship’s beholder master, Vaelorix, and shares his name with his maker, the original Ostekk, a beholder tinkerer who also built Ostekk-10, the mechanical behir that destroyed the rest. His core is sound, and a charge from the containment engine set his ruined body rebuilding. What he knows about the ship has already proved worth having.
