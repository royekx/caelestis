Briefing

The crew has been sent to recover an adamantine meteor that struck the spire on H’catha, a world of beholders. They board an abandoned tyrant ship to install a helm on its command deck and fly it through H’cathan space unnoticed — but the vessel is choked with a lantern-wisp bloom, prowled by clockwork horrors, and said to hide a shape-shifting mimic. The way up runs through its access shafts, and a deck below still waits unexplored.
