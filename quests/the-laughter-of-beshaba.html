When Sol spent his own luck to spare a crewmate, a woman's laughter answered — and he knew it for Beshaba. Now a curious, unhurried voice speaks to him in the night of fate and whether he has truly escaped his origin, saying it looks forward to watching how he wields what lies ahead. What these presences want of him is not yet clear.
