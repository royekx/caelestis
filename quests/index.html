The Caelestis Burglaries QuestAssigned by Mirt 1/3 Open ›
A wave of thefts and now an arcane attack have struck Caelestis. The device that nearly killed a chamber of cadets bore the seal of Vocath, an old enemy of Mirt who bears him a grudge, putting anyone connected to Mirt at risk. A crate at the Sky Dock carries the same seal and routes through the quartermaster Mr. Blip. Mirt has asked the cadets to check the teaching rooms and look into Joffrey and a man named Jeffrey.
Objectives
  • Recover the arcane device from the wrecked simulation chamber
  • Look into Joffrey and the man named Jeffrey
  • Trace the marked crate’s route through Mr. Blip

The Tyrant Ship and the H’Catha Meteor QuestAssigned by Boatswain Tarto 3/5 Open ›
Boatswain Tarto has sent the crew to recover an adamantine meteor that struck the spire on H’Catha, a world of beholders. Arriving as themselves is not an option. An abandoned tyrant ship drifts in Toril’s orbit: commandeer it, install a spelljamming helm on its command deck, and fly it through H’Cathan space as one of their own — the vessel itself the disguise. Reach the spire under that cover and bring the meteor back.
Objectives
  • Board the tyrant ship
  • Commandeer the tyrant ship
  • Install the spelljamming helm on the command deck
  • Fly to H’Catha
  • Recover the adamantine meteor
