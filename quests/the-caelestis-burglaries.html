Briefing

A wave of thefts and now an arcane attack have struck Caelestis. The device that nearly killed a chamber of cadets bore the marking 'Vocath' — an old enemy of Mirt who bears him a grudge, putting anyone connected to Mirt at risk. A crate at the Sky Dock carries the same symbol and routes through the quartermaster Mr. Blip. Mirt has asked the cadets to check the teaching rooms and look into Joffrey and a man named Jeffrey.
