Briefing

A wave of thefts and now an arcane attack have struck Caelestis. The device that nearly killed a chamber of cadets bore the seal of Vocath, an old enemy of Mirt who bears him a grudge, putting anyone connected to Mirt at risk. A crate at the Sky Dock carries the same seal and routes through the quartermaster Mr. Blip. Mirt has asked the cadets to check the teaching rooms and look into Joffrey and a man named Jeffrey.

Objectives1 of 3
  • Recover the arcane device from the wrecked simulation chamber
  • Look into Joffrey and the man named Jeffrey
  • Trace the marked crate’s route through Mr. Blip
