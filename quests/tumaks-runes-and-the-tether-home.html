Tumak has begun to manifest powers he does not understand — telepathy, fire, and a soul knife drawn through a small portal — and bears two glowing half-runes, each half of a separate whole. When he called the knife, his runes flared and he felt a tether toward the home he has been seeking, and a voice he calls the Rune Keeper spoke of all things finding their way home.
