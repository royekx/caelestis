Briefing

The first thing Caelestis ever asked of the crew: a printed list of six tasks, handed out by Boatswain Tarto, to be worked through in whatever order they liked. They did all six in a day — and in the course of it met the quartermaster, took their first turn at a helm, argued over bunks, and noticed a dock officer taking a bribe.

Objectives6 of 6
  • Collect the welcome pack from Administration
  • Report to quarters for bunk assignment
  • Report to the Sky Dock for spelljamming ship inspection
  • Collect service weaponry from Mister Blip in Stores
  • Report to the Spelljammer Nexus for basic training
  • Complete the gymnasium assault course
Voyage 001
  • Sor’Kur received them in Administration, her voice arriving in their minds rather than their ears
  • Bunks were settled the hard way — Veena went for Boogie’s corner, Sol stepped in behind him, and Tumak traded for something darker
  • At the Sky Dock they inspected a ship and saw a dock officer take a bribe; Gregory recalled the code of conduct that forbids it
  • Mr. Blip issued from Stores — Boogie charmed a light hammer out of him, and Gregory found the autognome unnervingly like himself
  • Saerthe ran their first helm exercise in the Nexus, over a floating field of miniature ships, under the tension of an ectoplasmic breach elsewhere in the tower
  • All of them finished the gymnasium assault course — Casey with ease, Sol by getting back up after a hard fall, Boogie hauling Gregory up the last climb
