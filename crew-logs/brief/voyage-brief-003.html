The Weight of What's Real
Voyage 003 Brief
The salvage simulation ends in a real explosion — an arcane charge planted in the chamber. Gregory recovers the device, marked Vocath. The crew investigates: Mirt confirms Vocath's old grudge against him, grants them sailor-level clearance, and points them toward Joffrey and the phrase fleet intelligence archive. That night each of them is touched by something larger — Sol by Beshaba, Boogie by a dream of countless other Boogies converging. Then Tarto wakes them at four in the morning: an adamantine meteor has struck the spire on H'Catha. They board a beholder-carved tyrant ship to reach it — and in its cargo hold find clockwork horrors feeding on a broken autognome that wears Gregory's face.

After the Blast
  • The salvage simulation ends in a real explosion. The pods smoke; rival cadets are down. Casey and Bartholomew pull the injured clear; Miken works to revive a fallen crewmate.
  • Gregory finds a metal rod jammed in the wall — arcane dynamite, built to feed on ambient magic and discharge. Boogie recognizes what it is; Gregory confirms it's spent but dangerous near strong magic.
  • Handed to Tarto and Saerth. Boogie glimpses one word on the device before Saerth draws it back: Vocath. Saerth asks them to report anyone moving through the academy out of place.
Splitting the Investigation
  • Gregory and Tumak to the Nexus. Gregory searches the terminals — a category on arcane artillery is locked behind fleet intelligence archive clearance and turns his cadet star away. On Saerth's desk, a note about Bartholomew: does shifting form bear on spelljamming?
  • In the bathroom, Tumak checks his foot: a half-rune glows there — curved, flowerlike, half a star above it — and a second, different half-mark on his shoulder. Two halves of two separate wholes.
  • Boogie and Sol to the Sky Dock. They find the marked crate behind others — sealed, not hollow, nothing living inside. Sol learns from a cargo worker that shipments route through Mr. Blip, with Toril and H'Catha as common destinations.
The Meeting with Mirt
  • All six summoned to the bridge. Mirt thanks them, asks what they saw. Tumak raises Joffrey's suspicious dock exchange. The crew shares pieces of their own histories — Boogie's childhood sacrifice and the sense of being watched, Tumak's search for family, Gregory's dead creator.
  • Saerth names the device's mark: Vocath. Mirt goes still — Vocath has held a grudge against him for years. Anyone near Mirt is a target. He tells them to keep it quiet, check the teaching rooms, and look into Joffrey and a man named Jeffrey.
  • Tumak clocks three words on Mirt's notepad: fleet intelligence archive — the same phrase from the locked terminal. He and Gregory argue for expanded access; Mirt grants sailor-level clearance and tells them not to abuse it.
The Infirmary
  • Vena and Derek lie bandaged but stable. Miken is unhurt but shadowed — a wince he can't quite hide. When the doctor moves the crew out, Miken asks to stay beside his crewmates. This is his crew.
Night
  • Gregory rereads his master's journal — mending a brass aligner from the inside, reaching a thing before the break does. He thinks about arriving before the damage.
  • Sol is visited again by the unhurried voice. It asks whether he's truly escaped where he came from; he says he's accepted what lies ahead. Fate can still be wielded, it tells him, and it looks forward to watching.
  • Boogie dreams of infinite other Boogies — every version he almost was — multiplying into a network, and something pressing down through all of them toward him. He wakes half-dissolved, hums himself back together. The half-ship and logbook are still in his chest.
The Call to H'Catha
  • Days pass quietly. Then Tarto wakes them at four in the morning: an adamantine meteor has struck the spire on H'Catha, a world of beholders, and they're to recover it.
  • Their vessel is a tyrant ship — stone-carved in a beholder's shape — meant to pass through H'Catha's patrols unchallenged. The plan: crew it, install the helm on the command deck, fly it themselves. Ryeback comes along to cook and command the deckhands, Crick Lit and Fred.
  • The Flighty Foundling drops them in the tyrant ship's cargo deck and departs. The stone closes around them.
Something in the Hold
  • The ship is meant to be abandoned. Almost at once they hear skittering — metallic, ahead. Casey rounds the corner first, wrapped in ice, and goes still.
  • Bronze mantis-like machines crouch over wreckage. In the center: an autognome, lower half gone, voice box destroyed — eyes and mouth still moving, trying to speak, waiting a long time for anyone to come.
  • Gregory looks at its face and sees his own. He casts before he's fully decided to — the spell still arcing through the dark as the night ends.
