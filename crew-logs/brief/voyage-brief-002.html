What the Crew Offers
Voyage 002 Brief
Bartholomew joins the crew over lunch at the Weeping Goddess. The six begin to knit together — Casey draws out Ryeback, Boogie shows the crew the half-ship from his chest, and the table sorts out who sits where on a real vessel. Casey works a Sea Dock job for Rindle Gearloft and feels the familiar presence at the Sky Dock rail. The next morning: a salvage simulation against Miken's crew. The hammerhead wins the race, recovers the logbook, fends off a githyanki ambush — and Sol, feeling misfortune reach for Boogie mid-jump, takes it onto himself and hears Beshaba laugh for the first time.

A New Cadet
  • Boatswain Tarto receives Bartholomew — a wilder, newly arrived, orientation already behind him. She tells him what he is changes nothing aboard a crew, points him to the Weeping Goddess and the dragon scale pizza, and sends him to find his crew.
The Weeping Goddess
  • Bartholomew finds a seat with Miken, a nervous cadet who talks about the simulation as if they'd both been through it. An empty chair at the table goes unnamed.
  • The crew filters in — Gregory first, the others orbiting his pace. Boogie and Sol take the open seats. Recognition of the sixth crew member stays soft and unspoken; no one had been told what he'd look like.
  • Tumak studies the notice board for anything from home and finds nothing. Casey pulls a Sea Dock cargo notice without explaining why.
  • Ryeback serves the table with affectionate brusqueness — soup for Sol (knock-your-mama's-socks-off soup), a silver moonlight starfruit for Boogie, spare utensils offered kindly to Gregory.
  • Casey draws Ryeback out: hot baths, knitting. Both make things whole, one piece at a time. Ryeback pauses, then gives him an extra portion.
  • Boogie tells Miken the crew has things to discuss; Miken leaves graciously. Then Boogie sets the half-missing model ship from his chest on the table. Tumak notes things have been going missing across the academy. Nobody picks up what Boogie set down.
  • The crew sorts roles: Tumak captain, Gregory navigator, Boogie medical, Casey guns. The spelljammer's seat stays open — Boogie quietly insists it should be Gregory.
  • Casey follows Tumak to the bathroom, hears rummaging behind the stalls, sees Tumak's jacket askew. Both say nothing. When Ryeback brings the second of Tumak's two meatloafs, only Casey notices.
The Missing Ship
  • Boogie, Gregory, and Bartholomew ask to practice the helm. Tarto takes them to the Nexus and sends for Saerth Abyzine.
  • Saerth's composure cracks — his personal miniature ship has been stolen. He catches himself, apologizes, moves on.
  • Boogie realizes the stolen ship is the same model that came out of his chest. He says nothing and does the exercise. Bartholomew takes to the helm as if born to it — the spelljammer's seat answers itself.
The Sea Dock
  • Casey walks the cargo notice to the Sea Dock looking for Joffrey, to ask about reading more than three stars. He finds Rindle Gearloft instead — brusque, distracted — and works cargo for an hour before Rindle realizes a cadet filled the job and pays him.
  • Casey climbs to the Sky Dock rail afterward, thinks of his brother, and feels the familiar presence settle beside him without sound — the same one from Mirt's wardrobe. He doesn't look. By the time he rises, it's gone.
Night in the Quarters
  • Tumak snores with a strange doubled rhythm — two breaths never quite sharing one beat. Miken, two bunks over, casts a bubble of silence with a spell above a cadet's year. The night passes.
The Salvage Race
  • Morning briefing on the Simulation Deck: two crews, one derelict, one logbook. First ship back with the prize wins. The rival crew is Miken, Vena, Barret and their squad.
  • Bartholomew takes the hammerhead's helm; the crew takes the lead off the launch. Gregory plots a line through an electrical storm of silver eels; they push through rather than around and hold the lead.
  • Boarding the wreck across drifting asteroids, Boogie misjudges a jump as mist swallows the next rock. Sol feels misfortune reach for a crewmate and takes it onto himself — strength drops out of him in a measured wave, and beneath his hearing a woman laughs. By the third laugh he knows: Beshaba.
  • Boogie lands clean, not knowing why he thought he wouldn't. Gregory and Bartholomew see a hair-thin arc of black lightning snap out of Sol and back into him. He winces.
  • The logbook lies behind a long-dead curled creature in the captain's quarters. Boogie collects it and slips it into his own chest.
The Githyanki Ambush
  • Gregory spots a githyanki ship slipping through the field — not the cadet rivals. He shouts the crew up from below.
  • Casey has Boogie bring the logbook back out and conjures a duplicate; Gregory follows with another. Three identical logbooks lie on the deck. Bartholomew drops a fog cloud over the hull.
  • The crew pulls away under fire. Boogie leaps onto the enemy deck alone, realizes it's more than he can finish, and turns back. Bartholomew slows the ship to give him a target; Tumak's rope-line misses by half a body; Gregory puts covering fire through the enemy hull. None of it strictly needed — all of it offered. Boogie makes the leap home.
  • Nearing the dock, Miken's crew drifts damaged and signals for help. Tumak asks Gregory, straight-faced, whether the manual requires stopping for stranded vessels. Gregory considers it seriously and concludes that no such provision appears to exist.
