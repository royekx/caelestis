T Tyrant ShipOrbiting Toril Vessel Voyage 006

The tyrant ship is a stone-carved beholder vessel in Toril's orbit, once the vessel of the beholder Vaelorix, who lies dead on its Command Deck. The crew holds it now. They burned the bloom off the Command Deck, put the mechanical behir out through the cargo hold, and seated a helm in the platform so they can fly it through H'Cathan space unnoticed. It flies, though only four of its ten great eyes answer at the crew stations. It has no fire suppression; most things leave it through the cargo hold.

Known aboard Ostekk-6, Winston Ryeback, Ostekk-2, Vaelorix

F Flighty FoundlingOrbiting Toril Vessel Voyage 006

The Flighty Foundling is a Caelestis Academy ship. It carried the crew out from the academy to the tyrant ship above Toril, dropped them into its cargo deck and left. Once the helm was seated it came back to meet them with Saerthe and Boatswain Tarto aboard, easing alongside until the two ships' air envelopes met. It is to carry Pffred and Krik'Lit back to Caelestis.

Known aboard Boatswain Tarto, Saerthe Abizjn

M MoonraiderIn simulation Simulation Voyage 001

The Moonraider was Captain Sardax's ship. The crew has been aboard it only in simulation: they woke on its deck in the middle of a losing battle against astral reavers, Sardax bleeding across the planks, until the scene froze and revealed itself as a simulation of her final stand, a test never meant to be won.

Known aboard Captain Sardax
