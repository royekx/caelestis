Summary

The Order recovered crystalline fragments, number not stated, from a registry-flagged world in system CSC 50743 in Cycle     . One was routed for analysis. The world's name is not supplied in the Order's routing and is illegible in Case #WM-003. Composition matches the material class catalogued in prior Fleet records, which the Order's registry terms Ortholith. Two features of the specimen are without precedent in any prior Fleet record of this material class.

Anomalous Properties

· Coloration. Deep black, refractive under direct light. No specimen of this material class on Fleet record has presented in this coloration. All previously catalogued examples take a coloration matched to their world of origin.
· Condition. Shattered. Material of this class has resisted every destructive process known to the Fleet. Cause of the shattering unknown.
