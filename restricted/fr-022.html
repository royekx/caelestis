Summary

A single crystalline fragment was recovered from a registry-flagged world in Krynnspace (CSC 16423), name illegible (Case #WM-003), earlier this cycle. Material composition is consistent with a class of structure documented in recovered monitoring documents (Case #WM-003). The Order's registry term for the class, adopted in Fleet usage at Above Bridge, is Ortholith. Two features of the specimen are without precedent in any prior Fleet record of this material class.

Anomalous Properties

· Coloration. Deep black, refractive under direct light. No specimen of this material class on Fleet record has presented in this coloration. All previously catalogued examples reflect surface properties consistent with their origin environments.
· Condition. Shattered. The specimen is one piece of an indeterminate number recovered from the site. Material of this class has resisted every destructive process known to the Fleet. The destruction event is unaccounted for.
