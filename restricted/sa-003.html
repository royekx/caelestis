Standing Order. This order remains in effect until rescinded by Bridge command.
Order

Crews returning from rotation at Hollis Post are held aboard for three days at anchorage before going ashore, and examined by a surgeon before release.

Background

A patrol crew of      returned from Hollis Post with a fever. It began two days out, with chills and a dry cough, and ran its course in eleven days. All recovered. The station's own crew reported no illness. The surgeons could not name the fever, and no source was found in the stores or the water.

Implementation

Two rotations since the order: no further cases.

Order under review. Recommended for rescinding after a third clean rotation.
