Mission Summary

Vessel: Copper Wren, contracted merchant transit.
Crew complement: nine.
Cargo: cloth and tools, outbound under Fleet contract.
Course:      lane, Realmspace to     .
Outcome: arrived at destination      days late. No losses.

Account

Compiled from the master's log and an interview with the helm.

On the      watch out, the helm reported that the lane "pressed in": the stars ahead drew together, the ship felt narrower than it was, and the helm lost its sense of which way was forward for the length of a watch. The master ordered the ship held to her heading by the compass alone. Cargo shifted in the forward hold. The effect passed within the watch; stars returned to normal spacing. The ship hove to      watches to restow the forward hold.

Two of the crew report a ringing in the ears for some days after. The ship's cook reports that the bread would not rise for the rest of the passage. Neither complaint has recurred.

Follow-up

The survey cutter Osprey Vane ran the same stretch of lane on her next rotation and found nothing unusual. Instrument readings nominal.

Findings

Consistent with spatial effects recorded on the sector's outer lanes. Entered under Case #FR-005. No action required. Masters on outward lanes are advised to hold heading by compass if the effect is met.
