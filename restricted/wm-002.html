Mission Summary

Vessel: Bellwether Crow, light corvette class.
Crew complement:     .
Crew lead: Clint Dashforest.
Junior spelljammer: Joffrey.
Orders: look in on Cairn Station and report its condition.
Course: the Cairn lane, outward from Realmspace along the sector's arm.
Departure date:     .
Outcome: raided once on the Cairn approach, with heavy losses. Reached Cairn Station under damage and lay there      days under repair. Returned to Caelestis under its own power with a fraction of its complement.

Engagement on the Cairn Approach

Account compiled from the debrief of the surviving complement.

On the approach to Cairn Station, Bellwether Crow came on several vessels on the lane. Two lay alongside each other, with cargo and persons being moved from one to the other. The receiving vessel was unlisted, of a build none of the survivors could name, and its profile was not recorded. The others were small, fast craft.

The party turned on Crow at once. To draw the fight off his crew, Dashforest boarded one of the small craft and took it. A color pool then opened beside that vessel and took it, with Dashforest and its hostile crew aboard. The pool closed. Neither the vessel nor Dashforest has been recovered, and Dashforest is carried as lost to a color pool.

The fighting killed many of Crow's crew, the senior helm and the senior deck officers among them. Junior spelljammer Joffrey took the helm, flew the vessel out of the action, and held the survivors together. The remaining hostile vessels withdrew down the lane.

Hull Marking — Debrief Renderings

Several survivors glimpsed a marking on the hull of a hostile vessel during the action. At debrief each surviving crewmate was asked to render it. Each rendering differs from the others, and none matched the Bridge registry archive. Four are reproduced below as drawn; the rest are retained with the individual debrief statements in personnel records.

Debrief Renderings — Hull Marking — As Submitted

A composite of all submitted renderings was attempted, returned no registry match, and was withdrawn from this file as unreliable.

Repairs at Cairn Station

Crow made Cairn Station under damage. The station took the vessel in at once, and repairs took      days. Station relay to Caelestis, received during the repair:

"Cairn Station to Caelestis. Bellwether Crow in at Cairn under heavy damage, raided on the approach. Crew lead Dashforest lost. Survivors safe at the station. Repairs under way, estimate      days. Lane otherwise clear. Cairn out."

Station condition as observed by Crow's crew: crewed, at post, operational. Assistance rendered without delay. Several survivors described the station as quiet; this is attributed to shock and loss.

Findings

· Cairn Station. Found in order. The question raised in Case #LG-003 is answered.
· Losses. Attributed to raiders operating on the Cairn lane. Identity of the raiding party not established. Escort order issued (Case #SA-005).
· Color pool. Natural occurrence, not on any chart of the lane. Dashforest is carried as lost to a color pool.

Commendation, filed with the report. Junior spelljammer Joffrey took the helm after the loss of the senior helm, flew Bellwether Crow out of the action, and held the surviving complement together through the repair and the voyage home. Decoration awarded on the unanimous account of the survivors. Subsequent posting: Caelestis.
