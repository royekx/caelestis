Mission Summary

Vessel: Bellwether Crow, light corvette class.
Crew complement:     .
Crew lead: Clint Dashforest.
Junior spelljammer: Joffrey.
Orders: look in on Cairn Station and report its condition.
Course: the Cairn lane, outward from Realmspace along the sector's arm.
Departure date:     .
Outcome: raided once on the Cairn approach, with heavy losses. Reached Cairn Station under damage and lay there      days under repair. Returned to Caelestis under its own power with a fraction of its complement.

Engagement on the Cairn Approach

Account compiled from the debrief of the surviving complement.

On the approach to Cairn Station, Bellwether Crow came on three vessels lying together on the lane, with persons under guard being moved from two of them to the third. The receiving vessel was unlisted, of a build none of the survivors could name, and its profile was not recorded. The other two were armed craft: a larger ship and a smaller one sailing in company with her.

On sighting Crow, both armed craft ran up pirate colors, the common crossbones, and struck at once. The colors identify no one. The larger ship carried her name and a mark of her own on her hull, and the survivors read both at close quarters. As reported at debrief:
Name:     .
Hull mark:     .

Several survivors also report a figure aboard the smaller craft who gave orders to its crew and wore a seal on their person. The accounts agree on the seal's device. It is drawn from them under Case #FR-016.

To draw the fight off his crew, Dashforest went aboard the smaller craft and took her helm. Survivors saw him aboard with the figure wearing the seal. A color pool then opened beside that vessel and took it, with Dashforest, the figure and its hostile crew aboard. The pool closed. Neither the vessel nor Dashforest has been recovered, and Dashforest is carried as lost to a color pool.

The fighting killed many of Crow's crew, the senior helm and the senior deck officers among them. Junior spelljammer Joffrey took the helm, flew the vessel out of the action, and held the survivors together. After the pool closed the larger ship withdrew down the lane and was not taken. Survivors differ on why: some report that she broke off on her own, others that Crow's guns drove her off. She remains at large; see Case #SA-005.

Repairs at Cairn Station

Crow made Cairn Station under damage. The station took the vessel in at once, and repairs took      days. Station relay to Caelestis, received during the repair:

"Cairn Station to Caelestis. Bellwether Crow in at Cairn under heavy damage, raided on the approach. Crew lead Dashforest lost. Survivors safe at the station. Repairs under way, estimate      days. Lane otherwise clear. Cairn out."

Station condition as observed by Crow's crew: crewed, at post, operational. Assistance rendered without delay. Several survivors described the station as quiet; this is attributed to shock and loss.

Findings

· Cairn Station. Found in order. The question raised in Case #LG-003 is answered.
· Losses. Attributed to pirates operating on the Cairn lane. Their colors identify no one. The larger ship is known by the name and mark on her hull, entered above and issued to all Fleet vessels on the lane. Escort order issued against that ship (Case #SA-005).
· Seal. Worn by a figure aboard the smaller craft, now lost with it. Drawn from survivor accounts under Case #FR-016; no registry match.
· Color pool. Natural occurrence, not on any chart of the lane. Dashforest is carried as lost to a color pool.

Commendation, filed with the report. Junior spelljammer Joffrey took the helm after the loss of the senior helm, flew Bellwether Crow out of the action, and held the surviving complement together through the repair and the voyage home. Decoration awarded on the unanimous account of the survivors. Subsequent posting: Caelestis.
