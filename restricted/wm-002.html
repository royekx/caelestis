Bearing Status — Adverse Update

The edge run on the Tetherbreak bearing is designated for elevated monitoring following loss of contact with Cairn Station (Case #LG-003) and the subsequent failure of the dispatched relief vessel to reach it. Strake Station remains operational and is the authorised limit of transit on this bearing.

Relief Operation — Summary

Vessel: Bellwether Crow, light corvette class.
Crew complement:     .
Mission: investigate loss of contact with Cairn Station; render assistance; recover crew if possible.
Departure date:     .
Port of call: Strake Station, inbound and outbound, per standing routing on this bearing. Approach packet drawn at Strake.
Last known position: the edge run beyond Strake Station, on course for Cairn Station.
Scheduled return:     . Returned under own power by way of Strake, well behind schedule, with a fraction of complement. Mission aborted prior to arrival at Cairn Station.

Last Transmission — En Route

The following transmission was received from Bellwether Crow after departing Strake Station on the Cairn leg. It is the last communication on file from the relief crew.

"Bellwether Crow to Fleet relay — engaged earlier this watch by unknown vessel. Small, fast craft, hostile posture. Hull carries a marking. Could not read it clean from this angle and it matches nothing I know from registry. Boarded during the action. Crewmate Clint Dashforest took one of the boarders' craft with [consigned item, designation held at higher clearance] and made for a color pool off the bow, drawing them after him. Some of their craft went with him and some stayed, and we fended off the rest. Crewmate and craft unrecovered. Minor damage to port thruster array, helm operational. Continuing to Cairn Station per mission orders. Bellwether out."

No further transmissions received on the Cairn leg. The vessel came about, made Strake Station under damage, and reached Fleet anchorage days after the scheduled window, carrying survivors and a debrief. Cairn Station was never reached. Surviving complement reported a second boarding action shortly after the above transmission, by the same craft or one like it, and a withdrawal under damage. Accounts of the second action are inconsistent in detail and consistent in outcome. Crewmate Dashforest is carried as went overboard, unrecovered.

Identification — not obtained. Renderings of the hostile craft's hull marking were requested of each surviving crewmate at debrief. The submissions disagreed with one another and with the vessel's own transmission. A composite was attempted, returned no registry match, and was withdrawn from the filing as unreliable. No rendering of the marking is held at this clearance. Individual debrief statements are retained in personnel records.

Commendation, filed with the operation. Following Crewmate Dashforest's departure in the boarders' craft and the loss of the senior deck officers in the second action, Crewmate Joffrey held the surviving complement together, organised the withdrawal, and brought them off the deck alive. Decoration awarded on the unanimous account of the survivors. Subsequent posting: Caelestis.

Hypotheses

Possible explanations for the loss of Cairn Station and the mauling of the relief vessel are under review. None have been confirmed:

· Interception. The engagement occurred on the Cairn leg, beyond Strake, on the only bearing a relief vessel could have taken. Whether the unknown party was positioned there in expectation of a Fleet response is not established. The timing supports it.
· Approach material. Strake Station holds the only staged copies of the Cairn approach packet, revised three times in one cycle by Strake's cartographer (Case #LG-003). The packet was drawn and returned intact. Whether the unknown party engaged the relief vessel to reach it is not established.
· Hostile action. The relief vessel's last transmission documented an engagement with an unknown party, reported as resolved with one crewmate and one of the boarders' craft unaccounted for prior to the vessel's continued transit. Whether the same party returned after the initial withdrawal, or was involved in the prior loss of Cairn Station, is not established. Cairn's own final transmission referenced no hostile contact.
· Environmental anomaly. Anomalies on the perimeter watch (Case #FR-005) remain active and unexplained. Cairn's final transmission referenced an unidentified mass signature on station bearing, reading differently on each sweep, prior to silence; the nature of that signature is not on file.
· Spatial distortion. Long-range survey instrumentation has periodically logged anomalous gravitational variance along the bearing. Mechanism unidentified.

Current Monitoring Posture

The edge run is designated for passive monitoring only. Active Fleet patrols are routed short of Strake's limit until further determination. Standing order under preparation (see Case #SA-005). Recovery operations: suspended.
