Restricted Distribution. Raiding on the lane is the subject of a standing escort order; see Case #SA-005.
Mission Summary

Vessel: Bellwether Crow, light corvette class.
Crew complement:     .
Crew lead: Clint Dashforest.
Junior spelljammer: Joffrey.
Orders: look in on Cairn Station and report its condition.
Course: the Cairn lane, outward from Realmspace along the sector's arm.
Departure date:     .
Outbound passage: routine. One fault in the helm's attunement logged on the second watch and cleared by the junior spelljammer. Stores as provisioned.
Outcome: raided once on the Cairn approach, with heavy losses. Reached Cairn Station under damage and lay there      days under repair. Recovered persons held captive on the lane and carried them to Cairn. Returned to Caelestis under its own power with a fraction of its complement.

Engagement on the Cairn Approach

Account compiled from the debrief of the surviving complement.

On the approach to Cairn Station, Bellwether Crow came on three vessels lying together on the lane, with persons under guard being moved from two of them to the third. The receiving vessel was unlisted, of a build none of the survivors could name. It lay dark beyond the two armed ships for the whole action and its profile was not recorded. The other two were armed ships of comparable size.

Crow's officers saw the transfer for what it was and chose to intervene. The two armed ships ran up pirate colors, the common crossbones, and fought. The colors identify no one. The first hostile vessel carried no name on her hull, and the second hostile vessel's name is not recorded. The recovered persons heard the hostile crews name the commander of the first hostile vessel, the one that stayed in the action. As reported at debrief:
Commander: "Saltjaw".

Several survivors also report a figure aboard the second hostile vessel who gave orders to its crew and wore a large coin on a chain. A seal was struck on the coin's face. It is rendered under Case #FR-016.

Dashforest led a party of Crow's crew aboard the second hostile vessel. Together they took her helm and steered her away from the action, drawing half the hostile force off Crow. Survivors saw them aboard with the figure wearing the coin. A color pool then opened in that vessel's path and took it, with Dashforest, his party, the figure and its hostile crew aboard. The pool closed. Neither the vessel nor any of the party has been recovered. Dashforest and the crew who went with him are carried as lost to a color pool.

The fighting killed many of Crow's crew, her senior helm and deck officers among them; the hands who went aboard with Dashforest were lost with him. Junior spelljammer Joffrey took charge on Crow's side: he took the helm, led the recovery of the persons under guard from the first hostile vessel's deck, where the transfer had left them, and got the rest of the crew out. With the second hostile vessel gone, Crow drove the first hostile vessel off down the lane. Neither the vessel nor her commander was taken; both remain at large. See Case #SA-005. The receiving vessel pulled away during the action and was not pursued.

Repairs at Cairn Station

Crow made Cairn Station under damage. The station took the vessel in at once, and repairs took      days. Station relay to Caelestis, received during the repair:

"Cairn Station to Caelestis. Bellwether Crow in at Cairn under heavy damage, raided on the approach. Crew lead Dashforest lost. Survivors and recovered captives safe at the station. Repairs under way, estimate      days. Lane otherwise clear. Cairn out."

Station condition as observed by Crow's crew: crewed, at post, operational. Assistance rendered without delay. Several survivors described the station as quiet; this is attributed to shock and loss.

Findings

· Cairn Station. Found in order.
· Losses. Attributed to pirates operating on the Cairn lane. Their colors identify no one. The vessel that stayed in the action is commanded by the pirate the recovered persons named as "Saltjaw". Her description, from survivors and recovered persons, is issued to all Fleet vessels on the lane. Escort order issued against her (Case #SA-005).
· Seal. Struck on a large coin worn on a chain by a figure aboard the second hostile vessel, now lost with it. Rendered under Case #FR-016; no registry match.
· Persons under guard. Recovered by Crow, identified and returned to their homes; their accounts are held in Fleet records. Each was taken from a place along the lanes. They were held below, and hooded whenever they were moved. Their accounts describe the pirates, the name "Saltjaw" and the coin. None of them saw the receiving vessel, which remains unidentified. Referred to Case #SA-005.
· Color pool. Natural occurrence, not on any chart of the lane. Dashforest and the crew who went with him are carried as lost to a color pool.

Commendation, filed with the report. Junior spelljammer Joffrey took charge and the helm with the senior officers lost, led the recovery of the persons under guard, got the rest of the crew out of the action, and held the surviving complement together through the repair and the voyage home. Decoration awarded on the unanimous account of the survivors. Subsequent posting: Caelestis.

Honored, filed with the report. Crew lead Clint Dashforest, lost to a color pool with the crew who went with him. Their seizure of the second hostile vessel drew half the hostile force off Crow and made the recovery possible.
