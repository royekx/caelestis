Field Anomaly Reports — Perimeter Watch

Summary

Compiled reports of unexplained phenomena observed on the perimeter watch at the edge of Realmspace, along the Tetherbreak bearing, over a period of approximately      months. Reports drawn from routine patrol logs, transit vessel testimony, and station instrument readings. No determination has been made regarding cause.

Documented Anomalies

· Instrument calibration drift reported aboard the survey cutter Osprey Vane; recalibration on return to port restored normal function.
· Two small vessels reported overdue and have not been recovered: Mendren (Cycle     ) and Quivering Lark (Cycle     ). Wreckage not located.
· Crew of the merchant transit Copper Wren reported "passing through a region of compressed space" near the outer edge of the watch. Helm reported temporary loss of orientation; effect resolved on transit through.
· Gravitational variance recorded by long-range survey instrumentation. Effect intermittent. Mechanism unidentified.
· Strake cartographic filing (M. Tarroway): unidentified mass, Cairn bearing.

Stations and Outposts on the Watch

Active Fleet presence on the perimeter watch at the time of report:

· Strake Station — Fleet waystation, supply and refuelling. Reported instrument calibration drift in routine filings (see above). Operational status nominal as of last filing.
· Osprey Vane — survey vessel rotation; on station as of last filing.
· Hollis Post — outpost, scheduled rotation incomplete.

At the end of the edge run:

· Cairn Station — the far satellite of the watch, at the end of the edge run on the Tetherbreak bearing. Small standing complement; files through Strake Station on the resupply rotation rather than directly, so filings are irregular by design. Approach material is staged at Strake. Gravitational variance recorded above is strongest along this bearing, and strongest of all at Cairn's own instrument readings.

Recommendation

The perimeter watch is flagged for continued monitoring. Strake Station and Hollis Post both sit on the watch that covers the approaches to Realmspace, and perimeter filings are routed through the same rotation. No determination of cause is possible from available data. Phenomena may be consistent with natural spatial anomalies known to occur in fringe regions, or may reflect an unidentified factor.

Survey rotation increased until further reporting.
