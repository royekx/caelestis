Case #FR-005
Field Anomaly Reports — Outward Lanes
Gold — Bridge
Date Filed: [Redacted]
Filed By: [Redacted]
Distribution: Bridge Command Only
Clearance: Gold — Bridge
Origin: Caelestis Sector
Summary

Compiled reports of unexplained phenomena on the lanes that run outward from Realmspace along the sector's arm, toward the sector's outer zone, over a period of approximately      months. Reports drawn from routine patrol logs, transit vessel testimony, and station instrument readings. No determination has been made regarding cause.

Documented Anomalies

· Instrument calibration drift reported aboard the survey cutter Osprey Vane; recalibration on return to port restored normal function.
· Two small vessels reported overdue and have not been recovered: Mendren (Cycle     ) and Quivering Lark (Cycle     ). Wreckage not located.
· Crew of the merchant transit Copper Wren reported "passing through a region of compressed space" on an outward lane. Helm reported temporary loss of orientation; effect resolved on transit through.
· Gravitational variance recorded by long-range survey instrumentation on more than one lane. Effect intermittent. Mechanism unidentified.
· Strake Station cartographic filing (M. Tarroway): returns at the limit of Strake's range on the Cairn lane, not reported by Cairn Station. See Case #LG-003.

Fleet Posts on the Outward Lanes

Fleet presence on the lanes covered by this report at the time of filing:

· Cairn Station — small Fleet watch post on the Cairn lane, some weeks out from Realmspace by ordinary helm, and the Fleet's authority on that lane. Its long scope watches the lane, and a handful of crew stand its watches. Reports directly to Caelestis; stores supplied under Fleet contract. Operational status nominal; reports regular.
· Strake Station — Fleet waystation, supply and refuelling, on a lane of its own nearer home, off to the side of the Cairn lane. Its long-range scope reaches Cairn's stretch of lane only at the limit of its range. Operational status nominal.
· Hollis Post — Fleet post on a lane of its own; routine filings, operational status nominal.
· Osprey Vane — survey cutter on rotation across the outward lanes; on station as of last filing.

Recommendation

The outward lanes are flagged for continued monitoring. No determination of cause is possible from available data. Phenomena may be consistent with natural spatial anomalies known to occur in the sector's outer zone, or may reflect an unidentified factor.

Survey rotation increased until further reporting.
