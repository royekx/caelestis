Summary

Compiled reports of unexplained phenomena observed in the Tetherbreak sector over a period of approximately      months. Reports drawn from routine patrol logs, transit vessel testimony, and station instrument readings. No determination has been made regarding cause.

Documented Anomalies

· Instrument calibration drift reported aboard the survey cutter Osprey Vane; recalibration on return to port restored normal function.
· Two small vessels reported overdue and have not been recovered: the Mendren (Cycle     ) and the Quivering Lark (Cycle     ). Wreckage not located.
· Crew of the merchant transit Copper Wren reported "passing through a region of compressed space" near the sector's outer edge. Helm reported temporary loss of orientation; effect resolved on transit through.
· Gravitational variance recorded by long-range survey instrumentation. Effect intermittent. Mechanism unidentified.

Stations and Outposts in Sector

Active Fleet presence in the affected sector at the time of report:

· Strake Station — Fleet waystation, supply and refuelling. Reported instrument calibration drift in routine filings (see above). Operational status nominal as of last filing.
· Osprey Vane — survey vessel rotation; on station as of last filing.
· Hollis Post — outpost, scheduled rotation incomplete.

Beyond the sector's outer limit:

· Cairn Station — installation beyond the sector limit, at the far end of the Tetherbreak approach. Small standing complement; files through Strake Station on the resupply rotation rather than directly, so filings are irregular by design. Approach material is staged at Strake. Gravitational variance recorded above is strongest along this bearing, and strongest of all at Cairn's own instrument readings.

Recommendation

Sector flagged for continued monitoring. Strake Station and Hollis Post both sit on the perimeter watch that covers the approaches to SYS-01; perimeter filings are routed through the same rotation. No determination of cause possible from available data. Phenomena may be consistent with natural spatial anomalies known to occur in fringe regions, or may reflect an unidentified factor.

Survey rotation increased pending further reporting.
