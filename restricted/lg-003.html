Document Overview

Collected filings from Strake Station's cartographer, M. Tarroway, on contacts logged by Strake's long-range scope along the Cairn lane. Each filing sets Strake's contacts against Cairn Station's reports for the same watches. The filings rode with Strake's supply manifests and were forwarded to Caelestis by Strake's quartermaster, Halix Verrow.

Cairn Station is the Fleet's watch post on the Cairn lane, which runs outward from Realmspace along the sector's arm. Its long scope covers the lane, and it reports directly to Caelestis. Strake Station sits on a lane of its own, nearer home and off to the side of Cairn's. Cairn's stretch of lane lies at the very limit of Strake's scope.

Contacts Against Cairn Reports — Excerpted
Watch Strake long-range scope (Tarroway) Cairn Station report, same watch
     One faint return at range limit on the Cairn stretch. Held two watches, then lost. Lane clear. Nothing beyond scheduled traffic.
     Single large return across four sweeps, its shape changing on each pass. See plate below. Lane clear. Nothing beyond scheduled traffic.
Further entries held with the original filings. Not included in this distribution.
Sweeps Plate — Strake Long-Range Scope

Four sweeps taken at the limit of range toward the Cairn lane, filed with the second entry above. The return lies a little nearer on each pass. Plate reproduced from Tarroway's tracing of the scope glass.

Strake Long-Range Scope — Four Sweeps — Range Limit, Cairn Lane
Sweep Return
S-1 A long smooth oval, consistent with a hull
S-2 The same oval, with a deep notch in its leading edge
S-3 Split into two lobes, one far larger than the other
S-4 One mass again, with long filaments trailing from its lower edge

Cartographer's margin note on the plate (Tarroway, signed): "Not a vessel profile. Does not hold a shape. Cairn reports nothing on this bearing."

First Filing

Tarroway's first filing on the subject collected the two entries above, with the sweeps plate, and set them against Cairn's reports for the same watches. Covering note:

"Strake sees things on the Cairn lane that Cairn does not report. Our scope is at its limit out there, and I grant that. It is the same scope that reads true on every other bearing. Either Strake is wrong at the limit or Cairn's log is short. I would like someone to look."

Forwarding notation (Verrow, signed): "Forwarded. Strake's scope was checked this cycle and is within tolerance."

Disposition: actioned. Bellwether Crow was dispatched to look in on Cairn Station. The vessel's orders did not carry this filing. See Case #WM-002.

Later Filings

Tarroway filed again after Bellwether Crow returned, and continued to file on the same subject. The later filings add further contacts at range limit on the Cairn stretch, each set against a Cairn report of a clear lane.

Cartographer's notes, excerpted from the later filings:

· "The contacts did not stop when Crow came home."
· "Cairn's reports read the same every week. A watch post on a working lane should see something now and then."

Disposition: set aside. The question raised in the first filing was answered by Bellwether Crow's report, which found Cairn Station in order. Contacts at the limit of Strake's range are carried as unconfirmed.

Manifest notation, carried with the later filings and unrelated to them: one cadet intake referral, a plasmoid with no fixed home or registry, recruited in transit. Forwarded to Caelestis Academy.
