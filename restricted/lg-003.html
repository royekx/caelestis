Document Overview

Filings from Strake Station's cartographer, M. Tarroway, on what Strake's long-range scope logs across its bearings. The filings rode with Strake's supply manifests and were forwarded to Caelestis by Strake's quartermaster, Halix Verrow. Excerpted for archive.

Bearing Log — Excerpted
Bearing Strake long-range scope (Tarroway) Note
Inward, toward Realmspace Scheduled traffic logged; returns match the manifests. Nominal.
Strake's own lane Supply runs and one survey cutter, as scheduled. Nominal.
Hollis lane The lane's marker beacon read late on two passes. Reported; the beacon was re-timed on the next survey pass.
The shoal, range limit A ghost return near a bright star, held one watch. Scope bloom at the edge of range. Recalibrated; no contact.
Toward the Cairn lane, range limit A faint return, held two watches, then lost. On watch     , a single large return across four sweeps, its shape changing on each pass (plate below). To be confirmed with Cairn Station.
Further bearings held with the original filings. Not included in this distribution.
Sweeps Plate — Strake Long-Range Scope

Four sweeps taken at the limit of range toward the Cairn lane, filed with the bearing log above. The return lies a little nearer on each pass. Plate reproduced from Tarroway's tracing of the scope glass.

Strake Long-Range Scope — Four Sweeps — Range Limit, Cairn Lane
Sweep Return
S-1 A long smooth oval, consistent with a hull
S-2 The same oval, with a deep notch in its leading edge
S-3 Split into two lobes, one far larger than the other
S-4 One mass again, with long filaments trailing from its lower edge

Cartographer's margin note on the plate (Tarroway, signed): "Not a vessel profile. Does not hold a shape. Cairn reports nothing on this bearing."

First Filing

Covering note:

"Strake's scope reads true on every bearing but one. Toward the Cairn lane it logs returns, and Cairn's reports for the same watches read clear. Our scope is at its limit out there, and I grant that. I would like someone to look."

Forwarding notation (Verrow, signed): "Forwarded. Strake's scope was checked this cycle and is within tolerance."

Disposition: actioned. A vessel was dispatched to look in on Cairn Station. Its orders did not carry this filing.

Later Filings

Tarroway continued to file. Cartographer's notes, excerpted:

· "The contacts did not stop when Crow came home."
· "Cairn's reports read the same every week. A watch post on a working lane should see something now and then."

Disposition: set aside. The vessel's report found Cairn Station in order. Returns at the limit of Strake's range are carried as unconfirmed; Strake's earlier range-limit return on the shoal bearing proved to be scope bloom.
