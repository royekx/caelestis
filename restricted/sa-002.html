Summary

Fleet Intelligence maintains a standing registry of unusual celestial phenomena reported by Fleet vessels, surveyed parties, and contracted observers across surveyed systems. The majority of entries describe ordinary astronomical events closed without anomaly. A smaller subset remain open pending follow-up. The selected entries below are reproduced for record.
