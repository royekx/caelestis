Case #LG-001
Stores Audit — Strake Depot, Contracted Carriers
Gold — Bridge
Date Filed: [Redacted]
Filed By: [Redacted]
Distribution: Bridge Command Only
Clearance: Gold — Bridge
Origin: Caelestis Sector
Summary

Audit of stores received at the Strake Station depot against the manifests of the contracted carriers that delivered them, over one supply rotation. Raised on a quartermaster's complaint that the lamp oil would not light.

Discrepancies

· Six crates manifested as lamp oil held coiled rope. The oil was found at Hollis Post, manifested as rope.
· One crate of dried rations counted twice, once at loading and once at receipt, under two different manifest numbers.
· A crate of replacement lenses for the long scope arrived a rotation late, correctly labelled, behind a cargo of salt.
· Two carriers' manifests were signed off at the depot without the crates being opened. Practice at the depot is to open one crate in ten.

Findings

The crossed crates trace to one contracted carrier's loading clerk, who labelled two holds from the same list. No loss of stores beyond the double count. The carrier's contract is amended to require a second count at loading.

The depot is reminded of the one-in-ten practice.

Status

Closed.
