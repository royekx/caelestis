Standing Order — Above Bridge. This order remains in effect until rescinded by Bridge command. No subsequent review has been filed at this clearance.
Order

No Fleet vessel, surveyed party, or contracted asset shall transit beyond Strake Station on this bearing without written authorisation from Bridge Command. The restriction covers the edge run, the Cairn approach in its entirety, and Cairn Station itself.

Strake Station remains operational, crewed, and open to routine transit. It is the authorised limit of the bearing and the staging point for any authorised movement past it.

Order issued following the loss of Cairn Station (Case #LG-003) and the hostile action against the relief vessel dispatched in response (Case #WM-002), which aborted short of the station. Cause not determined. Recovery operations: suspended indefinitely.
