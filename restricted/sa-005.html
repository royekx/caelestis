Standing Order — Escorts on the Cairn Lane
Above Bridge
Date Filed: [Redacted]
Filed By: [Redacted]
Distribution: Bridge Command Only
Clearance: Above Bridge
Origin: Caelestis Sector
Standing Order — Above Bridge. This order remains in effect until rescinded by Bridge command.
Order

Escort vessels are assigned to the Cairn lane on a standing rotation. Fleet vessels and contracted carriers bound along the lane travel under escort or on the escort schedule. Escort crews call at Cairn Station for stores and rest.

Order issued following the raid on Bellwether Crow on the Cairn approach (Case #WM-002). The order stands against the pirate ship Saltjaw, reported in Case #WM-002, which takes people on the Cairn lane and transfers them to other vessels. She left the action on the Cairn approach untaken and remains at large. Her name is painted on her hull. In action she flies the common pirate crossbones, which identify no one; escorts know her by her hull. Escort crews hold Saltjaw as hostile on sight and report any sighting of her to Caelestis. Escorts are also to watch for transfers of people between vessels on the lane and report any they observe (Case #WM-002).

Cairn Station is thanked for the assistance it rendered to Bellwether Crow and its surviving crew. The station remains the Fleet's authority on the Cairn lane and continues to report directly to Caelestis.

SJN-2421  —  Dashforest, C.  —  Crew Lead  —  Lost to a color pool

Dashforest, C., led a party of the complement aboard a hostile vessel during the action, and they took her helm. A color pool took that vessel. Dashforest and the crew who went with him are carried as lost to it; neither they nor the vessel have been recovered. Their seizure of that vessel drew half the hostile force off Bellwether Crow and made the recovery of the persons held captive possible; they are honored for it.

Decoration awarded to one surviving crewmate, Junior Spelljammer Joffrey, for conduct at the helm during and after the action and for leading the recovery of the persons held captive; citation filed with Case #WM-002.

Implementation

Order effective from filing date. Lane designation updated in Fleet routing databases. Escort schedule and routes held at Caelestis and not reproduced in this file. Annual review of standing required.

Escort reports and Cairn Station's reports since the order: lane quiet. No transfers between vessels observed.
