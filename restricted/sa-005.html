Standing Order — Escorts Against "Saltjaw"
Gold — Bridge
Date Filed: [Redacted]
Filed By: [Redacted]
Distribution: Bridge Command Only
Clearance: Gold — Bridge
Origin: Caelestis Sector
Standing Order. This order remains in effect until rescinded by Bridge command.
Order

Escort vessels are assigned to the Cairn lane on a standing rotation. Fleet vessels and contracted carriers bound along the lane travel under escort or on the escort schedule. Escort crews call at Cairn Station for stores and rest.

Order issued following the engagement on the Cairn approach (Case #WM-002). The order stands against the vessel commanded by the pirate known as "Saltjaw", which was transferring captives to another vessel on the Cairn lane when engaged. The vessel carries no name on her hull. In action she flies the common pirate crossbones, which identify no one. Escorts know her by the description given by survivors and recovered persons. Escort crews hold her as hostile on sight and report any sighting of her to Caelestis. Escorts are also to watch for transfers of people between vessels on the lane and report any they observe.

Cairn Station is thanked for the assistance it rendered to Bellwether Crow and its surviving crew. The station remains the Fleet's authority on the Cairn lane and continues to report directly to Caelestis.

Implementation

Order effective from filing date. Lane designation updated in the Fleet's lane registers. Escort schedule and routes held at Caelestis and not reproduced in this file. Annual review of standing required.

Escort vessels are drawn from the rotation that also serves the Hollis lane; crews are relieved at Strake Station, the nearest depot. One escort reported a drifting hull off the lane, long abandoned and stripped, identity not established; logged and left.

Escort reports and Cairn Station's reports since the order: lane quiet. No transfers between vessels observed.
