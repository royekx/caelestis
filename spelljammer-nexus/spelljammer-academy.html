Cadet Welcome Brief — Caelestis Spelljammer Academy Issued to all incoming cadets upon assignment
Assignment to Caelestis

You have been assigned to Caelestis Station, home of the Caelestis Spelljammer Academy: a Fleet station in orbit around Toril, in Realmspace. Your time here is built around a single objective, which is to make you a working member of a spelljamming crew.

We do not train soldiers or explorers. We train crew, and the distinction matters. A soldier operates within a command structure directed at a specific enemy. An explorer operates largely alone, toward a personal objective. A crew member works with others toward a shared goal that changes with every mission. Caelestis produces people who can work in that third condition, under pressure, in the drift, beside strangers who will need to trust them.

The Caelestis Sector

Caelestis is one of the four sectors of the United Intergalactic Fleet: the Caelestis Sector, which includes Toril's system. The sector has two parts. The Caelestis Corps is the sector's branch of the Fleet and oversees the sector. The Caelestis Spelljammer Academy trains cadets to become Driftwardens, and it is one of only a handful of spelljammer academies in the galaxy. Both are housed at Caelestis, and the sector's command there is the Bridge.

The Academy is run by its headmaster. Boatswain Tarto manages cadet operations directly.

Cadets and Driftwardens

Everyone in the Fleet's service is a Driftwarden, a warden of the drift. You are training to become one. A cadet holds no Fleet rank until graduation; on graduating, a Driftwarden is assigned to a sector and holds the rank of a new graduate, with officers and commanders above. Rank is covered in the Fleet brief.

Clearance is a separate matter from rank. Your access star sets where on the station you may go and which records you may open. Administration issues access stars, and a star can be raised when your work requires it.

Structure of Training

Orientation covers the fundamentals: station layout, security levels, equipment issue, and the Nexus. You are responsible for knowing where you are allowed to go and acting accordingly. Security checkpoints throughout Caelestis are not procedural formalities.

After orientation, training moves through the Simulation Deck, practical exercises in the drift, and mission assignments of increasing complexity and independence. Each stage introduces conditions you have not met before you meet them in earnest. The sequence is intentional; do not skip ahead of it.

Instructors and officers are addressed by rank. Questions about assignments, postings and the training schedule go to Boatswain Tarto's office. Questions about the station itself, its history or the broader Fleet go to the Nexus reference library, where you are now.

A Note on Expectations

Caelestis does not produce cadets who know everything about the drift. It produces cadets who know how to work when they don't. Space is large, and most of it has not been charted to Fleet standard. Most of the situations you will meet aboard a working vessel are covered by no manual currently in this library. What training gives you is a framework that holds when the situation outpaces the manual.

Hold the framework. The rest is experience.
