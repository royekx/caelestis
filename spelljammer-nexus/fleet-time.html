Cycles

The Fleet keeps time by cycles, and the galaxy is the clock. Light runs out along each of the Erevos Galaxy's four spiral arms in turn, from root to tip, and when all four arms have lit, one cycle has passed. The count belongs to no world, which is why ships and stations across the galaxy can share it. Most people call it cycles, or Fleet time. The current cycle is 1491.

Kindlings and Embers

A cycle divides into kindlings. A kindling is one marker cluster lit along an arm as the light spreads, and sixteen kindlings make a cycle, numbered through it from the first. A kindling divides into embers. An ember is one day-length count on an armillary, and there are about thirty to a kindling.

An armillary is an attuned instrument that responds to which arm is lit and how far the light has spread. Fleet navigators carry one, and a ship's log is dated from it.

Writing a Date

A Fleet log writes a date as cycle, kindling and ember, separated by points: 1491.7.12 is the twelfth ember of the seventh kindling of Cycle 1491. A span runs from one date to another, 1491.7.3–1491.7.12. A passage is counted in embers: fourteen embers to H'Catha.

The current Fleet date is 1491.7.12.

Local Calendars

People on a world keep their own world's calendar, with its own days, months and years, and travellers convert. A span counted in a world's own years is converted to cycles before the Fleet files it.
