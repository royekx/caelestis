Who They Are

The Watchers are a priesthood that watches worlds. Each Watcher is posted across the Erevos Galaxy to a world or to a region of one, and stays there for years. Watchers watch, take note and report what they see, and for the most part they do not act on it.

Where They Are Posted

Watchers are posted where populations are large, where civilizations stand, and where a world understands magic deeply. A large world, or one with many peoples, may hold several Watchers at once.

The Watchers and the Fleet

The Watchers are the Fleet's most frequent passengers and its closest partners. Fleet ships carry Watchers to their posts and home again. A Watcher who sees trouble coming on a world passes a tip to the Fleet, and the Fleet takes contracts on those tips.

Glimpses

The Watchers call their trainees Glimpses. Glimpses train for about a century before they are christened Watchers. A Glimpse near the end of training may be sent to finish it in the field, under a Watcher already posted there.

How to Know One

The Watchers' emblem, borne on their shields, is a white spire crowned with a blue-violet light. A Watcher's magic shows as violet light and cosmic dust, with the shape of something larger standing behind the caster.
