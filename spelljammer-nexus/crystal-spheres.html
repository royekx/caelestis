Systems and Spheres

Everything beyond a world's sky lies within the Astral Sea. A system — a sun or suns and its orbiting bodies — carries its own name: Realmspace, Virenspace. The stretch of the Astral Sea between systems is Wildspace. The Astral Sea's intensity is not uniform; it changes between systems and between spheres. Caelestis Station operates in Realmspace, the system of Toril and its neighbours.

Neighbouring systems share a region of the Astral Sea. Vessels travel between them through Wildspace along lanes, graded in Fleet records by how well each route is known: charted lanes, long hauls, and uncharted routes. Refer to Navigation Records for current lane data.

Fleet navigators call each such region a sphere. Realmspace and its neighbouring systems make up one sphere. Fleet charts cover this sphere; other spheres are known largely by report.

The Flow

Beyond the bounds of a sphere lies the Flow, also called the phlogiston — the medium that fills the space between spheres. It is not Wildspace. It is a turbulent, luminous substance that has been described by those who have sailed it as beautiful, disorienting, and relentlessly hostile to the unprepared.

Every navigator bound for inter-sphere travel must understand two properties of the Flow without exception.

The first is combustibility. The Flow ignites. Open flame in the Flow does not produce a contained fire — it produces a catastrophe. Fleet vessels in Flow transit operate with all external flame sources sealed or extinguished. The heating and cooking arrangements aboard vessels are specifically designed for Flow operation. Any breach of this protocol risks the loss of the ship. This is recorded here as information, not as emphasis. The emphasis belongs to whoever is responsible for fire discipline aboard your vessel.

The second is divine silence. In the Flow, the connection between a mortal and their god is severed. Divine spells fail. Clerical contact with higher powers does not reach. The mechanism is not agreed upon — it has been the subject of scholarly argument for as long as there has been inter-sphere travel — but the effect is consistent. A paladin in the Flow is a paladin without their patron. Plan accordingly.

Inter-Sphere Transit

Inter-sphere transit is uncommon. Crossings into the Flow are restricted to senior crews and qualified navigators, and crossing procedure is withheld from cadet distribution. Cadet operations take place within this sphere.
