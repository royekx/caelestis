The Helm

A spelljammer helm is a magically prepared seat — in most configurations a throne or large chair — that allows a spellcasting occupant to propel a vessel through the Astral Sea. Attunement to the helm is near-immediate for a qualified caster. Once attuned, the spelljammer's will becomes the ship's propulsion: the vessel moves, turns, accelerates and stops at the spelljammer's directed intent. Experienced spelljammers report that the connection, at its most refined, feels like extended sensation, the hull as a skin and the drift as the space around it.

The helm takes the spelljammer's concentration, and crews plan their watches around it. A practised spelljammer handles a ship more surely than a new one.

Vessel Types in Common Use

The following vessel types are common in Realmspace and likely to appear in training.

Hammerhead

A mid-sized combat and multipurpose vessel: compact, durable, well armed for its size, and heavy at the bow. The hammerhead's main weapons bear forward, which suits it to pursuit and direct engagement.

Squid Ship

Fast, light and agile. The squid ship's long hull and aft appendages let it change heading more quickly than heavier vessels, and pirates and independent captains favour it for that. It carries less crew and fewer weapons than a hammerhead, so a squid ship is outgunned in a straight fight, and a chase turns on who is at the helm.

Galleon

A large cargo vessel adapted for the drift: slow, heavily loaded, and built for hauling rather than combat. Its galley feeds a large crew and its holds move significant cargo, which makes it the usual ship for resupply and transport.

Crew Roles

A spelljamming crew follows a naval pattern adapted to the drift. Its stations are the captain, the spelljammer at the helm, the navigator, the gunners and the shipmates who work the deck. The spelljammer is the one station with no equivalent on a surface ship.

On a small vessel each crew member covers more than one station: the navigator may also be the spelljammer, and a shipmate may double as a gunner. Assign stations, and train for every station your vessel requires.
