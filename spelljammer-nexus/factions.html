Faction Intelligence Overview — Known Powers of Wildspace, Realmspace Distribution Caelestis Corps · Cadet distribution

This brief covers the organisations and peoples most likely to be encountered during standard Fleet operations in and around Realmspace. It is not exhaustive. Disposition ratings reflect general Fleet posture and should not be substituted for current operational intelligence in specific contact situations.

Mercane Neutral — Commercial

Blue-skinned giants who trade across the drift as commercial intermediaries. Mercane operate under a strict commercial neutrality — they deal with most parties, take no sides in conflicts they are not party to, and protect their interests with considerable resources when those interests are threatened. They sell spelljammer helms and other goods a crossing needs.

Their word, when formally given in a commercial context, is considered reliable. Their prices are rarely open to negotiation. Their motives are entirely their own and not shared with customers.

Giff Neutral — Mercenary

Hippo-headed humanoids organised around a culture of professional soldiering and a deep institutional affection for gunpowder. Giff serve as mercenaries throughout the Erevos Galaxy. They maintain elaborate codes of military conduct, taken entirely seriously.

As hired crew or contracted forces, Giff are reliable, skilled, and largely indifferent to their employers' politics provided payment arrives correctly and operational parameters are established clearly upfront.

Githyanki Hostile — Default

Githyanki raid across the Astral Sea, operating against vessels that pass through regions they claim as territory. They are disciplined, well-trained, and capable spelljammers. Their relationship with worldbound peoples is one of contempt and practical predation. Fleet doctrine treats Githyanki contact as hostile unless specific intelligence indicates otherwise.

Githyanki in the employ of non-Githyanki parties are encountered occasionally. This reflects their mercenary availability, not a change in their general posture toward others.

Pirates and Independent Raiders Variable — Assess on Contact

Piracy in Wildspace is endemic and not confined to any single species or organisation. Opportunists operate from asteroid fields, derelict installations, and outer-system shadow zones, targeting cargo vessels and under-armed transits. Most piracy in Realmspace is opportunistic and small-scale. Fleet doctrine for suspected pirate contact: maintain distance, assess numerical and armament balance, do not close to boarding range without tactical clarity.

Neogi Hostile — No Exceptions

Arachnid-eel hybrids with a culture organised entirely around domination and the acquisition of slaves. The Neogi regard most other sentient species as property or tools and have no interest in any relationship with the Fleet or its vessels other than as a source of either. Their umber hulk thralls complicate any engagement significantly.

Fleet doctrine: treat Neogi contact as hostile without qualification. There is no recorded instance of productive diplomatic exchange with Neogi captains. There is no expected future instance.
