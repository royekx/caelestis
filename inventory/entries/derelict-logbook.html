Derelict Logbook
Ship Log · Incomplete

The salvage objective of the Voyage 002 simulation exercise: a ship's log aboard a derelict adrift in an asteroid field. It was found in the captain's quarters, behind the curled remains of something long dead.

Boogie collected it, held it a moment, and slipped it inside his own chest without quite explaining the impulse to himself. When the exercise called for the log to be produced, Casey had him bring it back out and conjured a duplicate; Gregory made another.

The exercise ended. The log did not go back. It is still in Boogie's chest.
