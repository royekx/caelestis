Derelict Logbook
Ship’s log

The logbook the crews raced for in the salvage exercise, found behind the body of something long decomposed. Boogie slipped it into his own chest and has carried it since. Casey and Gregory each bound a duplicate of its pages during the githyanki attack, so three identical copies exist.
