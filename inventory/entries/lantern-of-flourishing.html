Lantern of Flourishing
Containment engine, fourth series

A small iron lamp six or eight inches tall, with a handle and an iris shutter over a faint green light. Ostekk-6 knows it as the containment engine, fourth series, built by Ostekk and kept and studied by Vaelorix, who called it the Lantern of Flourishing. It holds three charges that renew each day, and each one mends a body, constructs better than the living. Ostekk-10 was sent to bring it back.

Voyage 006
  • Found by Gregory's pull beneath the roots, between two of the dead beholder's eyestalks
  • Read with a spell: three charges a day, each mending a body, constructs better than the living
  • A charge spent on Ostekk-6 set his body rebuilding below the waist
  • Hidden in Tumak's bag of holding while Tumak told Ostekk-10 nothing had been found
