Mirt's Wardrobe
Magical portal object

Mirt's wardrobe is an impossible object that opens onto a bounded stretch of astral night rather than ordinary space, a magical replica of a place beyond H’Catha in the Viren star system, gifted to Mirt by the spellcaster Zerathis.
