Mirt's Wardrobe
Magical portal object

Mirt's wardrobe is an impossible object that opens not onto ordinary space but a bounded stretch of astral night, a magical replica of a place beyond H’catha in the Viren star system, gifted to Mirt by the spellcaster Zerathis.
