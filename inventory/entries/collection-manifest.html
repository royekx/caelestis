Collection Manifest
Etched Slate · Tyrant Ship

A standing inventory kept aboard the tyrant ship, etched into stone rather than written. It records specimens by entry number, origin, and disposition — a collection catalogue for a vessel that was gathering living things.

Two hands are present on it. The clerical entries are machine-stamped and orderly. Written over and around them, in a second hand, are corrections, refusals, and instructions that do not read as clerical at all.

It gave the crew the autognome's designation: Ostekk-6. Its final line records a change of course.

The Object Itself

The manifest survived intact and can be read directly. Both hands are legible.
