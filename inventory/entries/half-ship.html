Incomplete Model Spelljamming Ship
Mysterious object

A tiny model spelljamming ship that formed inside Boogie for reasons he cannot explain, half of it inexplicably missing. It is the same model as the personal miniature ship stolen from the instructor Saerthe — a connection Boogie has kept to himself.
