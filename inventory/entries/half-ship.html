Half Ship
Model Spelljammer · Incomplete

A miniature spelljamming ship of the same make used in the Nexus helm exercises. Roughly half of it is missing — not broken away, not scorched, simply absent, as though the model had only ever been made to that point.

Boogie drew it out of his own chest on the first night at Caelestis. He has no explanation for how it formed there, why it is incomplete, or what the missing half would have been.

In Voyage 002 the crew learned that a miniature had gone missing from Saerth Abyzine's instruction set — and that it was the same model. Whether the half in Boogie's chest is that miniature, a copy of it, or something that only resembles it has not been established.
