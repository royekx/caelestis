Player Reference
Ship Mechanics
Flying, fighting, and surviving in Wildspace.
Your Ship
Armor Class
What an attacker needs to hit the ship, the same as a creature's AC.
Hull
The ship's health; at 0 the ship is dead in space. Hull comes back slowly, over repair days.
Bulwark
A buffer in front of Hull. Ship weapons wear it down first. The Boatswain patches it mid-fight, and it refills 10 minutes after one.
Speed
How fast the ship is, in feet. A faster ship has the edge when one side tries to escape.
Crew
The sailors who keep the weapons loaded. A weapon short of crew falls silent.
Mishap Threshold
Half the Bulwark maximum. A single hit that deals this much Hull damage starts a mishap.
Your ships Numbers, zones and parts for each ship are on its ship sheet: the tyrant ship and the Flighty Foundling. Your role's abilities are on Bridge Crew Roles.
✦ ✦ ✦
Turns
Initiative
Each side rolls once. Your Captain rolls d20 + Cha or Wis for the crew; the enemy's leader rolls for theirs.
Your Order
On your side's turn, the crew acts in any order you choose. Usually the Spelljammer goes first and sets the range.
Your Turn
You keep your whole turn: action, bonus action, movement and reaction. Everything your character can do still works.
Your Role
Your role adds a passive, a menu of bonus actions and at most one reaction. Using it is optional, and its passive always works.
Taking a Seat
Roles stay fixed for the fight. If someone goes down, anyone can take their seat as an action.

Shifting the range. Each ship's helm shifts the range on its own turn, for free: close one band, hold, or open one band. The Spelljammer's Burn shifts one band further.
When it can't. A slowed ship cannot shift the band or Burn. A Crippled ship cannot Burn.
Weapon reach. A ballista reaches 120/480 ft. A mangonel reaches 200/800 ft and cannot hit anything within 60 ft.

Zones
Four zones. A typical ship has a Helm, a Weapon Deck, Below and Rigging. The tyrant ship's zones are on its sheet.
Where you stand. Nobody tracks it until it matters. The Spelljammer is at the Helm and everyone else is on the Weapon Deck.
Marked zones. A mishap marks the zone it hits, and so do boarders at the rail. Fixing a marked zone needs someone in it.
Moving. One zone uses your movement. Two zones use your movement and your action (Dash).
Patching Bulwark works from any zone.

Ship Weapons
d20 + Proficiency + Str or Dex
At an eye, you may use your spellcasting ability instead.
Damage: the weapon's dice
The Gunner adds his modifier. Anyone else rolls the dice alone.
Always loaded. The crew keeps every weapon loaded and aimed. A weapon's crew number is how many sailors it needs to stay loaded, and a weapon short of crew cannot fire.
Firing is an action anyone can take.
Once a round. Each weapon fires once a round, unless it is hot loaded.
The crew's shot. The ship's crew fires one weapon each round. You fire the rest.
✦ ✦ ✦
Working Together
Help. Use the Help action to give another crewmate advantage on a role check or a ship weapon attack. Role checks are Quick Fix, Find Cover, the repair check and escape checks. You must be in the same zone, or at the same eye station. This advantage does not stack with Read the Line.
Hot load. A weapon that has already fired this round can fire once more: one of you spends an action loading it (two actions for a mangonel), and another spends an action firing it. Each weapon fires at most twice a round, and the Gunner's Quick Load counts as that second shot. The eyes carry no ammunition, so they cannot be hot loaded.
Stand in as crew. Spend your action working a gun as crew. You count as two crew members this round, which can bring a short-crewed weapon back into action.
✦ ✦ ✦
Damage and Mishaps
Bulwark, then Hull. Ship weapons wear down Bulwark first. Once it is gone, they hit Hull.
Your own attacks barely dent a hull. Aim them at the enemy's crew and officers instead; an area spell can drop several crew at once.
Immune. Ships take no poison or psychic damage.
Heavy hits. A single hit that deals Hull damage of at least the ship's mishap threshold rolls on the mishap table.
Critical hits always roll a mishap, and double their dice.
Crippled. At half Hull or less, the ship's speed is halved and it cannot Burn.

Parts
Every part is listed on the ship sheet: each weapon or eye, the helm, and the rigging or fins.
Working, disabled or destroyed. A disabled part can be fixed mid-fight with a Quick Fix. A destroyed part stays down until the repair days, and a destroyed helm or eye needs a port.
Bulwark can still be Patched whatever state the parts are in.
Breaking an enemy. An enemy ship gives up the fight once its Hull drops to half or less, two of its parts are down, or its leader falls. It then flees, surrenders, or makes a desperate try at boarding.
✦ ✦ ✦
Boarding
Close range, or a grapple, is needed to board.
Crossing over. Boarders cross as a group on their side's turn, as part of their movement, and act when they land. Defenders answer on their own side's turn.
Your role while away. If you board the enemy ship, you leave your role until you come back. A boarded ship fires only its crew's weapon.
Jump, fly, teleport. Any movement your character has works normally.
Big brawls. If a boarding fight grows large, the table may switch to individual initiative, with ship actions on the Spelljammer's turn.
✦ ✦ ✦
Repair
Bulwark refills 10 minutes after a fight.
Hull takes repair days, with the ship stopped. A day repairs 10 × proficiency bonus Hull: 20 a day at level 4, 40 a day at level 10. Each destroyed part adds 1d4 days, except the helm and the eyes, which need a port. Disabled parts are fixed along the way.
The Boatswain's check. When repairs start, the Boatswain makes one DC 15 check (Str, Dex, or Int with tools). A success takes a day off, to a minimum of one; a failure adds a day. Help gives advantage.
Underway, only Bulwark recovers. You choose: stop and spend the days, or push on damaged.
Spells. A repair spell of 3rd level or higher, such as fabricate, stone shape on the tyrant ship, or repair hull, cast during repair days counts as one extra day of repair, once a day. Mending fixes one disabled part outside a fight.
Ports repair 50 Hull a day at 10 gp a point. They are the only place to replace a destroyed helm or eye, and they do full overhauls.
✦ ✦ ✦
The Ship at 0 Hull
Dead in space. The ship cannot move, shift the band or Burn. Its weapons and eyes go quiet, Bulwark is gone, and an enemy can grapple it at will. The air envelope holds, so everyone aboard can breathe. Once repair days bring back 1 Hull, it moves again.
Destroyed outright. A ship at 0 Hull is destroyed only by further damage equal to half its maximum Hull, by 10 minutes of scuttling aboard, or by a Fire left burning for 10 minutes.
Abandoning ship. Take the scuttle ship, a spell such as fly or a teleport, or a boarded enemy ship.
Cut loose in space. You have 1 minute of fresh air, then 1 minute of fouled air with disadvantage on checks. Then you hold your breath for minutes equal to your Con modifier (at least 30 seconds), and then you suffocate. That leaves your crewmates roughly 2–4 minutes, or 20–40 rounds, to reach you.
✦ ✦ ✦
Escape
From Long range only. The Spelljammer spends an action on a spellcasting check contested by the pursuing Spelljammer.
Speed. +2 for every 5 ft the fleeing ship is faster, and −2 for every 5 ft it is slower.
Advantage comes from the Navigator's Find the Gap, or from a pursuer that is slowed or Crippled.
Two wins and you're gone. Wins are kept if the range closes, but the next check waits until the ship is back at Long.
Enemies escape by the same rule.
✦ ✦ ✦
Hazards
What they are. Challenges outside a fight: asteroid clusters, interstellar fog, fire on board, electrical fields. Each is a group skill check.
How they work. Each of you contributes one check, and the crew makes a single roll too. Count the successes: all, most, few or none decides the outcome. The Navigator's Plot rerolls one failed check per hazard.
Be creative. The listed checks are suggestions. If you have a better idea for how your character would help, pitch it to the DM.
