Tyrant Ship Carved-stone beholder vessel · held by the crew
Four of its ten great eyes answer, each at a crew station around the helm on the Command Deck, and all four work from the start. It has no crew weapon: the eyes are its guns, and they need no crew to keep them firing.

Disintegration Gunner (Sol) A ship weapon: 120/480 ft, 5d10 force, plus the Gunner's modifier. A Called Shot through it destroys the part it hits. A creature it drops to 0 turns to dust. +6 to hit, 5d10 + 4 5–6
Telekinesis Boatswain (Casey) Ends a grapple or seizes a grappling line. Or moves one creature (Str save) or an object up to 300 lb up to 30 ft. DC 13 At will
Fear Captain (Tumak) Against a crew: they make one Wis save at +4, and on a failure 1d4 + PB crew are frightened until the end of their next turn and cannot work guns. Against an officer: a Wis save, or frightened for 1 minute, repeating the save each turn. DC 14 4–6
Slowing Navigator (Gregory) One ship's Spelljammer makes a Wis save. On a failure, until the end of its next turn that ship moves at half speed, cannot shift the band, and cannot Jink or Burn. Your escape checks against a slowed ship have advantage. DC 14 4–6
Holding a station. You see through your eye and may cast your spells through it. Using the eye takes your action.
Numbers. Save DCs are 8 + PB + the holder's modifier, and Disintegration's attack is PB + the Gunner's spellcasting ability. The figures above are at level 4.
Reach. The eyes reach 120 ft. Disintegration also reaches Long range, at disadvantage.
Recharge. Every eye starts a fight charged. Roll each spent eye's recharge at the start of your side's turn.
Empty stations. The Medic and the Spelljammer hold no eye. An NPC can hold an empty station (+4 to hit, DC 12). If you leave your station, its eye falls silent unless an NPC takes it.
The other six eyes do not answer.
Zones
Command Deck. The helm and all four eye stations. Everyone starts here.
Shaft and gravity plane. Crossing it takes a DC 12 Dex (Acrobatics) check, or you fall prone.
Hollow Deck. The repair deck, and Below on the mishap table. Breaches and fires land here, and the Boatswain's Shore Up happens here.
Cargo Deck. The intake, and the only way in. The Boatswain's Expel blows boarders out through it once a fight.
Distances. The Hollow Deck and the Cargo Deck are each two zones from the Command Deck. A Torn Rigging mishap lands on an eye station.

Scuttle ship
Docked in the Cargo intake for the crossing: one scuttle ship, seating six. It carries a landing party down to a world, and it is the way off if the ship is lost.
