Retreat Is Always an Option

  • The crew went down to the Hollow Deck, found the ship’s other units torn apart, and woke Ostekk-2.
  • A mechanical behir nearly killed Bartholomew, and when clockwork horrors joined it Boogie called the retreat.
  • Below a plane where the ship’s gravity turns over lies the Command Deck. Magic fails there, wisps drain the living, and the helm belongs beneath a dead beholder.
What each of you did
  • Boogie built Ostekk-6 a pair of legs out of himself, and called the retreat
  • Casey split into copies to cut into the behir, and saw it coiled around a soft light
  • Gregory followed a pull in his chest to wake Ostekk-2, and felt it again at the beholder’s body
  • Sol stayed in the cargo hold with Ryeback and the deckhands to guard the helm
  • Tumak called up his soul knives, poured his potion into Bartholomew and worked out where the helm goes
  • Bartholomew went down in the behir’s jaws and came back, and found his magic would not answer on the Command Deck
What you learned
  • Ostekk-6 is one of nine units that served Vaelorix, a beholder; the creatures destroyed nearly all the others.
  • The Hollow Deck has a repair bay that might mend Ostekk-6.
  • Ostekk-2 believes the behir was sent by the original Ostekk, a brilliant tinkerer.
  • On the Command Deck the dead beholder’s central eye cancels magic, and wisps latch on and drain whoever they touch.
  • The helm belongs on the platform where the beholder’s body lies.
