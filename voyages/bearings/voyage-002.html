What the Crew Offers

  • Bartholomew joined the crew and took the spelljammer’s seat. Over a meal at the Weeping Goddess the cadets settled their shipboard roles.
  • Saerthe’s miniature spelljamming ship has been stolen, and it is the model that came out of Boogie’s chest the night before.
  • In a simulated salvage race through an asteroid field the crew recovered the logbook, fended off a githyanki vessel and came back first.
What each of you did
  • Boogie set the model ship on the table for the crew, then leapt onto the enemy ship and had to be brought back
  • Casey drew Ryeback’s private life out of him, worked a cargo shift at Rindle Gearloft’s dock and duplicated the logbook
  • Gregory plotted the course for the race and named the targets
  • Sol took a stroke of misfortune onto himself to save Boogie’s jump, and heard Beshaba laugh
  • Tumak took the captain’s chair and warned the crew that things going missing might matter
  • Bartholomew took to the helm as if born to it and slowed the ship to bring Boogie back aboard
What you learned
  • Saerthe’s own miniature ship was stolen, the same model that came out of Boogie’s chest.
  • The presence dogging Sol’s luck is Beshaba.
  • The presence that spoke from Mirt’s wardrobe found Casey again at the Sky Dock.
  • Ryeback mentioned a cadet named Barret talking about a hadozee who could read birthdays.
