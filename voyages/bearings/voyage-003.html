The Weight of What’s Real

  • The salvage simulation ended in a real blast. Gregory pried a spent arcane rod from the wall, and Boogie glimpsed the word Vocath on it.
  • Mirt named Vocath an old enemy, asked the crew to look into Joffrey and a man named Jeffrey, paid them and raised them to sailor-level clearance.
  • An adamantine meteor struck the spire on H’Catha. The crew boarded an abandoned tyrant ship to recover it and found clockwork horrors in the hold, feeding on an autognome that wore Gregory’s face.
What each of you did
  • Boogie understood the rod and glimpsed “Vocath” on it, traced the crate at the Sky Dock, and dreamed of other versions of himself
  • Casey helped rival cadets up in the wrecked chamber and went first into the tyrant ship in a shell of ice
  • Gregory pried out the rod, told Mirt of Gregory Prime, and cast at the horrors before he had decided to
  • Sol learned the crate’s shipments route through Mr. Blip toward Toril and H’Catha, and heard a voice ask if he had escaped his origin
  • Tumak found two half-runes on his body, glimpsed “fleet intelligence archive” on Mirt’s notepad, and argued for more clearance
  • Bartholomew pulled rival cadets upright in the wrecked chamber beside Casey
What you learned
  • The rod bore the marking Vocath. Mirt named Vocath an old enemy with a grudge against him, which makes anyone tied to Mirt a target.
  • The crate at the Sky Dock carries the same symbol; its contents route through Mr. Blip toward Toril and H’Catha.
  • “Fleet intelligence archive” appeared on a locked Nexus terminal and on Mirt’s own notepad.
  • A note on Saerthe’s desk asked whether Bartholomew’s shifting form bears on spelljamming.
  • Tumak’s two half-runes are each half of a different whole.
