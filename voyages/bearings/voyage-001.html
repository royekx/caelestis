The Shape of a Crew

  • Five cadets woke into a losing battle aboard the Moonraider. It was a simulation of Captain Sardax’s last stand, a test never meant to be won.
  • They ran orientation across Caelestis, from the quartermaster’s stores to a helm exercise at the Spelljammer Nexus and the gymnasium assault course.
  • A crate delivered to Mirt burst open with centipede creatures. Sol took the last one alive, and more burglaries struck the Academy at the same time.
What each of you did
  • Boogie chose to keep the ship alive over taking the helm, and promised Tumak the crew would help find his family
  • Casey slipped into Mirt’s wardrobe, stole a shirt and etched the constellations he saw
  • Gregory saw through the gymnasium’s first illusion and felt a new pull in his core
  • Sol stood silently behind Boogie in a bunk dispute and took a creature alive as evidence
  • Tumak claimed a crimson bag of holding and made a hidden corner of his bunk
What you learned
  • Sardax’s battle aboard the Moonraider was a simulation of his final defeat.
  • Mirt’s wardrobe is a magical replica of a place beyond H’Catha in the Viren system, a gift from a spellcaster named Zerathis.
  • A voice from the wardrobe told Casey to “Go there.”
  • The attack on Mirt was likely a diversion: other burglaries struck the Academy at the same time.
  • Boogie carries an incomplete model spelljamming ship that formed inside him.
