What Is Here Has Not Left

  • The crew held the tyrant ship and burned the bloom off the Command Deck.
  • Beneath the great flower Gregory found the Lantern of Flourishing, the device Ostekk-10 had asked for. They told it nothing was found and put it out through the cargo hold.
  • Bartholomew caught Miken speaking into a sending stone.
What each of you did
  • Boogie slept through his watch, and asked Ostekk-10 its name
  • Casey climbed head down into the garden and anchored the rope
  • Gregory held the last watch and followed his pull to the engine
  • Sol fought clockwork horrors in the hold beside Ryeback, Pffred and Krik’Lit
  • Tumak told the lie in the crew’s minds, and named Boogie second in command
  • Bartholomew caught Miken with a sending stone, and tore the great flower free as an ape
What you learned
  • The mechanical behir is Ostekk-10, sent by Ostekk, a beholder who made the ship’s units and the engine.
  • The engine is a small iron lamp with an iris shutter and green light: three charges a day, each mending a body.
  • The Command Deck flowers made both the wisps and the field that killed magic.
  • Most things leave the ship through the cargo hold, and nothing aboard puts out a fire.
  • Miken carries a sending stone.
