What Is Here Has Not Left

  • The crew held the tyrant ship. Sol, Ryeback, Pffred and Krik’Lit fought off clockwork horrors in the cargo hold, and the crew told Ryeback they would finish the mission themselves.
  • On the last watch the mechanical behir came up the garden shaft. Speaking through Krik’Lit, it named itself Ostekk-10 and asked for a device on the Command Deck, the containment engine, promising to leave once it had it.
  • The crew cut down the flowers that made the wisps and the field that killed magic, and Bartholomew, as an ape, tore the great flower from the dead beholder. Beneath it Gregory found the engine: the Lantern of Flourishing.
  • When Ostekk-10 returned, Tumak told it nothing had been found. It searched the ship, and Bartholomew ejected it through the cargo hold. The helm was carried up to the Command Deck and installed.
  • On the first watch Bartholomew caught Miken speaking into a sending stone. Miken said he was saying hello to his family.
What each of you did
  • Boogie slept through his watch, and asked Ostekk-10 its name
  • Casey climbed head down into the garden and anchored the rope
  • Gregory held the last watch and followed his pull to the engine
  • Sol fought clockwork horrors in the hold beside Ryeback, Pffred and Krik’Lit
  • Tumak planned the lie in the crew’s minds, told it to Ostekk-10 and named Boogie second in command
  • Bartholomew caught Miken with a sending stone, and tore the great flower free as an ape
What you learned
  • The mechanical behir is Ostekk-10, sent by Ostekk, a beholder who made the ship’s units and the engine.
  • The engine is a small iron lamp with an iris shutter and green light: three charges a day, each mending a body.
  • The Command Deck flowers made both the wisps and the field that killed magic.
  • Most things leave the ship through the cargo hold, and nothing aboard puts out a fire.
