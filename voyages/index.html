Voyage 002 What the Crew Offers Open Record
Bartholomew joins the crew over lunch at the Weeping Goddess. The six begin to knit together — Casey draws out Ryeback, Boogie shows the crew the half-ship from his chest, and the table sorts out who sits where on a real vessel. Casey works a Sea Dock job for Rindle Gearloft and feels the familiar presence at the Sky Dock rail. The next morning: a salvage simulation against Miken's crew. The hammerhead wins the race, recovers the logbook, fends off a githyanki ambush — and Sol, feeling misfortune reach for Boogie mid-jump, takes it onto himself and hears Beshaba laugh for the first time.

Voyage 003 The Weight of What's Real Open Record
The salvage simulation ends in a real explosion — an arcane charge planted in the chamber. Gregory recovers the device, marked Vocath. The crew investigates: Mirt confirms Vocath's old grudge against him, grants them sailor-level clearance, and points them toward Joffrey and the phrase fleet intelligence archive. That night each of them is touched by something larger — Sol by Beshaba, Boogie by a dream of countless other Boogies converging. Then Tarto wakes them at four in the morning: an adamantine meteor has struck the spire on H'Catha. They board a beholder-carved tyrant ship to reach it — and in its cargo hold find clockwork horrors feeding on a broken autognome that wears Gregory's face.

Voyage 004 All Things Find Their Way Home Open Record
The crew clears the clockwork horrors in the tyrant ship's cargo hold and finds the broken autognome — Ostekk-6 — whose name comes off the manifest. Its wounds are mechanical, past what magic can mend. Tumak manifests a telepathic link to speak with it, and Ostekk-6 warns of a mimic aboard and a bloom spreading through the ship. Given a floating dolly to move the spelljamming helm toward the command deck, the crew routes into the access shafts and finds the garden choked with lantern-wisp flowers. They clear the flower in a hard fight — Sol and Casey both casting Arms of Hadar, Tumak throwing his first fire bolt — but the wisps remain and bite harder than expected. Tumak ends it by summoning his soul knife for the first time, feeling a tether to home as the Rune Keeper's voice curves the blade true. Bloodied, the crew decides to rest before descending.
